## What this revision now explains

R1 named the required capabilities. R2 explains how the system decides among them:

```text
decision gap -> best capable source -> enrich, ask, convene or abstain
             -> natural answer grammar -> immediate route change
             -> durable receipt -> later correction-aware reuse
```

It also gives “magical” a falsifiable meaning. The product must arrive prepared, recognise the exact context, ask something answerable, show the consequence, remember appropriately and stay quiet when no move is earned.

## Strongest challenge

The added planner could become a large orchestration ontology that delays the actual product. The defence is a deliberately small headless proof: one coverage object, one enrichment choice, one typed asynchronous question, one session opportunity and one abstention across deterministic evidence states. Final schema normalisation and provider selection remain open.

The experience contract could also become another document that sounds intuitive without being intuitive. R2 therefore refuses to mark usability passed until fresh people can complete the rendered flow on actual devices without facilitator explanation.
