## Outcome

A busy leader can understand one proposed change to their Brain, decide whether it should become their current standard, inspect the evidence only when they want it and understand that they can undo the change while it remains current. The first screen must make sense in seconds on a phone. It must not expose the machinery the leader already paid the product to handle.

## Design task contract

| Field | R117 contract |
|---|---|
| State of use | Between consequential work, often one-handed on a phone, with little patience for setup or product vocabulary |
| User and action | The subject owner decides whether one checked learning should become part of the Brain |
| Governing rule | AI may propose and explain. Only the person may change the standard that represents their judgement |
| Data truth | One immutable R116 packet supplies exact before, after, evidence references, consequence and reversible-while-current standing |
| Materiality | New material leader-facing surface. It requires a rendered founder gate before product implementation |
| Brand and voice | Dark, quiet and instrument-like. Mindmaker icon only. Plain enough for a twelve-year-old. Warm with the person and exact about the evidence |
| Authority | Local proof and non-production preview only. No production, merge, release, customer data or legacy retirement authority |
| Proof | Functional wide, mobile and 320px render; keyboard and touch; reduced motion; ready, approved, rejected, stale, reversed, loading, error and long-content states; evidence and reversal detail on demand |

## Product contract

- One proposed change, one plain consequence and one primary action appear in the first view.
- The alternative remains easy to reach but visually secondary.
- Evidence, before and after wording, source standing and reversal mechanics use progressive disclosure.
- No confidence theatre, score, streak, profile-completion language or hidden effect.
- No generic business advice. The proposal must name the person's actual standard, what produced the learning and what future work changes.
- Approval cannot be mistaken for publication, deployment or permission to act outside the Brain.
- Stale or unavailable state becomes non-actionable. It never invites the leader to approve old information.
- The completed state confirms the human decision in one sentence and keeps undo available without turning the screen into an audit console.

## Independent concept gate

Before one direction is shown to Krish:

1. Render at least three materially different interaction spines.
2. Keep the brief identical and the candidates anonymous during review.
3. Judge comprehension, human agency, evidence honesty, consequential value, mobile interaction, accessibility, brand fit and implementation reality.
4. Reject any candidate that needs explanatory narration, makes the Brain sound like an oracle or turns the choice into e-learning.
5. If judges disagree, use a fresh blinded tiebreak rather than averaging incompatible opinions.
