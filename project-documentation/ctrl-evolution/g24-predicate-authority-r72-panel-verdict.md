## Required repair

R73 must:

1. separate mechanically observed facts, bounded normative human attestations and deterministic derived facts;
2. bind every fact to atomic source assertions or spans, subject, speaker, epistemic basis, applicability, purpose, audience, consequence, derivation policy and contrary evidence;
3. prove selection completeness with an authoritative universe, query policy, head or epoch and sealed complete member set;
4. return a typed disposition for every dependency, not only one tri-state summary;
5. classify every field as `server_verify_only` or `human_answerable` and forbid asking humans for administration;
6. bind every human-answerable fact to the accepted decision frame, answer grammar, unknown path and material route effect;
7. restore the intensive-proof continuation checkpoint and all inherited lifecycle details;
8. atomically join the predicate proof, exact human authority, dispatch snapshot, transaction time, predecessor and reserved receipt;
9. implement G13-complete correction and downstream dependency repair requirements at contract level;
10. mechanically constrain signed inputs and preserve a narrow exception for an external system that indisputably owns the source truth.

## Direction preserved

The panel did not reject the single-path architecture. A+C remains the preferred direction if repaired. The strongest improvement is an authority-species split: mechanical facts are proven mechanically, normative facts arrive as explicit bounded human attestations, and derived facts are deterministic projections of those two. One deterministic transition rule may require all three, but none may impersonate another.

R72 remains rejected evidence. It is not implementation or founder-lock authority.
