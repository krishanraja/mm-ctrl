# G25 human review binding R118 strategy

Status: implemented and verified in isolated development; production and release closed
Date: 2026-09-20
Depends on: R116 owner gate and founder-approved R117 human projection

## The gap R118 closes

R116 proved that only the subject owner can approve, reject or reverse a standard change. R117 proved how that decision should feel: one exact question, one proposed rule, one consequence and one evidence route. The remaining risk was interpretation drift between them. The R116 packet did not freeze every plain-language field the R117 screen needs, so a frontend or model could create a more persuasive explanation after the packet had been hashed.

R118 makes the words the leader sees part of the object the leader approves. The packet now freezes:

- the exact question;
- the current and proposed rules;
- the practical consequence;
- the risk if the change is wrong;
- the validation plan;
- at least one alternative explanation;
- at least two resolved ledger statements with source identity, date and relationship.

If any required meaning cannot be produced from the accepted proposal and its cited ledger rows, preparation fails closed. The product does not fill the gap with generic advice.

## Experience rule

The R117 first view is preserved exactly. Detail remains available behind one `See why` disclosure. The leader sees no conveyor, hash or internal state unless they deliberately open the technical record. Both action labels share one centred treatment. Saving uses one inline state. A stale packet removes the decision controls. Reversal always requires confirmation.

## Authority and data boundary

The V2 Edge Function is authenticated and has `no-store` responses. Only its preparation action points to the new presentation-complete RPC. Approval, rejection and reversal still use the R116 machinery. The private presentation helper is not executable by anonymous or authenticated roles. The public preview uses an in-memory fixture gateway and writes nothing.

The migration and function were applied only to isolated Supabase project `cgkcplcamsijghalintq`. Production writes, customer data, merge, release, cutover and legacy retirement remain closed.
