## Classification

- **Product direction:** still useful, but the private operator surface must optimise for work done, not editorial theatre.
- **Information structure:** failed. Desktop space is allocated to announcing the system rather than helping Krish understand and prepare the consequential decision.
- **Interaction:** a no-scroll desktop instrument is explicitly permitted and preferred when it preserves the important evidence and controls.
- **Copy:** failed. Large declarative titles feel pompous and self-satisfied even when their literal wording is accurate.
- **Visual execution:** failed. The current rhythm makes the page feel awkward and wastes the wide viewport.
- **Data truth:** not challenged.

## Binding requirements

1. On a 1440 by 900 desktop viewport, Krish can understand the current intervention, the evidence behind it, the unresolved risk and the next action without page scrolling.
2. The screen spends space on decision support, evidence, comparison and action. It does not spend space announcing its own intelligence.
3. Headings identify objects or actions. They do not perform as editorial proclamations.
4. The Living Brain remains inspectable and visually distinctive, but it must function as evidence for the decision rather than as an isolated showpiece.
5. The customer view, provenance, correction history and private operator boundary remain intact.
6. Mobile may use progressive disclosure, but it must not inherit oversized announcement copy merely because the desktop did.

## Reset decision

R2 is not approved. This is the second rejected revision inside the Intervention Lens spine. The next step is fresh, blinded divergence using a sanitized brief. No R3 refinement of the same spine is authorised. The new synthesis must be shown cold and receive explicit founder approval before implementation or any later material surface.
