# G23 product spine concept B: The working proof

**Concept revision:** B1
**Artifact:** one responsive founder alignment surface
**Approval state:** concept spine only

## Design contract

**State of use:** The founder sees this remotely on a phone or desktop, with one minute to understand the product and enough interest to inspect one deeper example.
**User and action:** The founder must understand what the private AI Brain is, how the leader, expert operator and Brain work together, and why the relationship is more useful later than at the start. The only approval is the product spine.
**Governing rule:** The Brain prepares, questions, remembers and repairs. The operator challenges and chooses when an interruption is worth it. The leader sets the intent, makes the call, applies taste and owns the result.
**Data truth:** Every claim carries both a source type and a standing. Sparse, provisional, disputed, stale and unknown states remain useful and visible.
**Materiality:** Material. This sets the governing product relationship.
**Brand and voice:** Cool technical ground, clear human language and one amber signal colour. Amber means that the standing of an idea changed. It is not decoration.
**Authority:** This artifact can secure approval for the product spine. It does not approve an interface, implementation, feature set or release.
**Proof required next:** A rendered phone and desktop version, tested with sparse, long, stale, disputed, loading and error states, plus keyboard, touch, zoom and reduced-motion checks.

## 1. Governing visual and interaction metaphor

The product is shown as a **working proof**, like a page that becomes clearer through careful marks and revisions before a final decision is owned.

The page is always the leader's. The Brain prepares the first draft and adds evidence notes. The expert operator writes challenges in the outer margin. The leader's purpose, judgement and final call sit in the centre. A correction never wipes out the old mark. It creates a new version, shows what changed and repairs any later preparation that depended on it.

This is not a document editor. The proof is the recurring product unit for any high-consequence decision about category, organisation, people or the leader's own role. It makes the relationship visible without turning the Brain into a person or suggesting that it can know someone completely.

The surface reads from top to bottom as one unfolding proof. Desktop gives the centre page room, with narrow contextual margins. Phone places each margin note directly after the line it qualifies. There are no panels with their own scrolling.

## 2. What the founder sees in the first ten seconds

The opening view contains three things only.

> A private AI Brain that gets better through your real decisions.
>
> It arrives prepared, asks only when an answer could change the call, and leaves the judgement with you.

Directly below, one compact role key reads:

**Leader decides and owns** · **Operator challenges and times the interruption** · **Brain prepares, tests and learns**

Below that sits a paired glimpse of the same proof unit at two moments:

- **At the start:** a provisional reading, clearly marked evidence, one material unknown and one small hard question.
- **Later:** a sharper preparation whose changed parts point back to accepted evidence, judgement or correction from earlier work.

The difference is visible in the annotations, not claimed through a score, graph or decorative animation.

## 3. How the thirty days are understood

The month appears as six dated editions of the relationship, not six lessons or milestones:

1. **Before the first session:** public research and supplied context form a useful but provisional first proof.
2. **An early real decision:** the Brain prepares the ground and asks one question only where the answer could change the call.
3. **A human call:** the leader chooses, the operator records the challenge, and the Brain records the basis without treating the decision as universal truth.
4. **A correction:** a disputed interpretation remains in history, changes standing and triggers repair of dependent material.
5. **A quiet interval:** the dated page says that no interruption earned attention. Capture and background synthesis can continue without demanding a check-in.
6. **A later decision and owner copy:** preparation begins further ahead because accepted earlier material has become usable context. The leader can inspect and take away the current Brain in human-readable and machine-usable form.

The editions are arranged by date, but there is no percentage, streak, completion state or prescribed pace. Several small decision leaves from category, organisation, people and role sit inside the month so one case cannot stand in for the whole product.

## 4. How the three roles remain distinct

Each role owns a fixed part of every proof and is named in words as well as position.

| Role | Place on the proof | What is visible there |
|---|---|---|
| Leader | Centre page | Purpose, success condition, judgement, chosen human boundary, final call and polish note |
| Expert operator | Outer margin | Context added, weak idea challenged, interruption decision and unresolved consequence |
| Brain | Evidence margin and revision marks | Research, synthesis, source type, inference, unknown, ranked question, dependency and repair |

Colour is not required to tell the roles apart. Position, labels and mark shapes carry the distinction. Amber appears only when the standing of an item changes, such as provisional to accepted or disputed to withdrawn.

## 5. How the Brain's compounding becomes visible

Every later preparation can carry small provenance hooks back to earlier accepted material. Selecting a hook reveals a plain chain:

**Earlier evidence or judgement → standing granted by the leader or operator → where it improved this preparation**

When a correction occurs, the old reading remains legible but struck from active use. Any affected later proof shows a repair mark beside the changed line and a short reason. This makes improvement and self-repair inspectable without claiming that the Brain is complete.

The strongest comparison is local and direct: the first useful question sits beside a later useful question. The later one can skip what is already settled, expose a fresher contradiction or test a consequence that only became visible through prior work. Compounding is therefore shown as better preparation and better questions, not more stored material.

## 6. Where the supporting organs appear

- **Low-friction capture:** A slim capture line belongs to each proof. Voice, short reply, pasted work, forwarded email and document input enter through the same place. Ownership is chosen or confirmed at capture, with personal and company material kept separate.
- **Automatic research and synthesis:** The first visible proof is already prepared before a session. Its notes distinguish public evidence, supplied context, inference and unknowns.
- **Adaptive ranked or tappable questioning:** One question slip appears inside the exact line it could change. When ranking helps, the options are tappable and the Brain states why the question earned an interruption.
- **Deeper decision comparison:** A proof can open into a full-width comparison leaf. Options are tested against the leader's success condition, evidence, consequences, uncertainty and what must remain human. It closes back into the same decision record.
- **Prepared intelligence:** Each upcoming decision or meeting receives its own proof leaf. Category shifts, organisation choices, people consequences and role boundaries share the same relationship model.
- **Transparent Living Brain:** A persistent edge tab opens the current understanding as a readable index of accepted, provisional, disputed and unknown material. It is an inspection layer, not a graph.
- **Evidence, correction and self-repair:** Source and standing sit beside each claim. Correction changes standing, preserves history and marks every dependent repair.
- **Portable release or export:** The final owner copy includes the full context, source types, standing, permissions, decision history, corrections and unresolved unknowns in readable text and structured data.
- **Quiet periods:** A dated quiet leaf records that the threshold for interruption was not met. It proves restraint without pretending that nothing happened.

## 7. Exact final approval moment

The approval sits on the decision proof itself, directly below the leader's call. Before it becomes available, the page shows the purpose and success condition, what is supported, inferred and unknown, the operator's strongest challenge, material consequences and the work that remains human.

The leader taps **This is my call**. A visible record then states who approved it, when, which version was approved and whether its evidence or judgement may shape later work. The approval freezes that decision record, not the leader or the whole Brain. A secondary **Keep open** state preserves the work without converting it into accepted judgement.

For the founder alignment artifact, the final page uses the same pattern once. It asks only whether this relationship is the product spine. No visual system, feature list or implementation detail is bundled into that approval.

## 8. Mobile treatment

Phone is the primary compression test. The centre page becomes one natural vertical reading column. Brain evidence notes follow the exact sentence they qualify. Operator challenges follow the relevant choice. The role label stays attached to every note, so meaning never depends on left and right placement.

The opening proposition and paired early/later glimpse fit in the first phone viewport. Each dated edition is collapsed to a two-line summary, with one edition open at a time. The current question and its response remain together. Tap targets are at least comfortably thumb-sized, focus order follows reading order and every voice or drag interaction has a tap and keyboard path. Draft input persists, with a visible saved state and owner-copy access throughout.

## 9. Strongest risk

The editorial proof metaphor could make the product feel like a document review tool and understate live research, conversation and organisational change.

The defence is structural. The first ten seconds name the private Brain and the human outcome. The month includes different decision arenas, voice and meeting capture, prepared intelligence, interruption restraint and a deeper comparison leaf. The proof remains the place where those capabilities meet, not the claim that all work is a document.

This risk becomes blocking if a cold viewer describes the offer as better document editing after one minute. The concept survives only if they can instead say that it is a private Brain that improves high-consequence AI decisions through real work while the leader stays accountable.

## 10. Why this is materially different

It is not a case workflow because no single sequence is presented as the product. The repeated unit is a decision proof used across category, organisation, people and role questions, with timing set by real work rather than a prescribed funnel.

It is not a dashboard because there is no navigation grid, performance score, feed, widget set or overview demanding attention. The founder reads one relationship and one visible change in the quality of preparation.

It is not an architecture map because the three roles are separated by what they contribute to a live decision, not by boxes, arrows or system components. Evidence standing, correction, permission and approval are experienced where they change the work.

## The call

Take **The working proof** forward as the Concept B spine. Its sharpest edge is that trust and compounding are shown in the same mark: every useful later line can reveal what earned the right to shape it.

The harder version removes all month labels after the opening and lets dated proof editions carry the relationship alone. That would feel less explanatory, but more like a product that demonstrates itself.

The next useful test is a cold one-minute read on one phone and one desktop render. The pass condition is that a viewer can explain the paid outcome, all three roles, the correction rule and why later work improves, without calling it a course, dashboard, document editor or autonomous adviser.

The counterpoint is that the metaphor asks the visual design to make revision marks feel calm and premium rather than bureaucratic. It also depends on strong plain-language examples in the eventual render. Weak examples would turn the proof into process theatre.
