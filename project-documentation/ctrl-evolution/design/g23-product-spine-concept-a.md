# G23 product spine concept A: The Working Proof

**Artifact revision:** Concept A, R1
**Approval state:** Independent concept for blinded comparison
**Downstream owner:** G23 synthesis and judging
**Pending action:** Spine fitness decision before rendering

## Design task contract

- **State of use:** A founder reviews one coherent artifact on a phone or desktop, often between meetings, and needs the product to make sense within one minute.
- **User and action:** The founder needs to understand the paid relationship, see how it improves consequential AI decisions over thirty days, and approve or reject the product spine only.
- **Governing rule:** The Brain may prepare, question, organise and repair. The leader owns purpose, judgement, the consequential call and final quality. The operator decides when challenge or interruption is worth the leader's attention.
- **Data truth:** Every item states whether it was said, supported, inferred or remains unknown. Provisional interpretations cannot quietly become accepted truth.
- **Materiality:** Material product concept.
- **Brand and voice:** Warm, precise, instrument-like, plain British English. Visual meaning comes from revision and standing, not ornament.
- **Authority:** Concept spine only. No approval of copy, implementation, visual system, data model or release.
- **Proof:** The eventual render must be checked at narrow and wide widths, including provisional, accepted, corrected, unknown, quiet, sparse, stale and error states.

## 1. Governing visual and interaction metaphor

The product appears as **one working proof that becomes more useful each time it is tested against real work**.

This is the kind of proof an editor, lawyer or senior decision-maker would recognise: a clean page in the centre, disciplined notes in the margin, visible corrections, and one accountable sign-off. It is not a portrait of the leader and never claims to be complete.

The proof has four clear kinds of line:

- **Said:** the leader's own view or instruction.
- **Supported:** evidence with a source and date.
- **Inferred:** a proposed reading that has not yet earned standing.
- **Unknown:** a gap that could change the decision.

Standing is shown with both a short word and a simple mark, so colour is never the only carrier of meaning. A correction remains visible as a replaced line with a link to the work it changed. The central page is the current usable understanding. Its margin is where challenge, uncertainty and provenance live.

The recurring interaction is a **proof turn**. A tap moves between three views of the same page: what was prepared, what changed through human judgement, and what later work can now use. This is a comparison of meaning, not a carousel of features.

## 2. What the founder sees in the first ten seconds

The first frame contains one sentence, one changing proof, and three named hands on the work.

**A private AI Brain that learns through real decisions, so each later decision starts better prepared.**

Below it, a single decision proof sits open. The visible hard question concerns AI's effect on one of four live fields: category, organisation, people or the leader's own role. A small field switch shows that this is a repeated product pattern across the four fields, not one case presented as the whole product.

The centre shows the Brain's prepared question and the evidence that earned it. The operator's challenge sits in the margin. The leader's call sits at the foot of the page as the only final mark. A short line beside it says: **The company pays for better change. The private Brain is how the work compounds.**

No navigation, activity feed, architecture or product tour appears in this first frame.

## 3. How the thirty days are understood without becoming a progress course

Thirty days appear as **three editions of the same living proof**, not as lessons, milestones or completion percentage.

1. **Starting proof:** Before the first session, ethical public research and supplied context produce a clearly provisional page. It already prepares useful questions. Blank areas are labelled unknown rather than filled with guesses.
2. **Proof under pressure:** Meetings, voice, pasted work, documents, email replies, decisions, corrections and outcomes alter the page through real work. The leader receives only the smallest hard question that could change a consequential decision. Quiet is shown as a valid state: **Nothing has earned an interruption.**
3. **Working proof at day thirty:** The page remains living, inspectable and owned by the leader. It contains accepted judgement, sourced evidence, unresolved questions and correction history. It can leave as a human-readable and machine-usable release.

A thin date line identifies when each edition was true. It does not score progress. The relationship reads as repeated preparation, judgement and repair, with value present from the first edition.

## 4. How the three roles remain distinct

Each role occupies a stable place on every proof.

| Role | Place on the proof | What only that role does |
|---|---|---|
| Leader | The intent line and final mark | Sets the purpose and success condition, decides what stays human, makes the call, applies voice and taste, judges independent review and owns what ships |
| Expert operator | The outer margin | Adds backstage context, challenges weak logic, compares options more deeply and decides whether an interruption is earned |
| Brain | The prepared body and evidence notes | Researches, organises, spots patterns and contradictions, drafts ranked questions and options, tracks uncertainty, proposes learning and repairs dependent work after correction |

Their marks never merge. The Brain can prepare a proposed call, but only the leader's final mark changes the page to **decided**. The operator can recommend an interruption, but cannot sign for the leader.

## 5. How the Brain's compounding becomes visible

The proof includes a narrow **carried forward** edge. Opening it reveals the exact earlier items that improved the current preparation:

- an accepted judgement;
- a supported fact;
- a correction;
- an outcome that strengthened or weakened an earlier belief.

The current hard question is connected to those items with plain language such as **Prepared differently because you corrected this on 8 September**. The viewer can compare the earlier and later preparation side by side. The later version is more specific because named evidence or judgement changed it, not because a progress meter rose.

When a correction lands, the affected later lines visibly return to provisional until repaired. Their history remains available. This makes self-repair observable without pretending the Brain has perfect knowledge.

## 6. Where the supporting organs appear

The organs belong to the proof rather than becoming destinations of their own.

- Capture appears at the foot of the current line as one-tap choices, voice or a short reply.
- Automatic research and synthesis appear as dated source notes beside the prepared body.
- Ranked or tappable questions appear only when a choice can reduce the leader's effort.
- The deeper decision-comparison instrument opens inside the operator's margin, beside the call it informs.
- Prepared intelligence is the proof itself, shaped for a decision, meeting or category shift.
- The Living Brain is the inspectable set of current proof lines and their standing.
- Evidence, correction and self-repair appear as source notes, visible replacements and affected lines returning to provisional.
- Portable release appears after sign-off as one owned package in readable and machine-usable forms.
- Quiet periods keep the closed proof visible with the line **Nothing has earned an interruption.**

Personal and company material use separate labelled leaves inside the same proof. A line can cite both only when permission allows it. Export keeps the leaves separate and includes source, date, standing, corrections and the leader's words.

## 7. The exact final approval moment

The leader reaches the final mark only after the prepared question, evidence, operator challenge and independent AI review are visible together.

The approval block reads:

> **Your call**
> This is the decision and quality bar you are prepared to own.
> **Personal record** / **Company record** / **Both, kept separate**
> **Final mark: I own this decision**

The ownership choice is explicit and never preselected. The final mark confirms the consequential call and where its record belongs. It does not approve every inference in the Brain, the product design, publication, implementation or release to other people.

## 8. Mobile treatment

On desktop, the centre proof and operator margin form one balanced spread. On mobile, they become one natural vertical page in the same reading order:

1. current decision and standing;
2. smallest hard question;
3. evidence and unknowns;
4. operator challenge;
5. leader response and final mark;
6. carried-forward evidence and correction history.

The current question stays close to its answer. Tap targets are thumb-sized. Voice, tap and type are equal paths, and input is saved visibly. The page scrolls naturally with no nested panels. The comparison view switches between earlier and later wording rather than squeezing two columns onto a phone. The final mark never becomes sticky before the evidence and challenge have been seen.

## 9. Strongest risk in the concept

The strongest risk is that **The Working Proof could look like a document review tool or one decision workflow**.

The concept fails if the visual language becomes tracked changes, comments and approvals. The defence is to keep the proposition visible, show the same living proof preparing several kinds of consequential AI decision, and make the carried-forward edge the signature interaction. The moat is not document editing. It is how often accepted evidence and judgement make later preparation materially more specific.

The concept becomes an A only if a cold viewer can explain that compounding relationship in one minute without describing it as better meeting notes.

## 10. Why this is materially different

It is not a case workflow because no single case supplies the story. The repeated object is the leader's inspectable working understanding, applied across category, organisation, people and role decisions.

It is not a dashboard because it does not summarise activity, status or progress. It presents one consequential question, the standing of what shaped it, the human judgement beside it, and the exact earlier learning that improved it.

It is not an architecture map because it does not explain components or information flow. It shows the product relationship through use: preparation, pressure, decision, correction and better preparation later.

## Concept call

**The call:** Render The Working Proof as the spine. It turns epistemic honesty, human agency and compounding into one visible behaviour.

**The sharper alternative:** Remove the field switch and show two different proof pages peeking from behind the current page. This gains immediate breadth but risks looking more like a document stack.

**Next step in 24 to 72 hours:** Test the first frame as a static phone and desktop render with five cold viewers. Success means each can state who pays, what the Brain does, what remains human and why day thirty is better within one minute.

**Counterpoints:** The editorial metaphor may feel too familiar for a category-defining product. The proof also depends on exceptionally clear status language, or provisional inference may look more trusted than it is.
