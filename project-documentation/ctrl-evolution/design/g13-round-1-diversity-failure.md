- Generator A: [`g13-concept-a.md`](g13-concept-a.md), The Fold

## Diversity test

| Pair | Sequencing | User agency | Primary interaction | Information structure | State model | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A vs B | portrait, selected item, unfold, source, correction, consequence | choose one item and inspect | tap to unfold or open and trace | folded portrait into local map | nearly identical progressive reveal | fail |
| A vs C | portrait, selected item, unfold, source, correction, consequence | choose one item and inspect | tap to unfold | closed page into open atlas | nearly identical progressive reveal | fail |
| B vs C | portrait, selected item, unfold, source, correction, consequence | choose one item and inspect | open and trace | folded map into open atlas | nearly identical progressive reveal | fail |

No pair differs on two load-bearing axes. Palette, copy and minor control differences do not count.

## Missing conceptual territory

The second round must explore three deliberately different structures without seeing round 1 candidates:

1. Spatial-first: the user begins inside the Living Map and navigates freely from one current focus.
2. Temporal-first: the user follows how judgement affected a live decision and how correction changes the future path.
3. Fixed-object lenses: one stable focal object remains in place while the portrait, connections and evidence change around it.

Round 2 must not use a folded page, unfolding map, atlas, paper or open-and-trace metaphor. It is the final divergence round under the current brief.

## Next action

Run three fresh generators against the unchanged product and data constraints, one per missing territory. Then perform pairwise distance testing and two independent blinded judgments before synthesis.
