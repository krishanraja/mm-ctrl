# G13 My Brain material-surface sanitized brief

**Revision:** 1

**Surface:** My Brain

**Materiality:** New load-bearing interaction and information architecture

**Generator boundary:** Use only this brief and the two linked G13 contract artifacts. Do not inspect existing CTRL Brain screens, prototypes, screenshots, earlier design candidates or another generator's output.

## State of use

A busy leader opens CTRL on a phone between meetings. They want to understand what CTRL currently knows that can help with a consequential marketing decision, see one meaningful connection and correct one standard that is too broad. They have less than two minutes and no interest in administering memory or learning technical concepts.

The same product must later work on desktop for deeper exploration, but the primary review frame is 390 by 844 pixels.

## User and action

The user must be able to:

1. orient themselves within seconds;
2. recognise one specific, valuable piece of their own judgement;
3. understand why two things are connected;
4. inspect where the understanding came from;
5. correct the over-broad standard in ordinary language; and
6. see that the correction changed the Brain without implying that delivered consequential work was silently rewritten.

## Governing product rule

One versioned Brain powers two complementary experiences:

- a concise current portrait for immediate comprehension; and
- an immersive Living Map for connection, exploration and visible growth.

They are projections of the same accepted state. A visual edge is allowed only for a current, typed, evidence-bearing relationship. Layout physics, distance and hub tethers are not semantic claims. Pending proposals and disputed items cannot look like accepted current judgement.

## Representative content

Use the `LB01` and `LB04` states in [`../g13-living-brain-proof-fixtures.json`](../g13-living-brain-proof-fixtures.json). The material moment is the correction from:

> Before consequential work ships, the human owns the final judgement and finish.

to the narrower meaning:

> Before externally consequential work ships, the human owns final judgement and finish; reversible internal drafts may remain delegated.

The correction automatically rebuilds the portrait and map. A board brief and an already shared release become review-required rather than being silently rewritten.

## Experience constraints

- One dominant action or question at a time.
- Tap-first; voice may be offered, typing remains optional.
- The user should never need the words ontology, node, edge, vector, embedding, schema, promote, invalidate or re-index.
- Do not use memory quantity, completion percentage or a health score as the hero.
- The map must feel alive and immersive without sparkle symbols, decorative AI motifs or constant purposeless motion.
- Purposeful thin-line iconography and quiet state-responsive movement are allowed.
- No reassurance copy without an action, evidence, state or meaning job.
- No graph legend that asks the user to learn the data model before receiving value.
- The result must feel more polished, intelligent and emotionally resonant than the current product standard.
- Avoid current template aesthetics, glass panels, gradient spectacle, mascot imagery and generic chat-first composition.
- The verdict or consequence appears at the point of action.
- Preserve visible focus, keyboard operation, reduced motion, touch targets and non-voice alternatives.
- No nested scrolling regions.

## Data states that must survive

- representative current Brain;
- one corrected item and changed relationships;
- private audience rejection;
- proposed or disputed material;
- sparse first-use Brain;
- 120 items and 180 relationships through semantic zoom;
- loading, stale and recoverable error states; and
- long labels on a narrow screen.

## Concept output required

Produce one concept spine, not rendered code. State:

1. the governing interaction metaphor;
2. the exact first 30-second sequence;
3. where the user has agency;
4. the primary interaction;
5. the information structure;
6. the state model;
7. how portrait and Living Map remain visibly one Brain;
8. the correction and repair moment;
9. how the concept handles sparse and dense Brains;
10. the strongest reason it could fail; and
11. at least three constraints that make it materially different from a generic dashboard, chat or decorative graph.

The concept must be feasible as a self-contained functional HTML proof using the supplied fixtures. Do not prescribe production implementation.

## Evaluation rubric

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| The product and value are understandable without explanation | 20 |
| Portrait and Living Map feel like one coherent Brain | 18 |
| Correction and repair feel trustworthy, immediate and consequential | 16 |
| Interaction is radically simple under time pressure | 14 |
| The experience feels immersive, alive and distinctive | 12 |
| Visual semantics cannot overclaim the data | 10 |
| Sparse, dense and adverse states remain coherent | 6 |
| The concept is feasible with the supplied fixtures | 4 |

Hard failures:

- unsupported semantic relationships;
- chat as the default container;
- memory administration as the main job;
- a decorative graph beside unrelated cards;
- hidden or silent correction consequences;
- a proposed or disputed item styled as accepted truth;
- dependence on mouse, drag or voice alone;
- conceptual sameness with another candidate on sequencing, agency, interaction, information structure and state model; or
- any violation of the authority boundary.
