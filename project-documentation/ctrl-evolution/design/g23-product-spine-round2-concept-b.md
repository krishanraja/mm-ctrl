# G23 round 2 concept B: The Crossing

**Revision:** Independent concept B, R1
**Date:** 11 September 2026

## Design contract

- **State of use:** Krish reviews one coherent founder alignment artifact on a remote call, using desktop or phone, with about one minute for the first read.
- **User and action:** The founder must understand the product relationship, see one judgement become useful in another arena, and approve or reject only the product spine.
- **Governing rule:** The Brain may propose a crossing. Only standing, permission and a human decision let that judgement shape later work.
- **Data truth:** The artifact shows a clearly labelled illustrative state. Sparse, uncertain, contradicted and quiet states are first-class.
- **Materiality:** Material product concept. No implementation or wider visual-system approval is implied.
- **Brand and voice:** Warm with the leader, exact about evidence and accountability, plain British English, no decorative AI imagery.
- **Authority:** Concept draft only. Founder approval would lock the spine, not the final copy, visual execution, code or release.
- **Proof required before synthesis:** First-read comprehension, narrow and wide layout, keyboard and touch paths, quiet and contradiction states, and readable ownership boundaries.

## 1. Governing visual and interaction metaphor

The visible object is **The Crossing**, a working field where one accepted judgement is tested in a different decision arena.

Four arena labels sit at the edges: category, organisation, people and own role. The centre holds one judgement that has already earned standing in its source arena. The viewer chooses a different arena. The Brain then shows the smallest question that would test whether the judgement belongs there too.

This is not a map of everything the Brain knows. It is a live test of whether the leader's judgement can travel without losing its meaning, permission or human ownership.

The core visual sentence is:

> One judgement, accepted here, is now useful there.

The judgement never crosses automatically. Its visible standing is always expressed through four conditions:

- **Relevance:** why this judgement may matter to the present decision.
- **Permission:** whether personal or company context may be used here.
- **Contradiction:** what current evidence cuts against the transfer.
- **Reuse:** whether a human has accepted this use, changed it for the new arena, or kept it in its original arena.

## 2. What the founder sees in the first ten seconds

The opening line states the product without requiring interpretation:

> A private AI Brain that makes each hard AI decision improve the next one.

A second line protects the human role:

> It learns how this leader judges category, organisation, people and their own role. The leader still makes the call and owns the final quality.

The Crossing is already showing one clear example:

- Source: **Accepted in organisation**
- Judgement: **AI decisions should stay close to the people who carry their consequences.**
- Target: **Now relevant to people**
- Brain's question: **Would this also mean a manager must review any AI input used in a performance decision?**
- Standing: **Relevant here. Company-only permission. No unresolved contradiction. Reuse awaiting the leader.**

Three equal choices sit immediately beneath the question:

- **Carries across**
- **Changes here**
- **Stays in organisation**

The response appears in the same place as the choice. Nothing important waits at the bottom of the artifact.

## 3. How the thirty days are understood

The thirty days are shown as one continuous working relationship, not as dates, stages or completion percentage.

A fixed sentence beneath the opening establishes the whole span:

> It arrives with a provisional view, becomes useful on the first real decision, improves through work and correction, and leaves as an owned, portable Brain.

The Crossing changes because the quality and standing of the material change, not because a day has passed. Public research and supplied context can create a provisional judgement before the first session. Meetings, voice, pasted work, documents, replies, decisions, corrections and outcomes can later strengthen, narrow or overturn it.

The relationship is visible in the growing repertoire of judgements that have earned use in more than one arena. This repertoire is not a score or curriculum. It is a small set of inspectable statements with clear provenance, boundaries and current standing.

## 4. How the three roles remain distinct

The roles are separated by action rights, not by illustrated characters or technical lanes.

- **Leader:** sets the purpose and success condition, chooses the arena, answers the hard question, accepts or changes the transfer, applies taste, makes the consequential call and owns what ships.
- **Expert operator:** sees the richer backstage context, adds missing evidence, challenges a weak transfer, protects the interruption threshold and decides when the leader should be asked.
- **Brain:** researches, organises context, proposes the crossing, separates statement from evidence and inference, surfaces contradiction, prepares options and repairs dependent work after correction.

Each contribution is labelled beside the relevant material: **Leader accepted**, **Operator challenged**, or **Brain inferred**. The Brain never receives a human name, face or speaking persona.

## 5. How the Brain's compounding becomes visible

Compounding is visible when an accepted judgement from one arena improves the preparation for a materially different later question.

After the leader selects **Carries across**, the result appears directly beneath the choice:

> Accepted for people decisions with company-only context. Future preparation may use this judgement when human consequences are material.

The source statement now carries two arena labels, **organisation** and **people**, plus the reason the second use was accepted. A future category or own-role question may surface it only when the four standing conditions permit.

The visible gain is not a larger store of information. It is a sharper question, better prepared context and less repeated explanation. Reuse is meaningful only when it changes the quality of a later decision.

If new evidence conflicts, the centre changes to a contradiction state. The accepted wording, conflicting evidence, inference and unknowns appear together. Dependent uses pause until the leader or operator resolves the standing. A correction preserves the earlier record, repairs every affected use and states what changed.

## 6. Where the supporting organs appear

Every supporting organ belongs to the same Crossing rather than opening as a separate product.

- **Low-friction capture:** voice, paste, forward and meeting capture enter through a quiet input dock at the edge. New material is not treated as accepted judgement.
- **Automatic research and synthesis:** the evidence behind a proposed crossing appears in an expandable **Why this may belong here** area.
- **Adaptive questioning:** the centre offers one ranked or tappable hard question when a decision can materially change.
- **Deeper comparison:** **This decision needs a harder test** expands the current centre to compare options against purpose, evidence, human boundary and downside. It does not leave the artifact.
- **Prepared intelligence:** relevant context, changed assumptions and open questions sit beside the active decision, not in a feed.
- **Transparent Living Brain:** **Current understanding** opens the leader's repertoire, with each judgement separated into what was said, evidenced, inferred and still unknown.
- **Evidence, correction and self-repair:** any judgement can be challenged or corrected in place. Affected reuse pauses and is visibly repaired.
- **Portable release:** **Owned copy** provides a human-readable and machine-usable export with personal and company material kept separate.
- **Quiet periods:** when no question has earned attention, the centre simply reads **Nothing needs your attention.** There is no empty feed to fill.

## 7. Exact final approval moment

The approval moment appears only after the founder has seen one complete crossing and opened the compact role and ownership line. The interaction then resolves into this question:

> Is this the product spine?

The only choices are:

- **Yes. The spine is right.**
- **No. One product rule is wrong.**

The second choice opens one short field labelled **The product rule that is wrong**. It does not ask for visual preferences or feature requests.

Approval locks only this proposition:

> A private AI Brain compounds a leader's judgement by carrying accepted learning into different AI decisions, while permission, contradiction and human accountability govern every reuse.

## 8. Mobile treatment

The phone version does not shrink the four-sided field. It keeps one judgement and one target arena in view.

The source judgement is a fixed, readable block at the top. A large four-choice arena switch sits beneath it. Choosing a target replaces the centre with one question, its standing and the three human choices. The answer appears directly below the chosen control.

The richer operator context becomes a separate private bottom layer. Provenance, contradiction and ownership open as natural-height sections with no nested scrolling. Voice has a tap alternative, and any movement gesture has a tap alternative. Input persists across interruption and return.

## 9. Strongest risk

The physical crossing metaphor could look like a clever collaboration canvas and hide the proposition, especially if four arenas are shown with equal visual weight.

The guardrail is severe focus: one explicit opening sentence, one active judgement, one target, one hard question and one human response. No counters, activity stream, navigation rail, completion state or decorative network. If a five-second test cannot recover both **private AI Brain** and **each hard decision improves the next**, the concept fails.

## 10. Why this is materially different

It is not a case workflow because it does not march one decision through repeated steps. The first meaningful act is transfer between unlike decisions.

It is not a dashboard because it shows no overview, score, feed, navigation or monitoring state. Only the judgement currently being tested is active.

It is not an architecture map because the roles are expressed through what each participant may do, and the Brain's inner mechanics appear only as provenance and standing beside a real decision.

The organising object is the leader's reusable decision practice. The product becomes more valuable when a judgement earns careful use elsewhere, not when the system accumulates more screens or information.

## Conceptual divergence by load-bearing axis

- **Sequencing:** The artifact opens on a cross-arena transfer already in progress. Proposition, standing and human response are understood before any wider repertoire is revealed.
- **User agency:** The leader selects the target arena and accepts, adapts or blocks reuse. The operator controls challenge and interruption. The Brain may propose but cannot grant standing.
- **Primary interaction:** The user tests one accepted judgement in a materially different arena and resolves its standing at the point of action.
- **Information structure:** Material is organised as a repertoire of transferable judgements, grouped by arena fit, permission and current use, not by feature, date or decision stage.
- **State model:** Every proposed reuse is a four-part state of relevance, permission, contradiction and reuse. Sparse and quiet states are valid. Contradiction pauses dependent use until a human resolves it.

## The call

Use The Crossing as the product spine if the first rendered synthesis can keep the active transfer obvious on both phone and desktop. Its strongest property is that compounding is demonstrated through a changed later decision, while the leader's authority remains visible at the exact moment the Brain asks to reuse what it learned.

The sharper version removes the wider repertoire from the first view entirely. Only one crossing is visible until the founder chooses to inspect what the Brain currently understands.

The next move is a low-fidelity narrow and wide render of the same illustrative crossing, including quiet and contradiction states, followed by the single spine approval question above.

The main counterpoint is that four arenas may still suggest a diagram if their labels dominate. A second counterpoint is that the crossing needs one carefully written AI-specific example to avoid reading as generic leadership coaching.
