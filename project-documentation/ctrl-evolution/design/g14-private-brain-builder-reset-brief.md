# G14 Private Brain Builder reset brief

**Revision:** 1
**Date:** 8 September 2026
**Audience:** Independent concept generators
**Authority:** Concept documents only

## User and state of use

Krish is privately preparing a thirty-day Mindmake customer sprint. He opens one customer between meetings or before a consequential session. The customer is a senior leader making a decision that could materially change their business. Krish needs to understand what matters now, why the system believes it, what remains uncertain and what to do next.

## Outcome

Design a private operator surface that makes one customer's AI Brain immediately useful for a consequential decision. At 1440 by 900, the primary task must be complete without page scrolling. A twelve-year-old should understand what the screen is for.

## Invariant product and data rules

- One current intervention or decision focus owns the surface.
- Every confident meaning is tied to inspectable evidence and provenance.
- A weaker or superseded interpretation remains available as history but cannot guide current action.
- Uncertainty stays visible and affects what the operator can do.
- Operator-private material never appears in the customer projection.
- Private questions cannot silently change durable Brain truth.
- The Brain is living and inspectable. Its relationships have types and direction.
- The product supports consequential decisions. It must not resemble coursework, micro-learning, project management or a generic chat shell.
- Sparse, quiet, loading, stale, error and rejected states must remain honest.
- The current synthetic fixture is sufficient for a feasible local proof. Do not invent new production capabilities.

## Experience requirements

- Desktop behaves like a working instrument, not an editorial page.
- Visible space goes to decision support, evidence, uncertainty, comparison and action.
- The product may feel immersive and magical, but never theatrical, childish or ornamental.
- Use plain British English. No em dashes.
- No boastful, self-awarding, coy or aphoristic AI copy.
- Headings name the object, decision or action. They do not announce importance.
- Preserve the established dark ink, warm cream, mint answer and amber change language.
- Mobile may progressively disclose detail while preserving the same meaning and task.
- Keyboard, touch, reduced motion and 200 percent zoom must remain viable.

## Required concept output

Produce one concept spine that is materially distinct in sequencing, user agency, primary interaction, information structure or state model. Describe:

1. the governing interaction metaphor;
2. the first five seconds;
3. the complete desktop information structure;
4. how evidence, uncertainty and the next move relate;
5. how the Living Brain is inspected;
6. how mobile changes without losing meaning;
7. feasibility against the current fixture;
8. the strongest failure risk;
9. why this is not a disguised version of a conventional editorial page, course, project dashboard or chat UI.

Do not inspect prior G14 renders, screenshots, implementation, rationale, rankings or rejected layout descriptions. Do not imitate their component shapes. Work only from this brief.
