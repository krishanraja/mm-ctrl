# G23 Round 2 Concept C: The Pressure Rehearsal

**Revision:** C1
**Date:** 11 September 2026
**Approval state:** Independent concept for blinded review
**Downstream owner:** G23 synthesis and judging process
**Pending action:** Judge the product spine, not its visual finish

**State of use:** A founder reviews one coherent artefact on a phone or desktop during a remote conversation. The first read takes under a minute. The leader may later use the same interaction before a consequential meeting.
**User and action:** The founder must understand why a private Brain plus expert operator produces more specific preparation than ordinary AI assistance, and how that relationship develops over thirty days.
**Governing rule:** The Brain may prepare and challenge. The leader owns purpose, judgement, the consequential call, final polish and accountability.
**Data truth:** Public research and supplied context begin as provisional. Every visible claim carries its source type, standing, owner and uncertainty. Sparse, disputed and quiet states are designed, not disguised.
**Materiality:** Material product spine.
**Brand and voice:** Cool technical ground, one amber signal colour, warm plain English, no decorative intelligence theatre.
**Authority:** This concept proposes the founder-facing product relationship only. It does not approve implementation or any later surface.
**Proof needed before build:** One live rendered artefact at narrow and wide widths, keyboard and touch paths, zoom and reflow, long and adversarial content, provisional and disputed claims, lost connectivity, correction repair, quiet state and export inspection.

## 1. Governing visual and interaction metaphor

The artefact is a **pressure rehearsal** for one real decision already on the leader's calendar.

At its centre is a single prepared position. Around that position sit only the conditions that could change it. One condition is visibly live, such as the timing of a market shift, the team boundary that must remain human, or the evidence threshold for restructuring a role.

The viewer changes that one condition. A competent ordinary AI answer remains plausible but broad because it does not know the leader's private boundary, the operator's challenge or which earlier evidence the leader accepted. The relationship-prepared position reacts differently. It exposes what no longer holds, asks the smallest hard question, and rebuilds only the affected preparation.

The visible object is therefore not a body of content. It is the preparation being tested under pressure.

The metaphor makes the product claim concrete:

> Ordinary AI can produce a reasonable answer. The Brain and expert relationship prepares this leader to own this call.

The visual language is restrained. The prepared position sits in warm cream on a cool technical field. Amber appears only where a condition changes, a challenge earns attention or dependent preparation reopens. Source, standing and permission appear in monospace. There is no avatar, decorative motion, generated image or network picture.

## 2. What the founder sees in the first ten seconds

The opening view contains three things and no navigation:

1. The proposition: **One decision. Two qualities of preparation.**
2. A real decision-shaped sentence: **Should this leader change [named part of the market, organisation, people model or own role] by [real decision date]?**
3. One large condition control labelled with two factual possibilities, for example **Category demand moves this quarter** and **Category demand moves six months later**.

Beneath the decision sits a short, credible ordinary AI position. It is not made foolish. It uses the public and supplied information available to a normal assistant and offers a sensible course.

When the condition changes, that answer barely changes. A concise label appears beside it: **Still reasonable. Not specific enough to carry into the room.**

In the same central space, the relationship-prepared position opens the missing context in three attributed cues:

- **Brain noticed:** an assumption in the preparation no longer has support.
- **Operator challenged:** the option fails a consequence the current answer has not priced.
- **Leader changed:** the purpose, boundary or quality bar is now explicit.

The prepared position then recomposes and names one remaining unknown. The founder sees the product difference through behaviour before reading any deeper explanation.

## 3. How the thirty days are understood without becoming a progress course

There is no calendar rail, completion score, streak or sequence of lessons. Thirty days is understood through the **standing of what can shape preparation**.

A compact standing drawer is attached to the rehearsal. It groups material by authority rather than age:

- **Provisional:** ethical public research or supplied context that may frame a question but cannot settle a call.
- **Leader confirmed:** intent, boundaries, taste or judgement that may shape the current preparation within its stated scope.
- **Seen in work:** meetings, voice, pasted work, decisions and outcomes that support a pattern.
- **Contested or repaired:** an interpretation that is held back, with affected preparation reopened until corrected.

Dates remain visible as small metadata, so the relationship is legible from its provisional start to the day-thirty release. Time does not grant authority. A day-two correction can outrank a day-twenty inference, and a later contradiction can move an earlier belief back to provisional.

Four short statements explain the relationship without turning it into stages:

- Before the first session, the rehearsal can already contain provisional research and supplied context.
- Value starts with the first useful question, not with a complete model of the person.
- Every accepted judgement, correction and observed outcome can improve later preparation.
- At day thirty, the leader can inspect and take away the current Brain in human-readable and machine-usable forms.

The month is a period in which preparation earns specificity. It is not a syllabus the leader completes.

## 4. How the three roles remain distinct

The three roles never share a voice, colour meaning or authority control.

**Leader**

- Names the purpose and success condition.
- Marks what must remain human.
- Confirms or rejects interpretations.
- Makes the consequential call.
- Applies voice, taste and final polish.
- Alone approves whether the preparation is fit for use.

Leader contributions appear as first-person statements with a clear personal or company ownership mark.

**Expert operator**

- Sees the richer backstage picture.
- Challenges the weakest assumption or consequence.
- Adds context that has an attributable source.
- Decides whether a question has earned an interruption.
- Reviews independent AI criticism without surrendering judgement to it.

Operator interventions appear as amber brackets on the exact part of the preparation they challenge. They do not become a chat feed.

**Brain**

- Researches, organises and synthesises context.
- Separates what was said, what evidence supports, what it inferred and what remains unknown.
- Prepares questions and options.
- Tracks standing, uncertainty and permissions.
- Repairs dependent preparation after correction.
- Carries accepted learning into later work.

Brain activity appears as neutral system language with source and standing metadata. It cannot mark an interpretation as leader-confirmed and cannot approve a call.

## 5. How the Brain's compounding becomes visible

Compounding is shown by **reuse under a new condition**, not by a rising score or a fuller picture of the person.

When the viewer changes the live condition, the Brain identifies which parts of the preparation still hold and which parts must reopen. A small line under each affected statement names why it moved:

- **Holds because:** supported evidence is unchanged.
- **Reopened because:** the condition breaks an accepted assumption.
- **Carried forward from:** an earlier leader-confirmed judgement or observed outcome.
- **Held back because:** the interpretation lacks standing or crosses an ownership boundary.

The stronger preparation is visibly made from accepted material that ordinary AI does not possess or cannot responsibly use. One earlier correction changes the current question. One prior outcome changes the current option order. One leader-owned boundary removes an otherwise attractive recommendation.

This is the moat signal for the concept: **the proportion of decision-critical preparation that remains useful under a meaningful change of conditions without hiding uncertainty**. The artefact does not display that as a vanity percentage. It demonstrates the mechanism once and leaves the underlying measure available for product evaluation.

## 6. Where the supporting organs appear

The supporting organs belong to the rehearsal. None receives a separate home screen.

- **Low-friction capture** sits beneath the live condition as a small voice, paste or one-sentence response tray. The system drafts first. The leader mainly confirms, selects or names one thing.
- **Automatic research and synthesis** appears inside the prepared position through concise source lines and provisional standing, not as a feed.
- **Adaptive ranked or tappable questioning** appears only when a different answer could materially change the preparation. The highest-value question sits beside the exact condition it tests.
- **Deeper decision comparison** opens in place when two live options remain. It compares them against the leader's stated purpose, human boundary, evidence and consequences, then folds back into the rehearsal. It does not become a general analysis area.
- **Prepared intelligence** is the central position itself, ready for a decision, meeting or category shift.
- **The Living Brain** is the standing drawer: an inspectable account of what is known, inferred, disputed and unknown, with ownership and scope attached.
- **Evidence, correction and self-repair** starts from any claim. A correction preserves the original statement, changes its standing and reopens every dependent part of the current preparation.
- **Portable release** follows leader approval. The leader receives a readable account and a machine-usable package containing context, source type, standing, ownership, uncertainty and correction links.
- **Quiet periods** use the same central object. It reads: **Nothing has earned an interruption. The current preparation remains available.** There is no empty feed to make the product look inactive.

Autosave and a visible save state sit outside the content hierarchy but remain persistent. Capture survives refresh, lost connectivity and return. Copy and download remain available even if an email route fails.

## 7. The exact final approval moment

Approval occurs after the final pressure change has been tested and while the leader can still see:

- the purpose and success condition;
- what remains human;
- the prepared position;
- the operator's material challenge;
- supporting evidence and its standing;
- the unresolved unknowns; and
- the ownership boundary for every private input.

The question sits directly below that preparation:

**Is this preparation fit for your call?**

The two responses are **Fit for my call** and **Needs another challenge**.

Only the leader can select the first response. It approves the preparation for use, not the decision itself. The leader still makes the call and owns what ships. The operator may recommend readiness. The Brain may report that required context is present. Neither may approve on the leader's behalf.

Selecting **Fit for my call** records the visible basis, ownership state and open unknowns, then makes the portable release available. Selecting **Needs another challenge** reopens the exact weak condition without discarding captured input.

## 8. Mobile treatment

Mobile keeps the same semantic object and sequence. It does not collapse into cards or a miniature desktop layout.

The decision sentence and live condition fill the first viewport. The two condition states use a large segmented control with at least a clear text label, visible focus and a generous touch target. The prepared position occupies natural page height beneath it. There is one page scroll and no component scroll.

The three attributed cues arrive one at a time in the same location after the condition changes. Reduced-motion mode replaces the transition with an immediate labelled state change. The leader can move through the cues by touch or keyboard without relying on colour, drag or voice.

The standing drawer becomes a full-width disclosure below the active preparation. Voice, paste and one-sentence capture stay above the virtual keyboard. The save state and return path remain visible. Long source names wrap, zoom reflows, and permission labels never truncate.

The final approval question remains adjacent to the complete prepared position. A sticky control is used only if testing proves that dynamic browser chrome and the virtual keyboard do not hide context or action. Otherwise it stays in natural flow.

## 9. Strongest risk in the concept

The strongest risk is a dishonest straw man. If ordinary AI is shown as generic, foolish or unable to revise when prompted, the counterfactual proves nothing and damages credibility.

The baseline must therefore be a strong, fluent answer produced from the same public and supplied context available at the start. The relationship wins only where it has earned private specificity: a leader-confirmed boundary, an operator challenge, accepted evidence, a prior correction or an observed outcome. Every difference must point to that cause.

This is a blocking risk. Before any founder render, the team should run the counterfactual with a capable ordinary assistant and remove every claimed advantage that cannot be traced to authorised relationship context or expert judgement. If the remaining difference is too small to understand in one minute, this spine fails.

A secondary risk is cognitive load. Source standing, permissions and repair could crowd the first read. The control is progressive disclosure: the first view contains the decision, the changed condition and the three attributed cues. Full provenance opens only when inspected.

## 10. Why this is materially different

This is not a case workflow because the decision topic is a replaceable specimen. The organising rule is what preparation does when a meaningful condition changes. The same pressure rehearsal can test a market move, organisation design, people consequence or the leader's own role without turning any one of them into the product.

This is not a dashboard because there is no overview, feed, metric grid or navigation model. One real decision and one changed condition occupy the surface. Supporting organs appear only where they alter that preparation.

This is not an architecture map because roles are distinguished through visible authority and action, not boxes and connections. The viewer learns the system by changing a condition and seeing who may notice, challenge, change and approve.

The arm differs on all five load-bearing axes:

- **Sequencing:** It begins with a competent ordinary answer, applies one counterfactual pressure, then reveals the relationship-specific preparation at the point where the generic answer stops being sufficient.
- **User agency:** The viewer changes the governing condition. The leader then sets the purpose or boundary and owns the readiness judgement. The experience is not a passive explanation of a month.
- **Primary interaction:** One condition switch stress-tests preparation. It is not browsing, progressing through stages, opening feature areas or following a process.
- **Information structure:** All information attaches to one prepared position as condition, challenge, standing, ownership or unknown. There is no feature taxonomy and no separate product organs.
- **State model:** Preparation is governed by quality and authority. A statement can be provisional, leader-confirmed, observed, contested, held back or repaired. State can move in either direction when evidence or permission changes, regardless of the day.

**The call:** Advance the pressure rehearsal if the live ordinary-AI baseline remains strong and the relationship-specific difference is still obvious in one interaction.
**Sharper alternative:** Remove the explanatory drawer from the founder review and let the counterfactual behaviour carry almost the entire proposition.
**Next step within 24 to 72 hours:** Build one cold, static interaction storyboard for a single real but safely anonymised decision, then test whether a fresh viewer can explain the difference, the three authorities and the thirty-day compounding in one minute.
**Counterpoints:** The concept depends on having one authentic relationship-specific distinction worth showing. Its restraint may also make the wider product feel smaller unless the standing drawer clearly signals that the same mechanism carries across market, organisation, people and role decisions.
