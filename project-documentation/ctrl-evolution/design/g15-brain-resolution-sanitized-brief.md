# G15 Brain Resolution material-surface brief

Date: 8 September 2026

## Outcome

Create one quiet but memorable way for a leader to feel that their Brain has become materially sharper over time without presenting the person as a test to finish. The surface must produce return desire, personal recognition and trust while remaining measurable beneath the experience.

## State of use

The leader opens their Brain on a phone between meetings or on desktop during a reflective review. They have seconds of attention at first and may choose to go deeper. They are not administering memory, taking an assessment or reviewing a business dashboard.

## Governing product rule

The surface may claim only that a named judgement territory is forming, tested, transferable or revising, supported by frozen synthetic evaluation evidence. It may show what became sharper and what remains uncertain. It cannot claim that the person, their intelligence or their Brain is complete.

The measurable construct is provisional `Judgement Resolution`: the weakest-link quality of grounding, discrimination, calibration, transfer and adaptation within a named important territory. The ordinary surface does not show a percentage. Activity and data volume never award progress.

## Representative synthetic state

Use Maya Chen, the synthetic founder of Aperture House, on day 11 of a thirty-day proof. Her Brain is strongest around creative direction, forming around delegation and intentionally untested in areas she has not placed in scope. A former interpretation that she resists delegation has weakened. The sharper interpretation is that her perceptual criteria are not yet transferable.

The proof data must include:

- at least three longitudinal Brain versions;
- named judgement territories with different resolution states;
- chosen, rejected and held-out comparative examples;
- a no-Brain baseline;
- one correct refusal;
- one correction that narrows an interpretation;
- one territory that is intentionally out of scope;
- exact provenance and clear synthetic disclosure.

## Required experience truths

1. The first frame answers what has become meaningfully different for Maya, not how many items exist.
2. Progress is grounded in unfamiliar work or transfer, not ingestion.
3. A healthy decrease in certainty can be represented as progress when correction makes the Brain more honest.
4. The person can inspect why a state changed without encountering psychometric or retrieval jargon.
5. No domain, file type or category is framed as a compulsory completion target.
6. The experience respects private and out-of-scope territory.
7. One obvious next interaction can strengthen the Brain through a real contrast with minimal input.

## Non-goals

- No progress ring, completion bar, score dashboard, streak, leaderboard, file counter, memory counter or gamified points.
- No personality assessment, intelligence score, clinical diagnostic, employee evaluation or comparison with another person.
- No “digital twin,” clone, autonomous stand-in or claim that CTRL can decide exactly as Maya would.
- No generic analytics dashboard, radar chart, five equal stat cards or technical evaluation console as the primary experience.
- No sparkle symbols, gradients, glass cards, mascot or decorative neural network.
- No silent mutation, generated evidence, external send, production write or real customer data.

## Brand and interaction constraints

- Preserve the approved deep ink, warm cream, mint-supported and amber-change visual language.
- Keep the experience personal, editorial, physical and instrument-like.
- Use purposeful line iconography and restrained state-responsive motion.
- One idea owns the first frame. Detail is progressively disclosed.
- Mobile is primary at 390 x 844; desktop must hold at 1440 x 900.
- All controls require visible focus, semantic labels, practical touch targets and reduced-motion behaviour.
- The page scrolls; components do not.
- No em dashes, stacked AI-copy fragments or guff reassurance.

## Concept divergence requirement

Produce three independent spines with different sequencing, agency, primary interaction, information structure and state model. The set must include:

- one spatial or perceptual experience;
- one test or proof experience;
- one longitudinal narrative experience that can work without an aggregate score.

Each concept must explain its first thirty seconds, data contract, sparse and correction behaviour, reason it could create return desire, and its kill condition. Generators must not inspect one another's concepts.

## Judge rubric

Score each concept out of 100:

- Personal meaning and return desire: 20
- Metric truth and resistance to Goodhart failure: 20
- Comprehension without explanation: 15
- Distinctiveness without novelty theatre: 15
- Fit with the Living Brain and intervention-first product: 10
- Mobile and accessibility feasibility: 10
- Commercial storytelling power without false claims: 10

Hard failures:

- implies personal completeness or deficiency;
- rewards activity, volume or agreement;
- hides a blocking weakness inside an average;
- makes a number the motivational hero;
- converts healthy uncertainty into failure;
- requires invented or unavailable data;
- becomes a dashboard, quiz result or generic AI visualisation.

## Authority

Local research, synthetic fixtures, concept documents, independent judging and one playable synthesis are authorised. Production implementation, customer scoring, external claims, account changes, deployment and release remain gated.
