# G13 Living Brain R2 revision brief

**Status:** Material revision in the same temporal-to-map spine. Founder reaction pending.

## State of use

A busy leader returns between real work moments on mobile or desktop. They are not primarily looking for another task list. They want to feel that CTRL understands how they are changing, see the structure of their own judgement, explore what supports that read, and choose what is safe and interesting to show another person.

## User and action

The leader should understand one unusually specific personal development insight within ten seconds, enter an immersive representation of the Brain, inspect any claim without technical knowledge, and create a deliberately bounded share portrait without exposing private material.

## Governing rule

The Brain may make a synthesis only from source-backed current items and typed relationships. It must distinguish direct statement, observed pattern, hypothesis and unresolved tension. Visual proximity cannot claim meaning. A share portrait includes only items individually marked shareable and previews the exact audience-safe content before any external action.

## Data truth

R2 uses one deterministic synthetic fixture for the authorised demo identity. Counts, sources, versions, corrections, audience labels and relationship meanings are derived from that fixture. It proves the information shape, not the production pipeline or a psychological diagnosis.

## Materiality

Material. This revision changes the opening promise and hero from a decision-centred work instrument to an individual-centred growth portrait, while retaining the approved correction and Living Map architecture.

## Visual and interaction call

- Open with one precise becoming statement over a living but still Brain field.
- Show an earlier, now and becoming movement that is explicitly synthesis, not fate.
- Give three useful personal lenses: compounding edge, live tension and growth frontier.
- Make the wider Brain a showpiece with meaningful clusters, calm motion and inspectable semantic relationships.
- Put provenance, version, audience and correction history one tap behind every claim.
- Treat sharing as a curated public portrait, never a dump or public graph.

## Rejected route

A comprehensive quantified-self dashboard was rejected. It would increase apparent data richness while turning personal development into metrics theatre and making the user administer their own model.

## Authority

Local synthetic fixture, self-contained rendered proof, deterministic validation, screenshots and Git snapshot are authorised. No real authentication account, production data, Supabase write, deployment, external share or release is authorised by this revision.

## Proof

The revision passes only if a cold user can:

1. say how CTRL believes they are changing;
2. distinguish an owned statement from a CTRL synthesis and a hypothesis;
3. identify their compounding edge, live tension and growth frontier;
4. enter a visibly richer Brain and explore typed relationships;
5. open source, version, audience and correction evidence;
6. preview a bounded share portrait without any external send;
7. complete the primary path at 390 × 844 and 1440 × 900 without clipping, horizontal overflow, undersized controls or console errors.
