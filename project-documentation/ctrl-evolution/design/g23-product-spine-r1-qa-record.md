## Independent verdict

The [independent review](g23-product-spine-r1-independent-verdict.md) failed R1 before founder presentation. The defining thirty-day relationship was collapsed below a dominant synthetic case, the static-file difference was not visible in the main read, the approval lock was incomplete and the full-page responsive evidence was stale. R1 was not deployed or shown to Krish.
