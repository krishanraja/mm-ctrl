# G14 product-name research brief

**Research status:** complete. The founder accepted the recommendation on 7 September 2026, removed Mindy from consideration and deferred final product naming to the pre-launch GTM gate. See [the findings and decision record](g14-name-research-findings.md).

## Research brief

**DECISION:** Whether to replace CTRL with Mindy as the product name, retain CTRL, or adopt a branded architecture in which CTRL names the owned system and Mindy names the participant or interface.

**CHANGE CONDITIONS:** Prefer a full rename only if Mindy can plausibly be owned, increases trust and participation without creating generic-assistant or false-human expectations, stretches to the product's long-term ambition, and does not weaken enterprise credibility. Prefer CTRL if the stronger technology and ownership signal outweighs its harshness. Prefer a branded architecture if system and participant roles create complementary value without confusing users or doubling the explanation burden.

**BOUNDARIES:** English-language UK and US leader-facing AI software and advisory context. Inspect current software, assistant, education and adjacent professional-service usage. Include likely search, domain, app-store and trademark crowding signals, but do not present this as legal clearance or trademark advice. Exclude general baby-name popularity and unrelated local businesses unless they materially affect discoverability or ownership.

**FRESHNESS:** Current live product and naming evidence retrieved 7 September 2026. Human-computer interaction research may be older where it remains methodologically relevant; preserve publication and population scope.

**QUALITY FLOOR:** Official product/company sources, official trademark-search surfaces where accessible, current domain or app-store evidence, and original peer-reviewed or institutional research for anthropomorphism, trust and expectation effects. Search snippets and naming sites are discovery leads only.

**TIME/COST CAP:** Twenty-five minutes of no-cost research. No domain purchase, trademark filing, account creation, outreach or external mutation.

**STOP RULE:** Stop when additional searches repeat the same material name collisions; the three brand architectures can be scored across ownership, warmth, trust calibration, enterprise credibility, memorability, category stretch and migration cost; and the remaining uncertainty can be reduced only by legal clearance or target-user testing.

## Current hypotheses

1. Mindy is warmer, easier to address and better suited to a visible participant in the Mindmake thirty-day proof.
2. Mindy is likely materially more crowded and semantically generic than CTRL.
3. Human naming may improve engagement while also increasing social expectations, perceived agency and over-reliance risk.
4. CTRL better communicates system, agency and technical ambition but can feel controlling, harsh and impersonal.
5. A system-plus-participant architecture may retain both strengths if users can understand it in one sentence.

All five remain hypotheses until the evidence pass is complete.
