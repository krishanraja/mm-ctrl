# G23 round 2 concept A: Who has the floor?

**Concept revision:** A1
**Approval state:** concept only, not rendered or approved
**Founder decision:** whether this is the right product spine

## Use conditions

- **State of use:** Krish sees this remotely on phone or desktop and should understand it in one minute.
- **User and action:** Krish decides whether the relationship between the leader, expert operator and Brain is clear enough to become the product spine.
- **Governing rule:** the Brain prepares and learns, the operator decides when challenge has earned attention, and the leader owns purpose, judgement, polish and the call.
- **Data truth:** the concept uses no invented customer result or live product data. Any worked content is visibly a placeholder for real accepted evidence, judgement or uncertainty.
- **Materiality:** this is a new material surface.
- **Brand and voice:** warm with the leader, exact about authority, and visually sharp with weak systems. The visual system uses one semantic signal for who currently has the floor.
- **Authority:** approval covers the spine only. It does not approve final copy, implementation, deployment or release.
- **Proof:** this is a specification. Narrow and wide renders, representative states, keyboard use, reduced motion, contrast, zoom, long content and adverse data still require proof.

## 1. Governing visual and interaction metaphor

The artefact is a shared working floor with three fixed positions: **Leader**, **Expert operator** and **Brain**. A single pool of light marks who currently has authority to act. The light is functional, not decorative. It changes the weight, language and available action at each position.

Five unnumbered authority states sit beside the floor:

- **No interruption earned**
- **Preparation under way**
- **Challenge earned**
- **Human call**
- **Learning accepted**

They are choices, not steps. There is no start marker, direction, percentage or completion state. Selecting one changes the division of work on the same floor. The user can move between them in any order because real decisions do not behave like lessons.

The default view is **Human call**. The leader is lit, the other two roles stay visible, and the centre reads: **The call stays human.** This makes the authority rule clear before any mechanism is explored.

## 2. What the founder sees in the first ten seconds

The first view contains one claim, one relationship and one interaction.

> Your private AI Brain prepares the ground, an expert challenges weak thinking, and you make the call.

Below it:

> For 30 days, accepted evidence and judgement make each new high-stakes decision better prepared.

The three named positions are visible at once. In the selected **Human call** state:

- the Brain offers evidence, options, uncertainty and a proposed comparison;
- the expert operator presses weak reasoning and protects the quality bar; and
- the leader sets what matters, decides, applies taste and remains accountable.

The authority choices are visible without explanation. The proposition does not depend on the user opening anything.

## 3. How the thirty days are understood

The thirty days are the boundary of a working relationship, not a sequence of lessons. A quiet line above the floor reads:

> 30 days together. Real work decides when the floor changes.

The floor revisits its five authority states whenever a category, organisation, people or role decision demands it. Before the first session, public research and supplied context let the Brain prepare a useful but visibly provisional starting point. It can help immediately. No long extraction session blocks value.

As meetings, voice, pasted work, documents, email replies, decisions, corrections and outcomes arrive, the current floor state can draw on more accepted understanding. There are no day markers. The relationship is understood through repeated, better-grounded judgement rather than visible progress.

Quiet time is a real state. When no contradiction, risk or opportunity has earned attention, the leader sees **No interruption earned**. The Brain continues research and synthesis, while the operator watches the threshold for interruption.

## 4. How the three roles remain distinct

Each role keeps one permanent position and one non-transferable responsibility.

| Role | Carries | Never receives |
|---|---|---|
| Leader | Purpose, success condition, human boundary, consequential decision, voice, taste, final polish and accountability | An automatic verdict that replaces judgement |
| Expert operator | Engagement context, challenge, interruption threshold, quality pressure and independent review | Authority to make the leader's call |
| Brain | Research, synthesis, evidence labels, options, questions, uncertainty, memory and repair | Permission to promote its own interpretation or decide what ships |

The text at each position changes with the selected authority state:

| Authority state | Brain | Expert operator | Leader |
|---|---|---|---|
| No interruption earned | Researches and organises quietly | Watches whether attention is warranted | Continues without an ask |
| Preparation under way | Builds context and labels its standing | Frames the stakes and checks relevance | Sets purpose or confirms only what matters |
| Challenge earned | Shows why now, the smallest hard question, evidence and unknowns | Opens the interruption and sharpens the question | Answers, defers or rejects the interruption |
| Human call | Compares options and stays advisory | Challenges weak reasoning and tests consequences | Judges, decides, polishes and owns the result |
| Learning accepted | Proposes what should change and what dependent work needs repair | Checks the interpretation against the work | Accepts, corrects or leaves it provisional |

Distinct type weight, position and labels preserve these differences without relying on colour alone.

## 5. How the Brain's compounding becomes visible

A narrow fold at the base of the floor is labelled **What this understanding rests on**. It is part of the same object, not a separate destination. When opened, it shows the current Brain in plain language under four truth labels:

- **Said:** the leader's own words;
- **Supported:** evidence that can be checked;
- **Inferred:** the Brain's proposed reading; and
- **Unknown:** what is still missing.

Each item also has a standing: **provisional**, **accepted** or **corrected**. An inference cannot feed future preparation while it remains provisional. A corrected item keeps its history, names what it affected and shows that dependent work has been repaired.

Compounding appears inside the live preparation, not as a score. A small trace can say:

> This preparation uses [accepted judgement] and [supported evidence]. It no longer relies on [corrected inference].

The brackets are populated only from real material. The viewer can see why current preparation is more specific without comparing two versions or trusting a claim of improvement.

The fold also keeps personal and company material in visibly separate ownership bands with independent permissions. The leader can inspect both where allowed, then release a human-readable and machine-usable copy of what they own.

## 6. Where the supporting organs appear

The organs appear only when their authority state needs them.

| Supporting organ | Place on the shared floor |
|---|---|
| Low-friction capture | A persistent, quiet capture point at the Brain position accepts voice, paste, supplied material and forwarded replies. Saved state is visible. |
| Automatic research and synthesis | Visible under **Preparation under way**, with source and standing labels. |
| Adaptive ranked or tappable questioning | Visible under **Challenge earned** only when the operator opens the interruption. The leader can answer, defer or reject. |
| Deeper decision comparison | Opens between the three positions under **Human call**. It compares options, consequences, evidence, uncertainty and what must remain human. |
| Prepared intelligence | Appears as the Brain's contribution in **Preparation under way** and **Human call**, tied to the real decision or meeting. |
| Transparent Living Brain | The **What this understanding rests on** fold is available from every state. |
| Evidence, correction and self-repair | Appears under **Learning accepted**, then leaves a visible repair note in the fold. |
| Portable release or export | Lives at the end of the ownership bands in the fold. The export carries its context, truth labels, standing, corrections and permissions. |
| Quiet periods | **No interruption earned** is the default when attention is not justified. Silence is shown as correct behaviour, not an empty state. |

Nothing becomes a top-level product area. The current authority state determines what is present.

## 7. The exact final approval moment

In **Human call**, the leader sees one compact decision strip directly on the lit floor:

- purpose and success condition;
- strongest supported route;
- strongest counterpoint;
- material unknowns;
- what remains human; and
- what the operator independently challenges.

The only final control reads **I own this call**. It becomes available after the leader has seen the counterpoint and unknowns. Selecting it records the leader's decision, conditions and final human changes. It does not certify the Brain as correct.

The floor then moves to **Learning accepted**, where proposed learning appears beside the leader's decision. Nothing enters accepted understanding until the leader accepts or corrects it. The expert operator can challenge that reading, but cannot accept it on the leader's behalf.

For founder review of this artefact, there is also one approval scope only: **This relationship spine is right**. Final visual design, words, implementation and release remain outside that approval.

## 8. Mobile treatment

The phone view preserves the same shared floor and authority model in one first screen.

- The claim sits first, followed by the three role names in a compact row.
- The current authority state appears immediately below, with the active role contribution directly beneath it.
- The five authority choices use a two-row thumb-friendly set with no numbering or arrows.
- The three roles stack in a fixed order, with the lit role first and the other contributions still visible.
- Voice capture is available beside tap input. Text is optional, autosaved and recoverable.
- The Living Brain fold opens to natural page height. It never creates a nested scroll area.
- Every action has visible focus, a text label and a non-motion state. Semantic light movement becomes an instant border and type change when reduced motion is preferred.

Desktop widens the same object. It does not add navigation, extra product areas or information required to understand the relationship.

## 9. Strongest risk

The floor could look too orderly and imply that every decision passes through a neat repeatable routine. That would misrepresent senior work and make the product feel like coaching software.

The design counters this with unnumbered authority states, no connecting arrows, free movement between states, and **No interruption earned** as a valid default. The copy states that real work decides when the floor changes. The operator can withhold an interruption, and the leader can defer or reject one.

The risk remains material. A rendered test must ask a cold viewer whether they saw a flexible division of authority or a fixed five-step method. If they report a method, the concept fails and should not be rescued with more explanatory copy.

## 10. Why this is a different product spine

This concept is not organised around one example, a collection of metrics or a technical flow. The same shared floor explains the whole relationship across category, organisation, people and role decisions.

It differs on every required axis:

| Axis | This concept's organising rule |
|---|---|
| Sequencing | The surface changes when responsibility and authority change, not when time passes or an artefact advances. |
| User agency | The operator decides whether an interruption has earned attention. The leader can answer, defer or reject it, owns the final call and controls what becomes accepted learning. |
| Primary interaction | The viewer selects an authority state to reveal who prepares, challenges, decides and learns. They do not browse features or replay a single workflow. |
| Information structure | Three permanent role positions surround one live locus of authority. Supporting organs appear only where that division of work requires them. |
| State model | State is governed by earned interruption, decision authority and the standing of proposed learning. Time, completion and system activity do not define progress. |

It is not a case workflow because no example is the organising spine. It is not a dashboard because nothing is monitored and there is no status overview. It is not an architecture map because the surface shows human authority and consequence, not components or data movement.

The call is to render this as one cold first-read artefact and test whether a founder can repeat the relationship in one sentence: **the private Brain prepares, the expert challenges, and the leader decides and teaches it what may carry forward.**
