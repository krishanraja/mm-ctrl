# G22 independent primary concept verdict: Judge Beta

**Date:** 11 September 2026

**Mode:** Fresh primary judgment. No other G22 judge verdict was read.

**Authority:** Concept selection only. No implementation, product lock, production change or release authority.

## The call

**Candidate B, The Consequence File, wins as the synthesis spine. None of the three is fit to implement unchanged.**

B is the only candidate whose governing structure makes the complete product legible as one causal record: work enters, a consequential decision emerges, Maya changes the route, a learning remains proposed until she rules on it, that ruling changes later work, and the same governed record becomes portable. That is the whole CTRL promise in one loop.

Its current form still contains two serious regressions. The five named moments and persistent dated rail can read as a lesson path, while the question about whether a quality standard can "travel without final rescue" repeats the abstract language already rejected in G20 R3. Those are veto-level issues, not finishing notes.

The right synthesis is B's causal case, A's restraint and plain intervention, and C's ownership seam. Do not merge their shells.

## Why B wins

### 1. It shows the product by changing the record

B's strongest rule is: **actions create events; events move the current edge**. That is not explanatory copy. It is a product mechanism. The user can see that the Brain did not merely remember a sentence. It preserved who said it, what standing it had, what Maya changed, and which later work depended on the accepted version.

### 2. The £1.2m decision remains the centre of gravity

The case opens with a marketing decision affecting £1.2m and eight roles. The approved Decision Table is earned only after the evidence and Maya-specific unknown establish the decision lens. Learning remains a consequence of consequential work, not a lesson, quiz or reason to return.

### 3. Compounding is inspectable

The before/because/now strip is the clearest compounding proof in the set. It names the earlier state, the accepted Maya judgment and the exact later change. It does not claim commercial efficacy from synthetic data.

### 4. Ownership closes the same loop

The release is a version of the same governed case. Personal judgment, company work, public research, CTRL proposals, correction history, the decision and later use remain distinguishable. Portability therefore feels like ownership of accumulated intelligence, not an export feature bolted onto a dashboard.

## B's non-negotiable strengths

Keep these intact through synthesis:

- One living consequential case, not separate product areas.
- Work already present on arrival, with source, subject, purpose and audience intact.
- The Decision Table as one earned deeper state, unchanged.
- An append-only event model in which correction never rewrites history.
- One accepted or corrected Maya judgment changing one named fragment of later work.
- Rejected learning excluded from every later projection.
- A portable release generated from the same governed record.
- Quiet, sparse and stale states that refuse to manufacture intelligence.

## What B must borrow

### From A

- **One prepared note, not a queue.** B should open on the current work and one earned intervention. The history should stay quiet until requested.
- **The plain route-setting question.** Use a concrete choice with tappable answers and show its effect directly beneath the chips.
- **Restraint as intelligence.** Keep `Nothing needs Maya today` and remove any status language that exists only to keep the shell busy.
- **The margin stitch, used locally.** Borrow the small source and correction mark beside the active claim. Do not borrow the permanent right-hand margin.

### From C

- **The ownership seam.** Personal and company material should look separate at the exact point where a claim crosses into a decision or release.
- **Written standing labels.** `Proposed`, `Accepted by Maya`, `Corrected by Maya`, `Rejected` and `Stale` must remain legible without colour.
- **The exact carry trace.** The accepted learning should connect to the changed sentence only when a stored dependency exists.

Borrow those components. Do not import C's field, four positions, object taxonomy, source weave or graph behaviour.

## Required surgery before rendering

1. **Remove the five-stage navigator from the default view.** Show the current case and a restrained dated history control. Existing events may appear as plain evidence history when opened. Future moments never appear.
2. **Rewrite the Maya question in concrete language.** `Can your quality standard travel without your final rescue?` fails the twelve-year-old test and repeats the G20 R3 mistake. A viable version is: `If the pilot ships more work but Maya still has to redo the final version, has it worked?`
3. **Make the route effect immediate.** The chosen answer must change one visible gate directly below the choices before the Decision Table opens.
4. **Keep one product noun.** Use `case` or `decision`, not case thread, current edge, moment, amendment sheet, source bundle and ownership event as equal customer-facing concepts.
5. **Put the consequential call above product status.** The £1.2m question and why Maya sees it differently must dominate. Dates and versions support the decision.
6. **Keep phone history optional.** Do not use a horizontally scrolling chronology. Maya's phone opens on the one action she owns, with a plain `History` control if needed.
7. **Make Brain inspection a projection of the case.** It should answer what CTRL uses, who owns it, what standing it has, and what later work it changed. No network view.
8. **End with owned portability after compounding has been shown.** The representative path must show the later changed preparation before Maya approves the version she takes away.

## Veto conditions

Reject the synthesis before implementation if any condition below appears in the render:

- The left rail reads as progress, a course, a five-step flow or a feature tour.
- Any visible question requires Maya to interpret `quality standard`, `travel`, `final rescue`, `capture step`, `quality signal` or similar internal language before answering.
- A large editorial title or metaphor consumes space needed for the decision, evidence or action.
- The shell presents several status blocks, cards or counts at once.
- The Decision Table becomes the home screen or is restyled to fit the new shell.
- The later preparation can remain the same after the relevant learning is corrected or rejected.
- A proposed, stale or rejected statement changes later work.
- Personal and company material merge into one undifferentiated export.
- The proof implies that synthetic evidence proves better decisions, business performance or personal growth.
- Desktop needs a permanent empty side panel, and mobile inherits a decorative history strip.
- The user needs an explainer to understand case, edge, moment, amendment, standing or release.
- The whole representative path needs more than eight primary actions or more than one immediate Maya action per phone view.
- The product could be mistaken for chat, a knowledge graph, a kanban board, a dashboard or an LLM wrapper.

## Why none is fit unchanged

- **A** is the best behavioural idea but not yet a whole-product proof. It starts too late, ends portability off-path and risks rebuilding the rejected right panel with better words.
- **B** is the best product idea but stages causality so explicitly that it may become e-learning. Its key Maya question also regresses into founder-rejected abstraction.
- **C** is a strong component library wrapped around the wrong governing metaphor. It makes the user understand the field before the field helps them understand the decision.

If the next decision requires selecting one candidate exactly as written, select none. If the decision permits a disciplined synthesis, select B under the required surgery and veto conditions above.

## Render gate

Render one wide desktop state and one narrow phone state first:

- **Desktop:** the current £1.2m marketing case, one earned Maya-specific intervention, the minimum evidence behind it and the action that earns the unchanged Decision Table.
- **Phone:** the concrete route-changing question, two or three tappable answers and the changed first gate directly below the answer.

Show both cold. The synthesis passes this gate only if a first-time observer can state, without explanation:

1. what work entered;
2. why CTRL interrupted now;
3. what CTRL understood about Maya that a generic model would miss;
4. what Maya alone decided;
5. what later work changed because of that decision; and
6. what Maya owns and can take away.

Failure on questions 1, 4, 5 or 6 kills the spine. Failure on question 3 means the product still feels like an LLM wrapper.

## Final position

**Build the synthesis from Candidate B. Strip out its staged sequence, use Candidate A's restrained intervention and plain choice, then use Candidate C's ownership seam only where provenance or release makes it necessary.**

The sharper version removes the visible moment labels entirely and lets the current consequential case plus inspectable dated evidence carry the story. That trades some first-use orientation for more product truth and less e-learning risk. It is the version I would test first.

The strongest counterpoint is A. If a cold render can make arrival, compounding and owned export obvious without preserving a right-hand panel, its companion behaviour may feel more intelligent than B. The second counterpoint is that B's chronology may be necessary for first-use comprehension. The render should settle that question. Do not answer it with more labels.
