# G15 Brain Resolution R2 founder gate

**Date:** 8 September 2026
**Artifact:** `g15-brain-resolution-proof-r2.html`
**Status:** Conceptually useful, rejected as a standalone material surface
**Production authority:** None

## Exact founder reaction

> Its good conceptually, but it just could risk coming across as e-learny is all? I am helping people solve the big million dollar decision, the app should not focus on micro learning for tiny moments in a workflow or one meeting

## Classification

- **Conceptual mechanism:** useful. A visible account of what the Brain used to believe, what changed it and what it believes now can remain in the product.
- **Product role:** failed. The receipt became the destination rather than evidence behind consequential work.
- **Interaction:** failed. A sequence of explanation, evidence cards and a small test resembles guided learning.
- **Strategic scope:** failed. Delegation inside one workflow made the product feel smaller than the high-stakes AI-transition decisions Mindmake exists to help leaders make.
- **Visual execution:** not the root problem. More polish would intensify the wrong product signal.

## Governing correction

The customer-facing unit of value is a consequential decision with material strategic, financial, organisational or personal stakes. The product opens on that decision and helps the leader see it more clearly, test their view, confront evidence, compare futures and own the call.

Small moments may still contribute evidence. They are an invisible learning substrate, not the product's apparent purpose. A meeting, transcript, correction, work artifact or quick choice earns its place only when it sharpens the person's Brain for consequential work.

Brain Resolution is therefore demoted from standalone destination to a quiet supporting signal. It may appear as:

- a compact change trace beside a live decision;
- evidence that the Brain's current view became sharper, narrower or less certain;
- an inspectable reason for a new strategic contrast;
- a post-decision receipt showing what the Brain learned; or
- a longitudinal proof point in the Living Brain.

It must not appear as:

- a lesson path;
- a curriculum or micro-learning feed;
- a sequence of small exercises;
- a test the user completes for its own sake;
- a standalone progress destination; or
- the main reason to return.

## Restored product hierarchy

1. **Consequential decision:** the active million-dollar question owns the frame.
2. **Thinking partnership:** CTRL reflects the real decision, earns the human prior, reveals a material contrast, examines evidence and preserves disagreement.
3. **Owned call:** the leader decides, defers for a named reason or defines the real-world test.
4. **Brain consequence:** the system proposes or displays only the learning that improves future consequential work.
5. **Living Brain:** the user can inspect, correct and own the deeper model when useful.
6. **Small moments:** low-friction evidence capture happens in service of the layers above and is not marketed as the product.

## Relationship to existing decisions

This correction does not create a new product thesis. It restores the already approved `D-052` vertical-slice rule:

> CTRL is a relationship-embedded AI-transition decision and category-design companion for willing leaders. It builds a portable model of their judgement by creating immediate value through consequential work.

The current `ACTIVE_CASE` remains the right flagship synthetic decision: whether to reorient marketing completely around AI and redesign the division around high-agency, high-taste judgement and systems-administration capabilities. Marketing strategy, future operating model and current-team transition remain coupled but distinct.

## Disposition

- Do not produce a G15 R3 standalone receipt.
- Preserve R2 as evidence for a subordinate change-trace component and for the simple visual language of old view, correction and current view.
- Keep the provisional Judgement Resolution contract as an internal measurement hypothesis. It is not yet a customer-facing efficacy claim or product promise.
- Return the material surface sequence to the consequential decision experience and the Private Brain Builder that supports it.
- No implementation, customer scoring, deployment or external claim is authorised by this record.
