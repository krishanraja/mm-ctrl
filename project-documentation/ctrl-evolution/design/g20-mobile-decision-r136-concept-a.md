# R136 concept A: Decision Loupe

A handheld loupe over a decision ledger. Mobile magnifies three things: the live decision, the Brain's current recommendation and the unresolved question most likely to change it. The ledger beneath holds everything else.

## Live view

1. A compact decision statement and open state.
2. A warm-cream recommendation window with one route, one governing mechanism and the evidence cut-off.
3. One highest-value unresolved question with two to four thumb-zone answers and voice.
4. One quiet `Inspect basis` route to a separate full-screen ledger.

The primary interaction is answering one question. The old recommendation stays visible during recalculation; changed reasoning is amber and the current recommendation is mint. A separate `Record my call` action preserves the boundary between advice and human authority.

The ledger has Evidence, Routes, Case against, Sources and History as natural-scrolling full pages. At 320 by 568, the live view keeps a two-line decision, compact recommendation and stacked answer choices without inner scroll. The strongest risk is false precision: if question ranking is weak, the loupe may confidently magnify the wrong unknown.
