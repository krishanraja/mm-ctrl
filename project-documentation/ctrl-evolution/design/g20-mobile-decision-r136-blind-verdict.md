# R136 mobile decision experience: blind verdict

## Why the reset was required

The previous phone surface was a desktop dashboard stacked into a narrow column. It exposed the recommendation, counter-case, review question, Brain recognitions, current view, three routes, a human-decision prompt, a test action, an alternative-route action and the causal bet in one continuous reading task. At 390 by 844 it rendered at roughly 1,402 pixels high; at 320 by 568 it rendered at roughly 1,492 pixels high. The defect was the interaction model, not merely spacing or copy length.

## Independent result

Two blind judges received the same rejection history and three independently generated concept spines in different orders. Both selected **Decision Session**.

- Judge one: B 70/80, A 66/80, C 63/80.
- Judge two: B 68/80, A 67/80, C 61/80.
- No tiebreak was required.

Their shared conclusion was that mobile minimalism must come from sequencing. One active turn remains on the surface. Completed material, evidence, route comparisons, provenance and history move into deliberate deeper layers instead of remaining as collapsed dashboard furniture.

## Locked synthesis for the founder proof

1. The first state contains only the exact decision, a short conditional recommendation, its honest standing and one consequential question.
2. The answer is decision-specific, may be given by tap or voice, saves immediately and remains undoable.
3. Answering a question never records the leader's decision.
4. The prior recommendation remains visible while the Brain recalculates. The result says whether it held, changed or remains provisional.
5. The strongest counter-case appears only as the next optional challenge turn.
6. `Inspect basis` opens a separate full-screen layer for evidence, routes, the case against, sources and history.
7. Sparse, stale, conflicted, loading and failed states suppress false certainty and preserve the last truthful recommendation.
8. The public proof must work at 320 by 568 and 390 by 844 without nested scrolling or broken assets.

## Founder boundary

This verdict authorises one rendered, branch-only proof. It does not authorise replacing the responsive React experience. Product implementation remains gated on the founder's reaction to the proof.
