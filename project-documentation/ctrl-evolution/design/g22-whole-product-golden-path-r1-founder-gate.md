# G22 whole-product golden path R1 founder gate

**Date:** 11 September 2026  
**Artifact:** `public/g22-whole-product-golden-path-r1.html`  
**Gate outcome:** Failed; no material product or visual approval

## Founder reaction

After using the protected preview, Krish said:

> "yeah I guess so, I am still a little lost - this project began with such excitement for me and we're now a week later, and I am not really sure what we're approving or working on any more"

The qualified opening is not approval. The decisive evidence is that the founder could not identify the object being approved or reconnect the proof to the product that originally created excitement. The cold-comprehension gate therefore failed.

## Why it failed

1. **The proof enlarged one organ rather than revealing the organism.** It carried one synthetic decision through a technically coherent causal loop, but still made a case workflow the primary object. It did not make the full CTRL proposition inevitable.
2. **The approval scope was illegible.** Product thesis, operating model, AI behaviour, data architecture, visual direction, one interaction spine and one synthetic story were compressed into a single review. Krish was being asked to approve an undefined mixture.
3. **The product had to be inferred.** The artifact did not plainly connect research, intake, real work, the private operator relationship, consequential decisions, personal becoming, Living Brain transparency, longitudinal compounding and portable ownership.
4. **Local polish masked programme drift.** Each approved or improved organ was individually defensible, but repeated page-level work made the original ambition feel smaller, more procedural and less exciting.
5. **The synthetic case set the wrong altitude.** The Maya case demonstrated correction and later use, but it could still be read as a decision-workflow prototype rather than a hyperintelligent companion that comes to know a leader and helps them rebuild a category, organisation or role around AI.
6. **The pass test was too narrow in practice.** Browser journeys proved mechanics, state integrity and responsive behaviour. They did not prove that a founder could state what the whole product is, why it matters, who it transforms or what is being approved.

## What remains useful

G22 R1 is retained as implementation evidence, not as the product direction:

- one causal identity can survive from source through decision, accepted or corrected learning and later changed work;
- proposed, corrected, rejected and stale material can be kept from silently steering future output;
- a concrete question can change a visible decision gate;
- source standing, ownership, version and later-use traces are essential trust mechanics;
- the Decision Table remains a useful private operator instrument;
- the customer experience should remain minimal on mobile; and
- the Living Brain and portable release remain necessary parts of the wider product.

These mechanics are organs available to the next approved product spine. They do not determine that spine.

## What is retired

- G22 R1 as a founder approval candidate;
- the assumption that one end-to-end case is sufficient to restore whole-product comprehension;
- further page-level or state-level visual approval before the product itself is legible; and
- any interpretation of "yeah I guess so" as an explicit material lock.

## Reset gate

The next review object is not another screen. It is one founder-readable product spine that makes the complete product legible before visual implementation resumes.

It must answer, in one coherent model:

1. who CTRL is for and who operates it during the Mindmake proof;
2. what it learns before, during and after the work;
3. how it becomes uniquely useful to one leader without becoming a flattering persona wrapper;
4. how it helps with consequential AI-transition, category, organisation and role decisions;
5. what the customer experiences versus what Krish operates backstage;
6. how human vision, judgement, verification, polish and accountability remain load-bearing;
7. how the Brain compounds, corrects itself, exposes its evidence and preserves taste;
8. what happens across a representative thirty-day relationship;
9. what the leader and company each own and can take away; and
10. why this is materially better than a general LLM, enterprise assistant, folder of Markdown files or self-built company brain.

The founder question at that gate is singular: **Is this the product we are building?** Only an explicit answer locks the product spine and reopens surface sequencing.

## Authority and consequence

This failure record changes project state only. It authorises no production implementation, database action, customer data use, external message, merge or release.
