## Call

**Select Concept B, The Crossing, as the winning spine.**

B is the only candidate whose persistent object is the leader's reusable judgement across materially different AI decisions. That makes compounding the product rather than a claim about the product. It best answers the G22 failure because one example can remain a specimen while the organising model is explicitly multi-decision and cross-arena.

B is not ready to render unchanged. It underplays why the company pays, how the leader changes, what the expert relationship adds, and why the result is not a governed folder of statements. Those are required surgeries, not reasons to abandon the spine.

**Overall grade: B+, becomes an A if the synthesis makes company-funded transformation and relationship-specific advantage explicit in the first read, while keeping the Crossing as a cross-decision act rather than a diagram or case workflow.**

Concept C is the strongest cold demonstration of difference from ordinary AI, but it repeats the most dangerous structural move from G22: one prepared decision under pressure carries the burden of explaining the whole product. Concept A gives the clearest division of authority, but its five selectable authority states risk reading as a method, operating model or architecture rather than the product leaders and companies buy.

## Is this disguised repetition?

**No, not as a set.**

The candidates do not merely rename one page, proof or case. A makes authority the organising object. B makes cross-decision reuse the organising object. C makes the gap between generic and relationship-prepared judgement the organising object. They differ in what the viewer does, what information surrounds that act and what causes state to change.

There is, however, one candidate-level repetition risk. C is structurally adjacent to the rejected G22 route because a single decision specimen, changed condition, prepared answer and readiness approval again become the full visible product. Its counterfactual is valuable, but it should be imported as subordinate proof inside B rather than promoted as the spine.

## Did any candidate regress an invariant?

**Yes. One direct regression and two collective partial regressions need correction before synthesis.**

1. **C regresses the whole-product and approval invariants.** Its persistent object is one prepared decision under one changing condition. That repeats the shape that made G22 legible as a workflow but illegible as the product. Its stated final approval moment is also the leader's in-product readiness question, not the founder's required product-spine question. Both are blocking if C is used unchanged.
2. **All three understate company-funded value.** Each explains private usefulness better than the organisational or commercial change a company is paying to create. This is a partial regression of the reset's product truth and the expensed-buyer requirement.
3. **All three make improved preparation more visible than leader transformation.** Better preparation is a mechanism. The product outcome is a leader who sees more possibilities, exercises more agency, makes sharper judgements and leads useful AI change. B comes closest because judgement travels across arenas, but even B does not land the transformation plainly enough.

A's orderly authority states are a serious workflow risk but not yet a proven invariant regression. B's four arenas are a serious diagram risk but not a regression if one active crossing, rather than the field itself, controls the first read.

The core invariants otherwise survive: useful value before exhaustive capture, human ownership of purpose and the call, evidence standing, correction history, dependent repair, quiet periods, separate personal and company permissions, operator control of interruption, portable ownership and customer simplicity.

## Required surgery to the winning spine

### 1. Rewrite the first ten seconds around the buyer and mechanism

The first read must say, in plain language:

> A private AI Brain and expert operator help one leader make better calls about how AI changes their category, organisation, people and own role. Each accepted judgement can make the next decision better prepared. The leader still makes the call.

Add one equally plain company-value line:

> The company funds the work so the leader can turn AI into useful organisational and commercial change.

This should be part of the opening hierarchy, not buried in rationale.

### 2. Keep one Crossing active and make the wider product unmistakable

Do not give all four arenas equal visual weight. That creates an architecture diagram. Show one accepted judgement in its source arena, one materially different target decision and one human resolution. Keep the other arenas as quiet scope labels that prove breadth without becoming navigation.

The worked judgement must be specifically about AI changing the category, organisation, people model or leader's role. A generic leadership principle would collapse the concept into coaching.

### 3. Restore A's authority clarity without copying its floor

At the active crossing, make the three action rights visible:

- the Brain proposes the relevance and shows evidence, inference, contradiction and unknowns;
- the expert operator decides that the question has earned attention and challenges weak transfer; and
- the leader accepts, changes or blocks reuse, then owns the consequential call and final standard.

Use labels attached to the action. Do not add A's five-state selector or fixed three-position stage.

### 4. Restore C's counterfactual honesty as subordinate proof

The Crossing needs one compact cause line showing why ordinary AI or static notes would not produce the same next question. The difference must trace to authorised relationship context, such as a leader-confirmed boundary, an operator challenge, accepted evidence, a correction or an observed outcome.

Do not show ordinary AI as foolish. Use a strong generic baseline and name the exact private, governed input that creates the difference. This imports C's strongest proof without inheriting its one-case spine.

### 5. Make the leader's change visible, not only the Brain's reuse

The resolution should show both effects:

- the current decision is better prepared; and
- the leader's decision practice has become more explicit, transferable and inspectable.

Use G14's becoming priority carefully. One precise line about what the leader now sees, judges or protects is enough. Do not add a growth dashboard, score or psychological diagnosis.

### 6. Make the Living Brain active rather than file-like

The inspectable repertoire must show current standing, source type, permission, correction and affected later use. A correction must visibly pause or repair dependent crossings. This is what separates the Brain from a Markdown folder.

Keep G13's inspectability and owned portable release. Do not copy the map as the hero, use graph proximity as evidence, or make visual richness stand in for intelligence.

### 7. Preserve the thirty-day relationship without a timeline

Keep B's concise arc: it arrives provisional, becomes useful on the first real decision, improves through work and correction, and leaves as an owned portable Brain. Add the expert operator's ongoing role and the valid quiet state. Time must not promote an inference. Standing and permission do.

### 8. Keep the customer surface radically smaller than the operator instrument

On phone, the first viewport should contain the proposition, one source judgement, one target decision and one hard question. The response sits directly under the choices. Provenance, ownership, contradiction and wider Brain detail disclose into natural page height.

The G20 Decision Table remains backstage for Krish. Do not reproduce its comparison machinery on the customer surface. Mobile legibility remains unverified until 390 by 844 and 1440 by 900 renders prove text size, reflow, touch, keyboard, zoom, long content, sparse state, contradiction state and no nested scrolling.

### 9. Repair the founder approval gate

The final view should ask exactly one question:

> Is this the product we are building?

That approval locks the product spine only. Do not mix it with a feature request, visual preference, implementation approval or the leader's in-product decision action.

### 10. Keep every product claim additive and honest

Change universal wording such as “makes each hard AI decision improve the next one” to a governed capability: accepted learning **can** improve later preparation when relevance, permission and current evidence permit. Label the worked state illustrative. Include sparse, disputed, corrected, quiet and ownership-boundary states before founder review.

## Material dissent

Concept C may beat B in a five-second cold test. Its proposition is concrete, its ordinary-AI counterfactual is credible when run honestly, and its pressure switch demonstrates consequential value with less explanation than the Crossing. If the first B render makes viewers describe a diagram, knowledge map or library of leadership principles, C should not be quietly discarded. The correct response would be to test whether C's pressure interaction can sit inside a visibly multi-decision relationship without becoming the organising case.

Concept A also owns the clearest statement of human authority. If the B synthesis cannot make the operator's interruption threshold and the leader's non-transferable judgement obvious at the point of action, A's authority model should govern those labels even though its floor should not govern the layout.

## Standard close

**The call:** Advance B as the spine, with the ten surgeries above treated as acceptance criteria rather than optional polish.

**The sharper alternative:** Strip the first render to one active crossing, three action-right labels, one relationship-specific cause and the exact founder approval question. Put the thirty-day arc and Living Brain inspection one disclosure deeper.

**Next step in 24 to 72 hours:** Produce one narrow and one wide rendered synthesis using an AI-specific but clearly illustrative crossing. Cold-test whether a fresh viewer can state the buyer, thirty-day relationship, three roles, leader transformation, company value, compounding mechanism and difference from a general LLM or static files within one minute.

**Counterpoints:** B can become an abstract diagram if the four arenas dominate. It can also become a static principles library if the visible act is storage rather than a changed later decision. Either result is a blocking failure and should reopen the spine rather than trigger explanatory copy.
