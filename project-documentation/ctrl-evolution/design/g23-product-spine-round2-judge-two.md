**Revision:** R1

## Verdict

**Advance Concept B, The Crossing, as the winning product spine. Do not render it unchanged.**

B is the only candidate whose persistent object is naturally larger than one case and smaller than an architecture map. An accepted judgement moving between category, organisation, people and own-role decisions makes the whole-product breadth and the Brain's compounding visible in one act. It also keeps the leader's authority at the point where reuse is accepted, changed or refused.

B earns **B+**, and becomes an **A** only if the first view makes the thirty-day relationship, the expert operator's causal value, the leader's increased capability and the company's reason to pay explicit without adding a second explanatory surface.

C is the strongest challenger. It proves the difference from ordinary AI and reaches the correct decision altitude more sharply than B, but it risks making one pressure-tested decision carry the whole product again. It also ends on approval of a piece of preparation rather than approval of the product spine. A makes authority unusually clear, but its five selectable floor states are too close to a workflow selector and do not demonstrate the commercial or compounding proposition strongly enough.

## Is this disguised repetition?

**No, not at set level.** Round 2 clears the diversity gate. The candidates are not the same proof document with different labels.

Every pair differs materially on sequencing, primary interaction, information structure and state model. User agency also changes in substance, not merely wording. The set explores the three missing territories requested after Round 1: relationship choreography, cross-decision compounding and counterfactual value.

There is still candidate-level regression risk:

- A could become a five-stage service workflow even though its states are unnumbered and reversible.
- C could become another polished single-case demonstration, with the ordinary-AI comparison replacing the former proof sheet.
- B could become a four-arena diagram or judgement-routing canvas if the crossing is rendered as a map rather than one active transfer.

Those risks are not equivalent. B's risk can be controlled by radical focus. A and C would need a more fundamental change to their organising object.

### A: B minus

A's opening sentence and permanent division of authority are immediately understandable. It also treats quiet as legitimate behaviour and makes the operator's interruption threshold visible. Its weakness is that the product becomes a set of five operating modes. A cold viewer may remember who acted in each state but still not know why this private Brain becomes uniquely valuable, how the leader changes or why a company pays.

A becomes a **B+** if it stops making the authority-state chooser the primary interaction and demonstrates one cross-decision gain at consequential AI-transition altitude. At that point, however, it would be moving toward B rather than remaining its own stronger spine.

### B: B plus, winner

B has the best causal product sentence in the set: one accepted judgement becomes useful in a materially different decision. That is simultaneously a demonstration of breadth, memory with standing, compounding and human control. The three response choices make agency visible exactly where the Brain proposes reuse. Its mobile adaptation preserves the semantic act rather than shrinking a desktop field.

B becomes an **A** if the active crossing plainly shows that this is a thirty-day expert-supported relationship which makes the leader more capable of producing valuable company change. The current concept leaves too much of that in explanatory copy below the first view.

### C: B

C is the strongest proof of distinction from ordinary AI. It respects the baseline by making ordinary AI competent, then attributes the difference to authorised private context, operator challenge, accepted judgement, correction and observed outcome. It is also the only candidate to name a plausible moat measure and classify its counterfactual risk as blocking.

C remains a **B** because the pressure rehearsal is still one decision-shaped specimen. The wider product has to be inferred from standing metadata and claims that the pattern can travel. Its final question, `Is this preparation fit for your call?`, is the wrong founder gate. It asks for approval of a preparation object, not `Is this the product we are building?`

C becomes an **A minus** only if a cold viewer can explain the whole thirty-day multi-decision relationship without describing the example decision or the comparison. That is a harder repair than B requires.

## Did any candidate regress an invariant?

**Yes. C regresses the approval invariant.** The required founder decision is the product spine. C's final approval moment concerns whether one prepared position is fit for a call. That repeats the G22 ambiguity between approving an instance and approving the product.

A does not yet hard-fail the same invariant, but it creates avoidable ambiguity by specifying both `I own this call` inside the product demonstration and `This relationship spine is right` for the founder. The rendered founder artifact must contain only the latter approval question.

B preserves the hard invariants most completely: human call, standing before reuse, correction history, dependent repair, separate personal and company permissions, immediate value from provisional context, quiet periods, inspectability and portable ownership. Its commercial and thirty-day gaps are comprehension defects, not truth regressions.

There is one set-wide weakness that must be treated as a release gate. **None of the candidates makes company-funded value strong enough in the first read.** Each says that decisions become better prepared, but none yet completes the commercial causal chain: the company funds the relationship because the leader becomes more capable of making useful changes to the category, organisation, people model and own role. Convenience, preparation quality or accumulated personal knowledge cannot substitute for that outcome.

## Required surgery to B

These changes are conditions of advancement, not optional polish.

### 1. Complete the first-read proposition

The first view must state, in plain language, all four causal parts:

- for thirty days, a private AI Brain and an expert operator work with one senior leader;
- they work on real decisions about how AI should change the leader's market, organisation, people and own role;
- accepted evidence and judgement from one decision can improve a later, different decision; and
- the company pays because the leader becomes more capable of producing useful organisational and commercial change.

This must be understood before the viewer opens `Current understanding`, provenance or the wider repertoire.

### 2. Make the month felt without making a timeline

Keep one active crossing, but make its relationship history legible in three compact facts: it began from provisional research before the first session, it became useful on the first real decision, and the current question is sharper because of one accepted judgement or correction from earlier work. The owned day-thirty release should be visible as the relationship boundary, not a completion prize.

Do not add day cards, editions, a progress rail or a before-and-after document comparison.

### 3. Put the operator inside the causal mechanism

The operator cannot appear merely in a role legend. The active crossing must show one material act that only the expert relationship contributes, such as challenging whether the judgement survives a people consequence, adding missing context or deciding that the question has earned an interruption.

The viewer should be able to say why a private Brain alone is not the whole offer.

### 4. Show leader transformation, not only memory reuse

The outcome of the crossing should name the new human capability. For example, the leader can now apply a consistent human boundary across organisation and people decisions, see when that boundary breaks and make the later call with less repeated explanation and more explicit consequence.

Avoid claims that the Brain has completed a model of the person or made the decision for them.

### 5. Preserve one active object

On desktop, do not present four equal arena nodes. Show one source judgement, one selected target, one hard question and the three human choices. On mobile, use the same order in natural page flow. The wider repertoire, standing and ownership open only on inspection.

If the render reads as a quadrant, network, capability map or judgement router, fail it.

### 6. Make the difference from generic tools explicit but subordinate

One short note beside the active crossing should explain the earned advantage: a general LLM can reason from supplied context, and files can preserve information, but neither by itself governs what may carry forward, adds an expert's live challenge, repairs dependent uses after correction or lets the leader control the standing of personal judgement.

This is an evidence-backed distinction, not permission to belittle ordinary AI.

### 7. Restore the exact founder gate

After one complete crossing and the compact thirty-day relationship statement, ask only:

> Is this the product we are building?

The response may approve the spine or identify one wrong product rule. It must not ask the founder to approve the example decision, visual finish, interface, copy, implementation or release.

### 8. Range-test the honest states

The synthesis must render representative, sparse, quiet and contradiction states at 390 by 844 and 1440 by 900. Contradiction must pause dependent reuse. Personal and company permissions must remain distinct and readable. The response must sit at the point of action. There must be no nested scrolling, tiny provenance text or motion-only meaning.

## Material dissent

The numerical margin between B and C is small. C is materially stronger on two important questions: it reaches consequential decision altitude faster, and it demonstrates why the offer is more valuable than a capable general AI assistant rather than merely asserting the difference. If the synthesis team weakens or removes that counterfactual clarity, B may become a tasteful memory-transfer diagram with no obvious reason to buy.

I also disagree with treating B as render-ready because it won. The company-funded outcome is currently a supporting inference, the operator is too easy to overlook, and the thirty-day relationship is summarized rather than experienced. Those are product-comprehension failures, not copy polish.

The flip rule is concrete: if a cold viewer cannot state within one minute that the company is funding a thirty-day expert-supported relationship which makes a leader better at consequential AI change, reject the B synthesis. If they explain only that the Brain moves judgements between four areas, the surgery failed.

## Final call

Advance **Concept B, The Crossing**, through the eight required surgeries above. Preserve C's honest counterfactual and A's authority clarity as subordinate proof, while keeping B's cross-decision transfer as the sole governing object.

The sharper alternative is C, but only if B cannot make the paid thirty-day relationship legible without becoming explanatory. C should not be advanced in parallel.

The next action is one cold narrow-and-wide synthesis of B using one AI-transition judgement that crosses into a materially different arena, plus quiet and contradiction states. Founder review should ask one question only: **Is this the product we are building?**

Counterpoint one: B's four arenas can become a diagram quickly. Counterpoint two: the relationship may still feel intellectually coherent but emotionally smaller than the original promise unless the leader's increased capability is visible in the result.
