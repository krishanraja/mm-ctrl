## Hard-constraint and invariant verdict

### Candidate Q

**Hard failure: primary-object regression.** The brief says the primary object is a supported customer intervention and instructs the surface to lead with one action-worthy meaning. Candidate Q instead makes the Living Brain room and navigation the opening object, with the intervention reached after selection. This recreates the exact risk the founder calibration resolved: Krish should not have to operate or explore the model before learning what to do next.

**Invariant risk:** spatial overclaim. Typed relationships are protected, but clusters, a change halo, orbital geometry, and a moving pulse can still imply importance or semantic connection not supplied by the kernel. This is not the decisive disqualification because the concept states the risk, but it raises the burden beyond what the operator job needs.

### Candidate R

**Hard failure: unsupported prediction.** `Likely response` and `what is likely to happen` represent a future customer's behaviour as probable. The brief prohibits unsupported prediction and requires every claim to resolve to explicit evidence. A generated rehearsal branch may be presented as a possible route to test, never as a likely response unless the fixture contains direct support and calibrated uncertainty.

**Invariant risk:** generic assistant drift. The bounded rehearsal is not a universal chatbot, but conversational branching becomes the primary interaction. Without a strict accepted-preparation card and evidence-linked branch limits, it can reproduce the generic AI-assistant surface the brief excludes.

### Candidate S

**No identified hard failure.** It preserves the intervention hierarchy, human authority, no-chat boundary, and bounded proof scope.

**Residual invariant gaps:** it must make staged versus durable standing and all three audience lanes behaviourally distinct; it must not let converging evidence lines masquerade as semantic relationships; and a simulated post-session change must be visibly local fixture state rather than implied production ingestion.

## Synthesis constraints

The functional synthesis should remain one Candidate S spine and obey all of the following:

1. **Intervention first.** Within ten seconds, show one supported customer move, why it matters now, and one primary preparation action. Do not open on navigation, a Brain map, status, or a system summary.
2. **No recommendation laundering.** Label the underlying interpretation by claim type and standing. Show the exact recent evidence, what changed or weakened, the unresolved contradiction, and why the current gap makes this intervention useful.
3. **Trace what it tests, not what it will cause.** The rail must read `new evidence -> changed interpretation -> current gap -> intervention -> signal to listen for`. Do not promise customer behaviour, personal growth, or session outcome.
4. **Human authority at the action point.** Krish can use, reshape, rotate to a materially different route, or reject the interpretation. Rotation must not be a carousel of paraphrases. The verdict and uncertainty sit beside the action they change.
5. **One primary action per state.** Ready-now value, the deepening route, private ask, evidence inspection, and customer preview stay subordinate and appear only when relevant. No panel wall or project-management furniture.
6. **Private ask is bounded.** Offer tap and typing as complete paths, with voice optional. It is context-bound, private, and nonlinear, but never the default container or a generic chat transcript. No ambient listening is implied.
7. **Audience lanes are explicit.** Operator-private notes, customer-private Brain meaning, and company-shareable transition material must have different visible and behavioural treatment. The customer preview shows the exact audience-safe content and cannot send or publish it.
8. **R2 continuity without R2 copying.** Preserve deep ink, warm cream, restrained mint and amber, purposeful line iconography, personal becoming in the customer preview, and optional Living Brain inspection. Do not make the R2 map the operator's opening architecture.
9. **Correction and change receipt are real states.** A correction preserves prior meaning, updates local staged interpretation, shows any proposed durable learning separately, and says what did not change. No click or passive behaviour becomes durable personal truth.
10. **Every required representative state is addressable.** Include rich recent change, supported intervention, unresolved contradiction, weakened interpretation, one deepening route, private question and response, customer preview, sparse, quiet, loading, stale, and recoverable error.
11. **Mobile preserves meaning.** At 390 by 844, keep the intervention, evidence boundary, verdict, and primary action intact. Use one page scroll, no nested scrollbar, practical 44px targets, visible focus, keyboard operation, and reduced-motion equivalence.
12. **Visual traces cannot invent relationships.** The optical rail is a labelled event and reasoning sequence. Decorative field lines, proximity, pulse travel, and convergence cannot imply a semantic relation unless the current kernel contains it.
13. **Deterministic proof boundary.** Use synthetic fixture data and bounded schema-validated output in a deterministic renderer. Simulated transcript arrival, private ask, preparation, and preview must not imply live ingestion, persistence, model inference, external upload, account creation, or production write.
14. **Zero guff and replaceable identity.** Use action, evidence, state, or meaning copy only. No reassurance copy, fragment stacks, em dashes, technical memory language, Mindy, or identity-dependent architecture. CTRL remains a replaceable working label.

## Most consequential residual risk

Candidate S concentrates the experience around one model-selected move. If that intervention is based on a merely staged interpretation, visually confident presentation can launder uncertain synthesis into operator authority. Krish may take a sharper-sounding but weak move into a consequential customer session, damaging trust while the UI appears to have done exactly what it promised.

This is a blocking credibility risk, not an additive visual flaw. The cold proof must establish that Krish can state:

1. what evidence changed;
2. which interpretation is current and how strongly it stands;
3. what remains contradictory or unknown;
4. why this intervention is the best current test rather than the answer; and
5. how to reject or rotate the underlying interpretation.

If he reads the intervention as the Brain's authoritative recommendation, or cannot distinguish staged meaning from durable customer truth, reject the spine. Do not rescue it with more explanatory copy. The sharper fallback is a less visually decisive intervention treatment with the evidence gap and human choice carrying equal perceptual weight.
