# G13 Concept S: The Focus Field

**Concept spine:** Spatial-first Living Map

**Primary frame:** 390 by 844 pixels, used by a busy leader between meetings

The experience begins inside the Living Map, already centred on the one current focus most relevant to the user’s task. The user can move freely from there. The map is not a diagram beside the product. It is the product surface through which the Brain is understood, inspected and corrected.

## 1. Governing interaction metaphor

The metaphor is a **focus field**: a bounded, navigable space of current understanding that reorganises around whichever meaningful item the user chooses to focus on.

The opening focus is the current decision, “How should marketing change for an AI-native category?” It sits near the centre because it is most relevant to the current task, not because it is more true or important in general. Around it are the other eligible current items from LB01. Only the three evidence-bearing relationships are drawn. Each relationship carries a short human verb, such as “supports” or “qualifies”, and reveals its full plain-language meaning when touched.

The field settles once when the view or accepted state changes, then becomes still. There is no ambient drift. Position and distance serve navigation and legibility only. They never imply closeness, certainty, strength or causality.

## 2. Exact first 30-second sequence

**0 to 3 seconds:** The leader lands directly in the field. A quiet top line reads “My Brain”, “Private” and “Marketing strategy”. The current decision is in focus, fully readable. A brief settling transition places five other eligible current items around it. Reduced-motion mode skips the transition.

**3 to 8 seconds:** One relevant relationship is already legible without instruction. The standard “Before consequential work ships, the human owns the final judgement and finish” is connected to the current decision with the verb “supports”. Other current items remain visible but quieter. No legend or tutorial blocks the view.

**8 to 15 seconds:** The leader taps the standard. The field recentres on it and preserves spatial freedom. The exact relationship to the marketing aim stays visible. At the touched connection, the consequence appears in ordinary language: “Human release judgement protects the quality of AI-native category work.”

**15 to 22 seconds:** A single action appears beneath the selected meaning: “Why is this here?” Tapping it moves to the item-and-evidence level while retaining a reduced strip of the local field above. The evidence reads “You said this in an interview, 7 Sep, private” and shows the source statement. The source audience and basis are visible without technical vocabulary.

**22 to 30 seconds:** Directly below the evidence, the dominant action is “Too broad? Narrow it.” Tapping it asks one question: “What should this apply to?” The primary tap choice is “Only work that goes outside”; “Say it myself” offers voice or typing. By 30 seconds the leader has oriented, recognised their own judgement, understood one connection, inspected its source and reached the correction action.

## 3. Where the user has agency

The initial focus is a useful starting point, not a forced route. The leader can:

- pan the field in any direction;
- tap any visible current item to make it the focus;
- zoom with pinch or persistent plus and minus controls;
- use “Find in my Brain” to jump to an eligible item without spatial hunting;
- switch between Map and Portrait at any time while keeping the same selected item;
- return to the task-relevant starting focus with one control;
- inspect a relationship, source, applicability, exception or change history;
- correct, dispute or leave an item unchanged; and
- open a separate review layer for suggestions that are not yet accepted.

Keyboard users can move focus among visible items with arrow keys, activate with Enter, zoom with labelled controls and leave detail with Escape. Drag, pinch and voice are conveniences, never the only paths.

## 4. Primary interaction

The primary interaction is **focus hopping through direct selection**. Tapping an item makes it the stable centre of attention and reveals a bounded neighbourhood of eligible current items. Tapping a visible relationship reveals its meaning at that relationship. Tapping the focused item moves one level deeper into meaning, scope, evidence, history and correction.

This creates three predictable depths without a wizard:

1. **Orientation:** the task-relevant field, capped at 18 current entities.
2. **Neighbourhood:** the chosen focus plus no more than 23 other directly relevant or related entities.
3. **Item and evidence:** one meaning, its applicability, exceptions, sources, change history and available action.

The user can move laterally at the first two depths and back out at any time. Focus changes use a short, purposeful repositioning transition that ends quickly. The selected item also receives a strong static focus outline, so motion is never required to understand state.

## 5. Information structure

The mobile surface has four persistent layers, each with one job:

1. **Identity line:** whose Brain this is, its audience and the current task context.
2. **Living field:** current eligible items and decisions, with only supported current relationships drawn.
3. **Focus sentence:** the selected meaning in full, followed by one contextual action. When a relationship is selected, this area instead states why the two things are connected.
4. **Mode and navigation controls:** Map or Portrait, Find, zoom and return to current focus.

Selecting an item replaces the lower part of the page with its evidence and action content. The document itself scrolls if the evidence is long; the field and detail do not become separate scrolling containers.

Item kind is conveyed by a restrained shape mark plus a plain-language label such as “Aim”, “Standard”, “Preference”, “Tension” or “Decision”. Size may vary only with relevance to the current projection. Any size variation is deliberately modest so it cannot masquerade as confidence or value. Long meanings use a short readable line in the field and always expand to the complete wording on focus.

Suggestions and disputed material live behind a clearly named “Review” control. They use a distinct provisional treatment and are never mixed into the confident current field. A disputed prior meaning remains available in history but cannot guide the current view.

## 6. State model

The concept has the following explicit states:

- **Current orientation:** LB01’s six eligible references and three evidence-bearing relationships. The inferred proposed pattern is absent.
- **Focused neighbourhood:** one selected current item or decision, with a deterministic bounded neighbourhood.
- **Relationship inspection:** the type and complete plain-language explanation appear at the selected relationship, with evidence access.
- **Item and evidence:** full meaning, source basis, audience, applicability, exceptions, history and one available action.
- **Draft correction:** proposed wording is visibly temporary and outside the current field until the leader confirms it.
- **Applying correction:** input is locked briefly, the action shows progress and repeated submission uses the same correction request so a double tap cannot create another change.
- **Repaired current state:** the new version is current, affected relationships now refer to it, the portrait and map are rebuilt, and every known dependency has a visible outcome.
- **Review layer:** pending suggestions and disputed material are inspectable but visually and behaviourally separate from accepted current judgement.
- **Private audience rejection:** no meanings, counts, positions or relationship hints are revealed. The surface states that this Brain is private and offers only a valid access action.
- **Loading:** labelled neutral placeholders reserve the field without fabricating entities or relationships.
- **Stale:** the last verified view remains readable with its time visible; correction is paused until refresh succeeds.
- **Recoverable error:** the last verified view is preserved when available, the failed action is named and Retry is placed at the failure point.

The self-contained proof can exercise these states directly from LB01, LB03, LB04, LB05, LB06 and the deterministic LB07 scale fixture.

## 7. How Portrait and Living Map remain visibly one Brain

Map and Portrait are two modes in one persistent control, not separate destinations. Switching modes preserves the selected item, task context, audience label and update time.

The Portrait is a continuous current readout organised by the five human lenses: What matters, How I judge, My calls, Unresolved and What changed. It contains at most 12 high-value current items. It does not introduce summaries that lack a matching current reference in the field. A meaning that belongs to two lenses may appear in both, but both appearances select the same underlying item.

The selected item keeps the same shape mark, wording and state treatment across both modes. Choosing it in the Portrait returns to that exact focus in the field. After a correction, both modes show “Changed just now” on the same item and lead to the same before-and-after history. This visible continuity, plus canonical-reference parity in the proof, makes the two modes feel like one Brain viewed at different compression levels.

## 8. Correction and repair moment

After the leader chooses “Only work that goes outside”, CTRL presents one draft in ordinary language:

> Before externally consequential work ships, the human owns final judgement and finish; reversible internal drafts may remain delegated.

The original wording remains visible immediately above it. The only dominant action is “Make this my standard”, with “Edit wording” secondary. Until confirmation, the draft has no confident current treatment and no visible relationships.

On confirmation, the old version moves into history and the corrected version occupies the same focus position. The relationship to the marketing aim updates to “Human release judgement protects consequential AI-native category work.” The preference relationship updates to “Distinctive human voice remains part of consequential final craft.” The unchanged tension relationship remains untouched. A one-time change accent marks the revised item and affected relationships, then resolves to a static “Changed just now” state.

The repair receipt appears at the action point:

- “Your current Brain has been rebuilt.”
- “Board brief needs your review.”
- “Shared release needs your review.”
- “Nothing already shared was silently rewritten.”

The two review-required outcomes are actionable rows. The prior version and its evidence remain inspectable. The old version is absent from every current projection, and replaying the same correction produces the same receipt rather than another version.

## 9. Sparse and dense Brains

**Sparse first use:** The field may contain one current item with no relationships. It sits in focus with its full meaning and source. The absence of links is treated as honest, not as a broken visual. The single action is “Add what matters now”, with tap choices and optional voice or typing. Nothing is fabricated to make the field look fuller.

**Representative Brain:** LB01 opens with six current entities and three current relationships. The pending inferred pattern is not visible in the current field.

**Dense Brain:** With 120 items and 180 relationships, orientation never shows more than 18 entities. Selecting one deterministically opens a neighbourhood capped at 24 total entities, including the focus. Free panning remains available within the current set; selecting or finding another item recomputes the bounded neighbourhood. The user never has to render or comprehend all 120 at once.

At narrow widths, relationship verbs appear only for the active connection, while all supported links retain generous touch targets. Labels can wrap to two lines in the field and are never reduced below readable size. Focusing reveals the full label. Desktop widens the same field and detail relationship without changing the information hierarchy or state meanings.

## 10. Strongest reason it could fail

The blocking risk is that people may assign meaning to spatial distance or prominence even when the interface does not. If a user reads nearby items as more strongly related, or larger items as more certain, the field overclaims the Brain and fails the trust requirement.

The proof should therefore be cold-tested at 390 by 844 pixels. After 30 seconds, a user must be able to name the supported relationship and explain that only the labelled connection carries meaning. They must not infer that proximity means strength or similarity. If that distinction does not hold without explanation, this concept should be rejected rather than repaired with a heavier legend.

The secondary risk is control friction on a small screen. The non-drag controls, Find action, stable focus outline and bounded neighbourhood are necessary safeguards, but they still require observed usability proof.

## 11. Constraints that make it materially different

1. The Living Map occupies the primary surface from the first frame. It is not a decorative visual beside summaries or metrics.
2. The experience starts on one task-relevant current focus and immediately expresses a useful relationship in plain language. It does not start with navigation, counts, setup or a conversation box.
3. Every visible semantic relationship must be typed, current, audience-eligible and evidence-bearing. Similarity and layout forces never become visible meaning.
4. Spatial position and distance are explicitly non-semantic. Relationship meaning appears only through a labelled connection and its evidence.
5. Free spatial movement is preserved, but bounded semantic zoom prevents the user from facing the full graph at once.
6. Correction happens on the selected meaning in place. The consequence and repair receipt appear exactly where the user acts.
7. A correction appends a new current version and repairs projections while flagging consequential outputs for human review. It never silently rewrites delivered work.
8. Portrait and Map preserve the same selected item, wording, audience and current version. The Portrait is not a separate dashboard with independently generated summaries.
9. Proposed and disputed material is excluded from the confident field and placed in an unmistakably provisional review layer.
10. Motion occurs only when focus or accepted state changes, then stops. There is no decorative animation pretending the system is thinking.
