# G20 post-rejection sanitized brief

**Revision:** G20-RESET-SB-R1
**Date:** 8 September 2026
**Status:** generator-safe brief for fresh concept divergence

## User and moment

Maya is the synthetic founder and CEO of Aperture House. The company pays for a thirty-day Mindmake engagement. Maya uses the product between sessions on mobile and during consequential working sessions on desktop. She is nontechnical, busy and already willing to rethink how her company operates around AI.

The decision in this proof has material organisational and commercial consequences: whether Aperture House should rebuild its marketing function around AI-native category creation now, run a contained pilot first, or keep the current team and add tools gradually.

## Observable outcome

Within ninety seconds Maya should:

1. recognise that the product has formed a specific and evolving understanding of how she thinks;
2. see one insight that changes how she frames the decision;
3. understand what evidence supports that insight, what remains unknown and where her own judgement is still required;
4. leave with a sharper decision or a concrete uncertainty-reducing move; and
5. be able to continue the work in Claude without losing the relevant Brain context, then bring work back for assessment against her own standards.

The product must feel like a thoughtful participant that has prepared before Maya arrived. It must not feel like storage, retrieval, a course, a dashboard of activity, a technical integration or an LLM chat wrapper.

## Product invariants

- The Brain helps Maya become more capable. Company value is created through stronger personal perception, judgement, standards and accountability.
- The product operates at consequential AI-transition altitude, not generic business advice, software training or administrative efficiency.
- Maya owns the call. The product may form a view, challenge her and recommend a test, but cannot impersonate certainty or decide for her.
- Insight requires provenance. Direct evidence, supported synthesis, provisional inference, correction and unknown remain visibly different.
- Productive challenge matters more than affirmation. The Brain must reveal at least one tension or counter-case that Maya has not cleanly resolved.
- Taste and judgement must become more transferable without flattening Maya or her team into one formula.
- AI output returns as separately attributed proposed material. It cannot become trusted memory or fact automatically.
- Sparse evidence produces a precise gap and a useful next move. It never manufactures a personal insight.
- Capture, privacy, audience and tool transport are quiet infrastructure. They appear only when an action makes them relevant.
- The complete experience must be plain enough for a twelve-year-old to retell accurately.

## Rich synthetic data available

The proof may use the complete fixture in `g20-reset-maya-rich-fixture.json`:

- twelve mixed evidence sources across direct words, work samples, operating data, customer evidence and an operator-private note;
- five accepted standards and anti-standards;
- four personal patterns, including one corrected interpretation;
- three materially different strategic routes;
- a current synthesis, live tension, counter-case and four explicit unknowns;
- a source-backed personal development frontier;
- an inspectable full context brief suitable for Claude when no connector is installed; and
- a deliberately generic Claude response that can be assessed against Maya's evidence and standards.

All data is synthetic and must remain labelled as such. The proof establishes interaction and information quality only, not real diagnostic accuracy or customer outcome.

## Interaction constraints

- One primary task and one obvious next action at a time.
- The opening must carry specific meaning before asking Maya to do anything.
- No repeated explanation of the same safety or custody state.
- No technical identifier, unresolved reference, token, connector setup or memory taxonomy in the normal path.
- Every active-looking control works in the proof.
- Mobile puts the current insight, decision and next action first. Desktop uses its space for evidence, comparison or consequence, not atmosphere.
- Voice is available where it reduces effort. Typing is optional.
- Navigation labels name destinations a nontechnical leader would choose.
- Visuals, icons, motion and spatial relationships must carry literal meaning with text equivalents. Decorative AI imagery, neural wallpaper, sparkle motifs and generic graph theatre are prohibited.
- The existing Mindmake visual standard is the floor: deep ink, warm paper, semantic mint and amber, purposeful instrument marks, editorial display type and visible craft.

## Claude continuation contract

The preview cannot rely on a live connector. Its normal test path must therefore copy a complete synthetic brief containing:

- the exact decision and desired output;
- Maya's provisional view;
- supported Brain synthesis and counter-case;
- applicable standards and anti-standards;
- important evidence with human-readable source labels;
- unknowns Claude must not invent;
- the requested reasoning method and output format; and
- instructions to separate supplied evidence from new reasoning.

The copied brief must produce a useful answer in an ordinary new Claude chat without any external reference resolver.

When an answer returns, the proof must identify generic reasoning, unsupported assumptions, missing numerical evidence, conflicts with Maya's standards and anything genuinely additive. It must propose how to sharpen the work without pretending that AI agreement proves quality.

## State range

The concept must remain honest across rich, sparse, contradictory, corrected, stale, slow, wrong-customer, offline-Claude, long-name, narrow-mobile and reduced-motion states. These states may be demonstrated in a test harness rather than occupying the main experience.
