# R136 concept C: Decision Sounding

A compact Brain cross-section where one live decision sits on the surface and supporting layers sit visibly beneath it. The user enters depth deliberately, one layer at a time.

## Live plane

1. A short decision and updated state.
2. The dominant Brain recommendation in mint.
3. One valuable unknown in amber.
4. Two or three answer choices in the thumb zone plus voice.

Subtle stacked edges signal four deeper planes. `Explore layers` opens a linear index: Case, Routes, Counter-case and Evidence. Each plane replaces the previous one; there is no pan, pinch or accidental swipe. A persistent return takes the user back to the live decision.

An answer saves first, then the recommendation either holds or changes with an amber text diff. The decision stays open until the human explicitly records it. At 320 by 568, stacked edges become thin offsets and longer answers move to a full-width sheet. The strongest risk is mystification: a spatial Brain metaphor can hide evidence and make the system feel more authoritative than it is.
