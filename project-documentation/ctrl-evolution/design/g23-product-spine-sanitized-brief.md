# G23 product spine sanitized divergence brief

**Revision:** R1
**Date:** 11 September 2026
**Audience:** independent concept generator

## Task

Create a concept spine for one founder alignment artifact. It must make a private AI product understandable in one minute and show what happens across a thirty-day relationship. This is not an app screen, dashboard, technical diagram, pitch deck or feature list.

## Product

The product is a private AI Brain for a senior leader. It learns how that specific person sees, decides and judges through real work. During a thirty-day engagement, it helps the leader and an expert operator make better high-consequence decisions about how AI should change the leader's market, organisation, people and own role.

The company pays because the leader becomes better at creating useful organisational and commercial change. The personal Brain is the mechanism.

## Three roles

- **Leader:** brings the real stakes, sets the intent, supplies or confirms context, makes the consequential call, applies taste, polishes the final work and remains accountable.
- **Expert operator:** conducts the engagement, sees the rich backstage picture, adds context, challenges weak thinking and chooses when the leader should be interrupted.
- **Brain:** researches and organises context, notices patterns and contradictions, prepares specific questions and options, tracks evidence and uncertainty, proposes learning, repairs itself after correction and makes later work more useful.

## Required thirty-day truth

- Before the first session, ethical public research and supplied context create a provisional starting point.
- Value begins before the Brain is complete. It must not impose a long extraction ritual.
- Meetings, voice, pasted work, documents, email replies, decisions, corrections and outcomes can deepen it over time.
- It asks the smallest hard question that could materially change a real decision.
- The decision concerns AI's effect on the leader's category, organisation, people or own role. It is not generic business coaching.
- A human owns the call and the final quality bar.
- The Brain distinguishes what someone said, what evidence supports, what it inferred and what remains unknown.
- A proposed interpretation cannot shape later work until its standing permits it. Correction preserves history and repairs dependent work.
- Later preparation is visibly better because of earlier accepted evidence or judgement.
- The leader can inspect a living representation of what the Brain currently understands and take away a human-readable, machine-usable version they own.
- Personal material and company material retain separate ownership and permissions.
- The customer's participation stays radically simple and mobile-friendly. The expert operator may use a richer private workspace.

## Supporting organs

The concept must place these without turning them into separate products:

- low-friction capture;
- automatic research and synthesis;
- adaptive ranked or tappable questioning when useful;
- a deeper decision-comparison instrument;
- prepared intelligence for decisions, meetings and category shifts;
- a transparent Living Brain;
- evidence, correction and self-repair;
- portable release or export; and
- quiet periods when no interruption has earned attention.

## Human agency

AI may carry information movement, analysis, manipulation, enrichment and administrative preparation. It must not remove the human from:

- setting the purpose and success condition;
- deciding what should remain human;
- judging validity, quality and consequences;
- making the consequential call;
- applying voice, taste and final polish;
- critically assessing independent AI review; or
- accepting accountability for what ships.

## Failure requirements

Avoid any concept that:

- makes one workflow or case look like the whole product;
- requires the viewer to infer the proposition from polished mechanics;
- resembles e-learning, a course, a progress tracker or generic coaching;
- leads with feeds, dashboards, feature navigation or technical architecture;
- uses a graph, generated image or decorative motion as proof of intelligence;
- relies on large editorial declarations, cryptic headings or verbal excess;
- flatters the leader, gives generic advice or behaves like a confident answer machine;
- promises a complete person model or diagnostic certainty;
- treats convenience, admin automation or time saved as the main value;
- anthropomorphises the product with a human name or persona;
- merges personal and company ownership; or
- asks the founder to approve more than the product spine.

## Expression constraints

- Plain British English, understandable by a twelve-year-old.
- No em dashes.
- No eyebrow above a heading.
- No commands, hype, invented outcome, empty superlative or AI-writing cadence.
- Warm with the leader and sharp with weak systems or ideas.
- Visual meaning must come from the product relationship and state change, not decorative imagery.
- It must work as one coherent artifact on remote phone and desktop.
- Deeper detail may exist, but the first read must stay simple.

## Required generator output

Provide:

1. the governing visual and interaction metaphor;
2. what the founder sees in the first ten seconds;
3. how the thirty days are understood without becoming a progress course;
4. how the three roles remain distinct;
5. how the Brain's compounding becomes visible;
6. where the supporting organs appear;
7. the exact final approval moment;
8. the mobile treatment;
9. the strongest risk in the concept; and
10. why this concept is materially different from a case workflow, dashboard or architecture map.

Do not inspect previous CTRL mocks, G22 concept files, screenshots, layout descriptions, judge verdicts or other generators' work.
