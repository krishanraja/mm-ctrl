# G23 product spine synthesis

**Date:** 11 September 2026
**Selected spine:** Round 2 Concept B, The Crossing
**Status:** selected for one founder alignment render; not founder-approved

## Selection

Round 1 failed because all three generators produced the same proof-document concept. The set was rejected before founder review. Round 2 used three distinct territories: relationship choreography, cross-decision compounding and counterfactual value.

Both independent primary judges selected **The Crossing**. Each judged every Round 2 pair materially different on all five tested axes. No tiebreaker was required.

The Crossing wins because the organising object is larger than one case and smaller than a system map: a piece of accepted judgement earns careful reuse in a materially different AI decision. That single act shows breadth, compounding, permission, contradiction, human authority and later usefulness.

## Product sentence

Mindmake builds a private AI Brain with one leader through thirty days of real work. Krish and the Brain prepare the decisions AI is forcing across the leader's market, organisation, people and own role. The leader keeps the judgement, final standard and accountability. Accepted evidence and judgement can make a later, different decision better prepared.

The company funds the work because the leader becomes more capable of producing useful organisational and commercial change. The personal Brain is how that capability compounds and remains owned.

## Governing visual act

Show one active Crossing:

1. one leader-owned judgement has already earned standing in an organisation decision;
2. the Brain sees that it may matter to a different people decision;
3. Krish challenges the weak or incomplete transfer and decides the question has earned attention;
4. the leader accepts, changes or blocks the reuse;
5. the later decision becomes more specific without surrendering the final call; and
6. the Living Brain records what may now carry, its scope, source, permission and correction history.

The other arenas remain quiet scope markers. They do not become equal nodes, navigation or a diagram.

## Illustrative crossing

This is illustrative synthetic product content. It proves the product model, not diagnostic efficacy.

**Accepted organisation judgement**

> AI carries the repeatable middle. People own the purpose, final standard and consequences.

**Different people decision**

> Which marketing roles still create value once AI can produce the first draft?

**What a capable general AI can prepare**

> Pilot the new roles, train the team and compare output quality before a wider restructure.

This is reasonable. It lacks the leader's accepted human boundary, the operator's challenge and the evidence that old output measures previously restored old behaviour.

**What the relationship adds**

- **Brain proposed:** carry the accepted human boundary into the role design.
- **Krish challenged:** changing roles while preserving volume-led measures preserves the old system.
- **Leader decides:** whether the judgement carries across unchanged, changes for this decision or stays in its original arena.

**Hard question**

> If the team keeps the same output targets, what will stop it producing more average work?

The response changes the target decision in place. It does not approve an interpretation globally.

## First-read structure

The first desktop and phone view must establish:

1. a private AI Brain and expert operator work with one leader for thirty days;
2. they work on consequential decisions about AI and the leader's market, organisation, people and own role;
3. the company pays for more capable organisational and commercial change;
4. the Brain proposes, Krish challenges and the leader decides; and
5. accepted judgement can improve a later, different decision.

No graph, dashboard, navigation, feature inventory or technical architecture appears before this is understood.

## Thirty-day relationship

Keep the month visible as four plain facts, not a timeline:

- it arrives prepared from ethical public research and supplied context, with uncertainty intact;
- it becomes useful on the first real decision rather than waiting to finish a person model;
- it improves through meetings, voice, replies, decisions, corrections and outcomes; and
- it leaves as an inspectable, human-readable and machine-usable Brain the leader can take away.

Quiet is valid. If no question has earned attention, the customer receives no manufactured activity.

## Roles

- **Brain:** researches, organises, notices contradictions, prepares options, labels standing and proposes reuse. It cannot promote its own inference or make the call.
- **Krish:** sees the rich backstage picture, adds relevant context, challenges weak thinking and controls whether and when the leader is interrupted.
- **Leader:** sets purpose and success, decides what remains human, accepts or changes reuse, makes the consequential call, applies taste and polish and owns what ships.

These action rights appear on the active Crossing. They are not a separate explanatory diagram.

## Supporting organs

- Capture feeds the Brain through transcripts, voice, short replies, paste and supplied work.
- The Decision Table remains Krish's deeper backstage instrument when a Crossing needs harder comparison.
- Prepared intelligence brings only evidence or category shifts that could change the live call.
- The Living Brain exposes what is said, supported, inferred or unknown, plus standing, ownership, correction and later use.
- Portable release separates personal and company material and remains understandable without Git knowledge.
- Email and customer prompting remain later governed routes under Krish's timing and approval control.

No organ becomes a competing product or first-level navigation item in this alignment artifact.

## Living Brain and difference from substitutes

The visible Brain is an active repertoire of judgement, evidence, exceptions, contradictions and decisions. Each item states what it may influence and where it was later used.

A capable general LLM can reason from whatever context someone supplies in the moment. A folder can preserve information. Neither alone supplies this complete mechanism:

- relationship-specific context gathered across real work;
- expert challenge and interruption judgement;
- explicit standing before personal judgement may carry forward;
- correction-aware repair of dependent uses;
- separation of personal and company ownership; and
- an inspectable, portable model of what the leader has actually authorised.

The artifact should demonstrate this with one compact cause line, not a comparison essay.

## Leader change

The Crossing must show a change in the leader as well as a change in preparation. The intended illustrative movement is:

> The leader can now apply a clear human boundary across organisation and people decisions, see where incentives break it and hold the final standard without becoming the bottleneck for every draft.

This is a bounded synthesis, not a diagnosis or performance claim.

## Mobile rule

The first phone view contains the product sentence, payer logic, three role rights and a visible entrance into the thirty-day relationship. The first short scroll completes the month and the difference from a general AI or folder. One source judgement, one target decision and one hard question then appear as compact supporting proof, not as the product or an interactive workflow. The rich Decision Table stays backstage. Provenance, ownership, contradiction and the wider Living Brain disclose into natural page height.

## Founder approval

The final question is exactly:

> Is this the product we are building?

Approval locks only the product spine. It does not approve the example, final copy, interface, visual system, implementation, data action, deployment, merge or release.

## Render vetoes

- no four-node map, quadrant or judgement-routing diagram;
- no single case presented as the product;
- no timeline, progress rail, stages or completion score;
- no proof sheet, document editor or margin metaphor;
- no feature navigation or dashboard;
- no full Decision Table on the customer side;
- no weak generic-AI straw man;
- no large pompous claim, cryptic heading or repeated trust copy;
- no decorative imagery, sparkle symbol or graph-as-intelligence theatre;
- no claim that time alone strengthens standing;
- no merger of personal and company permissions; and
- no more than one founder approval question.

## Render pass

The render reaches Krish only if a fresh viewer can state within one minute:

- who pays and why;
- what happens across the thirty days;
- what the Brain does;
- what Krish uniquely contributes;
- what the leader alone decides;
- how one decision can improve a different later decision;
- why this is not merely a general LLM or folder of notes; and
- what product spine the founder is being asked to approve.
