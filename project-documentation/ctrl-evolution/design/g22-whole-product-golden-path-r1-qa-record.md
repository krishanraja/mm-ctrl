## What was built

R1 is one responsive product shell carrying one consequential case through:

- current company work and decision stakes;
- a Maya-specific evidence-backed interruption;
- one concrete route-changing question;
- a deeper three-route Decision Table state;
- a human-owned working call and stop rule;
- an over-broad learning proposal;
- correction, acceptance and rejection;
- a later preparation changed by the accepted version;
- a Living Brain projection with written standing and ownership;
- a functional local download of a self-describing Brain bundle.

The default view exposes no staged navigator or visible future event. Existing events appear only through History. The customer-sized phone view contains one immediate question and hides the operator work sheet.

## Concept evidence

Three isolated generators produced materially different spines. The [distance record](g22-concept-distance-record.md) found every pair different on five axes. Two independent primary judges received different anonymous candidate orders and both selected The Consequence File. Both required the same surgery: remove progress-like chronology, use a concrete question, borrow one-note restraint, preserve a personal/company ownership seam and expose the exact later change.

The selected [synthesis](g22-whole-product-golden-path-synthesis.md) and [trace](g22-concept-trace.md) preserve that decision.
