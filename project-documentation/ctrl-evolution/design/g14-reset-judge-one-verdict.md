## Selection

### Winner: Candidate M

Candidate M is the strongest interaction spine for the founder proof. It turns the wide desktop into a working surface where current meaning, competing or historical meaning, exact evidence, uncertainty and next action remain visible together. That directly answers the founder's complaint that the previous surface wasted space announcing itself. It is also the most defensible under scrutiny because the operator can move from an action back through its reasons to exact source rows without leaving the frame.

Candidate K is the closest challenger. It has the strongest Living Brain integrity and the highest visual ceiling, but makes comprehension depend on a new spatial grammar. Candidate R has the best distinction between a current belief and a stronger commitment, but its central control promises a degree of clearance logic the current fixture may not contain.

### Strongest feature to preserve from each losing candidate

- **Candidate K:** uncertainty physically breaks the active relationship route. Preserve that consequence inside Candidate M's Brain lens so an unsupported link cannot look continuous or operative.

- **Candidate R:** separate `best current belief` from `safe basis for stronger action`. Preserve this as plain action wording in Candidate M's dock, without a lever, gauge or implied threshold.

## Render-critical synthesis advice

Render Candidate M as one decision bench, not as a generic enterprise dashboard and not as a collage of all three concepts.

1. The first read must contain only the decision, current meaning, one decision-relevant uncertainty and the fixture-backed next move. Use object labels, not announcement titles.
2. Use only the interpretation columns the fixture actually supports. Do not create a third alternative to fill space. Empty fields must state what is missing.
3. Keep at most five decision-facing comparison rows, and remove any row that does not change understanding, uncertainty or action.
4. Add one compact dated change trace inside the evidence bench: latest evidence, earlier interpretation, current interpretation and future test. It must not become a scroll-led story.
5. Increase the Living Brain allocation enough to show a legible local typed and directed route. Use only the fixture's allowed relationship types. An unsupported or historical edge must visibly stop before the current action chain.
6. Keep the action dock persistent, but label the local proof action precisely. Do not imply send, publish, durable write or production execution.
7. Preserve the private boundary in the frame and prove projection by exclusion before layout. A private working question may filter the view but must remain labelled as temporary.
8. Use mint only for supported current meaning or an available action, and amber only for change, tension, staleness or decision-relevant uncertainty.

## Material founder-gate risk

The winning render can still fail if the comparison table becomes a dense enterprise dashboard and the Living Brain becomes a tiny decorative thumbnail. That would replace the previous editorial waste with analytical clutter while still making Krish work to find the move. The cold founder test should ask one thing: within five seconds, can Krish state the decision, the Brain's current meaning, the uncertainty that matters and the next action, then inspect the exact evidence without a walkthrough?

Select Candidate M for one rendered founder proof, subject to the render-critical conditions above. This verdict does not approve implementation, deployment, production data, durable writes or release.
