## Advisory inference

Everything in this section is prediction from the direct record above.

### What Krish would reject on sight

Krish is likely to reject any render that presents one of these patterns, regardless of polish:

1. **A beautiful static recommendation.** Candidate T rendered as one large sentence with ornamental evidence lines would repeat the exact Phase 5 failure: visually tasteful, but indistinguishable from a quickly composed slide because the evidence arrival, changed interpretation, operator choice and learning loop are not observable.
2. **A graph before a reason to care.** Candidate U rendered as a full Living Brain with an amber halo and many labelled nodes before the intervention is legible would look like the operator is being asked to explore a visualization. The approved R2 map is a powerful customer showpiece, but reusing it as the operator's first job would bury the new G14 hierarchy.
3. **A chatbot without bubbles.** Candidate V rendered as staged lines of dialogue, typing affordances and generated customer replies would still read as a generic AI assistant. Removing bubbles does not remove the chat-first product model.
4. **A fake customer prediction.** Any claim about what the customer will say or do next, unless clearly framed as a rehearsal hypothesis with evidence and uncertainty, is likely to trigger a trust rejection.
5. **A cockpit that looks administered.** Multiple equal panels for evidence, gaps, tasks, progress, notifications and enrichment would collapse the product into project management or memory operations.
6. **An impressive visual that forgets the plan.** The founder's recorded ruin condition is loss of earlier decisions and holistic product continuity. A G14 surface that omits personal becoming, governed evidence, staged versus durable meaning, correction or customer benefit would fail even if the isolated interaction is competent.

### What Krish is likely to find genuinely impressive

- **Candidate T:** the evidence-to-action loop, if it visibly changes. New transcript evidence weakens an interpretation, a better intervention becomes ready, Krish reshapes or rotates it, and the interface shows what the next session could teach the Brain. This is product intelligence, not presentation.
- **Candidate U:** the strongest immediate visual moment. One meaningful amber movement travelling through a truthful Living Brain into a mint-ready intervention could extend the exact instrument-like aliveness approved in G13 R2.
- **Candidate V:** a genuinely nonlinear private ask that absorbs one tiny context update and improves the prepared session card without pretending to know the customer. If this works, it would make the system feel like a useful participant rather than a database.

The likely winning combination is not all three experiences. It is Candidate T's intervention spine, one bounded U-style state change as evidence, and one optional V-style private ask after the intervention is understood.

### 1. Candidate T: A-

Candidate T best preserves the locked product hierarchy. It opens on one intervention, gives it a causal trace, shows useful-now material, names one deepening route, supports a bounded private ask and ends in a change receipt. It is the clearest answer to the known founder rejection that a surface can look good without making the product gaugeable.

It becomes an A if the render proves that the intervention is not simply model advice. The first state must expose why it is timely, what evidence changed, what remains uncertain, how Krish can reshape or reject it, and what customer capability or insight the intervention is meant to unlock.

### 2. Candidate U: B+

Candidate U is the strongest visual and emotional concept. It preserves the approved R2 protagonist, makes growth visible and could produce the most immediate sense of a living, ownable product. It also serves Krish's direct wish for a per-customer Brain dashboard.

It does not win because its submitted sequence makes Krish inspect a movement before receiving the intervention. That reverses the brief's governing hierarchy and risks turning the operator surface into a richer copy of the customer surface. It becomes an A only if the active movement and the supported intervention are one immediately readable object, not a map state that must be explored to find the action. At that point, however, Candidate T remains the clearer governing spine.

### 3. Candidate V: B

Candidate V best expresses participant-like warmth and the real nonlinear session context. Its accepted session card is a useful artifact, and its private redirection model fits the channel-agnostic live-assistance boundary.

It becomes an A- only if the rehearsal never presents a generated customer response as prediction, never defaults to an open conversation, and always resolves to a compact evidence-linked intervention card. Those conditions remove much of what currently distinguishes its governing interaction, which is why it should remain a bounded secondary capability rather than the synthesis spine.

## The product-thesis call

Candidate T best preserves Krish's stated taste and product ambition.

The decisive mechanism is not that it is the simplest candidate. It is that it can make one full compounding loop legible in a glance:

`new evidence -> changed interpretation -> supported intervention -> operator judgement -> customer interaction -> new evidence`

That loop joins the two sides of the product without turning either into administration. Krish gets a sharper next conversation. The customer gets an intervention designed to help them see, judge or act differently. The Brain becomes richer because consequential work happened, not because either person maintained a memory system.

Candidate U protects the product's visual ambition but weakens the operator hierarchy. Candidate V protects the participant ambition but weakens truth calibration. Candidate T can contain the best bounded expression of both without surrendering its spine.

## The otherwise competent synthesis failure

The most likely competent failure is a well-designed collage: Candidate T's intervention card over Candidate U's full map, with Candidate V's private chat drawer beside it. Every requirement would appear to be present, yet the product would lose its answer to the first question: what deserves Krish's attention now?

That synthesis would also repeat the historical mistake of showing system richness instead of demonstrating system intelligence. The map would become background spectacle, the rehearsal would become a generic escape hatch, and the intervention would become one card among several.

The corrective rule is strict: **one intervention owns the frame.** The Living Brain may explain why it changed. The private ask may adapt the intervention. Neither may compete with it as a simultaneous product mode.

## Non-negotiable constraints before showing Krish

Do not present the rendered synthesis until every condition below holds.

1. **The first frame names one supported customer intervention in plain language.** Krish must know what deserves attention without opening a map, drawer or explanation.
2. **The action and its verdict occupy the same place.** The primary control must prepare, reshape, rotate or reject the intervention, and the resulting change must appear beside that action.
3. **The causal trace is visible and fixture-resolvable.** It must show the new evidence, the interpretation that strengthened, weakened or was discarded, the intervention, and the exact uncertainty or learning target. No decorative line may imply causality.
4. **Model authority is visibly bounded.** The intervention is a supported proposal, not CTRL's answer. Krish can reshape it, rotate to a genuinely different route, reject the premise or inspect the evidence before using it.
5. **Useful now and deepen next are both present, but not equal heroes.** One customer-ready insight or contrast sits behind the intervention. One lowest-friction evidence request is the only deepening route shown by default. No completeness score appears.
6. **The customer remains the protagonist.** The surface must state or demonstrate what the customer could newly see, judge or do, and the customer preview must use the same governed Brain rather than a separate summary.
7. **The Living Brain is an inspection and consequence layer, not the opening job.** If used, it shows only the items and typed relationships needed to explain the intervention and one real change. Spatial position, orbital geometry and proximity cannot invent meaning.
8. **The private ask is bounded and context-first.** It opens only after the intervention is understood, carries the current evidence context, accepts one tiny update, labels generated paths as rehearsal or hypothesis, and cannot silently mutate durable Brain truth.
9. **Change is observable.** The proof must show one transcript arrival, one interpretation revised or weakened, one previous reading retained in history, one intervention changed and one inspectable receipt. A static finished state is not enough.
10. **Quiet means quiet.** The quiet state says that no interruption has earned attention. Sparse state offers one bounded useful observation plus one evidence request. Stale state blocks confident preparation. Loading and error states invent no claim.
11. **The visual system extends the approved R2 standard without copying its information architecture.** Use deep ink, warm cream, Newsreader for the payoff, Archivo for structure, IBM Plex Mono for evidence and controls, Source Serif 4 for sustained reading, mint only for supported ready state and amber only for what changed. Use purposeful line instruments and meaningful motion only. No gradients, glass cards, sparkles, mascots, generic chat chrome or ornamental graph activity.
12. **The screen remains radically restrained.** One primary action per state, no equal dashboard panels, no system-health hero, no memory administration, no nested scrolling and no guff. Every line of copy must perform an action, evidence, state or meaning job.
13. **The proof is usable at both required viewports.** The representative and fallback states must render at 390 by 844 and 1440 by 900 with visible focus, keyboard access, reduced motion, 44px practical touch targets, no horizontal overflow, no clipped primary action and no console error.
14. **The proof is honest about what exists.** Synthetic data is visibly identified. The interface cannot imply production ingestion, live listening, persistence, external sending or deployed model inference.
15. **The product name remains replaceable.** Mindy does not appear. CTRL may remain a quiet working label, but the interaction cannot depend on a named synthetic persona.
16. **The founder sees one cold synthesis.** Do not show the three concepts, the ranking, the rationale or a feature checklist before his first reaction. Present the actual rendered interaction at its opening state and let him use it.

## Final advisory

Build the first proof around Candidate T. Borrow only Candidate U's single meaningful change pulse and Candidate V's context-bound private adaptation. If the result cannot make the evidence-to-intervention-to-learning loop visible without a graph-first home or conversational shell, stop and expose that as the unresolved product fork. Do not solve it by adding panels.

The final decision remains Krish's after an unanchored reaction to the rendered artifact.
