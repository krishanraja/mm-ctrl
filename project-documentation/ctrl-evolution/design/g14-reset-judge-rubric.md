# G14 reset judge rubric

**Date:** 8 September 2026
**Decision:** Select the strongest new interaction spine for one rendered founder proof

## Hard constraints

A candidate fails if any of these are false:

- The primary desktop task completes inside 1440 by 900 without page scrolling.
- One consequential decision or intervention remains primary.
- Current meaning, exact evidence, uncertainty and next action form an inspectable chain.
- Superseded meaning remains history and cannot guide current action.
- Operator-private material cannot enter the customer projection.
- Private exploration cannot silently change durable Brain truth.
- The Living Brain remains meaningfully inspectable through typed, directed relationships.
- The surface does not become coursework, micro-learning, project management, an editorial page or a generic chat shell.
- The design is feasible from the existing synthetic fixture without invented production capability.
- The language is plain, non-boastful and free of em dashes.

## Weighted criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Test |
|---|---:|---|
| Consequential decision usefulness | 25 | Does the operator understand and improve the next consequential decision? |
| One-screen information architecture | 20 | Does every visible region earn its space and preserve a clear first read? |
| Living Brain integrity | 15 | Is the Brain an inspectable reasoning structure rather than ornament or a hidden settings page? |
| Evidence and uncertainty honesty | 15 | Can every confident claim be traced, challenged and limited? |
| Plain-language interaction | 10 | Can a twelve-year-old explain what to do and what happened? |
| Distinctiveness and emotional pull | 10 | Does it feel alive, personal and worthy of showing, without theatre? |
| Feasibility and state resilience | 5 | Can the fixture support representative and adverse states across desktop and mobile? |

## Required verdict

1. Pass or fail each hard constraint with evidence.
2. Score each candidate against the weighted criteria.
3. Test pairwise distance across sequencing, user agency, primary interaction, information structure and state model. Every pair must differ on at least two axes.
4. Name one winner and one strongest feature worth preserving from each losing candidate.
5. Answer explicitly for every candidate:
   - Is this disguised repetition of the rejected direction?
   - Did it regress any invariant constraint?
   - Which useful strength from rejected work was discarded and should be restored without copying the rejected solution?
6. State the material risk that could still make the winning render fail Krish's founder gate.

Do not average away a hard-constraint failure. Do not choose the candidate with the most features. Choose the interaction spine most likely to make the private Brain useful in one glance and defensible under scrutiny.
