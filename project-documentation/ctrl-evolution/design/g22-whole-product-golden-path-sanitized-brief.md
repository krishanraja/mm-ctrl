# G22 whole-product golden-path sanitized divergence brief

**Revision:** R1

**Date:** 11 September 2026

## Outcome

Design one coherent interactive proof that lets the founder understand and feel the complete CTRL product through one synthetic leader's consequential work. The proof must show compounding value, not explain a feature set.

## User and state of use

The primary user is an advisor operating CTRL for one leader during a thirty-day Mindmake proof. They may be on a laptop between customer sessions or checking a meaningful change on a phone. The leader may contribute through short, deliberately timed mobile moments but does not administer the system.

## What the product must demonstrate

- Natural work residue or deliberate input enters with source, subject, purpose and audience intact.
- CTRL combines that material with existing Brain context and bounded external research.
- It surfaces one consequential AI-related decision or tension that deserves attention now.
- It understands the leader specifically enough to identify a criterion, counter-case or unknown ordinary LLM context would miss.
- It asks only a route-changing question, and only when the answer matters.
- The leader owns the call, the first gate, the final standard and the release decision.
- New learning remains proposed until accepted or corrected.
- A correction repairs affected downstream understanding without erasing history.
- A later preparation or decision is visibly better because of what was learned earlier.
- The leader can inspect a truthful Living Brain and take a useful version away without understanding the technical storage.

## Fixed product rules

- The Brain is the organising intelligence. A feed, audio, decision, brief or export is an output of the same Brain.
- The product is initially operated by the advisor. Customer interactions are sparse, mobile-first and controlled by the advisor.
- The product helps willing leaders rebuild their category, organisation and own role around AI. It is not general business advice, AI persuasion, micro-learning or admin automation.
- Personal capability is the mechanism for company value.
- AI handles governed information movement, analysis, manipulation and enrichment. Humans retain vision, taste, judgement, relationships, accountability and consequential release authority.
- The Brain preserves sources, standing, evidence, uncertainty, audience, version, corrections and history.
- Personal and company-owned material remain distinct.
- A Living Brain visual is an honest projection over one governed model, not a second truth or decorative network.
- Portability is a customer-owned GitHub or ZIP release from the live governed Brain, not GitHub as the only runtime database.

## Interaction and comprehension constraints

- One changing work region inside one coherent product shell.
- Eight or fewer primary actions for the complete case.
- One immediate action or question per mobile view.
- Progressive disclosure preserves sophistication without filling the screen with explanations.
- A twelve-year-old should understand every visible sentence.
- No explainer tour, slide sequence, e-learning progress mechanic or celebratory completion scoreboard.
- No giant editorial title consuming useful desktop space.
- No repeated reassurance about privacy, control or staging. Show status once where it affects a choice.
- No vague labels, fake controls, decorative generated imagery, sparkle symbols or meaningless AI graphs.
- Use purposeful line iconography, quiet state-responsive motion and data-bearing visuals.
- The result of an action appears where the action occurs.
- Typing is optional. Taps, voice and existing context do most of the work.

## Data truth

Use a rich but wholly synthetic case for Maya Chen, founder and CEO of Aperture House. The central decision concerns how far to rebuild marketing around AI, with a synthetic £1.2m operating budget and eight affected roles. Existing approved synthetic evidence includes Maya's comparative judgement style, her standard of making quality clear without killing surprise, a delegation tension, a broken volume-based scorecard, customer feedback and an unresolved question about whether her quality standard can travel without final rescue.

Every visible claim must identify whether it comes from Maya, customer evidence, company work, public research or CTRL's provisional interpretation. Synthetic evidence can demonstrate mechanics but cannot prove efficacy.

## Required states

- representative complete path;
- quiet or no-earned-interruption state;
- sparse evidence;
- stale evidence;
- proposed learning;
- corrected learning;
- rejected learning;
- later return linked to accepted learning;
- narrow phone; and
- wide desktop.

## Existing approved organ

The R4 Decision Table is the approved rich operator instrument for the consequential decision. Treat it as one earned deeper state. Do not redesign it or make its full density the default home.

## Technical boundary

The first proof is deterministic, synthetic, unlinked and non-indexable. It may use local state and approved fixture data. It makes no database write, sends no message, calls no model, creates no account and changes no production navigation. Every visible control must work within those boundaries.

## Evaluation

Judge the concept on whole-product comprehension, felt personal intelligence, causal continuity, meaningful compounding, human agency, information hierarchy, mobile use, visual distinctiveness, data honesty, feasibility and resistance to generic AI-product patterns.

Explicitly reject a concept that is only a dashboard, feature catalogue, product tour, chat wrapper, graph explorer or renamed version of the Decision Table.
