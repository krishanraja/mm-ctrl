## Product truth

CTRL is a private intelligence system that learns through a leader's real consequential work, helps the leader make and execute better AI-transition calls, and turns governed evidence, judgement, corrections and outcomes into a richer portable Brain that improves the next piece of work.

The paid near-term user is Krish operating CTRL inside a Mindmake proof. The customer receives small, deliberate moments and may later gain a radically minimal self-service surface. The Brain is the organising intelligence. Decisions, preparation, briefing, category intelligence, audio and export are expressions of it.

## Non-goals

- no production route or navigation change;
- no customer data, account or external send;
- no database, schema, email or model integration;
- no redesign of the already approved Decision Table;
- no final customer self-service interface;
- no feature inventory, slide deck, explainer tour or product-marketing page;
- no graph that claims to be the Brain's literal architecture; and
- no claim of diagnostic efficacy from synthetic data.

## Surface dependencies

`natural work arrival -> current Brain understanding -> consequential issue -> approved decision instrument -> human-owned call and test -> proposed learning -> correction or acceptance -> later useful return -> inspectable Living Brain -> portable release`

Each state uses the same synthetic source, item, decision and learning identities. The later return must depend visibly on the earlier accepted or corrected learning.

## Vertical slice

One stateful synthetic case follows Maya Chen and Aperture House from new meeting and work residue through a high-consequence marketing operating-model decision, a leader-owned test, one governed Brain update, a later piece of prepared help and a portable Brain release.

The proof must make the whole product understandable through interaction. It is not allowed to explain the product instead of behaving like it.

## First material surface

One coherent whole-product shell with a single changing work region. It may reveal different states in sequence, but must feel like one product carrying one live thread rather than a collection of linked mock pages.

## Authority

Local synthetic design artifacts, fixture work, automated checks, Git commits and the already-authorised protected non-production preview are in scope. Production, merge, live data, accounts, permissions, email, customer delivery and public navigation remain closed.

## Pass signals

The rendered synthesis passes only if:

1. a bright twelve-year-old can say what CTRL is doing after the first minute;
2. the complete value loop is usable in eight or fewer primary actions;
3. the Decision Table appears as one earned deeper moment, not the product home;
4. a Brain change has an evidence source, standing, audience and explicit human acceptance or correction;
5. the later return names the exact earlier learning that made it better;
6. the Living Brain adds transparent meaning and personal becoming, not decorative theatre;
7. the portable release explains what the leader owns without requiring Git knowledge;
8. the operator view is rich without making the customer experience administratively heavy;
9. desktop and mobile preserve the same meaning without nested scrolling, horizontal overflow or tiny controls;
10. copy uses plain British English, no large pompous declarations, no repeated trust reassurance and no AI-writing tells;
11. loading, sparse, stale, correction and rejected-learning states have honest bounded treatments; and
12. no control looks active without a working consequence in the proof.

## Material alternatives

The rejected route is a sitemap, feature dashboard or narrated product tour. It would restore conceptual coverage quickly but would not prove continuity, compounding or product value. The selected route is a stateful end-to-end case because the user's loss of the product was caused by experiencing only disconnected middle states.
