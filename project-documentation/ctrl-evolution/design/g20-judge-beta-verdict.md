### Candidate 4: Context Thread, 90/100

The strongest evidence is in sections 3, 5 and 8. Ordinary paste creates an immediate private receipt, the handoff receipt names purpose, audience, freshness, expiry, at least three sources and an exclusion, and the return retains the four required attribution classes. The fixed identity band and mismatch closure are especially strong.

The deduction is interaction weight. The representative route moves through receipt, job choice, packing receipt, handoff, task starter and return. Even without numbers, the continuous line plus earlier and future marks can read as a progress stepper. On mobile, a vertical thread with expandable prior receipts risks making a quick exchange feel like a process to complete. The concept itself names this risk in section 14.

Grade: B+. It becomes an A if a render proves that the thread is a custody trace, not a wizard, and if the normal Claude handoff feels like one review plus one action.

### Candidate 8: Exchange Tray, 94/100

The strongest evidence is in sections 1 through 4. The fixed plaque outside the moving tray is the clearest identity invariant in the field. `Place, inspect, pass` is literal, nontechnical and tied to the boundary CTRL actually governs. The retained receipt makes what stayed in CTRL visible, while the tray manifest makes what crosses visible. Section 3 also places the result beside the initiating action and keeps automatic prompt submission out of the claim.

The score is not higher because the metaphor can become ceremony if motion, three resting-place labels or torn-paper detail are overdrawn. Two statements also need correction before synthesis: State 1A says staging has not completed while ambiguity is answered, and the failure table says an unknown return source must be named before staging. Both are weaker than the governing invariant that the original should first be preserved privately, then clarified.

### Candidate 2: Context Aperture, 86/100

The strongest evidence is in sections 3, 5 and 7. `Claude will see` versus `Stays in CTRL` is an excellent plain-language boundary. The proposed minimum cut is purpose-bound, inspectable and adjustable, and the return correctly withholds mint because a Claude result is staged rather than trusted.

The deduction is structural. The aperture puts a visual selection mechanism over the Living Brain and invites item-level `Include` and `Keep here` decisions. That is the candidate most likely to turn quick use into context administration. It is also the hardest idea to preserve truthfully at 320 by 568 without becoming a list editor. The capture route ends at `Done`, while the Claude route is a separate secondary entry, so the full circulation spine is less continuous than the other two.

Grade: B. It becomes an A only if the default cut requires no item management and the governing optical spine is replaced rather than cosmetically renamed.

## Rejected G14 repetition and invariant regression

**Disguised repetition:** Candidate 2 is the one material concern. Its purpose-shaped optical opening over a focused Living Brain route is close to the rejected Intervention Lens spine under a new noun. It repairs the rejected long-page layout and pompous copy, so it is not a literal reuse of the failed artifact. But the governing optical device and Brain-centered stage are insufficiently fresh for a blinded divergence winner. Candidate 4 and Candidate 8 do not repeat the rejected Intervention Lens structure.

**Invariant regressions to correct:**

- Candidate 8 must save the original into the narrowest private staging state before asking an ambiguity question. State 1A must not leave durable preservation uncertain.
- Candidate 8 must also preserve an unclear returned item privately as unclassified material before asking for missing provenance. Provenance uncertainty is a reason to narrow status, not a reason to risk losing the source.
- Candidate 2 must remove `Keep with active customer` from the wrong-customer failure. An identity collision should close, preserve the input locally and require an explicit return to the customer named by the capture or cut.
- Candidate 2's direct include/keep editing must remain an exception. If it becomes the normal route, it regresses the founder requirement for quick use into Brain administration.
- Candidate 4 must not render future custody marks as active destinations or numbered progression. That would regress the one-ask-per-state and non-course constraints.

## Strengths from G14 that synthesis should restore

All three candidates retain provenance and the operator-private boundary, but they under-specify three approved G14 strengths:

1. **Current versus superseded meaning.** The selected-source inspection should show when an older interpretation or corrected source remains historical and cannot guide the current Claude task.
2. **Correction history.** Exact source inspection should preserve the correction trail and current version, not merely show a source label and timestamp.
3. **Customer-safe projection boundary.** The private handoff should state that the customer-safe projection remains unchanged and is not part of this exchange. This belongs in the receipt or detail view, not as a competing primary panel.

The synthesis should also preserve the approved G14 no-scroll Decision Bench, current decision, current read, open question, focused evidence route, icon-only shell and literal object/action headings. No announcement-scale copy should be introduced.

## Feasibility and data concerns

1. The provided evidence establishes a generic read-only MCP substrate, not an implemented purpose-bound capsule resolver. Workspace enforcement, audience intersection, expiry, revocation, version watermarking and audit status must be simulated explicitly in the synthetic proof and must not be described as production capability.
2. The supplied evidence does not establish that the current Maya fixture contains three versioned source references, a typed operator-private exclusion, correction status, all four provenance roles, or exact freshness and expiry fields. The proof must add explicit synthetic fixture fields or fail visibly. It must not infer them from prose.
3. Opening Claude and pre-filling or sending a prompt is not a reliable supported path. The working proof path should be `Copy task starter`; `Open Claude` may appear only if its exact route is verified, and it still must not claim submission.
4. Clipboard access, file input and microphone capture vary by browser and secure context. Every active control needs an observed success and failure state. Voice should be absent or visibly unavailable unless the proof records real audio.
5. Duplicate detection, delayed processing and return linking can be deterministic fixture branches. They must be labelled proof behavior, not evidence of deployed ingestion or provenance services.
6. The no-scroll 1440 by 900 composition is feasible, but only if the tray shows the current state rather than all three resting places at equal visual weight. Mobile may use natural page movement and should use written state changes instead of relying on tray motion.

## Final call

Take Candidate 8 forward as the sole synthesis spine. Keep the tray literal and quiet. Correct capture-first preservation and unclassified-return preservation before rendering. Borrow the strongest boundary wording from Candidate 2 and the compact custody receipt discipline from Candidate 4, but do not borrow either candidate's governing metaphor.

The sharper alternative is Candidate 4 if the tray proves visually theatrical in the first static wireframe. The next gate is one synthetic rendered proof of the corrected Exchange Tray, with the hard-failure table rerun against observed behavior at 1440 by 900, 390 by 844 and 320 by 568. The main counterpoint is that a skeuomorphic tray can become charming ceremony. Kill the direction if a cold viewer notices the animation before they can say whose Brain is active, what Claude can see, what stayed out and whether the return became memory.
