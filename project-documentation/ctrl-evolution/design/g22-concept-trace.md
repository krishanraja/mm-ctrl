## Independence result

The three generators worked from the sanitized brief in separate contexts. Pairwise comparison found material difference on at least five axes for every pair. No concept was rejected as disguised repetition before judging.

## Primary judging result

Both independent judges selected **The Consequence File** despite receiving different anonymous candidate orders.

- Judge Alpha received temporal as Candidate A and selected Candidate A.
- Judge Beta received temporal as Candidate B and selected Candidate B.

Both judges also ruled that no concept was fit to implement unchanged. No tiebreaker was required because the winner and hard constraints aligned.

## Shared reasons for selection

- strongest whole-product comprehension;
- clearest causal continuity from work arrival through later use;
- strongest append-only correction and dependency-repair model;
- best integration of the approved Decision Table as an earned state;
- portability feels like ownership of the same Brain rather than a bolted-on export;
- lower risk of becoming a generic chat, feed, graph or dashboard.

## Shared required surgery

- remove the visible five-stage sequence;
- keep history subordinate and never progress-like;
- replace the abstract route question with a concrete business outcome;
- use one restrained intervention rather than a queue;
- show what the answer changes immediately;
- make Brain inspection clearly expose current use, ownership, standing and later dependency;
- show the later changed preparation before release;
- keep personal and company material visibly separate;
- preserve the approved R4 Decision Table unchanged.

## Rejected governing spines

### The Standing Field

Rejected as the governing shell because its four positions, five object forms, relationship grammar and several visual systems create a new product ontology. Its ownership seam and exact carry trace remain useful components.

### The Margin

Rejected as the governing shell because it starts too late in the product loop, risks repeating the failed permanent side-panel composition and leaves arrival, Brain ownership and portability as surrounding utilities. Its restraint, concrete question and quiet-state discipline remain useful components.

## Implementation authority

The synthesis is authorised for one local, responsive, deterministic mock and a protected branch preview only. It does not authorise production navigation, database changes, live integrations, customer data, account creation, messages, production deployment or merge.
