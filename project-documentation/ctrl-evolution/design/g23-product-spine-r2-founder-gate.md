## What is now locked

CTRL is a private AI Brain developed with one leader through a Mindmake thirty-day relationship. The Brain prepares, Krish challenges and the leader decides. The company pays because the leader becomes more capable of producing useful organisational and commercial change without surrendering purpose, judgement or quality. Accepted judgement may carefully improve a later, materially different decision when its evidence, scope, permission and correction history travel with it.

This approval locks only:

- the thirty-day relationship as the product spine;
- the distinct authority of the Brain, Krish and the leader;
- leader-owned consequential decisions;
- company-funded value through a more capable leader; and
- careful, permissioned reuse of judgement across different decisions.

It does **not** approve the synthetic example, final copy, interface, visual system, implementation, data action, deployment, merge or release. Those remain open to further design, challenge and verification. The approved artifact is a product-alignment source, not a production screen or final GTM asset.

## Mandatory GTM retrieval contract

Before product naming, positioning, pricing, launch narrative, landing-page copy, sales materials, objection handling or competitive comparison begins, load this gate and the exact tagged artifact. Treat them as the canonical product-spine source.

The GTM stage must preserve:

- the leader transformation, not merely a list of AI features;
- the company-value and personal-ownership tension resolved through a more capable leader;
- the Brain–Krish–leader division of authority;
- the thirty-day relationship and work on real consequential decisions;
- cross-decision compounding with evidence, permission, correction and traceability;
- the honest distinction from a general LLM, enterprise assistant or static folder of files; and
- the promise that AI expands human agency, taste, judgement, accountability and strategic ambition rather than replacing them.

GTM may simplify or re-express the story, but it must not silently reduce CTRL to a dashboard, chatbot, content feed, knowledge base, generic company brain, e-learning product or administrative automation tool. The artifact is not final marketing copy; it is the product truth that later marketing must make compelling.

## Revisit rule

There is no arbitrary review date. Reopen this decision only if observable evidence shows that one of the following is materially false or the commercial offer itself changes:

- leaders do not gain meaningful capability through the thirty-day relationship;
- customers do not value the Brain–Krish–leader division of authority;
- leader-owned judgement cannot safely improve materially different later decisions; or
- the funded value cannot connect personal capability to useful organisational or commercial change.

A visual preference, a new feature idea or a strong single-session reaction is not enough to reopen the product spine.
