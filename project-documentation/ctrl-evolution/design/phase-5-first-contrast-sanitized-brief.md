## State of use

Krish has selected one customer while preparing for, sitting inside or returning from a consequential conversation. He may be holding a phone in one hand and cannot divide his attention between the customer and a complex interface. Later, the customer should be able to open the same product and complete bounded useful actions without Krish present.

The first reviewed viewport is `390 × 844` CSS pixels. The surface must remain credible at `360 × 800`, at 200% browser zoom and with a virtual keyboard present. Desktop is a later material surface.

## User and action

The user is a decision-owning leader who already intends to build for an AI-shaped future. They need to see one materially useful contrast about a live decision, understand which parts come from their words and available evidence, and choose what happens next without surrendering the call.

The reviewed moment begins after CTRL has captured the leader's provisional view and change condition. It ends when the leader chooses one obvious next move or safely leaves. It does not include the final call, Brain proposal or GitHub release screen.

## Governing product rule

The live Brain is a governed versioned runtime. GitHub or ZIP is the customer-owned portable release. The first surface proves the relationship through one five-minute human-first loop. CTRL reveals one useful contrast before any recommendation and eventually proposes no more than one optional inspectable Brain learning.

The visible experience is one thoughtful companion. It must never imply sentience, certainty or authority it does not have.

## Primary test state

Use only this sanitized decision evidence in the concept. Do not invent facts, people, percentages, forecasts or outcomes.

- **Decision:** Should the marketing function be reoriented around AI, including redesigning work and the capabilities the division needs?
- **Leader's current view:** Probably yes. The current team is hesitant to change, while too much time is spent on low-value manual production.
- **What would change the route:** Evidence that the present team can adopt the new operating standard quickly under a committed strategy and changed incentives.
- **Available evidence:** One recent social-post brainstorming meeting lasted about one hour. The organisation had not committed a clear next-state marketing strategy beyond encouraging more tool use. Incentives and success measures had not yet been redesigned.
- **Eligible private Brain context:** AI fluency is evidenced by noticing and redesigning work, improving handoffs, validating output and preserving judgement, not by tool-use frequency.

The interface may derive one contrast from this bounded input. It must distinguish what the leader said, what the evidence supports, what CTRL infers and what remains unknown.

## Invariant interaction requirements

- The leader's own decision and current view remain visible or immediately recoverable.
- The first reveal is a contrast capable of changing the frame, not an answer or employment recommendation.
- There is one obvious next action and one quiet safe escape.
- Voice is immediately available, with tap and text alternatives.
- One ask at a time. No multi-question form, chat transcript wall or report to scroll through.
- Input is locally acknowledged, preserved and resumable before remote work finishes.
- The user can accept, resist, correct or say the contrast is irrelevant.
- No named-person assessment, replacement recommendation or generic instruction to use more AI.
- No Brain maintenance, privacy configuration, repository, commit, branch, schema, model, prompt, retrieval or confidence-percentage language.
- No feed, dashboard, graph, progress ring, streak, notification bait or engagement mechanic.
- No recommendation can appear before the leader's prior is captured.

## Data truth and honest state range

The primary mock uses the bounded test state above and may show a concise source distinction. The concept spine must also explain how the same interaction model handles:

- no usable evidence, where CTRL asks one narrow question or abstains;
- slow analysis, where input is safe and work is cancellable;
- stale or conflicting Brain context;
- off-record capture, where nothing persists;
- an interrupted return at the last committed step;
- a correction that changes the contrast;
- zero Brain proposal after the decision because nothing reusable was learned.

No state may become confident merely to preserve visual drama.

## Brand and experience constraints

- Dark, warm, physical, patient and alive. An instrument that helps a human decide, never an oracle.
- Deep ink-green ground, warm cream and one semantic accent system. Mint means the answer. Amber means what moved or remains unsettled.
- Archivo carries structure. Newsreader is reserved for the claim. IBM Plex Mono carries labels, source states and time. Source Serif 4 carries running text.
- Every viewport has quiet ambient life, while content remains fully present without animation. Touch responds within the same perceived beat. Reduced motion preserves meaning and control.
- No gradient mesh, glass cards, robot, mascot, brain illustration, boardroom, hologram or person smiling at a laptop.
- No decorative eyebrow above a heading.
- No command tone, doom, boasting, cryptic headline, AI cliché, em dash or technical vocabulary.
- The result must exceed the current product's polish while feeling calmer, simpler and less administered.

## Accessibility and mobile reliability

- Semantic controls, visible focus, keyboard operation and screen-reader labels.
- Touch targets meet the current web target-size requirement or an applicable spacing exception, with the house preference for comfortably larger primary controls.
- Text and controls retain sufficient contrast in every ground and state.
- Reflow, browser zoom, safe areas, dynamic browser chrome and virtual keyboard cannot hide the primary action or erase input.
- Motion has a complete reduced-motion path and never carries unique meaning.
- Voice always has a non-voice alternative.

Observed validation remains required. A static concept cannot certify usability or accessibility.

## Technical feasibility boundary

The current React 18, Vite and TypeScript application already contains responsive shells, voice capture, local draft preservation, sheets, loaders, recovery states, evidence primitives and accessible controls. A concept may recompose those properties but must not preserve the existing navigation or decision layout by default.

The first mock may be a self-contained HTML artifact with synthetic data. It must be implementable as bounded structured data rendered by owned components. AI may supply validated text and state choices, never arbitrary markup or layout.

## Diversity assignments

Each independent generator receives this entire brief plus one bounded territory:

- **Territory A:** direct-manipulation instrument. The leader discovers the contrast through one reversible touch gesture. No card stack, carousel or chat sequence.
- **Territory B:** temporal editorial object. The contrast arrives through a deliberately paced before-and-after sequence. No slider, dial or spatial plotting.
- **Territory C:** spatial evidence field. The leader acts on the relationship between claims and evidence. No chat transcript, swipe deck, slider or linear briefing page.

These territories are scaffolds for conceptual distance, not visual directions. Each candidate must still satisfy the full brief.

## Judge rubric

Two fresh-context judges receive anonymized candidates in independently randomized orders, this brief and the complete relevant product history. Each judge must report:

- hard-constraint pass or failure;
- conceptual-distance pass or failure across sequencing, user agency, primary interaction, information structure and state model;
- first useful contrast clarity;
- human agency and warmth;
- cognitive load in the stated use moment;
- data and uncertainty honesty;
- state-range coherence;
- implementation feasibility;
- distinctiveness without novelty theatre;
- strongest candidate, strongest discarded feature and material dissent.

Material disagreement on winner, hard constraints or diversity requires a third blinded tiebreaker. Krish sees only the rendered synthesis unless the remaining fork is genuinely his product or taste decision.
