## Winning spine and required correction

The singular governing spine is:

> A dated receipt appears only when evidence materially changes what the Brain can honestly say about one named judgment territory. It shows before, now, why, the limit, and one next contrast.

For the current canonical fixture, the opening receipt must say no more than:

> **Delegation looks different now.**
>
> The earlier claim that you fundamentally resist delegation weakened after your correction. The current reading is that your evaluation standard is not yet transferable.
>
> **Revising. This has not yet survived a held-out operating decision.**

The receipt lineage should resolve only to the canonical source, correction, version, and territory references. It must not invent delegation choices, delegation holdouts, or a handcrafted-context comparison. Creative direction may separately use the canonical held-out evidence and its stated honest limit, but it should not be pulled into the first receipt as a second governing story.

## Strongest reason the winner could still fail

The receipt could become a tasteful activity feed and a prose version of a progress score. If users expect a receipt after every upload, or return to collect signs of improvement rather than to act on a changed interpretation, the concept has rewarded activity without showing a number. The kill test is whether a cold user can name the changed interpretation, its evidence, its limit, and the next real contrast after 30 seconds, while also understanding that no overall part of Maya or her Brain became more complete.
