## Approved direction

R4 is approved as the governing G20 operator interaction direction:

- a consequential decision owns the frame;
- the Brain surfaces a specific interpretation, counter-case and uncertainty;
- materially different routes can be compared without hiding the current provisional recommendation;
- deeper inquiry appears only when invited;
- one concrete tap-first question appears at a time;
- optional notes preserve nuance without making typing compulsory;
- evidence requests, leader answers and prior-decision warnings retain distinct standing; and
- Claude handoff must carry a complete human-readable working brief rather than an opaque reference.

This is a product and interaction lock. It is not a production release, diagnostic-efficacy claim or approval of every piece of final copy.

## Customer-surface boundary

Any interface the customer sees must be radically smaller than the operator instrument. It should feel alive and engaging without exposing the machinery required to produce the insight.

The future customer surface must:

1. be devastatingly simple and immediately legible;
2. work comfortably on a phone while the customer is on the go;
3. show where things stand overall at a glance;
4. surface at most the most important outstanding quick question or action in the immediate frame;
5. keep engagement alive through useful continuity rather than dashboards, lessons or administrative work;
6. preserve deeper context behind progressive disclosure rather than removing sophistication;
7. let Krish control what the customer is prompted about and when;
8. support governed sending and receiving through Krish's `themindmaker.ai` email identity when that integration is designed; and
9. remain undesigned until Krish has completed a thorough, concrete interview about this customer participation model.

Notification control means product-level editorial and relationship control, not merely a mute switch. The later design must establish who chooses the prompt, what evidence earns an interruption, which channel is appropriate, when delivery is allowed, what the customer can reply with, how replies enter the Brain, and how Krish can preview, suppress, reschedule or intervene.

The email requirement is an architecture and workflow requirement only. It does not authorise mailbox connection, OAuth consent, sending, receiving, impersonation, domain changes or production automation. Those require a separate reviewed contract and exact action-time approval.

## Deliberately deferred interview

Before designing the customer surface, interview Krish thoroughly using concrete rendered or scenario-based choices. The interview must cover at least:

- the customer's five-second home state;
- what overall progress or standing can be shown honestly;
- which question earns the right to interrupt;
- cadence, quiet hours, urgency and channel;
- Krish-authored, AI-proposed and fully automated messages;
- preview, approval, override and audit controls;
- email sender identity, reply routing and relationship tone;
- consent, audience, third-party information and off-record handling;
- customer correction, disagreement and uncertainty;
- failure, stale evidence and no-question states;
- what keeps engagement alive without creating homework; and
- the boundary between a useful customer moment and the full operator instrument.

Do not substitute an agent-authored answer for this interview or quietly design the surface from assumptions.
