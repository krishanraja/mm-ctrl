# G23 product spine reset preflight

**Date:** 11 September 2026
**Status:** product-comprehension reset; visual surface work paused

## Outcome

Give Krish one review object that restores the whole CTRL product at its intended emotional and commercial altitude. After one minute, he must be able to explain who it is for, what happens across a Mindmake thirty-day proof, what the Brain does, why the leader becomes more capable and what is being approved.

## Why this route

G22 proved one causal loop but failed the founder gate because it made one synthetic case carry the burden of explaining the whole product. Another case screen would repeat the error. A feature inventory or architecture deck would explain the machinery without recovering the lived proposition.

The selected route is a founder-readable product spine: one concise, visual account of the complete thirty-day relationship, with customer, Krish and invisible Brain behaviour kept distinct. It is a product-direction gate, not a UI proposal.

## Chain

`krish-principles -> mindmake context -> strategy-brief -> build-apps-with-krish -> krish-design -> krish-voice -> founder product gate`

Implementation, database work and production release remain paused. Verification of the eventual artifact will be independent of its producer.

## Product truth

CTRL is a private, leader-owned AI Brain that learns how one person sees, decides and judges through their real consequential work. Inside Mindmake's thirty-day proof, it helps Krish and the leader make better calls about how AI should change the leader's category, organisation, people and own role. It becomes more useful through evidence, decisions, corrections and outcomes while the human retains vision, judgement, polish and accountability.

Near term, Krish operates the rich private machinery. The customer contributes and benefits through brief, well-timed mobile moments. The company pays because the leader becomes more capable of producing valuable organisational and commercial change. Personal becoming is the mechanism, not a detached wellness claim.

## Source layers

- **Durable doctrine:** human agency; evidence before claims; radical simplicity; one ask at a time; personal and company ownership remain distinct; accepted or corrected learning may compound; stale, proposed or rejected material may not silently steer future work.
- **Current product requirements:** CTRL product context, the thirty-day Mindmake proof, G13 Living Brain direction, G14 private operator model, G20 Decision Table, hybrid governed runtime plus portable release and the G22 failure record.
- **Implementation evidence:** existing capture, auth, evidence, memory, decision, export, briefing and trust primitives; new Brain substrate; synthetic range lab; correction and later-use proof.
- **History:** approved organs and rejected screens are evidence. They do not dictate the product spine.
- **External evidence:** none is required for this bounded alignment artifact. No market or efficacy claim may be invented.

## Approval boundary

The only founder question is:

> Is this the product we are building?

Approval locks the product spine only. It does not approve a screen, information architecture, implementation, deployment, live data or release.
