# G14 Private Brain Builder R2 revision brief

**Date:** 8 September 2026
**State:** Authorised same-spine revision
**Source of truth:** `g14-private-brain-builder-fixture.json`

## Outcome

Krish should be able to open one customer and understand the next useful intervention without being spoken down to, distracted by theatrical copy or forced through an uneven page. The experience should feel calm, exact and privately useful before a consequential customer session.

## State of use

Desktop before a Mindmake session, or mobile immediately after useful evidence arrives. Krish has seconds to understand the move and minutes to prepare it.

## Governing rule

The intervention remains the opening object. R2 changes voice and page rhythm, not the canonical evidence, meaning, audience, authority or interaction sequence.

## Copy contract

- Plain enough for a twelve-year-old.
- No sexual ambiguity, coy phrasing or audience mind-reading.
- No self-awarding system language such as `earned`, `deserves`, `powerful`, `important` or `intelligent`.
- No generated `Not X, Y` aphorism where a direct sentence works.
- No line may exist only to sound authoritative.
- Technical provenance remains available but does not dominate the explanation.
- Historical fixture assertions remain exact inside evidence detail. Surface summaries may restate them more plainly without widening the claim.

## Opening

The first screen should say, in substance:

- **Task:** find the quality signal Maya sees before her team does.
- **Move:** bring two launch directions that fail in different ways and ask which is closer and what still blocks it.
- **State:** ready for tomorrow's session, based on three sources, with one open question.

The interface does not declare that the move has earned anything.

## Page rhythm

- Reduce desktop side padding and hero height so content begins sooner.
- Keep the hero and causal instrument balanced rather than allowing the headline to overwhelm it.
- Use one consistent maximum content width across screens.
- Reduce the space before change comparisons and the Living Brain.
- Let dense evidence areas breathe internally instead of surrounding them with large empty bands.
- Keep mobile changes content-led. Do not shrink tap targets, type or evidence labels to force density.

## Protected strengths

- One intervention owns the first frame.
- The causal trace connects new evidence, old interpretation, current interpretation and future test.
- The weaker interpretation remains visible in history.
- The private ask cannot change durable truth.
- The Living Brain remains optional proof.
- Customer and operator-private views remain separate.
- Sparse, quiet, loading, stale, error and rejected states remain honest.

## Proof

- Complete browser flow at 390 by 844 and 1440 by 900.
- No opening horizontal overflow.
- Visible keyboard focus and mobile controls at least 42 pixels.
- Reduced-motion equivalence.
- Exact fixture evidence and audience boundaries.
- No console or page errors.
- Programmatic rejection of the two founder-cited lines and self-awarding copy.
- High-resolution inspection of opening, change and Living Brain on mobile and desktop.
- Cold founder reaction before explanation.

## Authority

Local proof revision, validation, screenshots, Git commit and branch push only. No production account, schema, inference, persistence, deployment or release.
