## The call

Candidate Y wins by a narrow but material margin. It makes the Brain's value understandable through a consequential owned decision, gives correction real temporal meaning, and preserves history while changing current and future use. Its wake is also the strongest emotional and interaction idea in the round. Candidate Z is more mechanically controlled and has the cleanest portrait-to-map identity device, but its fixed inspection stage risks reducing the Living Brain to a narrow standards inspector. Candidate Y better satisfies the whole brief if synthesis keeps its causality boundary explicit and treats the wake as the opening state of the My calls portrait, not as a third canonical product.

## Hard-failure screen

### Candidate X: disqualified

Candidate X breaks the prohibition on unsupported semantic relationships.

Evidence: in section 2, at 3 to 8 seconds, it says the standard is "connected to the current decision with the verb 'supports'." LB01 does not contain that edge. `relation-standard-aim-v1` connects `item:item-standard@v1` to `item:item-aim@v1`. The decision is a separate entity and has no semantic edge from the standard in the fixture.

The concept later refers correctly to the standard's relationship with the marketing aim, but that does not cure the first-frame overclaim. The submitted concept visually asserts a relationship the contract does not support. This is a hard failure, so Candidate X cannot proceed in this round.

### Candidate Y: eligible

No hard failure is established. Its only semantic relationship in the wake is the fixture-backed standard-to-aim `supports` relationship. It explicitly labels the other beats as "recorded with this decision" and says this is not a causal edge. Proposed and disputed states stay outside accepted truth, correction consequences are visible, non-voice and non-drag paths remain available, and the authority boundary is preserved.

### Candidate Z: eligible

No hard failure is established. Its Connections lens uses the fixture-backed `supports` and `qualifies` relationships, while portrait placement is explicitly non-semantic. Proposed material cannot occupy the accepted focal point, correction consequences are previewed and receipted, and interaction does not depend on drag, mouse or voice.

## Disguised repetition of round 1

- **Candidate X is not a disguised Fold.** It starts inside a freely navigable spatial field, uses focus hopping as the main action, and treats portrait as an alternate compression mode. Its failure is contract truth, not repetition.
- **Candidate Y is not a disguised Fold.** The mandated jobs still occur in a familiar order, but the governing sequence is decision at Now, evidence-bounded wake, correction that splits history from future use, then entry into the wider map. Agency, information structure and state meaning are temporal rather than an unfold around a selected portrait item.
- **Candidate Z has the strongest surface resemblance to round 1, but is not a hard-failure repeat.** Its first 30 seconds still move from a selected statement to connection, source and correction. That familiar value order costs it distinctiveness. However, direct lens switching around a stationary object changes the primary interaction, user agency and information structure enough to clear the diversity bar. It does not reuse the fold, atlas, open-and-trace interaction or folded-to-map state model.

The shared presence of evidence inspection and correction is not itself repetition. Those are required user jobs. Repetition is judged on the governing spine, not on unavoidable task steps.

## Constraint regression

- **Candidate X:** blocking regression. It turns a fixture-backed standard-to-aim relationship into a standard-to-decision edge in the opening frame.
- **Candidate Y:** non-blocking but material regression risk. The phrase "recorded cause or consequence" and the temporal choreography can outrun the evidence even though the detailed copy later limits the claim. Synthesis must use neutral language such as "recorded with this decision" for co-present beats and reserve causal verbs for exact typed relationships. The wake must be framed as the opening My calls portrait state so the product still has one portrait and one Living Map, not three modes.
- **Candidate Z:** non-blocking regression. The concept is semantically disciplined, but it underdelivers the requirement that the Living Map feel immersive and alive. Calling other items "nearest human meanings" also invites a proximity reading even while the copy denies it. Use "other current meanings in this view" if any of its microcopy survives.

5. **Parity failure restraint:** if portrait and map canonical references do not match, preserve the last verified state and refuse to present a mixed-current Brain.

## Strongest residual risk

The blocking residual risk is **false causality**. A polished temporal sequence can make recorded co-presence feel like a complete explanation of why the user made the decision. The first cold proof must test this directly. Users should be able to distinguish the one typed, evidenced relationship from items merely recorded with the decision. If they infer that CTRL knows the causal story of the decision, reject the spine rather than adding more explanatory copy.

## Synthesis instruction

Proceed with Candidate Y only. Preserve its temporal spine and resolve its two material conditions before visual refinement:

1. make the wake explicitly the opening state of the **My calls** portrait, with the same selected decision entering the Living Map; and
2. allow semantic verbs only where a current typed relationship exists, while every other temporal beat is labelled as recorded context.

The acceptance signal is a self-contained mobile proof in which a cold user can identify the owned call, name the one supported relationship, inspect its source, narrow the standard, distinguish preserved history from changed current use, and identify both review-required artifacts without inferring an unsupported causal chain.
