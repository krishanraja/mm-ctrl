## Hard-constraint findings

### Candidate Q2: The Counsel

**Compliance:** mostly holds, with one evidence-link gap and one data-truth regression.

The opening is the closest of the three to the required prepared recognition. Section 1 names a personal pattern, a protected anti-formula standard, a corrected interpretation and the unproved transfer of Maya's quality standard. The counsel sheet then separates evidence, uncertainty, counter-case and Maya's owned judgement. The complete Claude brief and exact returned-work annotations satisfy the two corrected bridge jobs in the founder gate.

The opening does not say that its three recognitions are visibly linked to human-readable evidence until the next frame. The reset requirement says the opening itself must contain evidence-linked recognitions. More seriously, `Maya's provisional view in her own words` is not supported by the fixture. The fixture provides a provisional view, but does not identify it as a direct quote or source-backed verbatim statement. That label must become `Maya's current view` unless direct provenance is supplied.

The three actions under `Make one call` also need a single governing question and one visually primary route. If rendered as equal calls, they would regress the one-obvious-next-action rule.

**Disguised repetition of the rejected experience:** No. Transport, receipts and custody are subordinate. The material risk is different: it could recreate the same rejected verbal excess through an editorial counsel page.

**Invariant regression:** Yes. It overstates the provenance of Maya's provisional view. The opening evidence links are also deferred rather than visibly present.

**Useful rejected strength to restore:** Restore one concise, truthful transport statement only at `Work this through in Claude`, and one return-state line stating that the pasted proposal is staged, not trusted. Do not restore the tray, repeated custody language or connector explanation.

Grade: B. It becomes an A if every recognition is source-linked at first paint, the unsupported `own words` claim is removed and the counsel compresses into a working instrument rather than a long argument.

**Invariant regression:** Yes, but bounded. The required three-part recognition opening is incomplete. The mobile route selector could also regress comparison into hidden navigation if it shows one route without preserving the actual fork.

Grade: B+. It becomes an A when the personal pattern, live tension and important unknown all sit visibly on the opening table and each changes how at least one route is understood.

## Mandatory synthesis corrections for R8

1. **Fix the opening without adding a prelude.** The first table state must visibly contain three source-linked recognitions: Maya becomes precise through concrete comparison; she wants judgement to travel without formula; and it remains unknown whether another leader can apply the standard to unfamiliar work without Maya rescuing it. The live tension about quality-system transfer versus incentive design must also remain visible.
2. **Make personal becoming causal.** State the frontier plainly: Maya is moving from final quality filter to designer of how quality is noticed earlier. Show exactly how that changes the evaluation of each route. If the portrait is merely attached to the table, the concept fails.
3. **Keep the routes causally different.** Rebuild now, prove the system first and add tools gradually must differ in work design, evidence and accountability, not only pace. Offer `Another route` as a quiet escape so the three synthetic options do not imply completeness.
4. **Replace `evidence weight` with inspectable meaning.** Use named supporting and opposing sources, plus supported, provisional, corrected, stale or unknown standing. Do not invent scores, percentages or visual mass that implies quantitative certainty.
5. **Preserve one obvious action.** The current working route may be selected, but the primary progression is `Design the test`. Counter-case, source inspection and another route are secondary actions.
6. **Make the Claude proof standalone.** Copy the full fixture brief, including exact decision, desired output, provisional view, supported synthesis, counter-case, standards, anti-standards, important evidence, forbidden assumptions, output shape and source-versus-reasoning instruction. No opaque reference may appear. The preview must show enough of the payload for the user to understand what will be copied.
7. **Use T5's return precision without adopting its spine.** Attach the five fixture-backed findings to the returned plan fields, preserve the one additive staged-rollout point and offer one improved instruction. Maya decides whether to keep, repair or reject the proposal. Nothing becomes memory automatically.
8. **Explain privacy once.** At the copy action say that a complete private brief is copied and nothing is sent automatically. At return say that the Claude plan is proposed material, not Brain truth. Do not add receipts, custody rails or repeated status prose.
9. **Protect mobile comparison.** At 390 by 844 and 320 by 568, show the current insight, the decision and `Design the test` before supporting detail. All three route names and the current selection must remain visible without drag, but no lower panel may exist merely to balance desktop.
10. **Demonstrate the honest range outside the hero.** The harness must cover sparse, contradictory, corrected, stale, slow, wrong-customer, offline-Claude, long-name and reduced-motion states. Sparse data yields the fixture's exact known pattern, unknown and next contrast, not a thinner version of the rich portrait.

## Allowed borrowing and forbidden hybridization

R8 may borrow Q2's immediate personal recognition and T5's exact-word return annotations. It may not borrow Q2's vertical counsel sequence or T5's artifact-first opening.

The synthesis must remain one Decision Table spine:

- no tray, pass-through animation, receipt edge or custody stepper;
- no editorial prelude before the actual fork;
- no separate personal-profile page;
- no annotation tour before the decision is understood;
- no card dashboard, score, percentage, opaque reference or decorative image;
- no mobile carousel that makes Maya remember the other routes;
- no automatic memory learning from a clean Claude answer.

## Material dissent

The strongest dissent against R8 is that it can become polished strategy software with a personal observation pinned to it. Q2 is better at making Maya feel recognised immediately. T5 is better at proving that the Brain can preserve and sharpen her taste in real work. Those are not minor virtues. The synthesis must import both as evidence-bearing mechanics.

The counterpoint is that importing too much would destroy R8's advantage. If the opening becomes a counsel page and the return becomes an annotation journey, the result will be another page collection. Recognition should alter the route table at first paint. Return annotations should attach to the selected route and its test. Nothing else should become a new mode.

## Final decision

Take R8 forward as the governing spine. Do not render the candidate unchanged. First produce a corrected synthesis document that satisfies the ten mandatory changes above, then freeze its data bindings against the Maya fixture. The next proof passes only if a cold viewer can state, within five seconds, what the Brain has learned about Maya, what large decision she faces, what tension and unknown matter, and what she should do next.

The sharper alternative is T5 if the Decision Table cannot make Maya's personal development causally visible without adding a profile layer. Kill R8 if the first static frame looks like three strategy cards, if the personal pattern can be removed without changing the route recommendation, or if mobile hides the comparison Maya needs to make.
