## Decision

The experience is a living receipt, not a score.

A receipt appears only when evidence materially changes what the Brain can honestly say about one named judgement territory. It shows the earlier reading, the current reading, what caused the change, the limit, and one proposed next contrast. A later receipt may narrow, replace or reopen an earlier interpretation without erasing it.

Both independent judges selected the receipt spine at 93 out of 100. Candidate A's spatial plane is visually distinctive but risks turning focus into personal competence. Candidate B makes unfamiliar-work evidence unusually clear but risks becoming a flattering quiz result. Neither metaphor becomes a second product area.

The exact-evidence appendix retains the contract's causal order: frozen Brain response, later owner record, then claim boundary. This is evidence hygiene already required by the selected concept, not a second interaction metaphor.

## Governing interaction

One receipt owns the page. The interaction is read, inspect and shape the next test.

The user may:

- inspect the earlier and current interpretations;
- inspect exact provenance and audience;
- mark the current reading too broad or wrong;
- reopen the earlier reading as unresolved;
- stage one proposed contrast;
- leave without contributing anything.

The user never manages files, takes a test, reviews a score or completes a Brain category.

## Visual structure

The proof preserves the existing ink, warm cream, mint and amber language, but it behaves like an editorial instrument rather than a dashboard.

- The current change is the only first-frame hero.
- A fine vertical lineage carries Before, Now, Why and Next.
- Amber marks changed or weakened meaning.
- Mint marks only evidence-backed standing, never success or personal quality.
- Purposeful line icons distinguish interpretation, evidence, correction and proposed test.
- The receipt grows down the page after deliberate actions. It does not become a feed.
- Earlier receipts remain one quiet route. No receipt count appears.
- The full Living Brain remains available as context, but not as the opening surface.

## Longitudinal expression

The visible product language is `Brain Resolution`. The internal construct remains provisional `Judgement Resolution`.

The surface does not say that the Brain is more complete. It can say:

- `A preference became a distinction.`
- `The distinction held on unfamiliar work.`
- `A confident reading became less certain and more accurate.`
- `This territory is revising.`
- `No change has earned a new receipt.`

The working product line is:

> Your Brain does not get fuller. It comes into focus.

This remains candidate product language, not an efficacy claim or final go-to-market copy.

## Sparse, quiet and adverse states

- **Sparse:** `A first reading, not a pattern.` One source-bound contrast is offered.
- **No eligible territory:** `Nothing here has earned a judgement yet.` The user chooses one important territory and one real contrast.
- **Quiet:** `Nothing new has earned a receipt.` The previous verified receipt remains available.
- **Intentionally out of scope:** `Not part of this Brain by your choice.` No empty slot or prompt appears.
- **Loading:** the page reserves the receipt title and date but shows no provisional meaning.
- **Stale:** the last verified receipt remains readable and dated. New mutation is paused.
- **Recoverable error:** the last verified receipt and any staged wording remain. Retry starts at the failed action.
- **Correction:** prior and proposed wording appear together before confirmation. A correction can move a territory from tested to revising.

## Metric and anti-Goodhart contract

- No visible percentage in ordinary use.
- No reward for files, memories, sessions, dwell time, agreement or receipt count.
- No aggregate can hide a blocking dimension.
- No missing or private territory becomes personal deficiency.
- No progress from an unrun, contaminated or post-hoc holdout.
- Appropriate refusal is useful only when the missing evidence is specific and later confirmed.
- Universal refusal earns nothing.
- Every receipt must change a current interpretation, claim boundary or next action.

## Playable proof acceptance

The local proof passes only if a cold user can state after thirty seconds:

1. what changed in Maya's delegation reading;
2. what caused the change;
3. what remains unproved;
4. what the next contrast is for; and
5. why this does not mean Maya or her Brain is more complete.

It also requires:

- the canonical fixture and no candidate-specific evidence;
- one governing receipt spine;
- mobile-first behaviour at 390 by 844;
- desktop behaviour at 1440 by 900;
- keyboard operation, visible focus, practical touch targets and reduced-motion equivalence;
- honest sparse, quiet, stale, error and correction states;
- no horizontal overflow or component scroll;

## Remaining risk

The receipt can still become a tasteful activity feed. The render must make the changed interpretation and its consequence dominant, not the date or historical accumulation. If the page encourages collecting receipts, it fails even without a score.
