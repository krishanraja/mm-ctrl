**Winner: Candidate A7, with 94/100, as the synthesis spine.**

It provides the best concrete demonstration of Maya's standards judging AI work, but it begins too far downstream and can collapse the product from consequential decision partner into an intelligent redlining tool.

## Candidate A7 judgment

### Product and personal value

The concept starts at the correct altitude: "How far should Maya rebuild marketing around AI now?" The three routes expose different causal bets, not merely different amounts of technology. The most valuable mechanism is the shift from route selection to a test that resolves customer value, judgement transfer, role design and incentives together. That makes Maya more capable because it changes what evidence she seeks before reorganising the company.

The personal layer is useful but not yet fully earned. The marker "You are treating speed as the danger" correctly reframes Maya's decision through the fixture. The linked pattern about diagnosing wrongness without transferring the positive standard matters. However, the concept needs one more visible connection from Maya's standards to the route comparison so the personal Brain cannot be removed while leaving an ordinary strategy table intact.

### Claude and return

The route, assumption, standard, evidence and unknown structure is an excellent schema for the self-contained Claude brief. The returned plan is tested against incentive diagnosis, customer proof, budget, accountability and useful staged rollout. This matches the fixture and avoids vague AI evaluation.

### Visual and mobile potential

The physical support and pull of evidence against route lanes is the strongest visual-semantic idea in the set. It can show causality without decorative AI imagery. Mobile is credible only if the selected route carries a compact comparison to the other two. Previous and next controls alone must not hide the fact that three materially different routes exist.

### Explicit reset questions

**Is it disguised repetition of the rejected experience?** No. It retires the Exchange Tray as hero, removes receipt and transport exposition, and begins with consequential intelligence. The Claude bridge is an action from the decision surface, not the product's governing object.

**Did it regress an invariant constraint?** It partially regresses the exact reset requirement for the opening because the important unknown is not guaranteed in the first frame. It also leaves several adverse states unspecified. No authority, provenance, human-accountability or trusted-memory invariant is regressed.

**Which useful rejected strengths were discarded and should be restored?** The rejected proof's bounded custody and audience mechanics disappear almost entirely. Restore one concise audience and destination statement in the Claude brief preview and one concise "proposed material, not Brain memory" state on return. Preserve its acknowledged visual craft only as precise instrument detail. Do not restore the tray, receipts, repeated privacy labels or decorative imagery.

## Candidate B4 judgment

### Product and personal value

Candidate B4 best demonstrates a personal Brain through discrimination. The annotations prove that Maya's standards alter what the system accepts, and "Not my standard" creates a correction at the exact point of disagreement. That is genuine augmentation, not a flattering personality summary.

The weakness is altitude and sequence. The first frame is already a bad Claude answer. A new viewer must infer the strategic decision from its failure. The concept therefore begins at the return loop instead of the consequential fork and does not meet the reset's opening requirement. Without a stronger decision frame, the Brain can look like an advanced editor or brand checker.

### Claude and return

This is the strongest return assessment in the set. It attaches generic reasoning, unsupported assumptions, standard conflicts and additive material to exact work. It also rebuilds the prompt visibly and lets Maya judge both AI output and the Brain's critique.

### Visual and mobile potential

Exact annotations give every mark a literal job. The mobile one-passage-at-a-time model is feasible, though annotation density must not hide the whole causal argument. The warm work surface is functional rather than decorative.

### Explicit reset questions

**Is it disguised repetition of the rejected experience?** No. It does not centre context transport, custody prose or an unavailable reference. The familiar generic Claude answer is used as evidence to discriminate, not presented as product value.

**Did it regress an invariant constraint?** Yes. Its opening breaks the reset requirement for three evidence-linked recognitions including a tension, personal pattern and important unknown. It also risks regressing consequential-decision altitude by focusing on phrases rather than the operating-model causal bet. The return, correction, provenance and human-ownership invariants hold.

**Which useful rejected strengths were discarded and should be restored?** It discards the useful audience and custody boundary entirely. Restore one quiet statement when the complete brief is copied and one quiet private-staging statement when the answer returns. Do not add receipt history. Preserve the rejected proof's acknowledged visual craft through exact annotation semantics, not generated imagery.

## Candidate C9 judgment

### Product and personal value

Candidate C9 has the strongest opening recognition. The line "You are ready to redesign marketing. The part you have not proved is whether your standard can travel without you" is specific, consequential and recognisably about Maya. The corrected delegation interpretation provides unusually strong evidence that the Brain evolves rather than freezes a flattering profile.

The counsel structure protects Maya's agency by placing her provisional view first and making pressure-testing the action. Evidence, non-proof, counter-case and Maya-only judgement form a clear decision boundary. The risk is that the experience becomes an elegant essay. A linear argument can repeat the founder-rejected pattern of too much interface prose even when the prose is better.

### Claude and return

The complete brief is correctly subordinate to an already valuable Brain read. The return assessment is strong, but its four labels are more generic than Candidate B4's exact standard application. The rendered synthesis should attach each criticism to a fixture standard, source or unknown.

### Visual and mobile potential

The mobile disclosure model is the simplest in the set. Desktop is weaker. A vertical paper counsel with a narrow evidence column may underuse the wide viewport and recreate an editorial page rather than a working decision instrument. The growing mint rule risks becoming decorative progress unless it always maps to a named supported claim.

### Explicit reset questions

**Is it disguised repetition of the rejected experience?** No. It starts with personal insight and consequential guidance, not moving context. Its material risk is not repetition of the Exchange Tray but recurrence of verbosity, atmosphere and panel-shaped editorial theatre.

**Did it regress an invariant constraint?** No core product invariant is intentionally regressed. The concept preserves agency, uncertainty, evidence, correction, complete Claude context and proposed return status. Voice and most adverse states are insufficiently specified and therefore cannot be treated as passed.

**Which useful rejected strengths were discarded and should be restored?** Like the other candidates, it largely discards the useful audience and custody mechanics. Restore them once at handoff and once at return. Preserve the rejected proof's visual craft through evidence-linked marks and disciplined spacing. Do not restore right-hand balancing panels, repeated safety copy, receipt theatre or decorative images.

## Mandatory synthesis corrections for Candidate A7

1. **Complete the opening recognition.** Before any action, show one tension, one personal pattern and one important unknown, each with a human-readable evidence route. The unknown should be the fixture's test of whether another leader can apply Maya's standard to unfamiliar work without final rescue.
2. **Make the personal Brain structurally necessary.** Each route must show which named Maya standard or pattern changes its interpretation. If removing Maya's personal evidence leaves the table mostly intact, the synthesis has failed.
3. **Borrow B4's exact return discrimination.** Attach each criticism to an exact phrase, fixture source, Maya standard or named missing evidence. Preserve one genuinely additive fragment. Do not use a generic red, amber or green score.
4. **Borrow C9's sovereign opening boundary.** Keep Maya's provisional view visible before the Brain's recommendation, and name the part only Maya can decide.
5. **Keep the important counter-case live.** The incentives counter-case must be available without changing route and must be strong enough to alter the proposed test.
6. **Show the complete Claude payload.** The proof must copy a self-contained brief with no opaque reference and verify the actual clipboard body. The preview should show what was added and allow exact full inspection.
7. **Restore quiet infrastructure once.** At copy time, state the audience and that the full inspected synthetic brief is being copied. At return, state that the Claude work is separately attributed proposed material. Do not repeat either point elsewhere.
8. **Add the full adverse-state harness.** Cover rich, sparse, contradictory, corrected, stale, slow, wrong-customer, offline-Claude, long-name, 320 px mobile and reduced-motion states. No state may manufacture a personal insight or leave a dead action.
9. **Preserve voice as an effort reducer.** Let Maya change a success measure, kill condition or challenge by voice or one short line. Do not add a general chat composer.
10. **Protect mobile comparison.** One route may be primary, but all three route names, the current selection, decisive difference and immediate next action must remain visible without horizontal drag.
11. **Label synthetic truth once.** A quiet fixture disclosure must cover all names, sources, numbers and outputs without interrupting the decision surface.
12. **Ban the retired solution.** No tray, receipt timeline, custody hero, connector explanation, vague left rail, decorative generated imagery or repeated private/read-only copy may enter the synthesis.

## Material dissent

- Candidate C9 may outperform Candidate A7 in the cold five-second founder test because its opening recognition is more personal and immediately legible. If the A7 synthesis cannot make Maya's personal pattern visibly change the route comparison, C9 should replace it.
- Candidate B4 may outperform both candidates at demonstrating actual judgement augmentation. Its annotation system is the best evidence that the Brain can distinguish generic AI work from work Maya would accept. This strength is mandatory to borrow even though B4 should not govern the complete product sequence.
- Candidate A7 can fail by becoming ordinary strategy software with a personality insight attached. Its win depends on personal standards, corrected interpretation and uncertainty being causal inputs to the table, not sidebar content.
- Candidate C9's prose is better than the rejected proof's copy, but better prose does not solve a spatial or product problem by itself. If the render needs extended reading before Maya can act, the dissent becomes a veto.

## Owner call

Render Candidate A7 only after the twelve mandatory corrections are reflected in a frozen synthesis contract. Use Candidate B4's exact standard-based return audit and Candidate C9's personal opening and human-decision boundary as bounded components. Keep the Decision Table as the sole governing spine. Kill the synthesis if context transport becomes visible infrastructure, if the personal Brain can be removed without changing the decision logic, or if a twelve-year-old cannot say within five seconds: Maya is deciding how far to rebuild marketing around AI, the risk is changing structure before her quality standard can travel, and the product is helping her test that before she commits.
