# CTRL Phase 5 first-contrast synthesis record

**Record revision:** `SR-001`

**Status:** Selected for one provisional rendered mobile synthesis. This is not founder approval, implementation authority or release authority.

## Selection record

Three independent generators produced concept spines from sanitized brief `SB-001` without access to one another:

- Evidence Lens: a reversible direct-manipulation comparison.
- The Second Reading: a temporal editorial proof.
- Evidence Bench: a spatial evidence relationship field.

Two independently ordered blind judges passed the set's conceptual distance but materially disagreed on the winner. Judge 1 selected The Second Reading. Judge 2 selected the Evidence Bench. A third fresh-context judge, kept blind to both prior verdicts and the evolution README, selected the Evidence Lens as the tiebreak winner.

The tiebreaker also found the Evidence Bench ineligible for this slice because its primary action asks the ordinary user to classify an evidence item and its evidence wording broadens one observed meeting into a wider verified pattern. This conflicts with the no-classification and zero-tolerance evidence-integrity boundaries.

## Synthesis rule

The governing interaction remains the Evidence Lens:

- one persistent statement;
- one reversible comparison from the leader's current view to the evidence-bounded contrast;
- one obvious response action;
- one quiet safe escape;
- immediate voice availability with tap, text, keyboard and screen-reader equivalents.

Exactly one idea may be borrowed from the Evidence Bench: a quiet, visibly unfilled `Still unknown` region beside the revealed endpoint. It exists to make incompleteness perceptible and counter any impression that the right endpoint is truth.

Do not borrow evidence slips, claim ledges, placement, classification, settling, a spatial canvas or relationship controls. Do not borrow The Second Reading's staged evidence sequence. The result must remain one interaction model rather than a compromise collage.

## Render gate

The rendered synthesis must be inspected at `390 x 844` CSS pixels and remain credible at `360 x 800`, 200% zoom, reduced motion and with a virtual keyboard present.

It fails before founder review if:

- the evidence endpoint reads as CTRL's proven answer or objective truth;
- the leader cannot distinguish their words, observed evidence, CTRL's interpretation and what remains unknown;
- the comparison requires a drag gesture or fine motor precision;
- the full contrast or next action is obscured at zoom or with a keyboard;
- a report, dashboard, feed, chat transcript or staged presentation appears;
- any visible statement exceeds the bounded fixture evidence;
- the user cannot restore their own view, correct CTRL, reject relevance or leave safely.

If fresh observed testing later shows that the lens is intrinsically read as a truth revealer, do not polish around the failure. Replace the governing spine with The Second Reading.
