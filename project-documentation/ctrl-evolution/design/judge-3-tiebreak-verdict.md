## Verdict

**Winner: Candidate Quartz, The Evidence Lens.**

Quartz is the strongest spine for the first reviewed mobile moment because the interaction and the argument are the same thing. One reversible gesture moves from the leader's own view to the evidence-bounded contrast without turning the surface into a briefing, a questionnaire or an analytical workspace. The contrast sits at the point of action, preserves the user's words, and makes resistance and correction first-class.

This is a narrow win over Sable. Quartz's governing risk is serious: a lens can imply that CTRL reveals the truth. It remains the winner only if the synthesis makes the right endpoint visibly partial and unresolved, and observed testing confirms that users do not read it as a proven answer.

Juniper is not eligible to win because it fails two product-contract boundaries: it asks the leader to classify an evidence item, and it upgrades a one-hour brainstorming meeting into support for the broader proposition that manual production is consuming time.

### Candidate Quartz: scored reasoning

- **Clarity, 4/4.** Its contrast sharply separates the leader's attribution of team hesitancy from the untested operating conditions. The unknown is specific and immediately decision-relevant.
- **Agency and warmth, 3/4.** Reversibility, preservation of the original words, neutral response options and a quiet exit create respectful control. It loses one point because dragging mainly reveals CTRL's prepared interpretation rather than asking the leader to contribute judgement, and the right endpoint can feel privileged.
- **Cognitive load, 3/4.** One statement, one gesture and one next action fit the distracted, one-handed use moment. The masked text-inside-lens treatment may become harder to parse at 200% zoom or with long content, so the rendered treatment must prove that the comparison remains legible without fine motor precision.
- **Honesty, 4/4.** `You said`, `Evidence shows`, `CTRL infers` and `Still unknown` are correctly partitioned. Mint is withheld. Stale material is excluded rather than blended.
- **State-range coherence, 4/4.** Sparse, slow, stale/conflicting, off-record, interrupted, corrected and zero-proposal states all remain recognizable instances of the same instrument. Correction appropriately withdraws the old contrast and resets the lens.
- **Feasibility, 3/4.** The state and data contract are bounded and fit existing React, voice, preservation, recovery and evidence primitives. Accessible range equivalence is feasible. The clipped dual-text lens and responsive reading behavior are more demanding than ordinary controls and require observed proof.
- **Distinctiveness, 4/4.** The lens is meaning-bearing rather than decorative. Reversibility expresses epistemic restraint and creates a memorable instrument without adding a dashboard or spectacle.

### Candidate Sable: scored reasoning

- **Clarity, 4/4.** The before-and-after language is immediately understandable and the final contrast is tightly calibrated to the evidence.
- **Agency and warmth, 3/4.** The leader can advance, pause, cancel, inspect and reject. The editorial metaphor is patient and non-technical. It loses one point because the system controls the rhetorical ordering, making the user more audience than co-reasoner.
- **Cognitive load, 2/4.** The fixed reading position avoids accumulated-card clutter, but three evidence beats, a second reading, a four-line ledger and a fairness mark impose serial attention during a live conversation. Recoverability helps memory but does not remove the time cost.
- **Honesty, 4/4.** Evidence, inference and unknown are exceptionally clean. The concept explicitly prevents stale context from entering the reading and preserves conflict or abstains.
- **State-range coherence, 4/4.** Every required exceptional state maps naturally to the same reading model, including reduced motion, interrupted return and correction-driven withdrawal.
- **Feasibility, 4/4.** This is bounded state-machine work using conventional semantic controls and existing primitives. Its reduced-motion equivalent preserves the same information structure with little technical risk.
- **Distinctiveness, 3/4.** The editorial proof metaphor is coherent and calmer than chat, but it is less product-specific and can collapse into a polished linear presentation if the pacing or typography does too much of the work.

### Candidate Juniper: scored reasoning

- **Clarity, 3/4.** The final line, that the team has not yet been tested against a clear new standard, is strong. The route to it is less immediate because the leader must interpret what a meeting bears on before CTRL reveals the frame.
- **Agency and warmth, 2/4.** The leader visibly causes the reveal and can leave with their view unchanged. However, that agency is purchased by transferring CTRL's evidence-classification work to a busy leader, which feels more administered than companion-like.
- **Cognitive load, 1/4.** Claim ledges, an evidence slip, three destinations, later settling evidence, unsupported space and a second response ask create a miniature analysis task at 390 by 844. The normal-flow fallback is more accessible but does not remove the classification burden.
- **Honesty, 2/4.** Empty unsupported space and separate source labels are excellent. The evidence statement about manual production materially exceeds the bounded record, and the user's placement risks being mistaken for verified support.
- **State-range coherence, 4/4.** The candidate handles the complete state range carefully, including preserving committed placement, withdrawing corrected relationships and treating zero learning as valid.
- **Feasibility, 3/4.** The bounded relationship types and owned components are implementable. Maintaining comprehensible spatial meaning across narrow width, zoom and non-spatial accessibility paths requires more testing and content discipline.
- **Distinctiveness, 3/4.** Making unsupported evidence physically absent is original and useful. The broader workbench risks novelty theatre because it approaches the analytical dashboard the concept itself names as its kill condition.

## Strongest discarded feature

**Juniper's deliberately visible unsupported space is the strongest discarded feature.** It turns uncertainty into something perceptible without inventing a confidence score or adding apologetic caveats. More importantly, it directly counters Quartz's greatest weakness by showing that the lens endpoint is incomplete rather than truer.

## Synthesis boundary

Borrow exactly one feature: **a quiet, visibly unfilled `Still unknown` region adjacent to Quartz's revealed endpoint**.

Do not borrow Juniper's evidence slips, claim ledges, placement task, settling behavior, spatial canvas or relationship classification. Do not borrow Sable's staged evidence sequence. The winning metaphor must remain one reversible evidence lens moving across one persistent statement. The borrowed gap is a static epistemic boundary inside that metaphor, not a second interaction model.

## Material dissent and flip condition

The strongest countercase is Sable. It is safer under reduced motion, easier to implement accessibly, and less likely to encode an implicit subjective-to-objective axis. If fresh, unaided mobile testing shows that leaders call Quartz's right endpoint the answer, treat the lens as revealing truth, cannot recover the source distinction, or struggle at 200% zoom, the winner should flip to Sable rather than receive another visual refinement pass. That failure would be conceptual, not cosmetic.

Conversely, Sable should not replace Quartz merely because it is easier to build. It loses the primary use-moment decision because it asks a leader in a consequential conversation to watch CTRL construct a case over several beats. Quartz creates the useful contrast with less serial attention and a stronger sense of human control.

## Final call

Proceed to one rendered mobile synthesis based on **Quartz**, with the single bounded borrowing above. Preserve the direct-manipulation lens, make the unresolved gap visible at the endpoint, and keep the concept provisional until observed mobile, zoom, keyboard, reduced-motion and fresh-context comprehension checks pass.
