# G15 concept C: The Becoming Receipt

**Territory:** Longitudinal narrative, receipt-first

**Status:** Independent concept spine for a later local proof. Not rendered, judged or approved.

## Concept in one sentence

The Brain returns a dated receipt only when real evidence has changed what it can honestly say about a named part of Maya's judgement, then offers one small contrast that could make the next receipt more useful.

## Governing interaction metaphor

The experience is a **becoming receipt**: a short, human account of what the Brain believed before, what it can support now, what caused the revision and what remains unproved.

The receipt is not a certificate, report card or progress update. It has no total, percentage, level, streak or comparison with another person. It exists because a specific interpretation changed materially. If nothing earned a receipt, the surface stays quiet.

Each receipt is append-only. A later receipt can narrow, replace or reopen an earlier interpretation, but it cannot rewrite the historical record. The narrative therefore makes healthy uncertainty feel like honest development rather than lost progress.

The governing example on day 11 is:

> **Delegation looks different now.**
>
> The earlier reading was that you resisted handing work over. Your correction and two contrasting work choices support a narrower reading: you will delegate when your perceptual criteria can travel with the work.
>
> This is revising, not yet proven on unfamiliar work.

The dominant action is **Show me the receipt**.

## Why this is a distinct spine

- **Sequencing:** meaningful change first, evidence lineage second, one future contrast last. It does not begin with a map, a test or a menu of territories.
- **User agency:** Maya can accept the receipt as a useful current reading, narrow it, reopen the replaced interpretation or choose one real contrast. She is not navigating a spatial model or trying to improve a result.
- **Primary interaction:** read and annotate one dated receipt. The interaction is editorial and longitudinal, not graph manipulation or question answering.
- **Information structure:** a current change statement leads into a chronological evidence spine and ends in one unresolved next proof. Technical evaluation detail remains in an inspectable receipt appendix.
- **State model:** append-only narrative events govern the experience. A receipt moves from new to inspected, corrected or retained. Territory states remain forming, tested, transferable or revising without being added into an overall score.

## Exact first 30 seconds

### 0 to 5 seconds: recognise the change

The mobile first frame contains only:

- **Maya's Brain, day 11**;
- the headline **Delegation looks different now**;
- the two-sentence receipt quoted above;
- the visible limit **Revising, not yet proven on unfamiliar work**; and
- one primary action, **Show me the receipt**.

Amber marks the changed interpretation. Mint is withheld because the revised delegation reading is not yet transferable. The screen does not show territory counts, evidence volume or an overall Brain state.

### 5 to 13 seconds: see before and now

Maya taps **Show me the receipt**. The headline stays in place while two labelled passages appear below it:

**Before, day 1**

> You resist delegation when the work is creative.

**Current reading, day 11**

> You will delegate when your perceptual criteria can travel with the work.

The former reading is labelled **Replaced, still part of the record**. The current reading is labelled **Revising**. Neither is styled as a verdict about Maya.

The single next action is **Why did this change?**

### 13 to 22 seconds: inspect the lineage

Maya taps **Why did this change?** A three-moment chronology opens in natural page order:

1. **Day 1, onboarding:** Maya said that she often takes creative work back because explaining what is wrong feels harder than doing it herself.
2. **Day 7, review:** she chose a direction with explicit perceptual criteria and rejected a polished direction that could not explain its choices.
3. **Day 11, correction:** Maya said the barrier is not delegation itself. It is handing off work before the visual bar is legible.

Each moment has a source label, audience label and **Open exact evidence** action. The chronology ends with the evidence sentence:

> The interpretation narrowed because Maya's correction explains the same contrast more precisely than the earlier reading.

The visible limit remains attached:

> No frozen unfamiliar example has yet shown that another person can use these criteria successfully.

### 22 to 30 seconds: choose the next proof

The dominant action becomes **Try one real contrast**. It opens two synthetic handoff briefs for an unfamiliar visual task and asks one question:

> Which brief would you trust someone else to use without you in the room?

The choices are **Brief A**, **Brief B** and **Neither**. Each is a practical touch target. Maya may add one sentence by voice or type, but the choice is enough to continue. The answer is new evidence, not points and not instant proof of improvement.

Before she chooses, the screen says exactly what the contrast can change:

> Your choice may sharpen the delegation reading. It cannot complete this territory or change anything outside it.

## Primary interaction

The primary interaction is **inspect, annotate and extend one receipt**.

A receipt has four faces in one linear page:

1. **What changed:** one current, personally recognisable difference.
2. **Before and now:** the exact interpretation lineage.
3. **Why:** the smallest evidence set that earned the revision and its honest limit.
4. **One next contrast:** a minimal real choice that could test transfer.

Maya may take four meaningful actions:

- **Keep this reading:** retain it as the current revising interpretation without claiming it is proven.
- **Narrow it:** correct the meaning or applicability in ordinary language.
- **Reopen the earlier reading:** mark the conflict unresolved rather than choosing a convenient story.
- **Try one real contrast:** supply one choice on unfamiliar work.

There is no generic response box. Typing and voice appear only after Maya chooses **Narrow it** or elects to explain a contrast.

## Information structure

### The current receipt

The opening always belongs to one material change in one subject-approved territory. It includes the required surface fields in human language:

- territory;
- forming, tested, transferable or revising state;
- what changed;
- one evidence sentence; and
- one honest limit.

### The receipt book

Past receipts form a chronological reading, not an activity feed. Each entry exists only for a material interpretation, test, transfer result, appropriate refusal or correction. Source ingestion by itself never creates a receipt.

Receipts are grouped beneath their named territory only after Maya opens **Earlier receipts**. Territories are not presented as compulsory slots. Creative direction can be transferable, delegation can be revising, a new territory can be forming, and another area can remain intentionally outside scope without any visual suggestion that Maya is deficient.

### The receipt appendix

**Open exact evidence** reveals provenance, frozen Brain version, chosen and rejected examples, held-out case result, no-Brain baseline, handcrafted-context baseline and correction lineage. Dimension names such as grounding or calibration stay in this appendix. The ordinary receipt translates them into direct questions:

- What did this come from?
- Did it distinguish your choice on unfamiliar work?
- Did it know when not to guess?
- Did your Brain improve the work compared with the same model without it?
- Did a correction repair the current reading without erasing history?

No dimension is averaged. A territory cannot be shown as transferable while any required dimension remains unresolved.

### The next contrast

The final section contains one unfamiliar, consequence-appropriate comparison tied to the receipt's weakest unresolved dimension. It does not ask Maya to upload files, complete a category or repeat known preferences. A contrast can also end in **Not enough context** or **Neither**, both of which can be useful evidence.

## Sparse, quiet and adverse behaviour

### One version only

The surface does not fabricate a change story. It opens with:

> **A first reading, not a pattern.**
>
> Maya chose one direction and rejected another in creative work. The reason is specific enough to remember, but not yet tested elsewhere.

The action is **Try one different example**.

### No eligible territory

The surface says:

> **Nothing here has earned a judgement yet.**
>
> One real contrast in a territory you choose would give the Brain something honest to work with.

It asks Maya to name or choose one important territory. No prefilled category implies what she should complete.

### Intentionally out of scope

An out-of-scope territory never appears among forming or revising territories. If Maya opens scope history, it reads **Not part of this Brain by your choice**. There is no empty slot, warning or deepening action.

### Quiet

If no interpretation, transfer result, correction or appropriate refusal materially changed, no receipt is issued. The return surface shows the last receipt date and says **Nothing new has earned a receipt** with one action, **Open the last one**. It does not invent an insight to create engagement.

### Loading

The page reserves only the receipt title and date positions. It shows no provisional claim, trajectory or decorative movement.

### Stale

The last verified receipt stays readable with its verified date. New contrasts and correction are paused behind **Refresh before changing this reading**.

### Recoverable error

The last verified receipt remains intact. Any unsubmitted wording is preserved locally for the proof session, the failed operation is named, and retry occurs at the failure point. No success receipt appears.

## Correction behaviour

Maya chooses **Narrow it** beside the exact current reading. The prior text remains visible. The interface asks one question:

> What does this reading miss?

It offers two evidence-grounded tap choices, including **The criteria need to travel first**, plus **Say it my way** by voice or text. Before confirmation, the surface shows:

- the current wording;
- the proposed narrower wording;
- the territory and conditions affected;
- the receipts and intervention that will rebuild; and
- what will remain unchanged.

After Maya confirms, certainty may decrease and the territory may move from tested to revising. The new receipt treats that as a more honest Brain:

> **This became narrower, not weaker.**
>
> The old reading remains in your record. The current reading now needs unfamiliar work before it can be called transferable.

No celebratory animation implies that agreement is progress. Amber marks the correction once, then settles. Mint returns only after every required dimension has evidence and no blocking failure remains.

## Return desire

The return mechanic is curiosity about meaningful change, not accumulation.

Maya returns because each receipt may answer one of three personally useful questions:

- What has become more precise in how I judge?
- What did real work disprove or narrow?
- Can this part of my judgement now travel into unfamiliar work or another person's hands?

The next contrast leaves a deliberate open loop. After Maya chooses a handoff brief, the interface does not announce a result. It says **This will be tested against unfamiliar work** and returns only when an evaluated outcome, correction or appropriate refusal materially changes the territory.

The concept succeeds if Maya wants to return to see whether a real choice altered the Brain's understanding, not because a streak or incomplete ring demands attention.

## Accessibility and mobile feasibility

- The 390 by 844 first frame fits the receipt headline, current reading, honest limit and one primary action without horizontal scroll.
- The page uses native headings, paragraphs, buttons, an ordered chronology and fieldset-based contrast choices. It requires no canvas or spatial precision.
- Before and Current are written labels. Replaced and Revising are not conveyed by colour, position or motion alone.
- Every control has a practical 44px target, visible focus and a descriptive accessible name.
- Keyboard order follows receipt, action, chronology, evidence and next contrast. Focus returns to the action that opened each disclosure.
- Screen readers receive the correction result and repair status through a polite live region only after user action.
- Reduced motion replaces the receipt arrival and history shift with immediate state changes. No meaning depends on transition.
- At 200 percent zoom and with long translated labels, the chronology becomes a single natural column. The document scrolls; disclosures do not create nested scroll regions.
- Voice is optional and appears only beside a normal text path. Choosing a contrast never requires speech, drag, hover or fine pointer control.
- Desktop at 1440 by 900 keeps the receipt narrative in a readable central measure and may place the exact evidence appendix beside it after selection. It does not turn the page into a dashboard grid.

## Commercial expression

The concept can be described without a score or false efficacy claim:

> **See where your judgement became more exact, what changed it and what real work should test next.**

For a thirty-day proof, the commercial story is a sequence of governed receipts. Each qualifying receipt shows a personal judgement becoming more grounded, discriminating, calibrated, transferable or adaptive in one named important territory. It also states the limit and preserves corrections.

This supports a credible value story:

- the leader sees personal development rather than a memory archive;
- the company sees whether useful judgement can travel into unfamiliar work or clearer handoffs;
- the Brain shows its evidence instead of claiming to replicate the person; and
- Mindmake can discuss specific change over time without selling a personality score.

No public claim that Brain context improves judgement is allowed from the synthetic proof. That claim waits for representative comprehension, stable results across reasonable holdout sets, prospective held-out lift, calibration and coverage evidence, correction replay, conformity checks and consequence review.

## Strongest reason it could fail

The receipt can become a tasteful activity timeline. If users read the dated sequence as passive history rather than as evidence that a specific interpretation changed and now alters the next useful action, the concept has lost the product.

It can also smuggle in completeness through narrative tone. A sequence that always sounds more resolved, more certain or more accomplished would recreate a progress score in prose.

## Kill condition

Kill the spine if cold users cannot do all of the following after the first thirty seconds:

1. state the one interpretation that changed;
2. name the evidence that caused the change;
3. repeat the honest limit;
4. identify the one next contrast; and
5. explain that the receipt does not mean Maya or her Brain is more complete overall.

Also kill it if repeated use makes users expect a receipt after every upload, rewards agreement, or treats lower certainty as regression. Do not repair those failures with a score, streak, progress ring, more celebratory copy or a dashboard of territories. The alternative must be a different governing interaction, not a more decorated receipt book.
