# G22 concept spine: The Standing Field

**Concept revision:** C1

**Source brief:** G22 whole-product golden-path sanitized divergence brief R1, 11 September 2026

**Status:** Independent concept proposal. Not approved for implementation.

## The call

Build the proof around one persistent working field called **The Standing Field**.

Every item that matters appears as a piece of work on the same field. A note arrives at the left edge. A consequential call earns the centre. Sources gather around that call. A proposed learning rests above it until Maya accepts, corrects, or rejects it. Accepted learning stays on the field as a named standard. Later work enters the same place and visibly picks that standard up. A released version leaves from the right edge with its ownership and history intact.

The field is not a picture of the Brain. It is the active projection of the Brain for the work that deserves attention now. Position has a fixed meaning. Standing has a written label. Relationships appear only when they explain why the current work changed.

This route wins because compounding becomes a visible change in the work itself. The materially different route would be a sequence of purpose-built screens for intake, decision, learning, preparation, Brain inspection, and export. That route could make each step locally clear, but it would make the user reconstruct the continuity between them. The Standing Field keeps continuity as the primary interaction.

## Design contract

**State of use:** An advisor uses the wide field between sessions. Maya enters only for one route-changing question, the decision gate, the learning decision, and final release. Those moments must work one-handed on a phone.

**User and action:** The advisor must know what deserves work and why. Maya must make the consequential calls without administering the system. Both must see that later help changed because of an accepted or corrected standard.

**Governing rule:** The Brain organises everything. The field never promotes an interpretation into learning, changes ownership, or releases a version without the named human action.

**Data truth:** All people, companies, work, evidence, research, dates, and outcomes in this proof are synthetic. Source class and standing remain visible on every claim. Synthetic evidence demonstrates product mechanics only.

**Materiality:** Material whole-product interaction concept. The approved R4 Decision Table remains unchanged and appears only after the call earns deeper comparison.

**Authority:** This document defines a concept spine and deterministic proof contract. It does not approve UI implementation, production navigation, live integrations, model calls, messages, accounts, or data writes.

**Proof:** A build should pass the complete, quiet, sparse, stale, proposed, corrected, rejected, later-return, narrow-phone, and wide-desktop fixtures described below. The complete path must remain at eight primary actions or fewer.

## The field

The product shell is deliberately quiet. It contains the current person and company, a clear operator mode, the field, and one status line. There is no permanent analytics strip, activity feed, feature navigation, or assistant chat.

The field has four semantic positions. They do not behave like columns in a board and cannot be rearranged.

1. **Arriving**, at the left edge. Work residue enters here with source, subject, purpose, audience, owner, and freshness intact.
2. **In hand**, at the centre. Only one consequential call, question, learning choice, or release choice can occupy this position. This is the only place with a primary action.
3. **Held**, on the upper rail. Accepted personal standards, company constraints, unresolved unknowns, and active gates stay here. Items on this rail can affect later work. Their owner and standing remain written on them.
4. **Carried forward**, at the right edge. Decisions, preparations, and releases move here after the human-owned action. The right edge shows what this work now changes, not a feed of everything that happened.

History sits in the field rather than in a separate product area. Selecting a current object reveals its earlier versions beneath it as aligned leaves. The top leaf is current. Corrected and rejected leaves remain readable, dated, and inert.

The user never pans an infinite canvas. The field fits the viewport at wide sizes and becomes one ordered vertical path at narrow sizes. Objects cannot be freely dragged. Their movement is a consequence of a state change, not a layout preference.

## Primary interaction

The primary interaction is **bring one piece into hand**.

Selecting an earned object moves it to the centre without changing its standing. The field reduces contrast on unrelated items and brings only the evidence, owner, and downstream consequence relevant to that object into view. The object itself expands in place. Its action and result occupy the same footprint.

A piece has two readable faces:

- The **work face** shows what needs attention, the smallest useful evidence, and one action.
- The **record face** shows source, subject, purpose, audience, owner, version, freshness, standing, and the specific objects that depend on it.

On desktop, the record face is revealed by **Inspect record** and replaces the work face in the same centre position. On mobile, the same control expands those fields beneath the active object. This is not a modal side panel. Closing it restores the action exactly where it was.

The central piece can pull supporting objects close, but users cannot create arbitrary links. CTRL creates a relationship only when the fixture names a typed dependency such as `supports`, `challenges`, `proposes`, `corrects`, `used_by`, or `released_in`. Selecting the relationship reveals one plain sentence describing its meaning.

Example:

> Maya's quality standard changed the pilot gate.

## Object language

Five object forms are sufficient for the whole proof.

| Object | Job on the field | Recognisable form | May change standing through |
|---|---|---|---|
| Work piece | Preserve incoming work and its intended use | A clipped sheet with a source tab | received, stale, set aside, used |
| Call | Hold one consequential tension and its evidence | A firm rectangular plate with an open centre seam | provisional, ready, owned, superseded |
| Learning | Offer a personal criterion without silently adopting it | A narrow rule placed just below the Held rail | proposed, accepted, corrected, rejected |
| Help | Show preparation or output shaped by the Brain | A folded brief whose opened face shows what changed | drafted, needs review, released, superseded |
| Release | Make a portable, owned version inspectable | A bound package split into personal and company sections | prepared, approved, downloaded |

Every form includes a written standing label. Shape and motion reinforce meaning but never carry meaning alone.

### Ownership

Ownership is a structural seam, not a privacy badge repeated across the UI.

- Personal objects carry a narrow edge labelled `Maya`.
- Company objects carry a broad edge labelled `Aperture House`.
- A relationship may cross the seam, but the objects never merge.
- A release package contains two visibly separate sections and a manifest that says what each section contains.

The field status line states this distinction once when it affects the current choice. Example:

> This standard is Maya's. The pilot plan belongs to Aperture House.

### Standing

Standing is always a noun or short phrase, never an unexplained colour.

- `Source`
- `CTRL view`
- `Proposed learning`
- `Accepted by Maya`
- `Corrected by Maya`
- `Not learned`
- `Needs a newer source`
- `Owned call`
- `Released by Maya`

Mint may mark a human-accepted answer. Amber may mark an object or dependency that changed. Neutral ink carries established source material. A stale or blocked state uses a labelled hatch plus icon so it remains legible without colour.

## Information structure

The shell and field form one hierarchy.

### Shell

- `Maya Chen · Aperture House`
- mode: `Advisor view` or `Maya view`
- one truthful status: `Nothing needs Maya`, `One call needs Maya`, or `Release waiting for Maya`
- access to `Inspect Brain`, which changes the field's projection rather than opening another app area

### Active object

The centre object shows, in this order:

1. what changed or what needs a call;
2. why it matters now;
3. the smallest evidence set that can change the route;
4. one action or one question;
5. the result in the same place;
6. record access for provenance and history.

### Supporting objects

Supporting sources gather around the active object in a fixed order: Maya, customer evidence, company work, public research, then CTRL view. Source tabs use both text and purposeful line icons. The source itself stays visually separate from CTRL's interpretation.

### Held rail

The upper rail shows only items that can govern future work:

- accepted or corrected personal standards;
- live company constraints;
- unresolved unknowns;
- active human gates.

Rejected interpretations never sit on the Held rail. They remain in history as `Not learned`.

### Brain inspection

`Inspect Brain` keeps the same field and replaces the current-work projection with an ownership projection. It groups the same objects under `Maya`, `Aperture House`, and `Shared relationship`, then lets the user bring any object into hand. It does not expose storage tables or technical vocabulary.

The first view answers four questions:

- What does CTRL currently use?
- Who owns each item?
- What is accepted, proposed, stale, corrected, or rejected?
- What later work used it?

The user can select `Current only` or `Show history`. These are inspection filters, not primary product actions.

## Meaningful data visuals

The concept uses four data-bearing visuals. None may render without the fixture fields named below.

### 1. Source weave

When a call is in hand, up to five labelled source strands terminate at the exact criterion or tension they support or challenge. Strand weight equals the number of distinct records in that source class, capped at three. A break in a strand means stale evidence. Selecting a strand lists its record IDs and one-sentence contribution.

This visual answers: **What is this call made from?** It must not imply statistical confidence or causality.

Dependencies: `source_type`, `record_id`, `supports_object_id`, `relation_type`, `recorded_at`, `fresh_until`, `standing`.

### 2. Decision boundary

The Call plate carries a £1.2m budget bracket and eight labelled role marks around its edge. Marks sit inside or outside the first gate according to the chosen route. Before Maya decides, the boundary remains open and is labelled `CTRL view`. After the Decision Table returns an owned call, the boundary closes and is labelled `Set by Maya`.

This visual answers: **How much and who does this call touch?** It is not an allocation chart and must not invent spend by role.

Dependencies: `operating_budget_gbp`, `affected_role_ids`, `gate_role_ids`, `decision_owner`, `decision_standing`.

### 3. Standing edge

Every object has a left edge divided into source, interpretation, and human-decision segments. Only segments present in the record appear. The text label sits beside the segment. A correction places the new edge above the old one, joined by a small repair stitch labelled with actor and date.

This visual answers: **What may the Brain treat as true now?**

Dependencies: `standing`, `standing_actor`, `standing_at`, `supersedes_id`, `owner_scope`.

### 4. Carry trace

On a later return, one visible line runs from an accepted or corrected item on the Held rail into the exact sentence, criterion, or gate it changed in the new Help object. Opening the trace shows `Before`, `Carried in`, and `Now`. It never claims the new output is better merely because a link exists.

This visual answers: **What did CTRL remember, and what did that change?**

Dependencies: `learning_id`, `target_object_id`, `target_fragment_id`, `prior_fragment`, `current_fragment`, `change_reason`, `relation_status`.

## Complete golden path

The proof uses eight semantic primary actions. Scenario switches used by the deterministic demo harness do not appear as product actions.

| # | Actor | Primary action | What changes on the field | Minimal visible copy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Advisor | `Work this` | Maya's note moves from Arriving to In hand. Company, customer, and research sources gather around it. | `A marketing call is forming.` |
| 2 | Advisor | `Ready for Maya` | The one route-changing question replaces the advisor action. The proof marks the local Maya view as ready. It does not send a message. | `One answer changes the first gate.` |
| 3 | Maya | `Quality must travel` | Maya answers the question. The answer appears under the two choices and changes the recommended route. | `Then volume cannot be the test.` |
| 4 | Advisor or Maya | `Compare routes` | The approved Decision Table opens inside the centre plate as an earned deeper state. The rest of the field stays present at lower contrast. | `Compare the routes.` |
| 5 | Maya | `Use this route` | The unchanged Decision Table returns the owned call, first gate, final standard, and decision owner. The Call closes and moves to Carried forward. | `Start with Brand. Quality must travel without final rescue.` |
| 6 | Maya | `Keep this learning` | The proposed Learning becomes `Accepted by Maya` and moves to Held. Correction and rejection use the same action position in their fixtures. | `Use this in later work.` |
| 7 | Maya | `Inspect Brain` | The field changes to the ownership projection. The accepted learning, source, owned call, later use, and full history remain connected and inspectable. | `This is what CTRL uses now.` |
| 8 | Maya | `Release ZIP` | A deterministic ZIP is generated from current governed objects. The Release object records `Released by Maya` locally and shows the two ownership sections. | `Your copy is ready.` |

The later-return moment does not require a ninth action. In the scenario, a new board preparation arrives on 1 October and becomes the active work on the advisor's next ordinary visit. Its Carry trace is already visible. The advisor can inspect the change, but CTRL asks nothing because accepted context is sufficient.

The leader-owned boundaries are explicit:

- Maya's answer sets what success means before comparison.
- Maya's Decision Table commitment sets the first gate and final standard.
- Maya accepts, corrects, or rejects the proposed personal learning.
- Maya alone releases the portable version.

## The Decision Table as an earned deeper state

The R4 Decision Table is an implementation dependency, not a surface to redesign in this concept.

It appears only when all of the following are true:

- the Call is consequential;
- the minimum evidence contract is met;
- the route-changing question has been answered or explicitly marked unknown;
- the advisor or Maya chooses `Compare routes`.

The table expands from the open seam in the centre Call plate. Desktop retains a narrow band of the surrounding field so source and ownership continuity remain visible. Mobile gives the approved table the full viewport with a persistent return label, `Back to the call`. Its internal content, controls, density, and decision mechanics remain exactly as approved in R4.

The table receives the decision ID, two route IDs, approved criteria, evidence references, budget, affected roles, decision owner, and current gate. It returns the selected route, owner-set gate, final standard, rationale if captured by the approved organ, timestamp, and version. On return, those values change the Call object in place. The user never lands on a new dashboard page.

If the minimum evidence contract is not met, the table does not appear. The centre object says `Not enough to compare routes yet` and presents the single remedy relevant to the missing field.

## Desktop choreography

At a wide viewport, the field occupies the useful screen below a compact identity line.

- Arriving work rests at the left edge with only its source tab and purpose visible.
- In hand occupies the visual centre and receives the strongest contrast.
- The Held rail sits above the centre, close enough for a Carry trace to be read without scrolling.
- Carried-forward work rests to the right, with the newest affected object nearest the centre.
- Supporting evidence moves close to the active object in a shallow arc. It never becomes a card grid.
- Selecting an evidence tab expands that source where it sits. The result appears there. No drawer covers the field.
- Quiet motion is limited to a piece settling into position, a standing edge closing after Maya's action, and a repair stitch joining corrected versions. Reduced motion replaces each with an immediate state change.

The layout uses ordinary DOM regions, CSS grid, and a small SVG relationship layer whose paths derive from known element anchors. It requires no canvas, physics engine, spatial database, zoom model, or user-authored coordinates.

## Mobile choreography

Mobile preserves the same semantics without shrinking the desktop field into a map.

The four positions become an ordered vertical path: Arriving, In hand, Held, Carried forward. Only the active object and the nearest cause or consequence are fully open. A compact position line at the top says where the object stands, for example `In hand · Call`.

Each phone view contains one immediate action or question:

- Question view: the question, two large choices, voice as an optional alternative where supported, and no free-text requirement.
- Decision view: the approved Decision Table in its existing mobile form.
- Learning view: proposed wording, source link, and the three mutually exclusive actions `Keep`, `Correct`, `Not mine`. Choosing `Correct` opens one short voice-or-type field in the same place.
- Release view: ownership summary, version, and `Release ZIP`.

The active state survives orientation change, dynamic browser chrome, keyboard opening, and back navigation. Every pointer action has a keyboard path. Voice always has a tap or type alternative. Touch targets, visible focus, labels, and status changes cannot rely on colour or motion. The page scrolls naturally. No object contains its own scrollbar.

The advisor's phone can show the full field path. Maya's deliberately timed link opens directly on the one active object and hides advisor-only controls. In this unlinked proof, `Ready for Maya` changes local demo state only and clearly says `Not sent`.

## Required state handling

### Quiet or no-earned-interruption

The centre remains open. The Held rail and last carried-forward object stay faintly present so the Brain still feels alive. No fake recommendation appears.

Status:

> Nothing needs Maya today.

Advisor action:

> Review incoming work

That action opens Arriving items only. It does not manufacture a call.

### Sparse evidence

`CALL-001` cannot reach `ready`. The source weave shows only the available strands and one labelled gap. CTRL states what is missing without asking Maya a premature question.

> Not enough to call this yet.

> We have Maya's note. We do not have current company work or customer evidence.

One remedy appears based on the fixture, such as `Add company work`. If the deterministic proof cannot add a real source, the control adds a clearly labelled synthetic fixture and updates the object in place.

No Learning object may be created from sparse evidence.

### Stale evidence

A broken Source weave strand and the label `Needs a newer source` identify the stale record. If the stale item could change the selected route, `Use this route` remains unavailable. The centre presents one decision:

> This scorecard is out of date.

> Refresh it or use it as background only.

`Use as background` keeps the source visible but removes it from route-setting evidence. The record face preserves who made that choice and when. If the source is not route-changing, work can continue with the stale limitation written beside the claim.

### Proposed learning

After the owned call, `LEARNING-001` appears between In hand and Held. It does not influence other work yet.

> Could this guide later work?

> Maya treats independence at the final pass as proof that quality travels.

Actions: `Keep`, `Correct`, `Not mine`.

### Corrected learning

Selecting `Correct` preserves the proposed wording beneath the active leaf and asks for one short replacement by voice or type. The correction fixture is:

> For brand work, one calibration pass is fine. Repeated final rescue means the standard has not travelled.

The new version becomes `Corrected by Maya`. `LEARNING-001-v1` remains in history as `Superseded`. Any later Help object using v1 receives `Needs refresh`. Regeneration in the deterministic proof swaps only the affected fragment and preserves the rest of the Help object.

The repair stitch states:

> Maya corrected this on 11 September.

### Rejected learning

Selecting `Not mine` changes standing to `Not learned`. The object moves under the current Call as history and never reaches Held. No future object can form a `used_by` relationship to it.

> Kept in history. Not used in later work.

An optional one-tap reason can be recorded after the rejection, but it cannot block the rejection or turn into a new learning without Maya's approval.

### Later return

`HELP-001` arrives on 1 October. Its Carry trace terminates at the exact changed review criterion. The active face leads with the changed work, not a retrospective product claim.

> One earlier standard changed this prep.

Opening the trace shows:

- `Before: Report whether content output rose.`
- `Carried in: Quality must travel without Maya's final rescue.`
- `Now: Show paired work and whether the team passed final review without Maya's rescue.`

This demonstrates causal continuity. It does not claim improved commercial outcomes or product efficacy.

### Correction after later use

If Maya corrects a learning after `HELP-001` used it, the Help object gains a visible amber stitch and `Needs refresh`. The old version remains inspectable. Until refresh, the help cannot be released as current. After deterministic refresh, only the linked fragment changes and the record face lists both learning versions.

### Release failure or interruption

If local ZIP generation fails, input and release intent remain preserved in local proof state. The centre says `Your copy was not created` and offers `Try again`. It does not record `Released by Maya` until the browser confirms generation.

If the user leaves before release, the object remains `prepared`. Returning restores the same version and action. A newer correction invalidates the prepared manifest and requires Maya to inspect the changed items before release.

## Deterministic implementation shape

The first proof can be implemented with:

- one fixture manifest containing every object, relationship, state variant, and visible string;
- one reducer that applies named events to immutable object versions;
- CSS grid for the four desktop positions and normal document flow for mobile;
- positioned SVG paths for the four permitted relationship visuals;
- the approved R4 Decision Table mounted inside the centre object's earned state;
- local persistence for proof progress;
- client-side ZIP generation from the current manifest;
- a non-indexable route and no network requests.

The state reducer needs these named events:

`FOCUS_WORK`, `READY_FOR_MAYA`, `ANSWER_ROUTE_QUESTION`, `OPEN_DECISION_TABLE`, `OWN_DECISION`, `PROPOSE_LEARNING`, `ACCEPT_LEARNING`, `CORRECT_LEARNING`, `REJECT_LEARNING`, `ARRIVE_LATER_HELP`, `OPEN_BRAIN_PROJECTION`, `PREPARE_RELEASE`, `RELEASE_ZIP`, `MARK_STALE`, `USE_AS_BACKGROUND`, `REFRESH_DEPENDENT`.

The demo harness may load state fixtures directly for review. Harness controls must sit outside the product frame and carry the label `Demo state`. They do not count as product controls.

## Why this is not a dashboard

- It has one active work object, not a collection of status tiles.
- There is no overview of metrics competing for attention.
- Position communicates the standing of work in one causal journey, not organisational categories.
- The only primary action lives on the object that needs it.
- History, provenance, ownership, and later use are faces of the same object, not separate reporting modules.
- Quiet state can be genuinely quiet.

## Why this is not a graph explorer

- Users cannot pan, zoom, arrange nodes, or browse arbitrary topology.
- The field has four fixed semantic positions and a stable reading order.
- Relationships appear only for the active object and only when they explain a route, correction, or later use.
- Objects look and read like work, calls, standards, help, and releases. They are not generic circles.
- Every line has a typed record and a plain-language meaning.
- `Inspect Brain` is an ownership and standing projection over the same governed objects, not a second network model.

## Failure conditions

Reject this concept before implementation if any of these become necessary:

- the field needs a second hidden model to decide where objects sit;
- users need pan, zoom, or free dragging to understand the current call;
- the complete path exceeds eight semantic primary actions;
- the R4 Decision Table must be redesigned to fit;
- a proposed or rejected learning affects later work;
- personal and company objects must be merged to make release simple;
- a meaningful visual lacks the fixture data required to justify it;
- sparse or stale evidence still produces a confident call;
- mobile shows more than one immediate action or question;
- the proof implies that synthetic continuity proves real-world efficacy.

## Verification acceptance

The concept is ready to render only if a deterministic review can mark all of these `pass`:

- One field carries intake, call, decision, learning, later help, Brain inspection, and release.
- The representative case uses eight or fewer primary actions.
- The Decision Table is deeper, earned, and unchanged.
- Each visible claim exposes source class and standing.
- Personal and company ownership remain distinct through release.
- Accepted or corrected learning changes one named fragment of later help.
- Rejected learning changes no later help.
- Correction preserves history and marks affected dependants.
- Quiet, sparse, stale, interrupted, and failed-release states remain useful and honest.
- Desktop and mobile preserve the same task and standing.
- Every visible control works locally without a model call, message, account, database write, or production navigation change.
- No dashboard, tour, chat, progress mechanic, or graph-explorer behavior is required.

The decisive human gate remains taste and comprehension: a cold rendered proof must let Krish explain, in his own words, what CTRL learned, who accepted it, what later work changed, and who owns the released version without needing a product explanation.
