# G20 mobile consequential-decision reset, R136

## Sanitized brief given to independent concept generators

Design one mobile interaction spine for a Mindmake operator or adviser using a leader's AI Brain to work on one consequential, high-value business decision.

The user is one-handed and distracted on a 390 by 844 or 320 by 568 phone. They must understand the live decision and the Brain's current recommendation at a glance, see the single most valuable unresolved question, and take one useful action. Evidence, alternative routes, counter-case and provenance must remain available but cannot compete for first attention. Desktop can remain a richer instrument.

The brand is deep ink green, warm cream, mint for answers and amber for changes, with type-led instrument quality. No sparkles, generic AI aesthetics, pompous pronouncements, business horoscopes, redundant trust copy or long paragraphs. Preserve human decision authority and exact data truth.

Each generator must specify sequencing, user agency, primary interaction, information structure, state model, 320px behaviour and the strongest counterargument. Generators may not inspect the current product, previous renders, rejected solutions or one another's work.

## Fixed product and data constraints

- This is a million-pound decision instrument, not micro-learning.
- One visible question is the ceiling, not a claim that only one unknown exists.
- Answering an unknown may update the recommendation but never records the human decision.
- Sparse, stale, conflicted, loading and failed data must remain honest.
- Deeper evidence is entered deliberately and never uses nested scrolling.
- The desktop instrument and Living Brain remain richer surfaces.
- No route, production or customer-surface authority is implied by this mock exploration.
