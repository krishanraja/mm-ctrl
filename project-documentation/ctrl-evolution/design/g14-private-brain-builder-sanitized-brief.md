# G14 Private Brain Builder sanitized divergence brief

**Revision:** 1

**Status:** current material-surface brief. Concept generation and judging may use this file. It deliberately excludes prior layouts, solution metaphors and rejected visual structures.

## Delivery preflight

**STATE_ROUTE:** `project-documentation/ctrl-evolution/README.md`

**SOURCE_LAYERS:** Durable doctrine is the locked CTRL decision corpus and Krish design system. Project requirements are the G14 founder calibration and approved G13 R2 Customer Brain. Historical mocks are evidence of standards and failures, not templates. External research is supporting context only. Static intake, project-management dashboards, completeness theatre and generic AI-assistant surfaces are obsolete.

**PRODUCT_TRUTH:** The near-term primary operator is Krish during one Mindmake thirty-day proof. One customer's governed Brain is the object. The operator surface must help Krish make the next customer interaction unusually valuable while the customer receives a personal, inspectable and increasingly useful Living Brain.

**NON-GOALS:** No CRM, project plan, task manager, universal chatbot, clinical diagnostic, employee evaluation, live-surveillance product, connector catalogue, completeness score, praise collection, raw evidence dump, technical memory administration or visualisation that invents semantic meaning.

**SURFACE_DEPENDENCIES:** Governed evidence and Brain truth precede operator synthesis. Operator synthesis precedes a customer intervention. Customer interaction creates new evidence. New evidence produces an inspectable change receipt and may propose durable learning. The locked Customer Brain remains a separate expression over the same kernel.

**VERTICAL_SLICE:** A consented new transcript arrives, the Brain revises one interpretation, Krish understands what changed and why, prepares one evidence-backed intervention for the next session, privately asks the Brain a nonlinear follow-up if needed, and can see what the customer will experience without publishing or mutating production data.

**FIRST_SURFACE:** One Private Brain Builder customer cockpit for Krish, rendered with a representative rich synthetic customer and enough interaction to judge the product rather than a static composition.

## State of use

Krish opens one customer's Brain on desktop while preparing for a session, or on mobile immediately after evidence arrives. He is busy, already understands the customer relationship and does not want a system-health report. Within ten seconds he must know what now deserves his attention. Within two minutes he should be able to enter the next conversation sharper than he would have without the product.

## User and action

The surface succeeds when Krish can:

1. identify the single best supported customer intervention;
2. understand what changed after the latest evidence and what was discarded or weakened;
3. see useful-now value without being told the Brain is forever incomplete;
4. prepare or adapt the intervention with near-zero clerical work;
5. inspect the evidence, standing, uncertainty, audience and correction path;
6. choose the smallest credible route to deepen the Brain;
7. privately ask the Brain about an unexpected direction without requiring ambient listening;
8. anticipate the customer's personal benefit, not merely the operator's workflow.

## Governing rule

The primary object is a supported customer intervention, not a task, memory, score, notification or system metric. It must be traceable to a current interpretation and the evidence gap it can close. The Brain drafts the move; Krish retains judgement and session ownership. The surface may be warm and participant-like, but cannot claim sentience, certainty or autonomous authority.

## Data truth

The proof uses one clearly labelled synthetic customer fixture. Every claim resolves to explicit evidence. Items distinguish direct statement, observed pattern, supported synthesis, hypothesis, unresolved tension and discarded interpretation. New evidence may alter confidence or standing but cannot silently rewrite durable truth. Operator-private notes, customer-private Brain meaning and company-shareable transition material remain distinguishable.

Required representative states:

- rich Brain with a recent material change;
- one supported intervention ready now;
- one unresolved contradiction;
- one interpretation weakened by new evidence;
- one useful deepening route;
- private during-session question and response;
- customer-facing preview;
- sparse, quiet, loading, stale and error fallbacks accessible inside the proof.

## Materiality

Material. This is a new operator information architecture and interaction model. It requires a rendered founder reaction before implementation or any later material surface.

## Brand and voice

- Deep ink green, warm cream and restrained semantic colour.
- Newsreader for payoff, Archivo for structure, IBM Plex Mono for evidence and controls, Source Serif 4 for sustained reading where available.
- Dark, warm, physical, instrument-like and alive through meaningful state response.
- Purposeful line iconography only. No sparkle symbols, mascots, cartoon faces, gradients, glass cards or decorative AI motifs.
- No guff, self-congratulation, jargon, em dashes, fragment stacks or generic AI copy.
- CTRL is a provisional working label and must remain structurally replaceable. Mindy must not appear.

## Interaction constraints

- Lead with one action-worthy meaning, not navigation or status.
- The verdict belongs beside the action it changes.
- One primary action per state, with optional inspection behind it.
- Voice and typing may both support the private ask; neither may be required.
- No nested scrollbars.
- Controls announce affordance and meet a practical 44px touch target.
- Keyboard access, visible focus and reduced-motion behaviour are required.
- Mobile preserves the same decision and evidence meaning rather than becoming a clipped desktop dashboard.
- No external send, real upload, account creation or production write.

## Feasibility boundary

The proof may use deterministic fixture data and local state. It may simulate a transcript arrival, a private query, intervention preparation, evidence inspection and customer preview. It must not imply production ingestion, model inference, persistence or live listening has been built. The selected concept must be expressible later through bounded schema-validated model output inside a deterministic renderer.

## Concept-distance requirement

Produce at least three concept spines. Every pair must differ on at least two of:

- sequencing;
- user agency;
- primary interaction;
- information structure;
- state model.

Palette, typography, copy and component skin do not count as conceptual distance.

## Judge rubric

Each judge must score and explain:

1. ten-second comprehension;
2. intervention quality and causal traceability;
3. personal growth versus work-dashboard balance;
4. usefulness to Krish during the thirty-day proof;
5. cognitive restraint and absence of administrative burden;
6. truth, authority and audience integrity;
7. participant-like warmth without personhood theatre;
8. mobile and state-range viability;
9. visual signature and ability to surpass the approved R2 standard;
10. feasibility with the current Brain kernel.

The judge must also identify hard-constraint failures, disguised convergence, the strongest rejected feature and the most consequential residual risk.

## Authority and proof

Authorised: local concept records, synthetic fixture, self-contained functional proof, local rendering, screenshots, deterministic checks, Git state and branch push.

Separately gated: real accounts, production data or schema, Supabase mutation, customer repository creation, main-branch merge, deployment, publication and release.

The surface passes only after:

- three concept spines and pairwise-distance evidence exist;
- two independent blinded judges agree or a blinded tiebreaker resolves material disagreement;
- synthesis feasibility is checked against the current repository and Brain contracts;
- the current artifact renders at 390 x 844 and 1440 x 900 with representative and fallback states;
- no console error, horizontal overflow, clipped primary action or inaccessible essential path remains;
- every visible evidence-backed claim resolves to the fixture;
- Krish gives an unanchored reaction to the rendered artifact.
