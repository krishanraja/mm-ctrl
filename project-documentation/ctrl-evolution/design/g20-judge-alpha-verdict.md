## Decision

**Winner: Candidate 7, with 95/100.**

Candidate 7 is the strongest complete interaction spine. Its fixed customer plaque, single moving tray and retained receipt make the three critical facts legible without explanation: whose Brain is active, what is crossing, and what remains in CTRL. It preserves the fastest ordinary paste path, proposes a useful decision-bound context bundle before asking for edits, and gives the returned Claude result a visibly different trust state.

Candidate 3 is the strongest source for selective-context legibility, but it risks turning a frequent handoff into visual context administration. Candidate 9 has the strongest custody language and resumable history, but it adds the most process and comes closest to a stepper wrapped around an otherwise conventional transfer flow.

### Candidate 7

**Perceived speed and low cognitive load, 18/20.** Sections 2 and 3 make ordinary paste immediate: "Paste is the default and works anywhere in the field" and "No submit button follows a normal paste." The current decision supplies a prepared manifest, while source editing is secondary. The deduction is for the possible ceremony of tray movement, a separate prepare action and a later handoff action. The concept names that risk honestly in section 14.

**Context and audience safety, 20/20.** Sections 1, 3 and 4 keep the Maya plaque and audience outside the moving tray, show exact selected references and an explicit exclusion before handoff, and close the boundary on customer mismatch or expiry. The retained receipt is especially strong because the proof of what stayed in CTRL remains visible after the tray moves.

**Usefulness inside Claude, 15/15.** State 2 includes the consequential decision, current supported read, open question, at least three sources and a named purpose. The task starter is small and the connector supplies the governed context. This is useful work context rather than a dump.

**Provenance-preserving return loop, 15/15.** State 5 keeps the whole return as "Claude suggestion", links it to the outbound purpose and context version, distinguishes four provenance classes, and prevents a Brain change from this surface.

**Fit with Decision Bench and Living Brain, 9/10.** Sections 4 and 10 keep the Decision Bench as origin and meaning owner and route source inspection through the existing Living Brain. One point is withheld because the added reference strip and three-region exchange instrument could visually compete with the approved Bench unless the render is tightly integrated.

**Nontechnical delight and emotional ease, 9/10.** A pass-through tray and retained receipt are concrete, calm and understandable. The fixed plaque solves identity without security language. The deduction is the risk that "Move tray", paper receipts and boundary motion become themed decoration or confirmation theatre.

**Responsive and technical feasibility, 9/10.** The state machine maps cleanly to React view state, existing dialogs, browser paste and clipboard APIs, deterministic fixture branches and a read-only resource. The mobile written sequence is credible. One point is withheld for the desktop three-region density and optional voice/Open Claude capability branches that must be rendered honestly.

## Rejected G14 repetition and invariant regression

### Disguised repetition of rejected G14 work

**No candidate is a disguised repetition of the rejected G14 Intervention Lens page structure on the supplied evidence.** None uses a long editorial page, oversized announcement titles, self-awarding copy or system-first theatre. All retain the approved no-scroll Decision Bench as the working origin.

Candidate 3 has the closest naming and spatial resemblance to the old Intervention Lens because it places a focal opening over the Living Brain. That resemblance does not make it the same spine. Its governing action is selecting what crosses an external context boundary, not presenting an intervention through a long editorial lens. The risk becomes real only if the render turns the aperture into a dramatic hero device or lets it displace decision work.

The supplied evidence names Decision Cockpit and Decision Watershed as rejected alternatives but does not describe their mechanics. No repetition of those concepts can be substantiated from this frozen review bundle, and none is apparent from the candidate descriptions.

### Invariant regressions

**No material invariant is intentionally regressed by the three concept documents.** Each preserves private staging, explicit customer identity, minimum purpose-bound handoff, read-only first connector, small task starter, explicit return and non-memory status.

Three latent contradictions must be closed in synthesis:

1. Candidate 7 says operator-private material is unavailable in normal selection, but its failure table also imagines an operator-private item "selected by mistake". The render must not make such an item selectable. The negative state should be injected at the data boundary and fail closed before a manifest can complete.
2. Candidate 3's wrong-customer row permits either "Return to Maya" or "Keep with active customer" depending on state. Identity collision must never be resolved by silently relabelling already associated material. Any re-routing choice is allowed only before durable association, with both identities visible and one explicit user decision.
3. Candidate 9's selected-context download is contractually allowed only as a visible manual export. It must not replace the small task-starter fallback by default or occur without previewing the exact exported contents.

## Historical strengths synthesis should restore

The rejected Intervention Lens retained useful provenance, correction, customer-preview and private-boundary strengths. The approved Decision Bench added current-versus-history comparison, exact evidence inspection, focused Living Brain evidence, uncertainty and next-session preparation. The candidates preserve much of the private boundary and evidence inspection, but all under-specify three carry-forwards:

1. **Correction history.** The retained capture and return receipt should link to correction/source history when it exists. It should not add a review workflow to G20, but the receipt must not become an evidence dead end.
2. **Current versus historical status.** Source inspection should preserve whether an interpretation is current, historical or rejected. Historical material may be inspectable but must not silently enter the default Claude selection or guide action.
3. **Customer-safe projection.** The first proof remains operator-private, but the receipt should state the customer-visibility ceiling and preserve a later customer-safe projection boundary. Do not add a customer-preview screen to this vertical slice.

The synthesis should also keep the Living Brain visually distinctive as evidence for why a source is included, while preventing it from becoming graph theatre or a second selection tool.

## Forbidden hybridization

- Do not show a tray, aperture and thread as simultaneous metaphors. The Exchange Tray remains the sole governing object.
- Do not add Candidate 9's persistent custody line or a row of past stages. That would make the surface feel like a stepper and duplicate the tray's state signal.
- Do not add Candidate 9's mandatory job-choice state. The Decision Bench should propose the named purpose, with one short edit route only when needed.
- Do not make Candidate 3's item-by-item aperture adjustment the primary path. Exact inclusion and exclusion must be inspectable, but the default is a useful deterministic cut that usually needs one review and one action.
- Do not turn the aperture's linework into a large focal spectacle, announcement title or decorative lens. That would reopen the rejected G14 failure mechanism.
- Do not surface manual full-content export beside the normal small task starter. Manual export remains an inspected emergency fallback.
- Do not add customer preview, correction workflow or trusted-memory approval to this first surface. Preserve their boundaries and routes without expanding the slice.

## Feasibility and data concerns

The synthetic React proof is feasible. Production readiness is not established and remains outside this verdict.

1. **Fixture completeness:** the proof needs at least three exact source records, one operator-private exclusion, source spans or stable references, current-versus-historical status, audience ceilings, freshness, expiry and the four provenance roles. If these do not exist in the current fixture, the render must fail visibly rather than invent them.
2. **Selection truth:** "smallest useful context" cannot be claimed from an untested retrieval model. For this proof, the selection must be deterministic and fixture-backed. The user can judge the interaction, not the quality of context compilation.
3. **Capsule substrate:** the current MCP substrate is migration material, not proof that purpose-bound capsules, revocation, source-version checks or audience-intersection enforcement exist. The local proof may simulate these states but must label them as synthetic. Production needs server-side tenancy checks and negative access tests.
4. **Identity closure:** the UI needs both the active workspace identity and the identity frozen into the tray. Changing customer must invalidate the handoff rather than mutate its label.
5. **Clipboard and Claude launch:** browser clipboard permission and Claude availability vary. Copy must have an inline text-selection recovery. Open Claude must be absent unless it is genuinely operable, and no prompt submission may be implied.
6. **Voice and files:** MediaRecorder availability does not prove transcription. Save audio with an honest receipt or render voice unavailable. File and URL flows must distinguish preservation of the original from successful extraction or fetching.
7. **No-scroll density:** the winner's fixed identity band, Decision Bench reference, exact manifest, exclusion, receipt and action must be tested together at 1440 x 900. Long source content belongs in deliberate inspection with natural page movement, not a nested scroller.
8. **Return attribution:** linking a pasted result to the outbound context version is reliable only when the task or capsule reference survives. When it does not, the result must remain private and ask one provenance question before being labelled Claude-authored.

## Owner call

Proceed with Candidate 7 as the sole rendered synthesis spine. Borrow only the bounded safety labels and receipt semantics listed above. The render should be killed if the tray adds ceremony to ordinary paste, if exact inclusion and exclusion are not legible beside the handoff action, or if a cold viewer cannot identify the active customer, what Claude receives, what stays private and why the returned result is not Brain memory.
