# G14 Concept B: The Living Portrait Console

**Generator:** root design producer, isolated concept pass B

**Status:** unselected concept spine

## Governing metaphor

The customer's Living Brain is the room. New evidence changes its shape, and the operator works by noticing and entering the most consequential movement rather than reading a list of recommendations.

## Sequence

1. Open on a spatial portrait of the customer with one movement visibly active.
2. Select the movement to see what changed, which evidence caused it and whether it is durable or staged.
3. Follow the active movement into one suggested customer intervention.
4. Explore adjacent tensions, standards and gaps only when useful.
5. Preview the customer's view of the same underlying Brain.

## User agency

Krish chooses which movement to enter. The system marks one as most consequential but does not collapse the experience to a single answer. Exploration can reveal a different route, and the intervention follows the selected meaning.

## Primary interaction

Navigate a semantically constrained living map. Movement, not task status, is the organizing primitive. Selecting a node or change opens a local action at the point of meaning.

## Information structure

- Central Living Brain field with typed clusters and relationships.
- Active change halo showing one newly strengthened, weakened or contradicted meaning.
- Contextual intervention attached directly to that movement.
- Evidence and audience inspector.
- Customer-view lens over the same canonical items.
- Quiet outer ring for gaps and hypotheses not ready to steer.

## State model

Graph-projection state. Canonical items remain stable while evidence changes standing, confidence and relationships. The UI animates a deterministic before/after projection and preserves a version history.

Sparse state renders a small intentional constellation. Quiet state presents stable structure with no highlighted movement. Error state preserves the last verified projection and marks it stale.

## Visual signature

A darker, denser evolution of the approved R2 Living Brain. Fine orbital geometry and line icons act as instruments. One amber change pulse travels across a typed relationship into a mint intervention mark. The customer-view lens shifts audience visibility without switching to a separate dashboard.

## Strength

Strongest continuity with the approved customer experience, most visually ownable and most likely to make the Brain feel alive and impressive.

## Risk

The operator may have to explore before knowing what to do. The map can become a beautiful gimmick, suggest false semantics through space or bury the intervention under visual fascination.

## Feasibility

Medium-high. Existing G13 item, relationship, source, audience and correction contracts support the projection. A new change-event and intervention overlay are required, but no new semantic edge type is necessary.
