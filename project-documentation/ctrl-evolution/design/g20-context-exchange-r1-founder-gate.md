## Verdict

R1 fails in substance. Its visual craft cannot compensate for the absence of personal intelligence, consequential guidance and a useful result. The surface makes context transport the product when context transport should be nearly invisible infrastructure.

The Exchange Tray is retired as the governing hero. Its useful custody and audience mechanics may survive behind the interaction, but the next proof must not be a revised tray, receipt or connector explainer.

## Classification

- **Product rule:** failed. The proof centres moving context rather than helping Maya become more capable at a consequential decision.
- **Interaction:** failed. The interface does not guide a nontechnical person towards a valuable next move. Its left navigation describes internal abstractions and behaves as theatre.
- **Intelligence:** failed. The visible state contains too little specific synthesis, productive challenge, remembered judgement or evidence-backed personal recognition.
- **Data truth:** failed. The preview copied an opaque capsule reference into Claude without a connected context service. The resulting prompt could not resolve its reference and therefore could not produce the promised grounded answer.
- **Information structure:** failed. Repeated custody and safety explanations consume the mobile page while decision-relevant context remains sparse.
- **Visual semantics:** failed. Flashy visual material carries no inspectable meaning. Space is used for atmosphere instead of understanding.
- **Copy:** failed. The interface repeats one trust point in several forms and sounds like product documentation rather than a thoughtful partner.
- **Personal value:** failed. Nothing on the reviewed path credibly helps Maya improve her judgement, taste, standards, agency or AI fluency.

## Durable correction

Capture, custody, audience limits and Claude transport become quiet utilities. The visible product begins with what Maya's Brain understands, why that understanding matters now, where it may be wrong, and the consequential decision it can help her think through.

The Claude bridge has two jobs:

1. send a complete, useful and inspectable Brain brief that can stand alone when no connector is installed; and
2. judge returned work against Maya's evidence, taste and standards before offering it back as a proposed source.

An opaque reference without a working resolver is forbidden in any user-testable proof.

## Reset requirements

The next G20 proof must satisfy all of these:

1. A twelve-year-old can explain within five seconds what CTRL knows about Maya and how it helps her with one large decision.
2. The opening contains at least three specific, evidence-linked recognitions drawn from a rich synthetic Brain, including one tension, one personal pattern and one important unknown.
3. The experience makes Maya more capable. It does not merely store, transfer, summarise or retrieve information.
4. Claude receives either a working purpose-bound connection or a complete self-contained synthetic brief. The proof cannot depend on an unavailable resolver.
5. Returned AI work is assessed against Maya's own standards and the supplied evidence. Generic ideas, missing numbers, unsupported assumptions and voice drift are made visible.
6. Privacy and custody are explained once, at the consequential action. Repeated labels, receipt theatre and technical status prose are prohibited.
7. Every navigation label names a real destination in plain language and every active-looking control works.
8. Every diagram, image, icon and motion mark must encode meaning that a text label can state. Decorative generated imagery is prohibited.
9. Mobile space belongs first to the current recognition, decision and next useful action. No right-hand or lower panel may exist merely to balance a desktop composition.
10. The Brain must reflect the complete founder intake: consequential AI-transition decisions, judgement, taste, standards, evidence, personal becoming, productive challenge and sovereign human accountability.
