# G14 Concept C: The Session Rehearsal

**Generator:** root design producer, isolated concept pass C

**Status:** unselected concept spine

## Governing metaphor

A private rehearsal with an unusually prepared thinking partner. The product does not ask Krish to manage the Brain. It places him at the consequential moment in the next customer conversation and lets him test how to open it.

## Sequence

1. Open with a short description of the moment likely to matter next.
2. The Brain plays the customer tension back in one sentence and proposes an opening move.
3. Krish chooses to hear the likely response, challenge the premise, or ask something else.
4. Evidence and uncertainty appear inline only when a claim changes the rehearsal.
5. The session card is left ready with the opening, contrast, listening signal and fallback.

## User agency

High and conversational. Krish can redirect at every turn, provide a tiny update and ask the Brain to rehearse an unexpected path. The system maintains one quiet next-best question but does not interrupt unless requested or materially necessary.

## Primary interaction

Conversational branching around a staged future moment. Taps choose response modes; voice or typing supplies optional context. The output is a prepared human conversation, not a chat transcript.

## Information structure

- Opening scene: what is likely to happen and why it matters.
- Three response branches: run it, pressure-test it, or redirect.
- Inline evidence receipts attached to exact claims.
- Session card that accumulates only accepted preparation.
- Post-session return point for transcript or voice summary.
- Customer benefit preview showing what the conversation could unlock.

## State model

Branching rehearsal state. `premise -> opening -> anticipated response -> intervention branch -> prepared card`. New context can invalidate the premise and regenerate the branch without changing durable Brain truth.

Sparse state rehearses a bounded discovery question rather than a confident intervention. Quiet state offers private ask only. Stale state warns that the rehearsal uses older evidence and disables implied certainty.

## Visual signature

A near-empty dark stage with one line of conversation at a time. Evidence appears as restrained marginalia. Accepted preparation gathers into a physical session card at the edge of the field. Motion is paced like breath and turn-taking, with no chat bubbles.

## Strength

Most participant-like and closest to Krish's real state of use. It makes nonlinear session assistance understandable without requiring ambient listening or a work-tool integration.

## Risk

It can read as a generic chatbot, hide the richness of the Living Brain and make systematic change harder to scan. Rehearsed customer responses may also imply prediction the evidence cannot support.

## Feasibility

Medium. The branching interaction is locally straightforward, but the product contract must forbid presenting generated customer responses as predictions. The session card and claim-linked evidence remain bounded and deterministic.
