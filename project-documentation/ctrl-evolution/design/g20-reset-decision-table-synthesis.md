Both require the personal Brain to alter the causal interpretation of each route. ## Governing rule

The Brain changes how Maya sees the decision before it helps her generate anything.

The proof opens on one strategic fork and three genuinely different operating routes. Maya's patterns, standards, corrections and unknowns are not profile content beside the decision. They are causal inputs that strengthen, weaken or qualify each route.

## First frame contract

The first wide and mobile frame contains, before any primary action:

1. the decision: how far Aperture House should rebuild marketing around AI now;
2. the synthetic stakes: twelve months, GBP 1.2 million operating budget and eight affected roles;
3. Maya's current view;
4. the Brain's direct read: the unproved quality system is a larger risk than speed alone;
5. three source-linked recognitions:
   - Maya becomes precise through concrete comparison;
   - she protects surprise from becoming a formula; and
   - it is still unknown whether another leader can apply her standard to unfamiliar work without final rescue;
6. the live counter-case: current volume incentives may be the stronger cause; and
7. three route names with the working route visibly selected.

The viewer should be able to say within five seconds: Maya is deciding how far to rebuild marketing around AI; the risk is changing the structure before her quality standard can travel; the Brain wants her to prove that before committing.

## Route model

### Rebuild now

- Causal bet: structural speed creates the missing learning.
- Personal effect: fits Maya's appetite to move, but risks automating output before her standard can travel.
- Evidence: high routine-work share and generic AI output support structural change; weak judgement-transfer evidence opposes immediate scale.

### Prove the new system first

- Causal bet: one separate campaign can test customer value, judgement transfer, role design and incentives together.
- Personal effect: uses Maya's strongest mode, concrete comparison, while protecting surprise and final accountability.
- Evidence: customer recall, work audit, scorecard and corrected delegation interpretation support it. The existing process may absorb the pilot unless its measures and authority are separate.

### Add tools gradually

- Causal bet: distributed adoption can improve the current function without a structural break.
- Personal effect: conflicts with Maya's belief that the current work and incentives are the problem, not only tool access.
- Evidence: lower disruption supports it. Current scorecard, workshop indecision and strategy draft oppose it.

No score or evidence weight appears. Every route uses named support, opposition, standing and unknowns. `Another route` remains available so the three fixtures do not imply completeness.

## Primary progression

The one primary action is `Design the test`.

The test uses one strategically important campaign and compares a separate AI-native operating model with the current process. It must resolve four unknowns together:

- customer usefulness;
- judgement transfer;
- future role responsibilities; and
- incentives and measures.

Maya may edit the success evidence or kill condition with one short line or voice. The action remains her decision, not a Brain prescription.

## Claude continuation

`Build the Claude brief` opens a preview organised by the same objects Maya has already seen:

- decision and desired output;
- Maya's current view;
- Brain synthesis and counter-case;
- personal patterns and standards;
- route evidence;
- important unknowns;
- forbidden assumptions; and
- required output shape.

The static proof copies the entire self-contained synthetic brief. It contains no opaque reference and needs no connector. At this action only, the interface states: `Copies a complete private synthetic brief. Nothing is sent automatically.`

The exact clipboard body receives a deterministic browser test for the decision, evidence, standards, unknowns, forbidden assumptions and output shape.

## Return assessment

The proof accepts pasted work or loads the fixture's deliberately generic Claude proposal. The result remains one plan attached to the selected route. Exact phrases receive five evidence-bound findings:

1. `AI-first marketing transformation` is generic and does not choose a causal operating model.
2. `automate content creation` treats production efficiency as the goal despite contrary Brain evidence.
3. `track engagement and output` repeats the incentive problem.
4. `human-in-the-loop` does not name who owns final release judgement.
5. `staged rollout` is worth keeping because it fits the board's staged-commitment constraint.

The visible verdict is `Do not use this plan yet.` The Brain shows the exact missing customer proof, budget gate, judgement-transfer test and kill condition, then offers one sharper instruction. Maya decides whether to repair, reject or keep the additive fragment.

At return only, the interface states: `Claude proposal, not Brain truth.`

## Visual semantics

- The decision table is one continuous physical surface, not three floating cards.
- Each route shares a common baseline. Support and opposition marks attach directly to the route they affect.
- Mint means supported direction or useful material.
- Amber means a live tension, changed belief or unresolved unknown.
- A crossed line means an interpretation was corrected and no longer steers the decision.
- Source, direct words, observed work and correction marks use distinct labelled instrument shapes.
- No generated image, neural graphic, decorative graph, sparkle or balancing panel appears.
- No permanent left rail appears in the focused proof.

## Responsive model

Desktop at 1440 by 900 shows the complete decision, three route names, selected route detail, current insight and `Design the test` without page scroll.

Mobile at 390 by 844 and 320 by 568 shows the current insight, decision, three route names, current selection and primary action before supporting detail. The three route names use a compact segmented selector with labelled buttons. No horizontal drag or hidden carousel is required.

Supporting evidence follows in natural page flow. There is no fixed lower panel and no component scrollbar.

## Quiet utility model

`Add evidence` remains a working secondary action in the header. It accepts pasted synthetic material into a simple private draft state and returns to the exact decision. It does not explain storage architecture.

Source inspection and the Living Brain remain subordinate working disclosures from exact claims. No vague navigation item or active-looking dead control is permitted.

## Honest state harness

The rendered proof includes a hidden test-state menu for rich, sparse, contradictory, corrected, stale, slow, wrong-customer, offline-Claude, long-name and reduced-motion states.

- Sparse uses one known pattern, one unknown and one useful comparison.
- Contradiction shows competing evidence without a recommendation.
- Corrected visibly retires the old delegation interpretation.
- Stale keeps evidence visible but removes it from the current steer.
- Slow preserves the full brief and capture input.
- Wrong customer fails closed before copy.
- Offline Claude keeps the complete copied brief available.
- Long name and narrow mobile reflow without clipping or horizontal overflow.
- Reduced motion removes animated movement without removing meaning.

## Feasibility and authority

The R2 proof uses only deterministic synthetic browser state. It can prove comprehension, interaction, clipboard completeness, responsive behaviour and return-assessment specificity. It cannot prove diagnostic accuracy, customer value, production context compilation, connector behaviour, persistence, encryption, expiry or correction propagation.

One static R2 artifact, local browser verification, rendered inspection, protected preview, Git durability and cold founder presentation are authorised by the existing material-surface loop. React implementation, database writes, production connectors, customer data, schema changes, public navigation and main merge remain gated.
