Read `g23-product-spine-sanitized-brief.md` first. This arm adds only the missing conceptual territory identified after the first round failed diversity.

The concept may not use a proof, page, sheet, document, file, case, margin, edition, revision trail, timeline, dashboard or graph as its organising object. Do not use an earlier-versus-later page comparison.

Deliver the ten sections required by the sanitized brief and explain how this arm differs on sequencing, user agency, primary interaction, information structure and state model.
