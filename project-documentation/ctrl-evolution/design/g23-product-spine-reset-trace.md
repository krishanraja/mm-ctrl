## Selection and surgery

The [synthesis](g23-product-spine-synthesis.md) uses cross-decision transfer as the smallest act capable of explaining the larger product. It borrows explicit authority from Arm A and an honest capable-general-AI contrast from Arm C.

The render makes these corrections required by both judges:

- states the company-funded value and personal mechanism together;
- makes the thirty-day relationship explicit;
- separates what the Brain prepares, Krish challenges and the leader decides;
- shows one accepted judgement informing a different consequential decision;
- keeps the Decision Table, capture, Living Brain and portable release subordinate;
- shows leader change, not merely better task output; and
- asks one exact product-spine approval question.

## Constraint regressions prevented

- no single synthetic case presented as the whole product;
- no e-learning or micro-improvement framing;

- no weak general-AI straw man;
- no claim that the Brain decides, promotes its own inference or silently carries rejected material;
- no technical repository or database story in the first read; and
- no production data, database, email, account, merge or release action.
