# G20 reset concept Ivory: The Counsel

**Generator:** local isolated concept pass I
**Brief:** `g20-reset-sanitized-brief.md` revision G20-RESET-SB-R1
**Status:** candidate only

## Governing metaphor

A prepared private counsel, not a dashboard. The Brain meets Maya with one considered read of the decision, the evidence that earned it and the exact part only she can decide. It behaves like a thoughtful partner who has read everything before the meeting.

## Sequence

### 1. Begin with recognition

The first frame shows Maya's provisional view in her own words, then one direct Brain read:

`You are ready to redesign marketing. The part you have not proved is whether your standard can travel without you.`

Three short recognitions sit beneath it:

- you diagnose weak work faster through comparison;
- you protect surprise from becoming a formula; and
- your earlier reluctance-to-delegate interpretation was wrong and has been corrected.

The only primary action is `Pressure-test this read`. A quieter `That is not quite me` opens correction.

### 2. Reveal the case

The next frame is a compact counsel sheet with four named blocks:

- `What the evidence says`
- `What it does not prove`
- `The strongest counter-case`
- `What only Maya can decide`

Each block links to human-readable sources. No ID is visible until source inspection.

### 3. Make one call

The Brain recommends a contained campaign pilot before role redesign. Maya can choose:

- `Use this as my working route`
- `Argue for rebuilding now`
- `I need a different route`

Her choice updates the visible working view but never claims a final decision.

### 4. Continue elsewhere

`Work this through in Claude` opens an inspectable brief with the decision, Maya's view, Brain read, standards, evidence, unknowns and requested output. The default proof action copies the full self-contained brief. A future working connector can replace the transport without changing the surface.

### 5. Return to Maya's standard

Pasted Claude work appears as a new proposal below the counsel, not as memory. The Brain places four short annotations beside exact phrases:

- generic;
- unsupported;
- conflicts with Maya's standard; and
- worth keeping.

The counsel then offers one revised instruction and asks Maya whether the criticism is fair. Her correction improves the working standard rather than silently accepting the output.

## User agency

Maya begins from her own provisional view, can challenge the Brain before seeing its recommendation and can correct the read at any point. The Brain holds a position but never turns it into an answer on her behalf.

## Information structure

One vertical argument: Maya's view, Brain read, evidence boundary, recommendation, external continuation, return critique. Deep evidence opens beside the exact sentence it supports. There is no permanent navigation rail in the focused flow.

## Visual language

The counsel is a warm paper object on deep ink. A thin mint rule grows only as evidence becomes supported. Amber appears only on changed beliefs, unresolved assumptions and returned AI material. Three small instrument marks represent direct words, observed work and external evidence. No decorative illustration appears.

On desktop the evidence opens in a narrow adjacent column. On mobile it opens directly beneath the sentence as a temporary disclosure and closes back to the same reading position.

## State model

`provisional view -> supported read -> challenged read -> working route -> external proposal -> assessed proposal`

Correction creates a new version of the read. Sparse state becomes one known pattern, one unknown and one useful contrast, never an empty counsel.

## Why it may work

- The first useful thing is a personal recognition, not interface chrome.
- The argument makes uncertainty and productive challenge visible.
- The Claude bridge is subordinate to an already valuable Brain read.
- The return assessment demonstrates taste preservation rather than generic retrieval.

## Main risks

- The counsel could become too verbal and repeat the same pompous editorial tendency already rejected elsewhere.
- A single recommendation may feel narrower than the scale of the decision.
- The linear sequence may underuse desktop space and make comparison harder.

## Kill conditions

- Maya has to read a paragraph before recognising herself.
- The recommendation appears before her provisional view.
- Evidence becomes a footnote warehouse rather than an inspectable reason.
- Claude receives anything other than a complete working brief in the proof.
- The Brain critique cannot point to exact words in the returned work.
