# G23 product spine round 1 diversity verdict

**Revision:** R1

**Date:** 11 September 2026

**Gate:** Failed

**Evidence boundary:** G23 sanitized brief R1, G23 Concepts A, B and C, and the complete G22 whole-product golden-path R1 founder failure record. No later G23 work or other judge verdict was read.

## Call

Reject the set before founder review. Do not select a winner and do not synthesize these candidates.

This is disguised repetition. All three candidates are the same product spine: a central proof document, evidence and challenge margins, a leader sign-off, dated revisions, provenance reveals, and an earlier-versus-later comparison. The differences are labels, edition counts and secondary reveal controls. They do not change the governing interaction.

The diversity threshold requires every pair to differ on at least two load-bearing axes. No pair does.

## Disguised repetition decision

**Yes. Blocking fail.**

The convergence is unusually strong:

1. A and B use the same concept name, `The Working Proof`.
2. All three make a proof the persistent product object.
3. All three place the Brain in evidence or revision marks, the operator in a margin and the leader in the centre or final mark.
4. All three explain thirty days as editions or revisions of that proof.
5. All three use the same compounding demonstration: an earlier accepted item changes a later line.
6. All three name the same strongest risk: the product may look like document review or editing software.

That shared risk is diagnostic. It is not three independent concepts encountering the same edge case. It is one concept written three times.

The central regression is simple: G22 failed because one coherent case workflow was still not the product. Round 1 replaced the case file with a proof sheet while retaining the same sequence, same causal demonstration and same approval pattern. The label changed. The product spine did not.

## Missing conceptual territory

Round 2 must explore a **relationship-first, multi-decision spine in which the persistent object is neither a case nor a document**.

The missing idea is the thirty-day working relationship itself: how the Brain, expert operator and leader repeatedly divide labour across different high-consequence decisions, how a personal judgement earned in one arena sharpens another, and why that relationship produces something a general LLM or folder of notes cannot.

Round 2 concepts must not use any of these as their governing object:

- proof;
- page;
- sheet;
- document;
- file;
- case;
- margin;
- edition;
- revision trail; or
- timeline.

Those mechanics may appear as subordinate evidence later. They cannot organise the artifact.

## Required round 2 exploration arms

Use three deliberately different arms so the next set cannot converge by wording alone.

### 1. Relationship choreography

Make the visible object the division of work between leader, operator and Brain. Sequence by changing responsibility and authority, not by document revision. The primary interaction should reveal who prepares, who challenges, who decides and what remains human at a consequential moment. State should be governed by earned interruption and decision authority.

### 2. Cross-decision compounding

Make the visible object the leader's growing decision practice across category, organisation, people and own-role questions. Do not arrange it as a dashboard, graph or dated journey. The primary interaction should show one accepted judgement becoming useful in a materially different later decision. State should be governed by relevance, permission, contradiction and reuse across arenas.

### 3. Counterfactual value

Make the visible object the difference between ordinary AI assistance and the Brain plus expert relationship. The primary interaction should expose what generic context misses, what the operator challenges, what the leader changes and why later preparation becomes specific. It must demonstrate the distinction through one compact comparison, not a pitch deck or feature list. State should be governed by the quality and authority of preparation, not by document versions.

These arms are territories, not prescribed layouts. Their candidates still need independent metaphors and must remain blind to one another.

## Round 2 distance requirements

Before any founder review:

1. Score every pair on sequencing, user agency, primary interaction, information structure and state model.
2. Require at least two material differences for every pair.
3. Reject any candidate whose persistent object is a renamed proof, case or decision record.
4. Reject any set where all candidates use the same role placement or earlier-versus-later reveal.
5. Keep source standing, correction, human accountability, mobile simplicity and separate ownership as invariants. These are product rules, not concept spines.
6. Ask whether a viewer can explain the relationship, paid outcome and whole product without discussing a page, workflow or interface.

## Final gate decision

**Round 1 diversity: fail.**

No candidate advances. Run one additional blinded round targeted at the missing relationship-first, multi-decision territory. If that round again converges on a proof document or case workflow, stop. The blocker would then be the brief, generator framing or unresolved product model, not a need for more visual refinement.
