# G15 concept A: The Focus Plane

**Revision:** A1

**Territory:** Spatial and perceptual experience

**Status:** Independent concept spine for later judging. Not rendered, selected, approved or authorised for production.

## Design task contract

- **State of use:** Maya opens her Brain on a 390 by 844 phone between meetings or on a 1440 by 900 desktop during a reflective review. She has seconds of attention before deciding whether to inspect further.
- **User and action:** Maya must recognise one meaningful change in her judgement, understand its evidence and limit, and add one low-cost contrast that could make the interpretation more transferable.
- **Governing rule:** The surface may describe resolution only inside one subject-approved judgement territory. It may never imply personal completeness, deficiency, intelligence, business performance or autonomous decision authority.
- **Data truth:** Every visible statement comes from the frozen synthetic contract in this document. Missing evidence stays visibly untested. A lower-confidence correction may represent progress when it removes a false certainty.
- **Materiality:** New interaction, information and state model. The concept requires independent judging and a rendered founder gate before implementation.
- **Brand and voice:** Deep ink ground, warm cream reading surfaces, mint only for supported current meaning and amber only for change. Editorial, physical and instrument-like. No decorative neural imagery, gradients, glass, mascot, scores or guff.
- **Authority:** Concept document only. No render, model call, production write, customer score, external claim, deployment or release.
- **Proof expectation:** A later proof must cover the exact representative, sparse, correction, loading, stale, error, reduced-motion, long-content and out-of-scope states at both required viewports.

## 1. Governing metaphor

The Brain is a **focus plane**. One named judgement territory occupies a fixed reading window. Evidence does not form a graph or accumulate into a meter. Instead, four evidence roles sit at fixed edges of the field:

- **Chosen** at the left edge;
- **Rejected** at the right edge;
- **Unfamiliar** above the focus;
- **Corrected** below the focus.

Their positions communicate role only. Distance, size, quantity and motion carry no claim about strength. Each role is written in full beside its mark, and screen-reader order states the same relationship linearly.

At the centre is the current plain-language interpretation and its honest limit. Tapping an evidence mark brings that one plane into focus around the unchanged central meaning. The user perceives how the read holds across contrast, unfamiliar work and correction without seeing an evaluation console.

The first field is **Delegation**, because that is where Maya's Brain has become more honest. The visual change is not a progress fill. An amber former reading moves out of the central plane while the narrower current reading settles into cream. Mint appears only on the exact evidence statements that support the narrower meaning.

The concept's central sentence is:

> The issue is not willingness to delegate. Maya's quality criteria are not yet transferable.

Directly beneath it:

> Forming. This is clearer than the earlier read, but unfamiliar work still exposes gaps.

## 2. Exact first 30 seconds

### 0 to 5 seconds: recognition

The first frame contains Maya's name, the synthetic disclosure, the territory name **Delegation**, and one large statement:

> This became more honest.
>
> You are not resisting delegation. Your quality criteria are not yet transferable.

An amber line beneath it reads **Earlier read weakened: Maya resists delegation**. The one primary action is **See what changed**. No territory list, percentage, graph, count or navigation menu competes with the statement.

### 5 to 12 seconds: perceive the difference

On tap, the focus window remains still while four labelled evidence marks arrive at their fixed edges. The **Corrected** mark receives focus first and opens one sentence:

> You corrected the old reading after showing that you delegate readily when the quality boundary is concrete.

The former reading stays visible as history, never as a current claim. The verdict appears beside the selected mark:

> Less certainty, better fit.

The next action is **Focus unfamiliar work**.

### 12 to 20 seconds: ground progress in transfer

The **Unfamiliar** plane replaces the correction detail without moving the central interpretation. It shows holdout `H-D1`:

> The Brain refused to predict whether you would delegate a new launch review because the quality boundary was missing.

The action-point verdict is:

> That refusal was useful. Guessing would have made the Brain look sharper than it was.

The honest limit remains adjacent:

> Transfer is still forming. One unfamiliar contrast is not enough.

### 20 to 27 seconds: inspect the contrast

Maya taps **Chosen** or **Rejected**. One paired example appears as two full statements, not two scores:

- Chosen: **Delegate the first pass when the review boundary and examples are explicit.**
- Rejected: **Hand off a one-line instruction and judge taste only at the end.**

The current read is visibly tied to this contrast and the correction source. **See source** is optional and reveals speaker, proof day, audience and exact assertion on the same page.

### 27 to 30 seconds: make one small contribution

The only primary action becomes **Set one new anchor**. It opens a fresh unfamiliar pair:

> Which route would you trust someone else to take first?

Two complete tap choices are shown. **Say why** offers voice or one sentence of typing after the choice, but the tap is enough to preserve the comparison as provisional evidence. No result, score or celebratory progress state appears. The immediate receipt says:

> Contrast staged. It will not change your Brain until it is checked against the current interpretation.

## 3. User agency and primary interaction

The primary interaction is **tap to focus an evidence role around one stable meaning**.

Maya controls:

1. whether to inspect the change;
2. which evidence role to bring into focus;
3. whether to inspect the exact source and audience;
4. whether to correct the central interpretation;
5. whether to place one new contrast;
6. whether to add a one-sentence reason by voice or typing; and
7. whether an out-of-scope territory remains out of scope.

The system selects the first territory only because the frozen fixture contains a material recent correction there. **Why this opened** states that reason. It never claims that Delegation is Maya's weakest personal trait or the most important part of her identity.

No interaction asks Maya to take a test, review a score, fill categories or manage evidence inventory. There is no drag, pan, zoom or hover dependency. Direct tap, Tab and arrow-key movement all reach the same four evidence roles.

## 4. Information structure

The surface is one field with progressively disclosed layers:

1. **Identity and boundary:** Maya Chen, synthetic fixture, subject-approved territory, private audience and proof day.
2. **Current focus:** territory, current state, change direction, evidence sentence and honest limit. These are the required surface fields expressed in ordinary language.
3. **Evidence compass:** Chosen, Rejected, Unfamiliar and Corrected. The compass is role navigation, not a chart.
4. **Focused evidence plane:** one exact example, holdout or correction with provenance and the verdict beside the action it changes.
5. **Next anchor:** one real contrast that could reduce the current weakest-link uncertainty.
6. **Other territories:** a quiet text route after the main interaction. Creative Direction reads **Transferable in the current synthetic proof**. Capital Allocation reads **Intentionally out of scope**. Neither appears as a comparative score or compulsory gap.

The ordinary surface never displays dimension values, averages, territory ranks, counts, percentages or a total Brain state. Under **How this was checked**, the five dimension names are translated into five questions and the weakest current boundary is named in prose. Technical fixture identifiers remain one further disclosure level down.

## 5. State model

The concept uses perceptual scene state, not progress state.

### Surface truth states

- `forming`: the interpretation has eligible grounding but unfamiliar work still exposes a named weakest-link gap.
- `tested`: frozen unfamiliar work has tested the interpretation, but transfer or adaptation is not yet strong enough for the transferable expression.
- `transferable`: Brain context improves unfamiliar work over both frozen baselines without a blocking weakness in the named territory.
- `revising`: new evidence or correction has invalidated part of the current read; the prior version remains inspectable while repair is incomplete.
- `intentionally_out_of_scope`: the territory is not eligible and contributes neither a penalty nor a silent zero.

### Interaction states

- `arrival`: one change sentence and one action.
- `field_open`: the four evidence roles are available around the stable meaning.
- `role_focused`: one exact evidence object and local verdict are visible.
- `source_open`: provenance, audience, source moment and exact assertion are visible.
- `contrast_staging`: one new chosen or rejected comparison is being captured.
- `correction_draft`: old and proposed meanings remain separate.
- `repairing`: no new current claim is shown until the correction lineage and dependent field state resolve.
- `receipt`: what changed, what weakened, what remains uncertain and what did not change appear at the action point.

### System and boundary states

- `sparse`: one eligible interpretation is shown with its honest limit and one best contrast. Empty compass roles do not appear.
- `quiet`: no territory has materially changed. The surface shows the last verified personal meaning and a quiet route to the Living Brain, with no manufactured prompt.
- `loading`: only the territory frame and synthetic disclosure appear. No evidence mark, state or claim is invented.
- `stale`: the last verified field remains readable with its proof day. New contrast capture is allowed locally, but it stays staged until the current version is restored.
- `recoverable_error`: the last verified field remains, the failed operation is named, input is preserved and Retry sits at the failure point.
- `private_rejected`: no meaning, topology, count or partial label is revealed.
- `reduced_motion`: every plane change becomes an immediate focus and outline change. Meaning never depends on blur, motion or spatial memory.

## 7. Sparse and correction behaviour

### Sparse Brain

Sparse does not look unfinished. The field contains the strongest eligible current interpretation, its source sentence, its honest limit and one evidence role only. The remaining compass roles are absent rather than greyed out. The next action is one specific contrast that can test the named boundary. An out-of-scope territory never appears as an empty slot.

If no territory is eligible, the first frame says:

> Nothing here is ready to carry a Brain Resolution claim.

It then offers one subject-approved territory choice or a quiet exit. It does not generate a weak personal insight to fill the frame.

### Correction

Correction happens at the central meaning, not in settings. **This is not right** opens old and proposed wording in reading order. Maya can narrow the claim, mark the territory out of scope or reject it. The complete new meaning and affected territory state appear before confirmation.

After confirmation:

1. the former meaning stays below the focus as **Corrected**;
2. the current interpretation occupies the same central plane;
3. the relevant state may move from tested to revising or forming without being framed as regression;
4. frozen holdout results remain attached to the Brain version they tested;
5. dependent surface claims rebuild only after repair receipts are complete; and
6. the receipt states exactly what changed, what became less certain and what remains untouched.

If repair fails, the old verified field remains visible, the draft persists and the interface says **Correction recorded, field not rebuilt** with Retry. It never shows the new claim as current while repair is incomplete.

## 8. Why this could create return desire

The return promise is not a larger Brain. It is a better moment of recognition:

> See where your judgement now travels, where it has become more honest and which one contrast could sharpen it next.

Each return opens only when one of three material events exists:

- unfamiliar work changes the named territory state;
- a correction weakens or narrows an interpretation; or
- new contrastive evidence creates a specific next anchor.

If nothing material changed, the quiet state does not manufacture excitement. It preserves the last verified meaning and offers the Living Brain without an interruption. This keeps return desire attached to earned movement rather than streaks, notifications or visual novelty.

The emotional payoff is that Maya sees a subtle truth about her own development: becoming more accurate can mean becoming less certain. The action payoff is equally concrete: she leaves with one contrast that may make a private quality boundary transferable to another person.

## 9. Accessibility and mobile feasibility

### Mobile, 390 by 844

- The first viewport contains identity, synthetic disclosure, territory, the central change statement, honest limit and one 48px primary action.
- Opening the field converts the four spatial roles into a two by two set of 48px labelled targets below the fixed meaning. Only one evidence plane is expanded at a time.
- Long evidence uses natural page height. The page scrolls; no field, panel or source view scrolls independently.
- A sticky return control may appear only after the first field opens and must not obscure content under dynamic browser chrome or a virtual keyboard.
- Voice is optional. Tap and one-sentence typing complete the path.

### Desktop, 1440 by 900

- The same meaning remains central while the four labelled roles occupy its edges.
- More space reveals provenance beside the focused evidence without adding simultaneous territories or metric panels.
- The first sequence and primary action remain identical to mobile.

### Non-visual equivalence

- DOM order is current meaning, honest limit, Corrected, Chosen, Rejected, Unfamiliar, focused evidence, next action.
- Each evidence target announces role, current selection and available action.
- Arrow keys move among evidence roles, Enter focuses, Escape returns to the central meaning and Tab follows normal document order.
- A visible high-contrast focus ring is present on every control.
- Colour, blur, size, location and motion are never the only state cues.
- Reduced motion removes arrival, refocus and correction transitions. A live text status announces the same state change once.
- At 200 percent zoom, spatial placement becomes the same linear reading order rather than shrinking type.

## 10. Commercial expression

The commercial sentence is:

> Make your judgement easier to use, challenge and improve across real work, without reducing you to a score.

For the leader, the value is recognition and growing sovereignty: the Brain can name what their judgement depends on, admit where it does not transfer and preserve correction. For the paying organisation, the value is better transfer of scarce judgement into unfamiliar work, clearer delegation boundaries and more human attention available for customers, strategy and distinctive final craft.

The measurable proof remains below the surface: prospective unfamiliar-work performance with Brain context versus the same-model no-Brain and handcrafted-context baselines, evaluated through the weakest dimension in each eligible territory. No public efficacy claim is allowed until the release gate in the contract passes. The concept sells the felt outcome and shows the evidence boundary; it does not sell a percentage or a digital stand-in.

## 11. Material distance from test and dashboard patterns

This concept is structurally different on all five required axes:

| Axis | The Focus Plane |
|---|---|
| Sequencing | Personal change first, then one evidence role, then one new anchor. No battery of questions or summary result. |
| Agency | Maya decides which evidence role to focus and whether to stage a contrast or correct the meaning. She is not evaluated through a fixed path. |
| Primary interaction | Perceptual refocusing around one stable interpretation. No quiz submission, chart scan, filter or score review. |
| Information structure | One central meaning with four evidence-role edges and one focused plane. No equal cards, table of metrics or multi-territory overview. |
| State model | Scene and truth-state transitions driven by unfamiliar work, correction and scope. No test-complete state or aggregate progress state. |

Three constraints prevent collapse into a generic visualization:

1. Spatial location always names an evidence role and never indicates quality, relationship strength or progress.
2. Only one named territory and one evidence plane may dominate at a time.
3. The field cannot render without a current interpretation, evidence sentence, honest limit and eligible synthetic evidence contract.

## 12. Strongest risk and kill condition

The strongest risk is that optical focus becomes a disguised competence metaphor. A user could read sharper as **I am better** and forming as **I am deficient**, even when the product intends only to describe evidence-grounded performance inside one named territory.

Kill the concept if any of these occur in a five-person cold comprehension test:

1. two or more people describe the field as a completeness score, personality assessment or rating of Maya;
2. two or more people think adding evidence automatically increases resolution;
3. two or more people interpret the correct refusal as failure rather than calibrated judgement;
4. fewer than four people can state the changed interpretation, its evidence and its honest limit after 30 seconds; or
5. a plain text sentence plus evidence disclosure matches the field on comprehension and creates equal or greater stated return desire.

Do not repair a failed focus metaphor with a legend, percentage or explanatory tour. If it fails, abandon the spatial spine and preserve only the exact synthetic contract, correction behaviour and one-next-contrast rule for a different interaction model.

## 13. Concept call

The Focus Plane is worth judging because it makes an abstract weakest-link construct emotionally legible without turning it into a number. Its sharpest bet is that a leader will return to see a more honest relationship between their current judgement and unfamiliar work. Its counterpoint is structural: visual sharpness carries cultural meanings of competence that may be impossible to neutralize. The later proof must test that risk before any visual polish is treated as product progress.
