## Candidate L

**Grade: C, ineligible as written.** It becomes eligible if every generated customer turn is framed and rendered as a disposable rehearsal hypothesis, not a likely response, and the missing truth, audience, correction and fallback states are made explicit.

Candidate L is strong where Krish actually works. Its accepted session card, listening signal, fallback and post-session return point can make preparation immediately useful. It also offers the warmest participant-like behaviour without using a person name or ambient listening.

The blocking issue is predictive semantics. Sequence line 15 offers to hear **the likely response**, while lines 52 and 56 admit that rehearsed responses may imply unsupported prediction and that a future contract still needs to forbid this. The brief requires every claim to resolve to explicit evidence and requires hypotheses to be visibly distinguishable. A control that promises a likely customer response makes the unsupported claim before any later caveat can repair it.

The interaction also approaches the prohibited generic-assistant shape. Line 25 defines conversational branching, and line 44 presents one line of conversation at a time. Removing chat bubbles changes the skin, not the underlying interaction. A bounded rehearsal can be valid, but it cannot become the default container for the cockpit or obscure the supported intervention, change receipt and Living Brain.

Truth coverage is incomplete. Inline evidence and uncertainty are present, but the concept does not specify standing, audience separation, a correction path, an unresolved contradiction, a discarded or weakened interpretation, loading or recoverable error. Mobile, keyboard, focus, target size and reduced-motion behaviour are also unstated. These omissions are insufficient evidence for those rubric items, but the unsupported predictive promise is a hard failure now.

## Candidate M

**Grade: B+, eligible and selected.** It becomes an A if the synthesis restores a visible customer becoming payoff, adds the complete required state and accessibility contract, and makes abstention as concrete as preparation when no move is sufficiently supported.

Candidate M most directly implements the locked hierarchy. Line 13 opens on **one intervention expressed as a decisive sentence**. Line 14 traces it through new evidence, changed interpretation and expected learning. Line 29 keeps one supported intervention as the hero, while ready-now value, one deepening route, a private ask and customer preview remain subordinate. This is the fastest route to the required ten-second answer without becoming a task dashboard.

Agency is adequate and visible. Line 21 lets Krish use, reshape, rotate or reject the move and the interpretation beneath it. This preserves session ownership and avoids praise collection. The event state also fits the locked operating rhythm: evidence arrives, interpretation changes, an intervention is prepared, the session happens and evidence returns.

No hard-constraint failure or invariant regression is identified. Two semantic guardrails are binding. First, **one best move** must mean the sharpest currently supported candidate, not a certainty claim or model-owned verdict. Sparse or contradictory evidence must be allowed to produce one question or deepening route instead of a manufactured intervention. Second, the causal rail's **expected learning** must be visibly prospective and provisional. It cannot look like an evidenced outcome before the session occurs.

The largest weakness is customer benefit. Line 52 admits that the model-selected move may underplay the customer's living personal portrait. Candidate M treats customer preview as a supporting shelf rather than showing how the person is becoming sharper. That is repairable without changing the intervention-first spine.

Candidate M does not yet specify an unresolved contradiction, a visibly weakened or discarded interpretation, loading, recoverable error, audience-specific preview behaviour, mobile reflow, keyboard access, visible focus, 44px targets or reduced motion. Those are binding synthesis gaps, not reasons to replace the spine.

Candidate N's own risk at line 52 is decisive: the intervention can be buried under a beautiful map, and spatial presentation can suggest meaning the data does not support. Missing loading behaviour, the full correction path, mobile fallback navigation and non-spatial access also leave render viability unproven.

## Most consequential residual risk

Candidate M can anchor Krish to a confident model-selected intervention that is plausible, elegant and wrong. Because the entire surface concentrates attention on one move, weak retrieval, a misread transcript or an unresolved contradiction could distort the next human conversation before Krish inspects the evidence. This is a blocking credibility risk, not an additive visual weakness.

The proof must therefore show the exact evidence and changed interpretation beside the preparation decision, label expected learning as provisional, expose the strongest contradiction, offer reshape, rotate and reject, and abstain to one evidence-gathering question when support is insufficient. A cold test should ask Krish what is known, what is inferred, what changed, what could falsify the move and who still owns the session. If he cannot answer those without opening an administrative inspector, the synthesis fails.

## Binding synthesis constraints

1. **Keep the intervention first.** The first ten seconds show one supported move, why now and one primary action. No map, navigation rail, status summary or rehearsal turn may precede it.
2. **Make the opening state-dependent.** A rich Brain may show an intervention. A sparse Brain shows one bounded useful observation plus the highest-value gap. A quiet Brain says no interruption has earned attention and offers only a private ask or return. Do not force an intervention when evidence is insufficient.
3. **Use a typed causal trace.** Render `new evidence -> prior interpretation -> revised or weakened interpretation -> supported intervention -> provisional expected learning`. Every step carries fixture references, standing, uncertainty and audience. Explicitly name what was discarded or weakened.
4. **Preserve Krish's authority.** The action set is prepare, reshape, rotate to a causally different route, reject or inspect. The product drafts; Krish owns the session. No approval is inferred from opening, dwell or preparation.
5. **Restore customer becoming without changing the hero.** Immediately behind the intervention, show the personal meaning that became sharper and the bounded customer benefit. The optional Living Brain view must use the same canonical items and typed relationships as R2.
6. **Use the rejected customer-view lens narrowly.** Preview the exact audience-safe customer expression in place. Operator-private notes, customer-private Brain meaning and company-shareable transition material remain visibly distinct. Include no send or publish control.
7. **Bound the private ask.** It accepts an unexpected direction and a tiny context update by tap, typing or optional voice. Its answer is private, evidence-linked and provisional. It cannot mutate the durable Brain or pretend ambient listening occurred.
8. **Specify the entire required state range.** Include rich recent change, supported intervention, unresolved contradiction, weakened or discarded interpretation, deepening route, private ask, customer preview, sparse, quiet, loading, stale and recoverable error. A failed synthesis preserves the last verified state and never fabricates a claim.
9. **Meet the interaction contract.** One primary action per state, verdict beside the action it changes, no nested scrollbars, practical 44px targets, keyboard access, visible focus and reduced-motion equivalence. Mobile at 390 by 844 preserves the same intervention and evidence meaning as desktop at 1440 by 900.
10. **Constrain visual semantics.** Evidence lines may appear only when they resolve to explicit fixture evidence and cannot resemble unsupported semantic relationships. Colour and movement require text equivalents. Motion occurs once for a real evidence, standing or intervention-state change and then settles.
11. **Keep the fixture and authority visible.** Label the customer as synthetic and the interaction as a local proof. Do not imply live ingestion, model inference, persistence, account creation, production writes or external sending.
12. **Keep identity replaceable and prose spare.** Mindy remains excluded. CTRL may appear only as a replaceable working label. Remove reassurance, self-congratulation and atmospheric copy without an action, evidence, state or meaning job.

## Owner decision

Advance Candidate M into a bounded synthesis with the constraints above. Candidate L and Candidate N should remain source material only for the named borrowable elements. The acceptance signal is a deterministic concept and feasibility record that preserves intervention-first comprehension, personal becoming, causal traceability, state and audience integrity before any render is treated as ready for founder review.
