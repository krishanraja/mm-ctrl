# G13 concept B: The Living Fold

## 1. Governing interaction metaphor

The Brain is one folded decision map.

When folded, it is a concise portrait that shows the few bearings most useful to the decision in hand. When opened, those same bearings spread into the Living Map and reveal only the relationships that can be explained and evidenced. The user never travels to a separate graph product. They unfold one thought, trace one connection, and fold back with their place preserved.

The physical rule is strict: an item does not disappear and reappear as a different visual object when the view changes. Its sentence, state and selected position persist through the fold. This makes the portrait and map feel like two scales of one Brain rather than a dashboard next to a visualisation.

## 2. Exact first 30-second sequence

**0 to 3 seconds:** The surface opens on a quiet folded sheet headed by the current decision: “How should marketing change for an AI-native category?” Beneath it is one plain-language orientation: “Three parts of your judgement are shaping this.” The dominant control says **See what guides this**.

**3 to 8 seconds:** A tap reveals the leading bearing, not a metric: “Before consequential work ships, the human owns the final judgement and finish.” Its lens label is **How I judge**. A smaller line says **In your words · private**. The only prominent next action is **Open this thought**.

**8 to 14 seconds:** The folded sheet opens in place. The selected judgement stays under the finger while the other five eligible LB01 entities settle around it. Three semantic connections draw once, only after their endpoints are present. No ambient lines or similarity links appear.

**14 to 20 seconds:** One connection is already offered as the next useful trace, from the standard to the marketing aim. Tapping it replaces the abstract line emphasis with the relationship sentence: “Human release judgement protects the quality of AI-native category work.” The labels **supports** and **Why this is connected** are visible without a legend.

**20 to 26 seconds:** Tapping **Why this is connected** turns up a compact evidence face on the same sheet. It shows the two supporting statements, their interview source, speaker, capture date and private audience. The map remains visible but quiet behind the evidence face, preserving context.

**26 to 30 seconds:** The user closes the evidence face and sees two direct actions on the selected judgement: **This is right** and **Narrow it**. They choose **Narrow it**, beginning the correction flow without entering a chat.

## 3. Where the user has agency

The system chooses an initial bearing based on the current decision and deterministic projection relevance, but the user owns every meaningful transition:

- choose another portrait lens or bearing;
- open the selected bearing into the map;
- choose which evidenced connection to trace;
- inspect the source and exact supporting statements;
- correct, narrow, dispute or leave an item unchanged;
- approve the consequence preview before a correction becomes current;
- open each review-required downstream artifact separately; and
- return to the prior version and correction reason through history.

The user never has to drag a graph into legibility. Tap, keyboard focus and directional controls all perform the same navigation. Pinch and pan can be conveniences on a larger map, but are never required.

## 4. Primary interaction

The primary interaction is **open and trace**.

A user taps one sentence to open its neighbourhood, then taps one supported connection to read why it exists. Only one trace is foregrounded at a time. Other current, eligible entities remain present as orientation, but their lines recede until selected. This keeps the experience immersive without asking a time-pressed user to decode a network.

Opening a thought has three deterministic depths:

1. **Folded:** the current portrait, with at most twelve high-value bearings across the five human lenses.
2. **Open:** orientation or a selected neighbourhood, with at most eighteen or twenty-four entities respectively.
3. **Turned up:** one item, connection or decision with applicability, exceptions, evidence, history and correction actions.

Each depth is a state of the same surface, not a nested page or scrolling panel.

## 5. Information structure

The surface has five fixed layers, revealed only as needed:

1. **Decision header:** the current purpose, task and consequence context. This answers “What is this Brain helping me with now?”
2. **Folded portrait:** up to twelve sentence-led bearings viewed through What matters, How I judge, My calls, Unresolved and What changed. These are lenses, not separate storage sections.
3. **Living field:** the same accepted canonical items and first-class decisions, arranged for legibility. Node kind may be differentiated, but size means only current projection relevance. Distance carries no meaning.
4. **Trace face:** a selected relationship expressed as a typed plain-language sentence, followed by its evidence. A visible line exists only when this face can be produced from a current, audience-eligible, evidence-bearing relationship.
5. **Change edge:** history, correction controls and repair receipts. Proposed material lives in a separate **For your review** pocket outside the accepted field. Disputed and superseded material is available in history but cannot steer the portrait or map.

Long labels are never reduced to cryptic names. The folded state may show a two-line lead and a continuation cue, while one tap opens the full sentence on the same surface. The document itself has one page scroll; no layer introduces a nested scrolling region.

## 6. State model

The interaction state machine is:

`loading -> folded portrait -> open orientation -> focused neighbourhood -> item or trace face -> correction draft -> consequence preview -> applying -> repair receipt`

Important side states are explicit:

- **Accepted current:** present in both eligible portrait and map projections.
- **Proposed:** held in the detached For your review pocket with a clear “Suggestion, not part of your Brain” label. It has no accepted visual treatment and no semantic line into the current field.
- **Disputed or superseded:** removed from current guidance and available only through history, with the reason and successor where one exists.
- **Private audience rejected:** no Brain content, counts or silhouettes leak. The surface becomes a closed cover stating that this Brain is private, with an account or access action if available.
- **Loading:** the fold shape and decision header can reserve space, but no placeholder relationships are drawn.
- **Stale:** the last verified portrait stays readable with a visible as-of time and one **Refresh** action. Opening new traces is disabled until verification succeeds.
- **Recoverable error:** the last safe state stays in place, the failed action is named, and **Try again** is offered. A failed correction cannot look applied.
- **Reduced motion:** fold and repair transitions become immediate state changes with focus moved to the resulting heading.

## 7. How portrait and Living Map remain visibly one Brain

The fold is a reversible spatial transformation of the same objects. A portrait bearing carries its sentence, state mark and selection continuously into the open field. Folding back returns that exact object to its lens position. There is one shared as-of label and one change marker for both scales.

For LB01, the portrait and orientation map therefore expose the same six eligible references: the decision, aim, context, standard, tension and voice preference. The proposed pattern is absent from accepted truth. The open field adds only the three valid relationship versions between those references. Layout tethers, if needed for physical stability, are invisible and cannot be selected or inspected as meaning.

A purpose change triggers one refold and rebuild of both scales together. The interface never lets the user view a newly rebuilt portrait beside a stale map.

## 8. The correction and repair moment

From the selected standard, **Narrow it** turns up a sentence editor already containing the current wording. The user can type, paste or dictate, with typing always available. For the fixture, they enter:

> Before externally consequential work ships, the human owns final judgement and finish; reversible internal drafts may remain delegated.

Before anything changes, the sheet folds once to show a consequence preview:

- **Brain now:** the portrait and Living Map will rebuild.
- **Connections:** two relationships will be rechecked and replaced with their supported narrower meanings.
- **Needs your review:** Board brief and shared release.
- **Will not happen:** delivered or shared work will not be silently rewritten.

The one dominant action is **Apply correction**. A secondary **Keep current wording** action returns safely.

After confirmation, the prior wording slips into visible history rather than dissolving. The corrected sentence occupies the same item position. Its two old connections withdraw, then the two evidenced replacement connections draw once to the aim and voice preference. This is purposeful change motion, not ambient animation. The tension relationship remains unchanged.

The final receipt appears at the point of action:

> **Your Brain is corrected.** Portrait rebuilt. Map rebuilt. Board brief needs review. Shared release needs review.

Each dependency has a terminal outcome. The two review-required artifacts are actionable rows, not reassurance copy. Repeating the same correction returns the same receipt and does not create another visible change.

## 9. Sparse and dense Brains

**Sparse first use:** The fold remains physically small rather than pretending to be populated. One or two accepted bearings can stand alone. Empty map space is not filled with fabricated links. A single source-bound prompt can ask, “What should CTRL understand before it helps with this decision?” with tap, type and optional voice capture. Captured material enters review first and does not appear as accepted truth until authorised.

**Dense Brain:** The folded portrait remains capped at twelve. Opening shows at most eighteen balanced, task-relevant entities. Selecting one replaces the field with its bounded neighbourhood of at most twenty-four entities. A visible **Back to bearings** control restores orientation. Users move by choosing named entities or connections, not by hunting in a 120-item hairball. Deterministic ordering means the same accepted state and context produces the same orientation even if source order changes.

**Long narrow-screen content:** The selected sentence becomes the vertical anchor and surrounding entities use short lead lines. No meaning depends on seeing every label simultaneously. Tapping or keyboard focus reveals the full label before any action is offered.

**Desktop:** The same fold can open wider and keep the trace face beside the field, but depth, budgets, semantics and correction sequence remain identical to mobile.

## 10. Strongest reason it could fail

The fold could be experienced as a polished transition instead of an immediately obvious information model. If users do not understand that **Open this thought** reveals connections, the concept loses its main advantage and adds ceremony under time pressure. The proof must therefore test comprehension with motion removed as well as present, keep the control language literal, complete the transition quickly, and never require the user to discover a gesture.

## 11. Constraints that make it materially different

1. **One transforming surface:** portrait and map cannot be separate tabs, columns or widgets. The selected object must persist through the fold.
2. **One trace at a time:** the default map cannot present an all-at-once relationship web. A connection earns emphasis only when the user asks to trace it.
3. **Every selectable line owes an explanation:** a semantic line must open a typed relationship sentence and valid evidence. Decorative, similarity and hub lines are not rendered as relationships.
4. **Correction includes a consequence gate:** the system must name automatic rebuilds and human-review dependencies before applying the change, then issue a complete receipt afterward.
5. **No conversational container:** ordinary-language correction is a focused sentence action, not a chat transcript or assistant exchange.
6. **No scorekeeping:** item counts, completion percentages, confidence scores and Brain health cannot occupy the hero or substitute for a recognisable piece of judgement.
7. **Provisional material stays physically outside accepted truth:** proposed learning cannot borrow the placement, connections or treatment of current held or trusted understanding.

## Proof feasibility

A self-contained HTML proof can model the fold as a finite set of view states driven by LB01 and LB04. The six eligible LB01 entities, three valid relationships, evidence face, sentence correction, two replacement relationships and four repair outcomes are sufficient to demonstrate the entire spine. Generated fixtures can swap in the bounded eighteen-item orientation and twenty-four-item neighbourhood to test density. Private rejection, proposed material, stale state and error recovery can be deterministic fixture switches. No production schema, migration or external service is required.
