# G22 whole-product golden-path synthesis

**Date:** 11 September 2026  
**Status:** Selected concept for a rendered local proof  
**Authority:** Mock implementation only. No production navigation, data, account, message, database or release authority.

## Decision

Build one living consequential case.

Work arrives with its intended use intact. CTRL notices a decision inside it, connects company evidence to the leader's accepted judgement, and interrupts only when one answer would change the route. The Decision Table is earned as a deeper instrument. The human owns the call and decides what CTRL may learn from it. Later work visibly changes because of the accepted or corrected learning. The same governed record can then be inspected and taken away.

The product is not a sequence of features. It is one case whose current work changes as real events occur.

## Selected spine and borrowed strengths

Use **The Consequence File** as the causal and data spine.

Borrow from **The Margin**:

- one prepared intervention rather than a queue;
- quiet as a successful state;
- one concrete question with tappable answers;
- immediate display of what the answer changes;
- companion intelligence expressed through preparation, specificity and restraint.

Borrow from **The Standing Field**:

- a visible seam between personal and company-owned material;
- written standing labels that do not depend on colour;
- one exact carry trace from an accepted learning to the later fragment it changed.

Do not borrow the permanent margin, named spatial field, multiple object positions, graph grammar, source weave or board-like overview.

## The cold first view

The default proof opens on one current consequential case, not on product navigation or a Brain dashboard.

It must establish within one viewport:

1. the decision: how far Aperture House should rebuild marketing around AI;
2. the stakes: a synthetic £1.2m operating budget and eight affected roles;
3. the tension CTRL found: the plan promises a rebuild while the current scorecard still rewards volume;
4. what CTRL knows about Maya that changes the work: she judges through comparison and wants clarity without killing surprise;
5. the unresolved issue: whether another person can deliver launch work to her standard without Maya redoing it;
6. one human-owned answer that changes the first gate.

The concrete question is:

> If the pilot ships more work but Maya still has to redo the final version, has it worked?

Representative answers:

- No
- Only if the rewrites fall quickly
- Yes, if customers prefer the work

The selected answer updates one visible first gate directly beneath the choices. It does not open an explanation screen.

## Product shell

The shell contains only:

- the Mindmake icon;
- Maya Chen and Aperture House;
- one plain case title and current standing;
- one changing work region;
- quiet access to evidence history and the Living Brain.

The default shell has no feature sidebar, permanent right panel, progress indicator, greeting, feed, assistant composer, explanation of privacy, reassurance stack or decorative image.

Existing events may be inspected through **History**. Future events do not appear. History is evidence, not progress.

## Golden-path states

The proof may render these states in the same changing work region:

1. **Current case:** the work, stakes, evidence-backed tension and one concrete question.
2. **Decision work:** the approved R4 Decision Table as an earned deeper state.
3. **Owned call:** Maya's chosen route, first gate and final standard.
4. **Learning proposal:** one bounded interpretation with Accept, Correct and Reject.
5. **Corrected learning:** the earlier wording remains in history and the accepted version becomes eligible for later work.
6. **Later preparation:** the exact sentence that changed because of the accepted version, with the earlier version available on request.
7. **Living Brain:** a projection of what CTRL currently uses, who owns it, its standing and where it was used.
8. **Portable release:** a plain-language manifest of the governed version Maya can take away.

These are not visible steps or menu destinations. Actions create events and replace the current work. The current state can be revisited through history after it exists.

## Representative causal thread

1. A synthetic marketing operating plan arrives with a £1.2m budget, eight affected roles and a volume-led scorecard.
2. CTRL connects it to Maya's accepted standard: make quality clear without killing surprise.
3. Maya answers that the pilot has not worked if she still needs to rewrite the final version.
4. The first gate changes from output volume to independent launch quality.
5. The existing Decision Table compares three routes and Maya owns the working call.
6. CTRL proposes an over-broad learning: Maya keeps the final review for all marketing work.
7. Maya corrects it: she keeps the final call only on launch work; weekly work may use the agreed quality check.
8. A later launch preparation changes its approval owner and separates weekly work from launch work because of that accepted correction.
9. Brain inspection shows the exact accepted version, the superseded proposal, its sources, ownership and later use.
10. The portable release preserves personal and company material as separate owned sections.

## Visual direction

Use the Mindmake instrument world: deep ink green, warm paper, precise mint and amber, structured sans, editorial serif and restrained mono labels.

The signature visual is not a graph. It is one causal join that connects the accepted Maya learning to the exact later sentence it changed. It appears only on request and only when the fixture contains a stored dependency.

Correction may create one layered edge that corresponds to a real superseded version. Ownership is expressed as a structural seam between Maya and Aperture House, not repeated privacy copy.

No giant announcement title. No permanent empty side panel. No decorative particles. No sparkles. No fake controls. No nested scrolling region. Motion responds to an action or data change and disappears under reduced motion.

## Mobile rule

Maya's phone opens on the one action she owns now. It does not reproduce the operator workspace.

- One concrete question or governance choice per view.
- Large tappable answers.
- The result appears in the same place.
- Optional history stays behind one control.
- The dense Decision Table may state that it needs a larger screen.
- Inputs survive rotation and reload.
- The virtual keyboard and browser chrome cannot hide the action.

## Data and state contract

The proof uses deterministic synthetic fixtures and an append-only local event log. Every visible claim resolves to a source, standing, owner and version.

A correction:

1. records a new event;
2. preserves the earlier wording;
3. supersedes the old interpretation;
4. identifies every dependent staged object;
5. marks those objects as needing refresh;
6. creates a repaired later version only after the dependency is resolved.

A rejection makes the proposal inactive and prevents it from shaping later work. It may not silently disappear from history.

Synthetic evidence demonstrates product mechanics only. It does not prove personal growth, decision quality or company performance.

## Render acceptance

A cold viewer should be able to say, without an explanation:

- what work arrived;
- why this decision matters now;
- what CTRL understood about Maya that a generic model would miss;
- what Maya alone decided;
- what CTRL was allowed to learn;
- what later work changed because of that learning;
- what Maya owns and can take away.

Fail the concept if the viewer instead describes a timeline, course, dashboard, smart sidebar, graph, collection of features or AI chat wrapper.

## Vetoes

- No staged navigator, visible future moments or Next control.
- No abstract questions containing internal product language.
- No large editorial claim consuming working space.
- No more than one immediate Maya action on phone.
- No restyling or weakening of the approved Decision Table.
- No proposed, stale or rejected material influencing later work.
- No merged personal and company ownership.
- No claim that a synthetic example proves efficacy.
- No implementation beyond a branch-only protected proof without a new explicit gate.
