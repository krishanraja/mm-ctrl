## Why R82 exists

R81 bound signed envelopes, but all seven durable roles found places where the meaning inside those envelopes could still be supplied, relabelled or regenerated by the caller. A person could answer one visible question while the stored record claimed a different judgement. Facts could award themselves authority. Close obligations, correction records and external policy could originate from the object being checked. A valid signature did not make those meanings valid.

R82 repairs those authority seams. R81 remains useful evidence and exact source material. It is not effective authority.

## Effective contract

R82 materializes one closed contract from seven source modules. It imports only named exact sections from frozen R81 Git bytes and replaces every other R81 rule. Unknown or conflicting inheritance fails closed.

The contract now executes these relationships:

- Every atomic human judgement resolves to one versioned visible question-and-effect contract. Purpose, source boundary, decision frame, meaningful value, checkpoint and revisit judgements no longer share a generic hidden label.
- Every displayed answer option has a signed positive path and a deterministic, material accepted-decision effect. Free expression becomes authoritative only after the named human confirms the exact interpretation and consequence.
- Mechanical and normative assertion bytes, authority species, Brain standing, contrary assertions and derived input receipts resolve from the contract's sealed authority domain. The profile under review cannot certify them.
- Completed-close obligations come from sealed authoritative case state. Caller-supplied conditional IDs cannot remove a required owner or revisit dependency.
- Correction validation resolves one of multiple authoritative graphs, recomputes its closure and exact repair records, binds each receipt to this authority bundle and rejects unresolved trigger, proof, use, artifact, history or descendant-block references.
- Effective external authority reads only the contract's effective policy registry, which remains empty. Test-only policies are explicitly non-authoritative. Lease and consume evidence are closed and exact, terminal receipts are resolved and fingerprinted, time skew cannot be negative and replay or consume state is shared by an authoritative transactional registry.
- Canonicalization accepts only finite JSON. Malformed values return typed rejection rather than gaining authority or colliding with `null`.
- The exact 83-vector adversarial inventory is pinned so coverage cannot silently shrink.

## Human experience protected

The machinery stays backstage. Its purpose is to make one simple question mean exactly what it appears to mean.

A leader should never have to understand dependency IDs, signatures, receipts, predicates or transaction state. They see one short question tied to the real decision, a few intelligible choices and one sentence explaining what the answer changes. Choosing any offered answer works. Choosing “Something else” preserves their language and requires confirmation before CTRL treats an interpretation as theirs.

R82 also protects human agency from false certainty. AI may assemble evidence and expose the most valuable unresolved judgement, but it may not manufacture the leader's purpose, standards, boundaries, checkpoint or permission to proceed.

## Independent proof

It resolves the frozen R81 commit and exact effective-contract blob from Git, reconstructs the R82 bundle, verifies registered Ed25519 events and executes 83 positive and adversarial vectors across authentication, semantic authority, human judgement, final approval, predicate completeness, question design, correction and external finality.
