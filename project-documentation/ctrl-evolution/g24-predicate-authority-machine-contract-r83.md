## Human experience protected

The user still sees one clear question, not machinery. Every offered choice works and changes the real decision in the way the visible consequence says. If their own words are interpreted, they see the interpretation before it can become theirs. If several important unknowns exist, CTRL does not pepper them with questions; it prepares one purposeful conversation with Krish.

These controls protect agency without exposing technical complexity. AI may research, organise and challenge. It cannot silently change who said what, transfer one person's evidence to another, turn an interpretation into consent or make a test fixture steer a consequential decision.
