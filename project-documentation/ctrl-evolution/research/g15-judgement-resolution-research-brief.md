# G15 Judgement Resolution research brief

Date: 8 September 2026

## Decision

Determine whether one longitudinal construct can honestly represent how a person's Brain becomes more useful, distinctive and transferable without becoming a completion score, activity counter or business KPI. Select the construct and measurement contract that should govern concept divergence and product-market testing.

## Change conditions

The provisional `Judgement Resolution` direction should change if primary evidence shows that:

- its dimensions cannot be separated from simple data volume;
- a composite would hide a blocking weakness such as poor calibration or audience leakage;
- held-out predictive fidelity necessarily rewards imitation or freezes the person;
- users cannot understand the construct without false precision;
- another construct has materially stronger validity, personal meaning and commercial legibility.

## Boundaries

- Primary subject: a decision-owning leader building a private, portable Brain during a Mindmake proof.
- Construct: personal judgement as expressed in consequential work, including taste, standards, boundaries, corrections, abstention and transfer.
- Exclusions: personality diagnosis, intelligence scoring, clinical claims, employee ranking, productivity, business outcomes, file or memory volume, engagement streaks and comparison between people.
- The metric may compare a Brain against its own previous versions and no-Brain baselines. It must not rank people.

## Freshness

- Current evaluation and AI-risk standards: retrieve live on 8 September 2026.
- Foundational measurement theory may be older when it remains the primary source.
- Existing CTRL product contracts and fixtures use the current repository revision.

## Quality floor

- Original research papers, official standards or first-party technical guidance.
- Claims must distinguish established measurement principles from proposed CTRL product design.
- The construct requires content validity, held-out predictive evidence, calibration, correction sensitivity and an explicit anti-Goodhart boundary.

## Time and cost cap

- One bounded research pass using accessible sources only; no purchases, account creation or external contact.
- Stop after the decision-bearing measurement principles recur across independent primary sources and no unresolved conflict would change the concept brief.

## Stop rule

Stop when the research can support or reject all of the following: use of held-out cases; calibration and abstention; non-compensatory treatment of blocking weaknesses; temporal drift and correction; contrastive evidence; and a user-facing representation that does not imply a finished person.
