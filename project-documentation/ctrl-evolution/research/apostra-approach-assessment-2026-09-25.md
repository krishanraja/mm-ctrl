## The useful idea

The strongest transferable idea is decision archaeology. A useful AI system should not begin by asking a leader to restate everything it could already reconstruct from authorised work. It should gather the available evidence, recover the apparent history and assumptions behind a decision, show what it believes is true and then ask only for the judgement that remains human-owned.

The governing CTRL sequence is:

`decision archaeology → provisional candidate → human confirmation → living lineage → useful context where work happens`

This is not permission for invisible memory mutation. Reconstructed material remains a candidate with evidence, source standing, freshness and uncertainty. Confirmation, correction and rejection are different durable events. Only the leader can convert a proposed answer into a leader-owned answer.

## What CTRL should borrow

- Reconstruct before interrogating.
- Make the evidence and the inferred candidate inspectable.
- Preserve how a decision changed, not only its latest wording.
- Return the smallest useful context inside the work instead of forcing a separate search ritual.
- Use company and category material to make the next question specific, while keeping public facts, private evidence and inference as different types.

## What CTRL should not borrow

- Company-search or enterprise-knowledge positioning as the product promise.
- Retrieval volume as a proxy for intelligence.
- A single blended company brain that erases personal authority or audience boundaries.
- Automatic promotion of a model reconstruction into durable truth.
- More integrations as a substitute for a better consequential decision.

## CTRL product rule

Do not make the leader tell CTRL everything from scratch. Reconstruct what appears to be true, show the evidence and ask only the question that human judgement must answer.

The differentiated outcome remains a more capable leader with stronger judgement, taste and agency. Archaeology is a way to reduce burden and increase specificity. It is not the product's positioning.

## Implementation consequence

The first implementation cut must keep two ingress paths distinct:

1. Evidence-derived material enters as a provisional candidate. It cannot answer a question or alter accepted Brain truth.
2. A leader's direct answer, confirmation, correction or rejection enters through an authenticated human-authority path with append-only lineage.

This principle is locked as `D-067` in the CTRL evolution state.
