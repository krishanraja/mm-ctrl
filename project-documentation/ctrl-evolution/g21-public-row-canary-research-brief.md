# G21 public-row canary research brief

Status: research brief frozen before collection

Date: 10 September 2026

## Decision

Can CTRL use current public evidence to become progressively more specific about a consequential business context without pretending it privately knows a named leader?

This canary selects four manifests across the external evidence range. It is the first safe row of the wider four-by-four harness:

1. one wholly fictional cold-start profile at `none_or_unusable` external evidence and `none` internal evidence;
2. one real public subject at `sparse` external evidence and `none` internal evidence;
3. one real public subject at `useful` external evidence and `none` internal evidence;
4. one real public subject at `rich_longitudinal` external evidence and `none` internal evidence.

Real named people are deliberately excluded from every internal-evidence coordinate. Those cells will later use genuinely consented material, consented anonymised material or visibly fictional identities.

## Change conditions

Change or stop this route if any of the following becomes true:

- a named subject cannot be represented using attributable public evidence alone;
- a depth label depends on source count rather than decision-bearing coverage;
- a profile would need an invented motive, preference, weakness, private quote or decision history to feel useful;
- current primary evidence contradicts the proposed identity, role or company context;
- the sparse case cannot yield a precise gap, question or abstention without simulated insight;
- a source contains sensitive personal material that is unnecessary for the test.

## Boundaries

- Purpose: product research and deterministic diagnostic evaluation, not assessment of the named individual.
- Population: public business leaders and their organisations, selected for different public-evidence depths and relevance to consequential AI-era decisions.
- Geography: open, with preference for leaders and businesses legible to a UK-based Mindmake buyer.
- Internal evidence: exactly zero for every real public subject.
- Protected or private traits: excluded.
- Company facts and person claims: stored and evaluated separately.
- Public statements: attributable and scoped to their date and setting.
- Inference: permitted only as an explicit business-context hypothesis with a named disconfirming test. It cannot become a fact about the person.
- Diagnostic runs, council rulings, database writes and product UI: out of scope until the manifests and oracles pass deterministic review.

## Freshness

- Current identity, role and company facts must be rechecked on retrieval day.
- Time-sensitive strategy, performance, product and organisation claims should normally be no older than eighteen months unless used as an explicitly historical comparison.
- Longitudinal evidence must span materially different dates and decision contexts.
- Every source records publication date when available and retrieval date.

## Quality floor

- Prefer current official biographies, filings, annual reports, earnings materials, company newsrooms, investor materials and direct public talks or interviews.
- A search result, snippet, generated summary or article repeating another source is a lead, not evidence.
- `sparse` means a small attributable envelope that establishes identity and limited business context but leaves major decision questions unanswered.
- `useful` means enough independent decision-bearing public evidence to identify a real strategic tension, a countercase and a route-changing question.
- `rich_longitudinal` means decision-bearing evidence across time, formats and observable company actions, with enough history to distinguish continuity from a recent claim.
- Evidence depth is a coverage judgement, not a count of URLs.

## Time and cost cap

- No paid sources, accounts, contact with subjects or metered collection.
- Stop after one defensible four-profile manifest set and its claim oracle are frozen.
- Do not scale to the remaining twelve coordinates in this pass.

## Stop rule

Collection stops when each selected profile has:

- a valid namespace and evidence coordinate;
- an attributable source envelope appropriate to its claimed depth;
- a concise set of allowed factual notices;
- explicit unknowns and contradictions;
- expected diagnostic notices;
- forbidden claims;
- at least one question whose answer could genuinely change a consequential route;
- a deterministic validator pass.

If those conditions cannot be met honestly, the canary remains incomplete rather than being filled with plausible prose.

## Predeclared verification

The canary passes only if:

- all four manifests pass the namespace and evidence validators;
- the external depths cover `none_or_unusable`, `sparse`, `useful` and `rich_longitudinal` exactly once;
- every real public subject has `internalDepth: none` and at least one direct source locator;
- every decision-bearing factual notice resolves to a source record;
- expected notices and forbidden claims are non-empty and specific to the case;
- no allowed output claims private knowledge of a real person;
- the sparse case resolves to a precise gap, question or abstention rather than generic advice;
- no source is treated as current beyond its recorded scope or retrieval date.

## Material alternative rejected for this pass

A diagonal four-profile canary spanning both external and internal depth would exercise more of the matrix immediately. It is rejected because it would either attach fictional private depth to real named people or mix several safety questions into the first test. The public-only row isolates one hard question and leaves internal-depth testing for properly consented or wholly fictional fixtures.
