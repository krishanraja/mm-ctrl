## Locked meaning

Preservation is the floor. Improvement is the mandate.

The existing systems are evidence of value and known failure behavior. They are not presumed to be the best implementation, workflow, scoring method, interface or product shape. Age, current use, prior spend, complexity and polish confer no authority.

The safe route is:

`characterize earned value -> benchmark current behavior -> improve, recompose or replace -> prove improvement and non-regression -> retire only through the exact gate`

A successor must preserve required value and controls while improving a material dimension such as decision usefulness, user burden, speed, cost, provenance, correctness, agency, accessibility or resilience. It may deliberately remove behavior shown to be redundant, confusing, theatrical, generic or contrary to the target product.

This clarification prevents two equal errors:

- freezing the old product under the language of preservation;
- deleting useful machinery under the language of transformation.

## Authority boundary

This decision authorises local characterization, benchmarking, adapters, replacements, fixtures, tests, documentation and commits. It does not authorise production or database mutation, deployment, merge, release, customer-facing change or legacy retirement.
