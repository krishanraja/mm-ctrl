## What this proves

One dormant local adapter now accepts already-qualified news and decision-return signals and produces one governed prepared-intelligence object. The written view and audio script are projections of the same ordered content blocks. Neither mode can quietly develop its own facts.

The object keeps:

- the person, audience and purpose boundary;
- source provenance and evidence standing;
- the exact Brain context used to select each signal;
- a plain reason for inclusion;
- correction dependencies and feedback targets;
- explicit topic exclusions and durable negative-feedback suppression;
- an honest held state when nothing deserves the person's attention.

Decision returns require a real decision link and a decision observation. Credible ordinary news cannot masquerade as proof that a live decision changed. Conflicting facts cannot reuse one evidence identity.

## Product meaning

News curation and audio briefing survive, but in their correct place:

`Brain and live consequential work -> qualified signals -> one prepared object -> read or listen`

This protects the strongest old machinery without restoring a feed-first product. The Brain decides why something matters to this person now. Reading and listening are calm delivery modes over that governed result.

## Deliberate limits

This is not a new feed, player or production service. It does not prove:

- external gathering, clustering, ranking or curation quality;
- whether a decision-watch trigger is genuinely load-bearing;
- speech synthesis, playback, recovery or grounded talk-back;
- durable correction propagation or storage behavior;
- real tenancy, customer usefulness or commercial value.

No runtime imports the module. No database, model, external source, customer route or deployment was touched.

## Why R2 is bounded this way

Connecting the old news and briefing systems immediately would mix three separate questions: whether their producer semantics are still good, whether one prepared-object boundary is sound, and whether the new product should call them. R2 answers only the middle question. That makes the result inspectable and prevents a passing formatter test from being mistaken for a world-class intelligence engine.
