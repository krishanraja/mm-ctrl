## Effective contract

R81 materializes one effective contract from seven source modules. It imports only exact named sections from frozen R80 Git bytes and replaces every other R80 rule. Unknown or conflicting inheritance fails closed.

The contract now executes these relationships:

- A registered Ed25519 authenticator signs login, answer, free-expression confirmation and final-authority events. No workload or model is a trusted issuer.
- A final approval is a separate signed event, not an inference from login. It binds the exact transition, human, subject, case, purpose, audience, predecessor, predicate proof, contract bundle, rendered consequence and reserved receipt version.
- A normative receipt binds the exact question and version, judgement owner, signed answer, visible consequence, current accepted decision, precommitted answer effect, actual after-state and material delta.
- A free-form answer has no authority until the same authenticated human confirms its verbatim text, structured interpretation and visible consequence.
- Every transition derives its exact dependency set from the frozen transition catalogue. Missing, extra or duplicate results fail before aggregation.
- Mechanical, derived and normative facts run through their complete semantic profiles. Derived facts prove their exact input set, derivation function, freshness intersection and non-widening of audience, use or consequence.
- Question validation reads canonical case data and durable session state. Case-name decoration cannot rescue a generic question. Total copy, per-control copy, answer count and technical-language limits are executable.
- Correction walks the authoritative dependency graph, seals the complete descendant closure and joins one immutable, non-steering terminal receipt to each exact dependency tuple.
- External authority runs a typed reservation-to-finality state machine with active policy checks, strict time, compare-and-set, protocol-specific lease or consume proof, lost-acknowledgement recovery, collision detection, terminal receipts and replay rejection. No external fact policy is currently active.

## Human experience protected

The machinery remains backstage. It exists so a leader can see one plain, consequential, case-specific question without having to understand predicates, receipts or state machines. The reference question asks what proof would justify rebuilding Aperture House's marketing team. Each answer changes the same real decision and its proof obligations. A vague business horoscope, technical label, excessive explanation or automatic question queue fails.

This contract protects human agency at both ends of consequential work. AI may prepare, connect and test evidence, but it cannot invent a person's answer or treat a valid session as approval. The named human sees the consequence and signs the exact transition that will occur.
