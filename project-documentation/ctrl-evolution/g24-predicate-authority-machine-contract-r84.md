## Why R84 exists

R83 proved strong individual receipts but not one coherent authority path. Opposed answers and conflicting values could jointly satisfy a transition; routing evidence did not enter predicate authority; derived facts did not resolve and execute over versioned source bytes; an initial review could reopen closed work; malformed companion arguments could still throw; and external replay did not bind exact mode and request bytes.

R84 repairs those failures forward. R83 remains immutable source evidence and exact imported material. It is vetoed and is not effective authority.

## Effective contract

R84 materializes one closed contract from seven source modules. It imports only seven named exact sections from the frozen R83 effective-contract blob and replaces every other R83 rule. Unknown or conflicting inheritance fails closed.

The contract now executes these relationships:

- Every normative answer is composed into one reconciled after-state. A stop-versus-proceed route or two different values for the same material path produces `contradicted`, not success.
- Predicate proof and final human authority both bind the exact reconciled decision fingerprint, predecessor version, purpose, audience and visible consequence.
- The sealed direct-question reservation or Krish-session receipt enters render, normative and predicate evidence. A multi-gap set cannot be satisfied by unrelated one-question receipts.
- Reopening after close has a transition-specific question and purpose, compares against the closed predecessor, requires post-close issuance and cannot reuse the initial review receipt. “Keep the previous decision closed” blocks the transition.
- Derived facts resolve every registered input from a versioned source store, including complete sets, member IDs, values, issuance events and times, revocation references and epochs. The checker executes the registered derivation rather than trusting its label.
- Exact replay binds mode, policy, idempotency identity, nonce and submitted request bytes. Changed bytes collide; test completion cannot satisfy effective mode; early protocol evidence is closed. The claim is deliberately limited to one checker process: R84 does not claim runtime or cross-process transactional authority.
- Public validators reject null companions, cyclic objects, accessor-backed objects, sparse arrays and malformed mutation paths without throwing.
- Positive predicate and final-authority proofs cover every one of the thirteen lifecycle transitions exactly once, alongside targeted adversarial cases.

## Human experience protected

The machinery stays backstage. The person receives one clear, case-specific question or one purposeful session with Krish, never a technical proof interface. Their answers cannot be averaged into false agreement, their request to stop cannot be relabelled as progress and settled work cannot quietly re-enter the present.

AI may organise evidence and expose a decision. It cannot invent consent, reuse an earlier judgement for a new lifecycle meaning, or claim that incompatible human answers form one decision. The person and Krish still provide the final consequential authority.
