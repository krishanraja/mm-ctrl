## Human experience preserved

The contract does not prescribe a technical interface. It protects a simple one.

- Zero unresolved human judgements means no question.
- One atomic judgement for one named person may become one complete question.
- More than one becomes a safe hold or one Krish-led session agenda, never a queue.
- The default question surface permits one heading, one question, one control group, one optional note and one primary action.
- `Unknown` cannot satisfy anything.
- Free expression stays verbatim and non-authoritative until the same named human confirms one structured interpretation and its visible consequence.
- A question fails if its answers change only lifecycle, receipt, queue or notification state.

The executable case asks:
