The post-race checks proved no snapshot drift, no forbidden evidence link, no forbidden answer or human prior, no backdated revocation against a committed use, no revoked call, no duplicate evidence atom and no receipt for either rejected answer or rejected call. The same-assertion race proved that a busy loser fails fast, a clean retry reuses one atom and the first session's authority remains usable. The distinct-assertion race proved that a shared source is not an unnecessary serialisation boundary. The former lock-inversion schedule proves that session one's stale partial A→B attempt rolls back completely when B changes during its wait, session two's updated-B atom survives, and a clean retry creates or reuses exactly A and updated B without deadlock. The direct waiting-materializer schedule proves that an update committed during a provenance wait cannot acquire an earlier materialisation timestamp. The answer and prior schedules prove that a busy governing version produces a clean retry boundary with zero atom or consequential record. The inverse row-lock and identity schedules prove that a version-holding consumer never waits on busy provenance or atom identity. The reverse ordinary-batch schedule proves identity locking is universally fail-fast; the four-identity reverse-version schedule proves the same rule at the governing layer and verifies complete rollback of the loser's earlier work. The final pair proves owned-call admission cannot wait through a successor seal on stale standing: whichever action owns the governing version first commits, the contender receives the stable retry signal, and a clean retry evaluates current truth.

Before the two-session schedules, the same hosted transaction executed R142's semantic causal controls. It rejected the current decision as its own supposed precedent and separately rejected a draft version of another decision. It refused subject answers to a Brain-research question, an operator-research question, a proposed question and a suppressed question, while admitting and receipting exactly one asked leader question. It then staged a supposed human starting view after analysis generation and required sealing to fail. It removed the sole working recommendation from a complete three-route analysis, bound authority to the changed snapshot and again required sealing to fail. Every evidence link materialised exact assertion and source provenance in an immutable atom, and after sealing the test refused update and deletion of both the used assertion and source while persisting legitimate updates to an unreferenced assertion and source; exact replay remained valid. Finally, it created successor authority while the predecessor remained draft, sealed the predecessor, proved that the successor snapshot digest changed because it includes the predecessor's sealed digest, and rejected both the original premature authority and a new valid-digest authority whose occurrence time was one microsecond before the predecessor seal. The repaired exact bytes also rejected future source/assertion timestamps, bound atom materialisation and causal watermark into the digest, and required every evidence link, human record and governing authority to follow the provenance it consumes.
