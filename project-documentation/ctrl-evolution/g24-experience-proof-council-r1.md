# G24 experience proof council R1

**Status:** active for every new or materially changed customer or operator surface

## Honest audit of the existing judges

The existing seven-role council is rigorous and has been used. It has issued real vetoes, preserved dissent, sealed history-free first passes and then used durable history during cross-examination. It is strong on human agency, epistemic integrity, subject and audience safety, consequential usefulness, Living Brain integrity, comprehension boundaries and implementation realism.

It is not a complete experience council. Its own rulings repeatedly state that rendered comprehension, delight, accessibility, one-handed mobile use, failed-save recovery and physical runtime behaviour remain unproved. The G22 and G23 design work added two independent concept judges, Playwright, screenshots and protected preview checks. That was materially better, but it was scoped to individual artifacts and did not create permanent owners for visual taste, emotional tone, interaction craft, device resilience, accessibility, performance or state range.

The recent G25 backend recovery work did not run a customer-experience council. That is correct for schema and provider work, but the record did not make the non-applicability obvious enough. Machine checks cannot be allowed to look like experience approval.

## The corrected system

The seven truth judges remain. Eight permanent experience specialists now own:

1. immediate comprehension and cognitive load;
2. interaction choreography and affordance;
3. visual taste, brand and distinctiveness;
4. empathy, voice and human dignity;
5. cross-device, input and environment resilience;
6. accessibility and inclusive semantics;
7. performance, motion and perceived quality; and
8. state range, recovery and AI truth.

This is not fifteen opinions on every commit. The scope router selects the smallest valid court and records why excluded roles are not applicable. A backend-only change uses relevant truth judges and machine proof. A material customer or operator surface uses all eight experience specialists plus the four truth judges most capable of catching a superficially delightful but strategically wrong product. A release candidate uses the complete truth council, complete experience council and every applicable reality lab.

## Blindness that is real enough to trust

A same-context self-review is not blind. The first pass receives frozen hashes, a sanitized user and task, constraints, named states and rendered evidence. It does not receive builder rationale, conversation history, prior verdicts, predicted founder reaction, the preferred candidate or another judge's output. At least two independent contexts are required, candidate order is randomized for concept comparisons and verdicts are sealed before durable history is opened.

Role diversity is not cognitive independence. Multiple AI roles may still share blind spots. For that reason no AI panel can substitute for fresh people, physical devices, assistive technology or longitudinal customer evidence. Model-family diversity is useful when available, but observed reality is the non-negotiable counterweight.

## What 2027-grade means here

It does not mean more effects, futuristic decoration or a busier Brain map. It means the system feels prepared; deep intelligence arrives as one calm and consequential move; visualisation carries real truth; the product has a recognisable grammar that is not current AI fashion; motion makes it feel alive without fake personhood; and the leader becomes more capable rather than more dependent.

## Enforcement

Every material surface gets a durable run receipt containing exact revisions, artifact hashes, matching deployment identity, tasks, state range, devices, browsers, inputs, sealed verdicts, cross-examination, reality evidence, dissent and the founder's exact approval scope.

The repository change gate checks material files in `src` and `public`. A UI change without a same-change receipt fails the gate. A `preflight` receipt permits honest exploration and rendering but carries no approval claim. Founder-ready standing requires sealed verdicts, rendered reality evidence and explicit remaining limits.

If that receipt does not exist, the surface is `not_approved`. An architecture pass, automated test pass, screenshot, one viewport or AI-judge pass cannot be promoted into a rendered, cross-device or human-usability claim.

The next material operator or customer surface must instantiate this protocol before it is shown as founder-ready. The current backend recovery can continue under its own machine and safety gates, with experience marked explicitly not applicable and no UI approval inferred.
