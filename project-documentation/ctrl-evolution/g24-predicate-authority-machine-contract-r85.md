## Why R85 exists

R84 could reconcile answers, but several links remained conventional rather than authoritative. A person could be asked to sign an opaque hash instead of the exact decision; free expression always supported progress; route receipts could be caller-minted; extra unreferenced evidence could steer reconciliation; freshness and grant validity trusted incomplete summaries; reopening used unrelated close conventions; failed external work could burn authority; and final validation did not resolve the proof and evidence it claimed to authorize.

R85 repairs those failures forward. R84 remains immutable evidence and is formally vetoed by the [R84 panel verdict](g24-predicate-authority-r84-panel-verdict.md). It is not effective authority.

## Effective contract

R85 materializes one closed contract from seven source modules. It imports only seven named exact sections from the frozen R83 effective-contract blob and replaces every other rule. Unknown or conflicting inheritance fails closed.

The contract now executes one joined authority path:

- Evidence-store keys must exactly equal the predicate's unique evidence references. Only validated referenced normative evidence may alter the reconciled decision.
- The final human surface renders the complete reconciled decision, every changed material path and a plain-language before-and-after delta. Final authority signs that exact render as well as the predicate and decision fingerprints.
- Free expression carries an explicit signed transition disposition. “Stop”, “pause” and “remain closed” are executable blocking meanings, not automatically recast as support.
- Direct-question and Krish-session routes are signed, issued from a private authority store, resolved at validation time and bound once to one predicate proof. Caller-minted or orphaned routes fail.
- Derived facts use closed dispatch. Dependency freshness is computed from the complete transition dependency boundary; active grants must be current, unexpired and not revoked.
- Fresh grant issuance is compared with an authoritative lifecycle boundary. Resume binds to the current paused boundary; reopening binds to one typed closed-predecessor record joining lifecycle version, close time, previous purpose, decision bytes, source heads and revocation epoch.
- Final validation revalidates the exact predicate, evidence, route receipts and human-visible render. It derives its binding from those validated bytes rather than accepting caller context.
- External seen, consume and completion mutations are staged and committed only after an accepted terminal result. A failed request does not burn the token or make an exact retry behave differently.
- Transition identifiers and all public companion arguments fail closed before interpolation or property access. Accessor-backed, cyclic, sparse and malformed values do not throw.

## Human experience protected

The machinery remains backstage. The leader sees the exact consequential call they are making, what changed, why it changed and what their answer will do. They are never asked to understand a proof hash, lifecycle label or routing receipt.

CTRL may gather, compare, challenge and organise evidence. It may surface one important question or prepare one purposeful Krish-led session. It cannot turn a person's refusal into progress, add unseen evidence to their decision, reopen settled work by convention or ask them to authorize a decision they were not shown.

It resolves the exact frozen R83 Git bytes, reconstructs the R85 authority bundle, verifies signed authentication, answer, route, free-expression and final-authority events, executes all thirteen predicate and final-authority transitions, runs 205 pinned positive and adversarial vectors and generates 300 malformed-input and whole-tuple probes.

It also pins targeted attacks for caller-minted and orphaned routes, self-declared lifecycle boundaries, incomplete freshness sets, expired grants, unknown derivations, orphan predicate hashes, unreferenced evidence, hidden decision deltas and failed-request token burn.

## Still not authorized

R85 is local contract data and deterministic conformance evidence only. It does not authorize or implement a semantic evaluator, result-producing lifecycle success branch, runtime integration, live registry, shared cross-process transaction adapter, database or migration, customer UI, production data, external research or service action, model spend, email, merge, deployment, release, production promotion, legacy-backend deletion or cross-venture decision-ledger write.
