## The decision

R2's product direction survives intact:

- the relationship may continue beyond the initial intensive proof window;
- CTRL prepares before it interrupts;
- each consequential gap goes to the least-burden eligible source;
- one naturally answerable question is visible at a time;
- Krish receives a prepared live-session opportunity only when conversation should beat research or an asynchronous answer;
- quiet and abstention are valid;
- the leader owns purpose, judgement, the call and final quality; and
- the customer never administers the machinery described below.

R3 adds no new customer surface and no second Brain. It closes the seam between the clear R2 prose and the executable system.

## Repair 1: close every R2 object onto R1

R2 introduced useful names for planner objects without stating whether they were canonical records, events, proposals or projections. This created room for a shadow evidence store, a second answer authority and a parallel engagement root.

The ownership map is now fixed:

| R2 object | R1 owner | Standing |
|---|---|---|
| `engagement_period` | existing consequential-work `engagement` aggregate | versioned engagement state, never a second root |
| `decision_requirement` | existing accepted `case/decision frame` | case-scoped child of one accepted frame version |
| `evidence_coverage` | existing canonical sources, assertions and accepted Brain versions | rebuildable projection; cannot award evidence or Brain standing |
| `enrichment_plan` | existing evidence-planner run state | proposed, bounded operational plan |
| `enrichment_receipt` | existing source and execution receipts | append-only attempt/result receipt linked to canonical assertions |
| `question_plan` | existing consequential-work question plan | versioned proposal, never a second question domain |
| `answer_receipt` | existing consequential-work answer event | immutable case evidence; not automatic learning or Brain truth |
| `session_opportunity` | existing intervention candidate | proposed or rebuildable operator projection until Krish acts |
| `intervention_delivery` | existing interruption decision, delivery intent and receipt chain | no new delivery authority |

### The inherited-integrity rule

Every decision-shaping R2 artifact resolves by reference to the current valid R1 objects. It cannot copy their labels and claim the same authority.

The controlling references are:

- workspace and subject;
- evidence namespace;
- accepted decision and human-frame version;
- canonical source, assertion and accepted Brain-item versions used;
- authority or permission version;
- purpose and audience ceiling;
- trusted evaluation cutoff;
- sensitivity, validity and retention state; and
- all input-version watermarks needed to invalidate the artifact.

Effective standing, audience, purpose, freshness and applicability come from those references. A model-authored output cannot upgrade them.

For each decision use, the artifact also records:

- the claim kind and what that source class is capable of supporting;
- supporting and contradicting assertion references;
- root provenance when independent corroboration is required;
- a use-specific sufficiency rule and result;
- causal standing; and
- why the prior item is applicable here rather than merely similar.

Causal standing distinguishes direct fact, attributable statement, observation, association, mechanism hypothesis, causal evidence and unknown. Public statements, observed outcomes and a leader's acceptance do not silently become causal proof or transferable judgement.

Syndicated copies and derivatives collapse to their common provenance root when independence matters. First arrival never resolves a contradiction. A contradiction either triggers the smallest admissible resolving route or remains visible in an abstention.

Unknown, missing, mismatched, future-dated, expired or invalid controlling references make the derivative ineligible before use. A controlling correction, permission change, audience change, identity resolution, freshness change or decision-version change invalidates or rebuilds every affected coverage result, plan, question, session opportunity, capsule and unsent delivery projection.

Public claims may be reused by immutable reference. Applicability, sufficiency, contradiction, audience and standing are recomputed inside each authorised case. Private reasoning never travels with the public reference.

## Repair 2: make authority and lifecycle executable

Commercial relationship state never grants source permission. Permission never arrives by inference from elapsed time, payment or a user's failure to object.

### Engagement transitions

| From | To | Human authority and precondition | Receipt effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| none | `preparing` | Krish opens a bounded operator-private preparation scope; only eligible public evidence or separately authorised supplied material may be used | records subject, purpose, permitted source classes, review date and no-contact state |
| `preparing` | `intensive_proof` | named leader and relevant Mindmake authority accept the engagement purpose and current permissions | records exact active grants, decision frame and checkpoint |
| `intensive_proof` | `continuing` | named leader and Krish explicitly agree that another period has earned value | records the next consequential decision or evidenced value, checkpoint and exit or revisit condition |
| `continuing` | `continuing` | fresh explicit agreement at the recorded checkpoint | creates a new period version; never silently rolls over |
| `intensive_proof` or `continuing` | `paused` | either named human may pause | invalidates unsent interventions and prevents new collection or preparation until reactivated |
| `paused` | `continuing` | named leader and Krish revalidate purpose, identity, grants, audience, standing and freshness | creates a new period version; expired authority remains expired |
| active or paused | `closing` | either named human starts an orderly close | blocks new decision-shaping work unless explicitly required for correction, access, release or close |
| `closing` | `released` | leader accepts the scoped release or close outcome and Mindmake records completion | ends operational eligibility; retained data follows its own authority and retention rules |
| `released` | `preparing` | a new explicit engagement decision | opens a new period and cannot revive old grants |

Absence of the required receipt never means continuation.

### Coarse capability policy

This table defines semantic eligibility, not present action authority. Customer contact, capture, delivery and external research remain closed until their later gates.

| State | Existing permitted read | New research | New capture | New question or session proposal | Delivery | Learning proposal | Release/export | Repair, access and deletion work |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `preparing` | yes | public or separately authorised only | no | operator-private preparation only | no | no | no | yes |
| `intensive_proof` | yes | only under current grants | only under current grants | eligible under current grants | separately gated | eligible as proposal | on explicit request | yes |
| `continuing` | yes | only under fresh current grants | only under fresh current grants | eligible under current grants | separately gated | eligible as proposal | on explicit request | yes |
| `paused` | limited existing state | no | no | no | no | no | on explicit access request | yes |
| `closing` | limited to close, access and repair | no | no | no | no | no | eligible for accepted release | yes |
| `released` | no operational use | no | no | no | no | no | customer-held release only | required residue-aware obligations only |

Reopening or continuing rechecks authority. It never revives an expired grant, widens an audience, changes a purpose or extends retention.

### Permission change before use

Permission expiry, withdrawal or audience narrowing is distinct from correction. It immediately makes every dependent unsent coverage result, enrichment result, question, session opportunity, context capsule and delivery intent ineligible. The system then rebuilds or quarantines those projections from the remaining canonical state and issues a receipt.

Correction repairs meaning. Permission change controls use. Erasure controls retained content. They may trigger some of the same dependencies, but they are not represented as one event.

### Intervention authority

Every new or recomputed leader-facing intervention begins in `proposed` state. Krish must issue a version-matched `approve`, `edit`, `hold` or `suppress` transition before any later delivery capability may act. A change to content, control, purpose, audience, channel, timing, evidence or frame version invalidates the approval.

The immutable answer event is persisted immediately. Its effect is split:

1. **case evidence:** what the person actually answered or declined;
2. **derived case update:** a rebuildable effect that follows from that answer; and
3. **human-owned proposal:** any change to purpose, success or kill conditions, human-versus-AI boundary, durable judgement or learning.

The third cannot become accepted merely because the first was recorded. It requires the named authority to accept, edit or reject it. The answer itself may also be the authority event only when the respondent is the named authority and the exact material effect was visible before commitment.

Unknown, defer, refusal and premise rejection create no adverse inference. They do not count as human failure and cannot automatically trigger re-asking, pressure or a live-session escalation.

A session opportunity is a proposal, not permission to schedule, contact, record, transcribe or learn.

## Repair 3: one total route-selection boundary

The planner has one semantic owner: the trusted application `intervention_selector`. Existing bounded model tasks may propose coverage, evidence or route candidates. They do not choose or persist the authoritative transition.

### Input

The selector receives references to:

- the accepted decision and human-frame version;
- one current decision requirement;
- the current evidence-coverage version;
- current authority, audience and permission state;
- one trusted `as_of` cutoff and freshness state;
- engagement lifecycle state;
- decision deadline;
- the permitted intervention set;
- current or recently suppressed intervention state; and
- a typed budget envelope.

The budget envelope names dimensions now, while values remain changeable implementation policy:

- wall-clock time;
- source requests;
- retrieval hops;
- model tokens or cost;
- leader interruptions; and
- open operator work.

### Hard precedence

1. Resolve identity and subject.
2. Check authority, purpose, audience and lifecycle eligibility.
3. Check validity, trusted cutoff, freshness, source capability, provenance independence and contradictions.
4. Check sensitivity, decision deadline and permitted routes.
5. Test use-specific sufficiency and expected material effect against the accepted R1 decision frame.
6. Compare burden, cost and interruption only among eligible routes.

Least burden never makes an incapable or unauthorised source eligible.

### Exactly one result

The selector returns exactly one of:

- `reuse`: a current, authorised and applicable Brain item is sufficient;
- `enrich`: an eligible source can resolve the gap inside the budget and deadline;
- `ask`: one capable respondent can answer one bounded variable in a fitting grammar;
- `session`: interdependent or tacit gaps make prepared conversation materially better than the eligible alternatives; or
- `abstain_hold`: no route has earned safe standing now.

Every result carries:

- a stable reason code;
- the unresolved gap;
- expected material effect bound to the accepted decision frame;
- eligible alternatives considered and why the others lost;
- controlling version watermarks;
- expiry; and
- the event that requires replanning.

Stable reason families include current sufficiency, source eligible, one-human fact required, tacit interdependence, insufficient authority, source incapable, unresolved contradiction, unknowable, deadline, budget, no material effect and invalid input.

Missing, stale, ambiguous, contradictory or invalid input returns `abstain_hold` or a typed provisional result. It never guesses, throws an unhandled failure or silently selects the first available route.

If evidence conflicts, the selector preserves the conflict. It seeks the smallest admissible resolving evidence or abstains. It never awards standing because one source arrived first or because several pages share one root.

Any controlling input-version change cancels or invalidates the result before use. A model may propose the route and rationale; trusted code validates canonical references, the hard precedence and the permitted transition.

R3 does not create a second decision-value ontology. The selector must bind to the accepted R1 consequential decision frame and name the material effect it expects to create. Concrete high-value and low-value decisions, a competent same-evidence baseline and real decision improvement remain proof questions at G24.C and later gates.

## Repair 4: one versioned human-facing intervention atom

### Question atom

One `question_plan_version` binds:

- exact visible question wording;
- rendered control payload and answer grammar;
- complete options, unit or comparator;
- scoped write-in behaviour;
- `unknown`, `defer` and `refuse` behaviour where honestly possible;
- a non-inferential `premise_or_options_wrong` route for every closed, ranked or forced format;
- the material effect disclosed before commitment;
- the case-evidence effect of each answer;
- any separately pending human-owned proposal;
- answer-specific visible consequence wording;
- decision, evidence, authority and audience watermarks;
- sensitivity and purpose disclosure; and
- Krish's exact-version approval state.

Changing any one of these creates a new version and invalidates approval. Optional notes remain optional and never carry a required value.

The visible consequence distinguishes what changed from what remains unknown. It cannot congratulate CTRL, imply that a route update is a final decision or turn a refusal into pressure.

### Session atom

One `session_opportunity_version` binds:

- the exact agenda and leader-visible purpose;
- the accepted decision and human-frame version;
- expected material effect;
- the leader's ability to decline, reject the premise or reframe the purpose;
- Krish's proposal-state controls;
- the desired evidence, threshold, experiment or owned-decision end state; and
- an explicit statement that the opportunity does not authorise contact, scheduling, capture, transcription or learning.

The operator portfolio shows the customer and decision, one sentence on why live time is earned now and one primary action. The full agenda and secondary controls disclose after that.

### Question Yield correction

Question Yield remains an internal, unproven routing diagnostic. It may count independently observed decision-relevant evidence gain, uncertainty reduction or valid confirmation, not merely a route mutation the planner predicted itself.

Refusal, deferral, premise rejection and a useful confirmation that leaves the route unchanged are not failures. Question Yield cannot become a target that rewards compliance, interruption or state churn.

## Protected strengths

Every implementation and recheck must preserve:

- thirty days as the current intensive proof window, not a hard expiry or customer countdown;
- explicit human continuation and no permission renewal through commercial state;
- the approved R1 product spine and one canonical Brain;
- human-owned purpose, judgement, call and final quality;
- a decision evidence map focused on a consequential call rather than profile completion;
- accepted context and capable evidence before interruption;
- public statement versus private judgement and behaviour versus intention;
- one visible question, natural controls, leader prior, honest exits and visible consequence;
- quiet and abstention as successful states;
- prepared sessions only when conversation should beat the alternatives;
- Krish's pull-only control over the session opportunity;
- public-only real identities, fictional internal depth and no simulated consent;
- Qualified Judgement Transfer and Question Yield as unproven internal hypotheses;
- headless intelligence proof before material UI polish; and
- every currently closed external action.

None of the trust machinery above becomes customer administration or visible privacy boilerplate. Its purpose is to let the visible experience remain devastatingly simple without becoming deceptive.
