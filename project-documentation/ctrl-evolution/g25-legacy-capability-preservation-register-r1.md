## Outcome

The new CTRL may replace old screens, routes, labels, schemas, providers and implementations. It may not accidentally lose a useful capability, control meaning, data boundary or proven failure behavior.

This register changes the migration question from "did we remember the old product?" to five checkable questions for every protected capability:

1. What exact value must survive?
2. Which current component, caller, trigger and data contract provides it?
3. What role does it play in the new Brain-led product?
4. What characterization proof must a replacement pass?
5. What exact evidence is required before the old component can be retired?

## Why this route

The selected route is a machine-readable component register plus a deterministic checker. It wins over two alternatives:

- **Narrative reassurance only:** rejected because a paragraph saying "keep the good bits" cannot block a later deletion or expose an unmapped cron, cache or feedback writer.
- **Freeze the old backend wholesale:** rejected because preserving historical schemas and product hierarchy would force the new product to inherit the assumptions we are replacing.

The governing principle is: **preserve the organ's value and control contract, not its old location in the body.**

## The product hierarchy is still flipped

News and audio are protected because they are excellent machinery, not because they remain the hero.

The target relationship is:

`Brain context and live consequential work -> intervention policy -> prepared object -> read, listen, talk back, email or export`

The old feed-first relationship is not protected. The following are protected:

- cross-verified AI-native gathering, clustering, corroboration and qualification;
- personal ranking against role, business, objectives, decisions, watchlist and explicit tuning;
- honest thin-supply behavior and source provenance;
- briefing compilation over the same qualified pool;
- personalised audio, playback, recovery and grounded conversation;
- tuning, watchlist, excludes, voice steering, negative feedback and kill controls;
- the decision-watch to briefing return loop.

## Brain-control mechanics that cannot disappear

The following are easy to miss because they are not glamorous screens. They are protected explicitly:

- verify, correct, supersede, expire, contest and burn;
- preserve source, subject, speaker, audience, purpose and freshness;
- keep user statement, model interpretation and human acceptance separate;
- keep model recommendation separate from the human-owned call;
- one-tap rejection suppression without fabricated learning;
- off-record interaction with no durable content write;
- tune, watch, exclude, steer, pause, unsubscribe and revoke;
- inspect why context was selected and where it was used;
- rebuild projections and mark cached or exported derivatives stale after correction;
- return to a past decision only when a named load-bearing condition moves;
- read, listen, talk back, email, copy to Claude, export and MCP as modes over governed state;
- retain failure recovery, idempotency, rate limits, source checks and honest degraded states.

## Authority

This register authorises local characterization, adapters, fixtures, tests and documentation only. It does not authorise a production migration, external write, customer-facing UI, deployment, merge, release, feature switch or deletion.

## Pass signal

R1 passes its own structural gate only when:

- every mandatory capability ID exists exactly once;
- every current owner and evidence path exists in the repository;
- no capability is marked retired;
- each capability names value, target role, characterization proof and retirement conditions;
- the register includes the explicit founder lock and exact audited baseline;
- the checker passes from a clean repository state.

Passing this check proves inventory structure, not behavioral parity. Each protected capability remains `protected_pending_characterization` until its behavior is frozen and exercised.

## Retirement rule

No protected component may be disabled, deleted or made unreachable until all of the following are true:

1. its replacement passes the named characterization and data-boundary proofs;
2. every live reader, writer, cron, webhook, cache and route is mapped;
3. the old component has zero live callers or an explicit compatibility adapter;
4. authoritative readback proves the replacement state;
5. rollback to the prior known-good component is ready and tested;
6. retention, erasure, export and historical readability remain intact;
7. Krish gives exact action-time approval for the named retirement.

"The new interface works" is not retirement evidence.
