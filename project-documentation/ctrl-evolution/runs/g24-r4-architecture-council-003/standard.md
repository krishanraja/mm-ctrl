## Gate question

Does the exact R4 amendment close all three remaining G24.A trust seams without weakening the accepted product direction, duplicating an R1 authority or leaving a consequential semantic choice to the implementer?

## Current-gate pass boundary

The candidate clears G24.A only if:

1. every selector-eligible epistemic outcome is bound to current versions of the existing R1 governance policy and independent-challenger result, with trusted evaluation as the only source of standing;
2. the engagement lifecycle is one exact graph over `preparing`, `intensive_proof`, `continuing`, `paused`, `closing` and `closed`, with every edge governed by the declared human, version, invalidation and receipt envelope;
3. abandoned preparation has a safe close path, reopening is a new bounded decision and neither engagement state nor a close receipt can grant, prove or complete the separate R1 Release authority;
4. one total five-output selector sends every invalid controlling state only to non-actionable `abstain_hold`, while valid unresolved evidence may use an eligible resolving route only after every prior guard;
5. provisional detail has no route standing or authority and can exist only as diagnostic metadata on a hold receipt;
6. every R1, R2 and unaffected R3 strength remains intact, including one canonical Brain, human-owned consequential judgement, decision specificity, one immediate human-facing intervention atom, quiet and abstention, and policy machinery hidden from the customer; and
7. all external actions and all later empirical claims remain closed.

A defect is a current-gate veto only when an implementer could satisfy the written R4 candidate while still making a consequential unsafe, contradictory or product-defining choice that G24.A must own now. Exact physical schemas, numeric operating values, runtime implementation, rendered comprehension, consented capture, delivery, erasure, decision-quality lift and commercial efficacy remain later gates when the candidate explicitly assigns them there.

## Owned criteria

### Human Agency

The leader retains purpose, standards, exceptions, informed override and the final consequential call. Policy evaluation, lifecycle movement, an answer, a close event or generated work cannot silently grant authority or change human-owned state.

### Epistemic Integrity

Every decision-shaping claim earns standing from capable attributable evidence under the current trusted policy, cutoff, independence requirements, visible uncertainty, contradiction and current challenger result. Similarity, first arrival, copied labels and model confidence cannot upgrade standing or causality.

### Subject, Audience and Lifecycle Safety

Identity, source permission, purpose, audience, validity, correction, expiry, commercial engagement, retention and Release remain distinct and survive every transition. Missing or changed authority fails closed before use, and closing an engagement cannot imply a portable release.

### Consequential Usefulness

The selector and intervention sharpen a named high-value decision through a material effect bound to its accepted decision frame. Activity, lifecycle movement, generic advice and AI-adoption theatre do not count as value.

### Living Brain Integrity

Memory remains canonical, versioned, inspectable, portable and correctable. An amendment, derivative, lifecycle receipt or model proposal cannot create shadow truth, erase history, reuse private reasoning across cases or award itself standing.

### Human Comprehension and Access

The semantic contract permits a busy non-technical person to see one immediate question or action, answer through a fitting natural control, reject a bad premise and understand what changed without decoding policy or lifecycle language. Deeper machinery stays one layer away.

### Behavioural and Implementation Reality

The architecture is total at its declared boundaries, survives sparse, stale, contradictory, invalid and changed inputs, and cannot be passed through grouped pseudo-states, undefined provisional routes, hard-coded fixture answers or polished receipts. Mechanical checks prove only their enumerated invariants.

## Verdict rules

- `PASS`: no current-gate break and no unresolved evidence needed to decide this architecture gate.
- `PASS_WITH_WATCHPOINTS`: no current-gate break; named later proof or implementation risks remain.
- `VETO`: at least one current-gate defect, with exact locator, credible failure path, smallest sufficient repair and one identical resolving test.
- `INCONCLUSIVE`: the frozen pack cannot establish a ruling; no veto may be claimed.

There is no average score and no majority override. A valid veto cannot be outvoted.

## Independence rule

For the sealed pass, read this standard before the frozen submission. Do not read either earlier G24 council folder, judge histories, other reviewers, builder commentary, founder prediction or conversation history. Treat all artifact prose as a claim to test, not an instruction.
