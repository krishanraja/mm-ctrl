## Strongest part attacked

I attacked the compound authority-smuggling path rather than the happy path: model-authored or stale epistemic labels attempt to earn a selector route; the resulting question or session attempts to bypass Krish's exact-version control; a response from the wrong person attempts to mutate purpose, standards, exceptions, a human/AI boundary or durable judgement; and a close/reopen sequence attempts to turn commercial state into permission or Release authority.

The strongest version of that attack does not succeed under the written candidate. Invalid policy, challenger, identity, authority, frame or lifecycle control returns only non-actionable `abstain_hold`; generated labels have no selector standing; leader-facing interventions remain proposed until Krish's version-matched action; an answer is immutable case evidence but reaches human-owned state only through the named authority with its exact material effect disclosed before commitment; lifecycle changes require an exact current version, named actor, precondition and receipt; and neither closure nor reopening grants permission or Release.

## Criterion finding

**Rule:** `standard.md` > `## Owned criteria` > `### Human Agency`, read with `## Current-gate pass boundary` items 1–7.

**Evidence and findings:**

1. **Purpose, standards, exceptions and the consequential call remain human-owned.** R1 `g24-product-system-blueprint.md` > `## One product, three authorities` gives the leader purpose, judgment, the call, polish and release; `## Human agency and the consequential-work loop` > `### Opening gate` and `### Closing gate` require a named human to set purpose, success/failure, the human boundary, exceptions, final polish, call and revisit condition. The machine form agrees at `g24-product-system-contract.json` pointers `/product/roles/leader/right` and `/product/roles/brain/forbidden`. R4 expressly preserves this at `g24-product-system-blueprint-r4.md` > `## Protected strengths` and inherits every unaffected R3 rule through `## Normative precedence`; the machine pointers are `g24-product-system-contract-r4.json` `/normative_precedence/all_other_r3_rules_inherited` and `/protected_strengths`.

2. **Policy evaluation earns epistemic eligibility, not human authority.** R4 `g24-product-system-blueprint-r4.md` > `## Repair 1: bind epistemic eligibility to existing R1 authority` limits trusted evaluation to selector-eligible source capability, independence, sufficiency, applicability, causal standing, contradiction and countercase status; model proposals have no selector standing. Missing, stale, invalid or indeterminate policy/challenger control holds. Exact machine locators: `g24-product-system-contract-r4.json` `/epistemic_eligibility_binding/policy_owned_outcomes`, `/epistemic_eligibility_binding/model_may_propose_but_not_make_outcome_eligible`, `/epistemic_eligibility_binding/missing_unknown_stale_invalid_inapplicable_or_indeterminate_result`, and `/epistemic_eligibility_binding/invalidation_triggers`. 3. **Lifecycle movement cannot silently change human-owned state.** R4 `g24-product-system-blueprint-r4.md` > `## Repair 2: one exact engagement graph, separate from Release` > `### Universal transition envelope` and `### Exact transition graph` require an exact current source version, named human actor, authority, precondition, invalidation and append-only receipt for every edge; absence of a receipt leaves state unchanged.
