## Likely founder friction, in priority order

1. **The narrow repair could again become the apparent product.** R5 is not the reason a leader buys CTRL. It is one reason the leader can trust the private Brain when a later decision reuses prior judgement.
2. **The complete architecture is now five layers deep.** Krish should not have to reconstruct R1 through R5 from technical files. The lock moment should lead with one plain product sentence, the protected human loop and the exact next proof, with hashes attached as evidence.
3. **A safe system can still feel inert.** `abstain_hold` is correct only when it protects the decision and, when possible, identifies the smallest worthwhile evidence route. It is not an achievement by itself.
4. **The personal transformation can be lost inside headless correctness.** The next proof must eventually show that a leader becomes more capable across materially different decisions, not merely that invalidation receipts are correct.
5. **Backstage depth can leak into operator burden.** Full provenance must be available, but Krish should not manually reconcile dependency versions. The system should surface the decision, uncertainty, countercase and action, with technical details on demand.
6. **Architecture alignment is not experience, intelligence or commercial proof.** Founder lock must not be represented as proof of intuitive use, good questions, decision lift, customer return, willingness to pay or a world-class Living Brain expression.

## Exact founder lock

If final adjudication clears R5, the founder question should be:

> Do you approve the exact frozen R1 through R5 architecture as CTRL's governing product-system contract: one canonical living Brain; consequential AI-transition decisions; the Brain preparing, Krish challenging and the leader deciding and owning final quality; least-burden evidence or one earned intervention; current countercase-aware standing; correction-aware reuse; and a portable Release that cannot outlive the complete lineage that made its content trustworthy?

The same view should separate two possible authority choices:

1. **Architecture lock only.** This freezes the exact R1 through R5 contract as the implementation target. It opens no implementation by implication.
2. **Architecture lock plus bounded build authority.** This additionally authorises only the local, synthetic, headless G24.B/C Crossing proof already named by the architecture, with its exact validators, adversarial fixtures and observed state changes.

### What the lock would authorise

An exact architecture lock would authorise future implementation work to treat the frozen R1 through R5 chain as the governing contract rather than reopening settled product semantics. If Krish explicitly selects the second choice, it would also authorise the bounded local headless proof of:

- one canonical Brain and the mapped R1 owners;
- complete selector and Release dependency closure;
- the exact five-output selector and one intervention atom;
- a human-owned call, proposed learning, later reuse, correction and affected-lineage repair;
- the dependent-challenger mutation and unrelated-lineage control;
- local synthetic schemas, validators, fixtures and deterministic state inspection needed for G24.B/C; and
- no claim beyond what the observed proof actually establishes.

### What the lock would not authorise

It would not approve final schema or provider choices where the architecture reserves them, final UI, copy, visual system, customer experience, customer self-service, naming, GTM, pricing, product marketing, efficacy claims or production readiness. It would not pass G24.D, G24.E, G24.F/G or G24.H.
