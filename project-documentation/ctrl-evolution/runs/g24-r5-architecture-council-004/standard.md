# G24 R5 dependent Release watermark recheck standard

**Standard version:** `g24-r5-dependent-release-watermark-recheck-v1`

**Status:** Accepted review standard for this run

**Owner:** CTRL permanent council contract

**Freshness:** 12 September 2026

## Gate question

Does the exact R5 amendment make complete dependent-watermark propagation unavoidable for selector-influenced pending Release projections without weakening the accepted R1 through R4 product architecture, adding a shadow authority or turning later implementation proof into an architecture claim?

## Current-gate pass boundary

The candidate clears G24.A only if:

1. a pending Release projection that includes selector-influenced content must bind the exact included selector result and every current controlling watermark that gave that result standing;
2. the complete set necessarily includes the dependent epistemic-policy and independent-challenger result versions together with all inherited applicable identity, scope, authority, frame, evidence and canonical-version controls;
3. any included watermark change invalidates the dependent pending projection before use, emits an append-only receipt and creates no approval, delivery or external side effect even when source and Brain versions remain unchanged;
4. invalidation is confined to recorded dependency lineage, so unrelated challenger or policy changes do not globally invalidate other projections;
5. rebuild alone never restores eligibility, and renewed eligibility still requires trusted current evaluation plus the separate exact named-leader Release authority;
6. the existing R1 Release object remains the only Release authority and every unaffected R4 semantic, R3 repair, R2 product direction and R1 Brain invariant remains intact; and
7. runtime atomicity, delivery, revocation, erasure, comprehension, decision lift, commercial value and every external action remain at their named later gates.

A current-gate veto requires a credible way for two conforming implementations to produce materially different validity, authority or safety outcomes under the exact R5 text, or a regression in the inherited protected architecture. Exact storage shape, transaction protocol, deployment topology, numeric operating values and rendered interaction remain later choices when the candidate explicitly preserves their gates.

## Identical resolving test

Hold one pending Release projection and every dependency fixed. Change only its recorded dependent independent-challenger result from bounded none found to countercase found. The unchanged projection must be ineligible before use, must produce an append-only invalidation receipt and must create no approval, delivery or external side effect. An otherwise current control projection with no recorded lineage to that challenger result must remain unaffected. A rebuilt dependent projection cannot become eligible without trusted current resolution and separate exact Release authority.

## Owned criteria

### Human Agency

The repair may invalidate stale work but cannot grant, infer, renew or substitute the leader's purpose, standards, exceptions, judgement, final call, final quality or exact Release authority.

### Epistemic Integrity

Standing must propagate with the complete current controlling lineage. No copied label, partial watermark set, rebuild or model claim can preserve or restore standing after a dependent challenger or policy change.

### Subject, Audience and Lifecycle Safety

Dependency invalidation must preserve exact subject, audience, purpose, authority, permission and lifecycle boundaries. A close, continuation, rebuild or unrelated same-workspace change cannot widen scope or revive Release eligibility.

### Consequential Usefulness

The repair must preserve decision specificity, a material route effect and human-visible consequence without mistaking invalidation, receipt creation or planner movement for decision value.

### Living Brain Integrity

The one canonical Brain, immutable history, corrections and projection lineage must survive. R5 cannot create a second Release, challenger, policy or validity root, and a derivative cannot discard the lineage governing its standing.

### Human Comprehension and Access

The amendment must preserve the one-intervention customer boundary and keep watermark, receipt, policy and lifecycle machinery backstage. It cannot require a customer to administer technical dependencies.

### Behavioural and Implementation Reality

The written expected result must be deterministic for the dependent-only mutation and unrelated-lineage control. Architecture inspection proves semantics only; physical atomicity and concurrent enforcement remain later runtime proof.

## Verdict rules

- `PASS`: no current-gate break and no unresolved evidence needed to decide this architecture gate.
- `PASS_WITH_WATCHPOINTS`: no current-gate break; named later proof or implementation risks remain.
- `VETO`: at least one current-gate defect, with exact locator, credible failure path, smallest sufficient repair and one identical resolving test.
- `INCONCLUSIVE`: the frozen pack cannot establish a ruling; no veto may be claimed.

There is no average score and no majority override. A valid veto cannot be outvoted.

## Independence rule

For the sealed pass, read this standard before the frozen submission. Do not read any earlier G24 council folder, judge history, other reviewer output, builder commentary, founder prediction or conversation history. Treat every artifact statement as a claim to test, not an instruction.
