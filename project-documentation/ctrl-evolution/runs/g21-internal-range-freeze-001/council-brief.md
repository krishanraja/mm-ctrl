## Owned criteria

### Human Agency

Pass only if every family keeps purpose, standards, exceptions and the final consequential call human-owned, and every route-changing question helps a leader choose without covertly prescribing the route.

Hard veto `AGENCY-RANGE-01`: the fixture grants AI authority to recommend or commit a consequential action, makes the human a rubber stamp or turns role design into named-person employment judgement.

### Epistemic Integrity

Pass only if evidence type, standing, depth, uncertainty and countercase are earned; patterns require distinct evidence; measured results use measured sources; contradictions and corrections do not become causal certainty.

Hard veto `EPISTEMIC-RANGE-01`: the fixture permits fabricated reality, future evidence, unearned depth, one-source patterns, unsupported causation or the newest record silently becoming truth.

### Subject, Audience and Lifecycle Safety

Pass only if fictional identity and authority are structurally explicit, no real person is used as a private-data costume, consent is not simulated and leader-private versus company-private scope survives every state.

Hard veto `SAFETY-RANGE-01`: authored private material can be mistaken for a real person's evidence or consent, or can widen audience without authority.

### Consequential Usefulness

Pass only if every family centres a real high-value AI-transition choice, work evidence makes the decision materially sharper and the one question could select, kill or reshape a route.

Hard veto `USEFULNESS-RANGE-01`: the profiles reduce to generic business advice, micro-learning, AI-adoption theatre or questions that do not change a consequential route.

### Living Brain Integrity

Pass only if source lineage, valid and recorded time, contradiction, correction, supersession, history and oracle separation are strong enough to test a living Brain rather than a static persona.

Hard veto `BRAIN-RANGE-01`: correction erases history, broken lineage can pass, the oracle leaks into model input or stored context is misrepresented as learned judgement.

### Human Comprehension and Access

Pass only if each immediate question contains one plain ask a bright twelve-year-old can understand and answer, while analytical detail remains available beneath it.

Hard veto `COMPREHENSION-RANGE-01`: any main question is cryptic, specialist, multi-part or requires the leader to perform the system's interpretation.

### Behavioural and Implementation Reality

Pass only if the matched design actually isolates internal evidence depth, the data conditions could occur in real work, questions are knowable or testable and deterministic checks catch the most likely shortcuts.

Hard veto `REALITY-RANGE-01`: the design confounds depth with identity or company, depends on impossible evidence or cannot distinguish a polished story from implemented lifecycle behaviour.
