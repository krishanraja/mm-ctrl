### Where the prosecution overreaches

The prosecution is right that unexplained inheritance cannot carry new R2 objects safely. It overreaches when it pulls final storage fields, exact timeout and queue values, complete capture and erasure behavior, a competent-baseline implementation and real causal efficacy into G24.A. Those requirements either duplicate R1 or belong to the already declared data, intelligence, experience and consented-use gates.

Its proposed `decision_value_contract` is also too much as a new root. The accepted R1 decision frame already owns the decision, accountable human, purpose, desired change, success, unacceptable failure and human boundary. R2 needs a binding to that record and a named expected material effect, not a rival account of value.

### Where the defense overreaches

The defense correctly protects staged proof and rejects parallel evidence, permission and Brain systems. It overreaches by demoting the entire human-facing payload and several evidence-eligibility semantics to fixtures. A later fixture cannot supply an architecture rule that the implementer was never given.

In particular, the defense does not cure the direct mismatch between the blueprint's statement that wording, controls and route effects are one versioned object and the contract's omission of visible wording, version identity, control payload and visible consequence. It also relies too heavily on generic R1 freshness and lineage language without requiring the R2 selector to consume a trusted cutoff, source capability and independent provenance roots when it decides that a gap is supported.

## Root architecture defects

### 1. R2 is not closed onto the R1 ontology and trust root

R1 already owns engagement, source, assertion, decision, question plan, answer event, intervention candidate, interruption decision, delivery intent and append-only authority events in its [logical data domains](../../g24-product-system-blueprint.md#logical-data-domains). R2 nevertheless names nine logical objects without classifying which extend those records and which are proposed events or rebuildable projections in [“Logical objects added by this extension”](../../g24-product-system-blueprint-r2.md#logical-objects-added-by-this-extension). The R2 contract has no corresponding object map.

That ambiguity can produce a second answer authority, a shadow evidence standing, a parallel engagement root or a session queue detached from correction and audience changes. R1 inheritance is strong enough to avoid copying its fields, but not strong enough to identify an R2 object's owner when R2 never makes the link.

This root subsumes the Epistemic, Safety, Living Brain and Reality findings about source meaning, identity, audience, standing, cross-case reuse, correction and shadow memory.

### 2. Authority and lifecycle are not executable at the new transitions

R2 introduces open-ended `continuing`, optional `ends_at` and independent permission expiry in [“The relationship is longer than a timer”](../../g24-product-system-blueprint-r2.md#the-relationship-is-longer-than-a-timer), while its machine contract provides a flat state list and Booleans at `$.engagement_lifecycle`. It does not name the actor, authority receipt, precondition or permitted effects of entering, pausing, closing, releasing or reopening the relationship. This directly conflicts with the delta's prohibition on `engagement_continues_without_explicit_state_or_permission`.

The same seam exists inside interventions. R2 permits answers to change the decision frame, success or kill conditions and the human-versus-AI boundary, but does not separate an immutable answer event from acceptance of a human-owned state change. A recomputed question is not required to invalidate prior approval. Refusal, deferral and premise rejection are not protected from inference or escalation.

This root subsumes the Human Agency and Safety vetoes and the Living Brain lifecycle watchpoint.

### 3. The central route choice has no total semantic owner

The route tree chooses among reuse, enrichment, one question, a session and abstention, but predicates such as “already known,” “obtainable” and “would a conversation change the decision” remain prose in [“The amendment in one minute”](../../g24-product-system-blueprint-r2.md#the-amendment-in-one-minute). The contract has planner ingredients, not one versioned selector with a total input and output.

Without that boundary, implementation must invent precedence between authority, audience, freshness, source capability, decision deadline and burden. It must also invent what happens on missing, stale, contradictory or invalid input. The pre-announced Crossing quadrants can then be implemented as an answer sheet rather than a planner.

This root subsumes the Reality selector veto, the inspectable-abstention part of the Epistemic veto and the route-to-material-effect part of the Usefulness veto.

### 4. The machine contract does not preserve the claimed intervention atom

The blueprint says question wording, controls and route effects are one versioned object in [“Question contract”](../../g24-product-system-blueprint-r2.md#question-contract). The exhaustive machine list at `$.question_intelligence.contract_fields` omits visible wording, version identity, the rendered control payload and answer-specific visible consequence. Closed and forced formats also lack a guaranteed non-inferential “premise or options are wrong” path.

This is not a demand for final copy or UI design. It is a machine-contract consistency defect in a feature R2 proposes to lock and immediately exercise as a “correctly typed” question. A structurally correct control can otherwise ask for the wrong thing, hide a material effect until after commitment or coerce route movement.

This root subsumes the current-gate portion of the Human Comprehension and Human Agency findings. Actual comprehension remains unproven.

## Smallest sufficient repair contract

The repair is four amendments to the existing R1 architecture. It must not create four new subsystems.

### Repair 1: add one R2 extension map and inherited-integrity rule

Map every R2 logical object to its R1 owner and standing:

| R2 object | Required R1 ownership and standing |
|---|---|
| `engagement_period` | Versioned state of the existing consequential-work `engagement` aggregate |
| `decision_requirement` | Case-scoped child of the accepted R1 decision/frame version |
| `evidence_coverage` | Rebuildable projection over canonical sources, assertions and accepted Brain versions; it cannot award standing |
| `enrichment_plan` | Proposed, bounded evidence-planner run state |
| `enrichment_receipt` | Append-only source/execution receipt linked to canonical attempts and resulting assertions |
| `question_plan` | Version of the existing consequential-work question plan, not a second question domain |
| `answer_receipt` | Immutable R1 answer event; case evidence only until a separate authorised transition |
| `session_opportunity` | Proposed or rebuildable subtype of the R1 intervention candidate |
| `intervention_delivery` | The existing interruption decision, delivery intent and receipt chain; no new delivery authority |

Apply one reference-based integrity rule to every R2 decision-shaping artifact. It must resolve to exact R1 workspace, subject, accepted decision/frame, source/assertion/Brain and permission or authority versions. The effective audience, purpose, freshness, applicability, sensitivity and validity come from those canonical references, not an output label.

The rule must also establish one trusted evaluation cutoff, preserve contradiction, distinguish claim standing from source capability, collapse derivative sources to their provenance roots when independence is required and forbid observational evidence from silently becoming causal or transferable judgement. Unknown, missing, mismatched, future, expired or invalid controlling references make the artifact ineligible. A controlling correction, permission, audience, identity, freshness or decision-version change invalidates or rebuilds it before use.

Do not duplicate the R1 source, assertion, Brain, authority or event stores.

### Repair 2: add one coarse authority and lifecycle transition policy

Using R1's append-only event rule, define allowed transitions, actor, authority, precondition, version match and receipt semantics for engagement state and decision-shaping interventions.

At minimum:

- entry into `continuing` requires explicit, auditable agreement by the relevant named humans, plus a next consequential decision or earned value, checkpoint and exit or revisit condition;
- absence of a continuation receipt cannot mean continuation, and reopening cannot revive an expired permission;
- each engagement state declares whether read, research, capture, question, session, delivery, learning, export and repair are eligible at the semantic level;
- commercial state never grants source permission or widens audience, purpose or retention;
- expiry, withdrawal or audience narrowing makes dependent unsent coverage, enrichment, question, session, capsule and delivery projections ineligible before use, then rebuilds or quarantines them from canonical state;
- every new or recomputed leader-facing intervention returns to `proposed`; a previous approval cannot survive a material content, control, audience, purpose, channel, timing, source or frame-version change;
- the immutable answer event may persist immediately, but a change to human-owned purpose, success or kill conditions, human boundary, durable judgement or learning remains a proposal unless the respondent is the named authority and the exact material effect was disclosed before commitment; and
- unknown, defer, refusal and premise rejection create no adverse inference and do not automatically trigger re-asking, pressure or a live-session escalation.

Capture, delivery and erasure remain closed. A session agenda or privacy plan grants no permission to perform them.

### Repair 3: name one total, bounded route-selection boundary

Add one versioned selector over references to the canonical R1 state. Its input must include the accepted decision/frame version, current requirement, evidence coverage, authority and audience state, freshness cutoff, lifecycle state, decision deadline, permitted intervention set, current intervention state and a typed budget envelope.

It returns exactly one of `reuse`, `enrich`, `ask`, `session` or `abstain_hold`, with stable reason codes, the unresolved gap, expected material effect, rejected eligible alternatives, expiry and replanning trigger.

The contract must state:

- identity, authority, audience, validity, sensitivity and deadline guards run before optimisation;
- source capability, permission, freshness, use-specific sufficiency, independent-root and counterevidence rules are satisfied before least burden is compared;
- a contradiction is preserved and either seeks the smallest admissible resolving evidence or abstains; first arrival does not decide truth;
- missing, stale, ambiguous, contradictory or invalid input produces `abstain_hold` or a typed provisional state, never a guess or unhandled failure;
- the selector binds to the accepted R1 consequential decision/frame and names a material effect, rather than creating a second decision-value authority;
- a model may propose a route, but trusted code validates canonical references and the transition; and
- any controlling input-version change cancels or invalidates the proposal before use.

Budget dimensions must exist now. Their numeric values, provider policy, retry counts, queue caps and final algorithm belong to G24.C.

### Repair 4: complete the versioned human-facing intervention atom

For a question, bind one version across exact visible wording, rendered control payload, answer grammar, complete options or unit, scoped write-in, honest unknown/defer/refusal behavior, a non-inferential premise-or-options-wrong route for every closed or forced format, pre-disclosed material effects and answer-specific visible consequence. Separate immediate case evidence from any pending human-owned proposal.

For a session opportunity, bind the agenda version, leader-visible purpose, accepted decision/frame, expected material effect, leader ability to decline or reframe, Krish's proposal-state control and desired evidence or decision end state. State explicitly that the opportunity neither schedules nor authorises capture.

Question Yield remains an internal routing diagnostic. It may count independently observed decision-relevant evidence or uncertainty reduction, not merely a planner-authored state change. Refusal, deferral, premise rejection and a valid confirmation of the existing route are not human failure and cannot create pressure.

This repair locks the semantic payload shape. G24.C freezes exact examples and judges their meaning. G24.D tests whether real people understand them.

## Protected strengths

The repair and every recheck must preserve:

- thirty days as an intensive proof window rather than a hard product expiry or customer countdown;
- no automatic relationship continuation and no permission renewal through commercial state;
- the approved R1 product spine, one canonical Brain, human-owned purpose and call, governed learning and append-only correction;
- the decision evidence map's focus on variables that can change a consequential call rather than profile completeness;
- accepted context, authorised work and capable evidence before interruption;
- public fact versus claim, public statement versus private judgement, preference versus behavior, intention versus commitment, and missing versus unknowable;
- one visible leader question, natural answer controls, leader prior before system preference, correction, honest exits and visible consequence;
- quiet and abstention as successful states, with no activity or completion incentive;
- live sessions only when prepared conversation beats research or asynchronous input;
- a decision-specific session agenda, pull-only operator route and Krish's edit, snooze, dismiss and suppress control;
- public-only real identities, fictional internal depth and no simulated consent;
- Qualified Judgement Transfer as proposed and unproven;
- headless intelligence proof before interface polish, followed by observed experience proof; and
- every currently closed external action.

The repair stays underneath the experience. Grant identifiers, provenance roots, lifecycle state and causal standing must not become customer-facing administrative burden.
