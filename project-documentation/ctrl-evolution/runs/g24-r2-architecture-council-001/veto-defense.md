# G24 R2 veto defense

**Run:** `g24-r2-architecture-council-001`

**Role:** Independent defense of the frozen R2 architecture against the seven specialist verdicts

**Date:** 12 September 2026

**Decision:** `PROCEED_TO_REPAIR`

## Ruling

The direction survives the council. R2 is a coherent and unusually disciplined extension of the approved product spine. It should not be rejected, redesigned around the vetoes, or burdened with every runtime schema and test fixture before the founder can lock its product architecture.

The exact frozen bytes are not clear as-is. Three narrow architecture ambiguities remain in the new material:

1. open-ended continuation has no explicit human transition authority or machine-readable effect when a source permission expires or is withdrawn;
2. several R2 objects overlap R1 records without saying which are extensions, events or rebuildable projections, leaving room for a second canonical ontology or shadow authority; and
3. the central choice among reuse, enrichment, question, session and abstention has no named total decision boundary, so its authority, precedence and fail-closed result would otherwise be invented during implementation.

Those defects are repairable without changing the product direction. Most of the remaining veto demands are valuable acceptance tests for G24.C, G24.D or later consented use, but they are not reasons to block G24.A. The repair should be concentrated into three cross-cutting clauses, not expanded into five separate governance systems.

## Review basis and boundary

This defense intentionally read the [council brief](./brief.md), all seven verdicts and all four frozen R2 artifacts. It is therefore a council-level adversarial response, not an eighth isolated specialist verdict. It also read the frozen R1 blueprint, machine contract and QA record where a veto depended on what R2 inherits.

PowerShell `Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256` reproduced all four hashes in the brief and all three R1 hashes frozen in the R2 delta. Both R2 JSON files parsed successfully. That establishes the reviewed bytes and syntax only.

The gate boundary matters:

| Gate | Question this defense applies |
|---|---|
| G24.A Product-system lock | Can the founder lock the system direction, authority boundaries and first slice without leaving a consequential architecture choice to the implementer? |
| G24.C Intelligence proof | Does a headless implementation select and execute the right route across frozen, adversarial and safe-novel fixtures? |
| G24.D Experience proof | Can real people understand, answer, defer and recover in an approved rendered interaction? |
| Later service, founder and pilot gates | Do runtime permissions, capture, delivery, cost, latency, erasure, usefulness and customer value hold with real infrastructure and consented material? |

A missing architecture rule blocks G24.A when it creates contradictory authority, permits competing canonical state, or leaves the owner of a consequential decision undefined. A missing schema field, timeout value, queue cap, semantic fixture or observed-use result belongs to its later gate when R1 and R2 already state the governing rule.

## Defense against each veto

### Human Agency: sustain one defect, narrow the rest

The [Human Agency verdict](./human-agency.md) correctly preserves a major strength before objecting: R1 gives the Brain proposal rights, Krish interruption control and the leader purpose, judgement and call ownership. R2 expressly inherits `brain_krish_leader_authority` and `human_owned_consequential_work`, requires the leader prior before system preference, keeps delivery closed and says models may only propose the new objects. These are operative inherited rules, not decorative assurances. See [R1, "One product, three authorities" and "Human agency and the consequential-work loop"](../../g24-product-system-blueprint.md), plus R2 contract fields `/preserved_r1_invariants`, `/authority` and `/question_intelligence/leader_prior_before_system_preference`.

The continuation objection is valid. R2 adds `continuing`, says it has no assumed end and forbids continuation without explicit state or permission, but `/engagement_lifecycle` names neither the actor nor receipt that authorises entry. This is a new R2 decision and cannot be repaired merely by pointing back to R1.

The broader question-path veto overstates the gap at G24.A. In R1, the opening gate requires a named human to supply or confirm purpose, success and failure, the human boundary and provisional view. The closing gate reserves the call and accountability. R2's "persist it immediately" sits inside that inherited case and memory model: an answer can be preserved as an immutable case event without becoming an accepted Brain item or owned decision. The proposed architecture does not authorise application code to promote it. The missing R2-to-R1 object map makes this easier to misimplement, but that is one shared ownership defect, not a separate agency regime for every answer.

The demanded version-matched delivery approvals, premise-rejection cases, stale approvals and no-escalation outcomes are good G24.C fixtures. Exact leader comprehension of the premise and consequence belongs to G24.D. Session delivery and capture remain closed. They should become blocking before those later claims or capabilities, not before the founder approves the headless product direction.

**Disposition:** Partially sustained. Keep the explicit continuation authority repair and cover question/session authority through the shared object and selector contracts. Do not duplicate the whole R1 human gate inside every R2 field list.

### Epistemic Integrity: preserve the adversarial cases, reject a parallel evidence architecture

The [Epistemic Integrity verdict](./epistemic-integrity.md) demonstrates real failure modes: public evidence promoted into private personhood, one upstream source counted several times, future evidence, unsupported causality and abstention that leaks a diagnosis. Those attacks must survive into the intelligence proof.

Its proposed architecture repair is too expansive because it treats the R2 overlay as if it replaced R1. It does not. R1 already requires an immutable source ID and content hash, subject and originating actor, exact source spans, epistemic type, exact audience and purpose, currentness and recheck rule, and full lineage to every derivative in "Universal source envelope". R1 also separates Brain maturity, standing, audience and consequence permission, makes retrieval deterministically filter subject, audience, standing, validity and consequence permission, and requires each material claim to link to a source span or be labelled as new reasoning. R2 explicitly preserves source and audience integrity, correction and repair, and the canonical Brain target.

The valid gap is narrower: R2 does not explicitly say that `evidence_coverage`, `enrichment_plan`, `question_plan` and `session_opportunity` are projections over those canonical R1 versions rather than independent objects able to award standing. One derived-artifact integrity clause and the R1 object map close that risk. Recreating all source, assertion, capability, time and contradiction fields in every R2 object would create duplication and drift.

The trusted cutoff, independent-root collapse, source-capability mutations, causal-standing cases and complete abstention receipt are appropriate G24.C semantics and fixtures. The selector's result should include a reason, unresolved gap and blocked route so abstention is inspectable, but the architecture does not need final storage shapes to lock.

The objection to five-or-seven-point scales and a five-item ranking limit is not a truth-integrity blocker. These are explicitly defaults and product constraints, not claims that research proved universal superiority. The evidence note and R2 both keep the actual burden and efficacy claims unproven for observed testing. A founder may lock a provisional interaction default while retaining the later evidence gate.

**Disposition:** Mostly demoted to G24.C. Sustain only the shared derived-artifact binding and selector boundary. Preserve every adversarial epistemic case as a later blocking fixture.

### Subject, Audience and Lifecycle Safety: sustain the duration seam, respect the closed scope

The [Subject, Audience and Lifecycle Safety verdict](./subject-audience-lifecycle-safety.md) finds the clearest contradiction in the frozen overlay. R2 says permissions expire independently of the relationship and the delta forbids continuation without explicit state or permission. Yet the machine overlay reduces this to a Boolean and does not state what expiry or withdrawal does to dependent coverage, questions, session opportunities or unsent interventions. Because open-ended duration is one of the reasons R2 exists, this must be repaired before lock.

The rest of the veto repeatedly asks R2 to rebuild contracts that R1 already owns or to specify capabilities that remain closed:

- R1's Identity and authority domain already owns workspace, subject, membership, role, audience grant, consent and retention. Its universal source envelope binds consent, retention, audience, purpose, sensitivity, ownership and lineage. R2 should reference that authority, not add a competing `authority_receipt` root.
- R1 already defines the real-public, consented-real, synthetic and anonymous-consented namespaces. R2 repeats the real-public limitation and keeps consented material at a later gate. Exact malformed and mixed-namespace attacks belong in G24.C.
- R1 already makes outbound contact an operator-controlled delivery intent with exact content, recipient, channel, timing, expiry and source case. R2's first route is pull-only, unsolicited push is false and email send, scheduling, connectors and customer data are closed. Atomic send-time revalidation is mandatory before a delivery capability opens, but there is no current delivery architecture to approve.
- Session recording, transcription and durable learning are not authorised by a prepared agenda. Participant-visible modality consent, multi-speaker handling and mid-session withdrawal must block the consented founder or pilot path. They do not invalidate a headless session-opportunity proposal that cannot schedule or record anyone.
- Erasure is distinct from correction, but full raw-store, cache, export and DSAR behavior belongs to the data, service, complete-slice and cutover gates already named by R1. The R2 duration repair must make expired or revoked material unusable now; it need not pretend to finish the later erasure architecture.

**Disposition:** Partially sustained. Repair human continuation authority and permission-expiry invalidation. Move sensitive rendered disclosure, capture, send-time enforcement and residue-aware erasure proofs to the gates where those actions first exist.

### Consequential Usefulness: the product spine already supplies the missing altitude

The [Consequential Usefulness verdict](./consequential-usefulness.md) is right that route movement is not customer value. It is also right that Question Yield must not reward a bad state change. R2 already calls Question Yield an `internal_unproven_diagnostic`, forbids it as a customer score and preserves the later efficacy and pilot gates.

The veto nevertheless duplicates the approved architecture when it asks G24.A for a new `decision_value_contract`. R1 already limits the product to consequential AI-transition decisions, names why the company pays, requires an opening human frame covering the actual decision, why it matters, purpose, desired change, success, unacceptable failure and human responsibility, and requires two materially different decisions in the Crossing. R2 then restricts the evidence map to variables that can materially change the call and records why each is load-bearing and what would change. A trivial meeting-summary preference would fail that combined contract even if a fixture labelled it high consequence.

Freezing a concrete high-value decision, a low-value near miss, every branch effect and a competent baseline is how G24.C should test the architecture. Observed decision-quality improvement, willingness to pay and outcome lift belong to the founder and pilot gates. Requiring those artifacts before the founder can lock the route architecture reverses the declared sequence and risks turning the product into a large value-assessment form before the smallest useful path exists.

The selector repair should bind each route choice to the accepted R1 decision version and a named expected material effect. That makes the architecture's existing consequential boundary executable without adding a second account of value.

**Disposition:** Veto not sustained at G24.A. Carry its high-value, low-value and competent-baseline challenges as blocking G24.C fixtures, and keep market efficacy unproven.

### Behavioural and Implementation Reality: sustain ownership and selector seams, defer implementation machinery

The [Behavioural and Implementation Reality verdict](./behavioural-and-implementation-reality.md) identifies two genuine architecture ambiguities.

First, R1 already owns engagement, question plan, answer event, intervention candidate, interruption decision and delivery intent, while R2 calls `engagement_period`, `question_plan`, `answer_receipt`, `session_opportunity` and `intervention_delivery` newly added logical objects. Without a map, an implementer could create parallel canonical records. That conflicts with R1's single Brain and event model and must be closed at architecture level.

Second, R2's central route tree is clear to a human but `/enrichment_planner`, `/question_intelligence` and `/session_opportunity` do not identify one owner or total boundary for choosing among the routes. R1 names question and evidence planners separately. The cross-route choice still needs a finite output, precedence for hard authority and evidence guards, and a fail-closed result. That is a small architectural interface, not a full algorithm.

The rest of the veto crosses into implementation-detail creep. Exact wall-clock, request, token, cost, retry, concurrency and queue limits are environment and fixture decisions. R1 already locks bounded retries, token budgets, capped retrieval, idempotency and explicit terminal states. G24.C should instantiate and test those limits with delayed, failed, stale and duplicate inputs. G24.D should prove the operator portfolio does not become clerical work. Final values should change without reopening founder product architecture.

Likewise, hidden evaluator answers, semantic reversals, malformed inputs, safe-novel identifiers and observed state diffs are the correct design of the G24.C proof. They do not belong in the product-system ontology. The headless gate is deliberately where a selector implementation earns the claim that it works.

**Disposition:** Partially sustained. Repair the R1 object map and the minimal selector boundary. Defer budgets, queue policy values and semantic-oracle mechanics to G24.C fixtures and operator proof.

## The two non-veto verdicts resolve the gate dispute

The [Living Brain Integrity verdict](./living-brain-integrity.md) reaches the sound architecture-level conclusion: R2 does not weaken the Brain normatively because it is a hash-bound additive overlay over stronger R1 source, standing, audience, correction and transfer rules. It correctly makes exact derived-object bindings, correction invalidation and lifecycle behavior mandatory before G24.C can claim implementation integrity. The object-map repair should make that inheritance impossible to overlook, but the council should not convert every implementation watchpoint into a new canonical layer.

The [Human Comprehension and Access verdict](./human-comprehension-and-access.md) also draws the right line. R2 materially improves the architecture for a simple, decision-bearing interaction, explicitly says the customer UI is absent, and reserves intuitiveness for cognitive interviews and actual-device proof. Missing machine preservation of exact wording, controls, escape routes and consequence copy must block G24.C from claiming question quality and G24.D from claiming comprehension. It does not block founder approval of the interaction doctrine.

Together these verdicts show why the council should repair the few architecture seams and preserve the staged proof model rather than treating G24.A as the data, intelligence, experience, consent and pilot gates combined.

## Minimum repair set before founder lock

### 1. Close R2 onto the R1 ontology and trust model

Add one explicit mapping, without choosing physical table names:

| R2 object | R1 owner | Required standing |
|---|---|---|
| `engagement_period` | Consequential-work `engagement` aggregate | Versioned engagement state, not a new product or Brain root |
| `decision_requirement` | Consequential-work `case/decision` | Case-scoped requirement owned by the accepted decision version |
| `evidence_coverage` | Sources and claims plus consequential-work projection | Rebuildable projection, never an authority that awards source or Brain standing |
| `enrichment_plan` | Evidence planner run state | Proposed, bounded operational plan |
| `enrichment_receipt` | Source and execution receipts | Append-only event/receipt linked to source attempts and resulting assertions |
| `question_plan` | Existing consequential-work `question plan` | Versioned proposed record, not a second question domain |
| `answer_receipt` | Existing consequential-work `answer event` | Immutable case event; no automatic learning or Brain promotion |
| `session_opportunity` | Intervention `candidate` | Rebuildable/proposed operator projection until Krish acts |
| `intervention_delivery` | Existing interruption decision, delivery intent and receipt chain | No new delivery authority |

Apply one inherited-integrity rule to every new decision-shaping object: it resolves to exact R1 workspace, subject, decision and canonical source/assertion/Brain version watermarks; uses the effective audience, authority, validity, freshness and applicability of those inputs; cannot promote standing; and becomes ineligible or is rebuilt when a controlling version changes. The exact storage fields belong to G24.B/C.

### 2. Make continuation and permission expiry executable at the architecture level

Add a coarse transition contract that names the human authority and receipt for entry into `continuing`, `paused`, `closing`, `released` and `reopened`. Absence of an explicit continuation decision cannot mean continuation. Reopening cannot revive expired grants.

State the invariant that commercial engagement state never grants source permission. Expiry, withdrawal or audience narrowing makes every dependent unsent question, coverage result, enrichment result, session opportunity, context capsule and delivery intent ineligible before use, then triggers rebuild or quarantine from canonical state. Retention and erasure remain distinct operations under their later gates.

This is the smallest repair that resolves the direct conflict with `g24-product-system-r2-delta.json#/forbidden_interpretations[1]` without designing the final lifecycle schema.

### 3. Name one total route-selection boundary

Add one versioned semantic boundary whose input is the accepted R1 decision/frame version plus the current requirement, evidence coverage, authority/audience/freshness state, deadline and permitted intervention set. Its output is exactly one of `reuse`, `enrich`, `ask`, `session` or `abstain/hold`, with a stable reason, unresolved gap, expected material effect, rejected lower-burden alternatives and expiry.

Hard authority, identity, audience, validity and sensitivity rules take precedence over optimisation. Missing, stale, contradictory or ambiguous inputs fail to `abstain/hold` or a typed provisional state. A model may propose the route; trusted application code validates the transition and canonical references. Any controlling version change invalidates the proposal.

Do not lock numeric weights, provider policy, timeout values, retry counts, queue sizes or a final selector algorithm in G24.A. Those are G24.C implementation fixtures as long as the total boundary and fail-closed semantics are fixed.

| Later gate | Blocking evidence to carry forward |
|---|---|
| G24.B/C | Exact schemas and version fields; source-root deduplication; future-time rejection; capability-compatible claims; correction and permission invalidation; bounded cost, retry, timeout and stale-result behavior |
| G24.C | Frozen high-value and low-value decisions; hidden semantic oracle; safe-novel and adversarial cases; every unknown, refusal, correction and abstention branch; independently observed state diff; exact question wording, controls, escape routes and consequence payload |
| G24.D | Fresh-participant comprehension; premise rejection; consequence comprehension; one-handed phone use; failed-save recovery; progressive disclosure; operator queue legibility and clerical-load evidence |
| G24.E/F/G | Consented capture by modality and participant; sensitive-purpose disclosure; real-data audience and permission enforcement; session usefulness; decision-quality delta; customer return and value evidence |
| G24.H and relevant data/compliance gates | Atomic last-mile delivery checks; retention expiry; revocation and erasure traversal; export residue and non-recall limits; zero-reader/writer and rollback proof |

These later checks should be recorded as gates now. They should not be copied into the R2 architecture as final schemas or claimed as already proven.

## Strengths the repair must not bury

- Thirty days becomes an intensive proof window, not an artificial product expiry or a customer countdown.
- The decision evidence map asks only about variables capable of changing a consequential call, not profile completeness.
- The least-burden route prefers accepted context, authorised work and truthful public evidence before interrupting a person.
- Public fact, public statement, private judgement, preference, behaviour, intention, commitment, missing and unknowable remain explicitly separated.
- One question, natural answer controls, leader prior, correction, defer/refusal and visible consequence remain one coherent experience doctrine.
- Silence and abstention are successful states. Low question volume is allowed to mean excellent preparation.
- A live session must beat research or asynchronous input and arrive with a decision-specific agenda, not generic discovery theatre.
- Krish receives a pull-only opportunity and retains schedule, edit, snooze, dismiss and suppress control. No new notification channel is smuggled into the lock.
- R1's human-owned call, immutable source history, governed learning, correction cascade, audience separation and Qualified Judgement Transfer standing remain intact.
- The headless proof precedes interface polish, and the submission is honest that synthetic checks cannot prove intuitiveness, efficacy, willingness to pay or production safety.
- Every external action remains closed.

The architecture's value is that exhaustive machinery produces a restrained customer moment. A repair that exposes grant IDs, causal taxonomies, queue mechanics and receipt jargon on the customer surface would protect the database while destroying the product. Keep those controls underneath the experience and add only the three governing clauses above.

## Owner handoff

Repair the frozen blueprint and overlay only on the three seams above, freeze new hashes and recheck those seams against all seven verdicts. Do not reopen the founder-approved product spine, invent final schema names, begin headless implementation, use external research, spend on models, use customer data or open any delivery channel before the repaired lock.

The strongest alternative is `CLEAR_AS_IS`, relying entirely on R1 inheritance and later gates. I reject it because the duration contradiction, object overlap and unnamed cross-route owner are precisely the kinds of ambiguities that implementation can silently turn into new authority. `REJECT_DIRECTION` is also unsupported: every judge preserves the planner, the human-owned call, the quiet experience and the closed external boundary.
