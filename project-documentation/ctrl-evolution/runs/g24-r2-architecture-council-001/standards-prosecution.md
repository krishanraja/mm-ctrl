## Prosecutor ruling

R2's strongest ideas should survive: thirty days is an intensive entry window rather than a product expiry; the system should prefer accepted context and earned enrichment to interruption; quiet and abstention are valid; one leader-facing question is visible at a time; sessions remain prepared, pull-only opportunities under Krish's control; the leader owns the consequential call; and all external actions remain closed.

The defect is the seam between those ideas and the machine contract. The blueprint says trusted code will validate and persist nine new logical objects ([R2 blueprint, "Logical objects added by this extension", lines 376-392](../../g24-product-system-blueprint-r2.md#logical-objects-added-by-this-extension)). The overlay instead contains lists of desired fields and behaviours. A direct key inventory found no `logical_objects`, `intervention_selection`, `derived_artifact_integrity`, `lifecycle_transitions` or `decision_value_contract` block. `first_crossing_extension.cases` contains four strings rather than typed cases. `engagement_lifecycle` is a flat state list plus Booleans. The exhaustive `question_intelligence.contract_fields` list omits visible question text, question version, rendered control payload and visible consequence.

R1 cannot cure that gap by inheritance alone. R1 already requires one canonical source and assertion kernel, exact source envelopes, typed model tasks, append-only transitions carrying actor, authority and input watermarks, deterministic retrieval filters, correction-driven quarantine and an explicit human opening and closing gate ([R1 blueprint, "Universal source envelope", lines 141-159](../../g24-product-system-blueprint.md#universal-source-envelope); ["Logical data domains", lines 233-248](../../g24-product-system-blueprint.md#logical-data-domains); ["Human agency and the consequential-work loop", lines 297-325](../../g24-product-system-blueprint.md#human-agency-and-the-consequential-work-loop)). R2 does not say how its new records resolve to those exact R1 records and transitions. An implementation can therefore satisfy every R2 list while creating a shadow evidence, memory, authority or intervention path.

That is why this is not clear as-is. It is also why rejection of the direction would be disproportionate. One executable extension contract can close the shared defect.

## Root defects and the symptoms they produce

### Root 1: R2's decision-shaping artifacts are not bound to the R1 trust root

The decision map, enrichment plan and receipt, question and answer, session opportunity and delivery intent are all capable of steering consequential work. None is required by the R2 overlay to resolve to exact canonical source and assertion versions, content hashes and spans, independent provenance roots, claim kind, source capability, subject, evidence namespace, audience, authority grant, validity cutoff, contradiction set, use-specific sufficiency result and decision version.

This one omission produces several specialist symptoms:

- a public statement can be promoted into private personhood or causal meaning;
- syndicated copies can masquerade as independent corroboration;
- future, stale, expired or revoked evidence can still appear current;
- a reused public source can carry a prior customer's standing or interpretation;
- a plan or session agenda can act as shadow memory without a learning transition;
- correction can repair one projection while expiry, revocation or erasure leave another live; and
- prose-only real-public and fictional namespaces can be mixed in a conforming fixture.

The Living Brain pass treats these as later implementation watchpoints. That is not tenable at G24.A. Its own required repair asks for exact subject, audience, version, applicability and invalidation bindings across every new object. R1's current-gate acceptance criteria already require the data model to support evidence, audience, version, correction, repair and later use, and require AI tasks to be typed and bounded ([R1 blueprint, "Acceptance criteria for this architecture", lines 597-613](../../g24-product-system-blueprint.md#acceptance-criteria-for-this-architecture)). A required architecture binding cannot be deferred to the implementation that must infer it.

### Root 2: authority and lifecycle are labels, not a total transition system

R2 makes `continuing` open-ended and says permissions expire independently, but its overlay does not define allowed engagement transitions, actors, preconditions or per-state capabilities (`g24-product-system-contract-r2.json#/engagement_lifecycle`). It also does not distinguish correction, permission expiry, revocation and erasure as events with different downstream effects.

The same defect appears at the intervention boundary. A recomputed question does not necessarily invalidate an old approval. An answer can be persisted and its consequence applied before the relevant human accepts a change to purpose, success or kill conditions, the human-AI boundary or durable judgement. A session capture plan is not participant permission. An edit to wording, audience, channel, timing or agenda is not required to invalidate delivery approval. Refusal, deferral or premise rejection is not guaranteed to suppress inference, re-asking or escalation.

This is the common root behind the Human Agency continuation and question vetoes, the Safety lifecycle and last-mile vetoes, and the Living Brain invalidation watchpoints.

### Root 3: the planner is neither total nor answerable to decision value

The route tree uses natural-language predicates such as "already known", "obtainable", "answer cleanly" and "would a prepared conversation change the decision" ([R2 blueprint, "The amendment in one minute", lines 19-47](../../g24-product-system-blueprint-r2.md#the-amendment-in-one-minute)). The overlay defines no selector inputs, value domains, precedence, thresholds, tie rule, invalid-input result, deadline, cost or retrieval budget, retry and cancellation behaviour, stale-result handling, queue bound or deduplication rule.

It also has no decision-value admission test. A trivial AI-adoption choice can carry a `decision_id`, change a route, obsolete a question and score Question Yield while moving no material commitment. The Crossing pre-announces the expected route for each evidence-density quadrant, so an implementation can switch on fixture labels and emit persuasive receipts without inferring the evidence state ([R2 blueprint, "Extension to the first Crossing", lines 394-411](../../g24-product-system-blueprint-r2.md#extension-to-the-first-crossing)).

The Consequential Usefulness veto is valid at this point, but real causal uplift against actual customers is not required for architecture lock. The present requirement is smaller: admit only a decision with explicit material exposure and a falsifiable improvement target, reject a low-value near miss, and compare the proposed intervention with a competent no-CTRL baseline in the frozen semantic test. Market efficacy remains unproven.

### Root 4: the human-facing intervention atom and its proof are incomplete

R2 prose correctly says wording, controls and route effects form one versioned object, yet the machine list omits the visible wording, version identity and answer-specific consequence ([R2 blueprint, "Question contract", lines 253-272](../../g24-product-system-blueprint-r2.md#question-contract); `g24-product-system-contract-r2.json#/question_intelligence/contract_fields`). It conditionally offers escape routes "where relevant", does not require a visible premise-or-options-wrong path for closed or forced formats, and does not distinguish an immediate case update from a proposed change to human-owned state.

The Human Comprehension pass correctly defers rendered intuitiveness, but incorrectly defers this atomic contract. R2 proposes to lock question eligibility, answer grammar and timing, then immediately implement a "correctly typed" question. Without exact visible semantics, the headless gate can pass a control type while the person is asked the wrong, incomplete or incomprehensible thing. That is an architecture-lock veto. Actual child-level comprehension, one-handed use and felt simplicity remain G24.D evidence.

## Contradictions that the repair must resolve

1. **"Persist immediately" versus human ownership.** Persist the immutable answer event immediately. Do not immediately promote it into the accepted decision frame, boundary, Brain item or learning. Show the observed case change and any pending human-owned proposal as different states.
2. **"If nothing changed, the question should not have been sent" versus honest evidence.** A useful answer can confirm a boundary, reduce uncertainty, reveal that the premise is wrong, or validly refuse without changing the route. Measure independently observed evidence change separately from route movement. Refusal and deferral are never negative human behaviour and never earn pressure.
3. **Least burden versus strongest evidence.** First require source capability, permission, freshness and use-specific sufficiency. Only then minimise burden, cost and interruption among eligible routes. A cheap incapable source does not win.
4. **"Stop when contradicted" versus epistemic integrity.** Stop silent promotion or resolution, preserve the contradiction, and either seek the smallest admissible resolving evidence or abstain. Do not let first arrival decide truth.
5. **Open-ended continuation versus explicit agreement.** Remove the hard day-thirty expiry, but require an auditable human transition into `continuing`. Continuation never renews a source grant, audience, retention period or delivery approval.
6. **Public-source reuse versus Brain isolation.** Reuse immutable public source and assertion references only. Recompute applicability, sufficiency, contradiction and standing inside each authorised case; never share a customer's mutable interpretation.

## Smallest architecture repair set

These are four changes to the existing R1 architecture, not four new products or stores.

### 1. Add one canonical R2 extension map and derived-artifact envelope

Map every R2 logical object onto the existing R1 domains and say whether it is canonical state, an append-only event, a proposed record or a rebuildable projection. At minimum:

- `engagement_period` extends the R1 engagement;
- `question_plan` extends the existing question plan;
- `answer_receipt` is the R1 answer event, not a second answer authority;
- `session_opportunity` is an intervention-candidate subtype; and
- `intervention_delivery` resolves to the existing interruption decision, delivery intent and receipt path.

Apply one shared envelope to every decision-shaping derivative. It must bind workspace, subject, decision and accepted frame versions; evidence namespace; exact source and assertion references, hashes and spans; root provenance and independence; claim kind and source capability; causal standing; one trusted `as_of`; supporting and contradicting assertions; audience and purpose; authority or permission grant; sensitivity, retention, expiry and revocation; applicability; use-specific sufficiency; and input version watermarks. Unknown, missing or mismatched bindings fail closed. Do not duplicate R1 source, assertion, Brain or event stores.

Exact numerical interaction defaults such as five versus seven scale points and the five-item ranking threshold must remain versioned prototype policy, not founder-locked empirical truth. Lock the semantic rule that control and construct must match.

### 2. Add one executable authority and lifecycle transition policy

Use R1's append-only event contract. Define allowed engagement, intervention, learning, capture and delivery transitions with actor, authority, preconditions, version match, idempotency key and before/after references.

The policy must make these consequences explicit:

- entry into `continuing` requires current, auditable agreement by the relevant humans and a named next decision or earned value, checkpoint and exit condition;
- preparing, intensive proof, continuing, paused, closing, released and reopened each have explicit capabilities for read, research, capture, question, session, delivery, learning, export and repair;
- reopening revalidates identity, grants, standing, audience and freshness and cannot revive expired authority;
- correction, expiry, revocation and erasure are distinct events with deterministic quarantine, recomputation or residue-aware deletion across sources, claims, Brain versions, indexes, coverage, questions, sessions, queues, capsules and releases;
- every new or recomputed leader-facing intervention returns to `proposed`; Krish's version-matched approval, edit, hold or suppression is required before delivery, and any material edit invalidates approval;
- sensitive capture or questioning requires action-time, purpose-specific participant permission, audience and retention disclosure, and a no-adverse-inference decline route; and
- an ordinary answer may update case evidence, but a material change to human-owned purpose, success or kill conditions, human boundary, durable judgement or learning remains a proposal unless the respondent is the named authority and the consequence was explicit before commitment.

### 3. Add one total, bounded, value-gated intervention selector

Create one versioned planner boundary over references to the canonical state above. It accepts typed decision, requirement, coverage, authority, lifecycle, current-intervention and budget inputs and returns exactly one of `reuse`, `enrich`, `ask`, `session` or `abstain` with stable reason codes, unresolved gap, expected material effect, rejected alternatives, expiry and replanning trigger.

The selector must include:

- deterministic precedence for identity, authority, audience, validity, sensitivity and decision deadline;
- a decision-value admission record naming the accountable human, live alternatives or commitment, baseline route, material exposure, reversibility, uncertainty CTRL may reduce, intended decision delta and observable success, kill or revisit condition;
- pre-registered source capability, sufficiency, independent-root and counterevidence rules before burden is compared;
- wall-clock, source-call, retrieval-hop, token or cost, and interruption budgets, plus timeout, bounded retry, partial result, cancellation and stale-result rejection;
- deduplication, coalescing, maximum active work, expiry, snooze and suppression semantics for the pull-only operator portfolio; and
- total ambiguity, missing-data and invalid-input behaviour that abstains or holds rather than throws or guesses.

Model judgement may propose a candidate and rationale. Trusted code validates policy and state transitions. Question Yield remains an internal routing diagnostic: it must use an independently observed evidence or state delta, denominator, attribution window, cost and harm guardrails. A self-authored route mutation is not proof of value.

### 4. Complete the atomic intervention payload and one shared adversarial proof matrix

For a question, bind one version across exact visible wording, control payload, complete options or unit, write-in and premise-rejection paths, unknown/defer/refusal behaviour, pre-disclosed effect, answer-specific visible consequence, evidence and frame watermarks, sensitivity disclosure and approval receipt. For a session, bind the exact agenda version, leader-visible purpose and ability to reject or reframe it, decision-value delta, capture permissions, Krish approval and desired evidence or decision end state. The first operator frame remains one decision, one reason live time is earned and one primary action; depth discloses after it.

Use one table-driven proof suite across the four roots, not seven parallel harnesses. Expected routes and evaluator answers stay outside planner inputs. The repaired gate must at least reject or safely handle:

- a public job advert mutated into private priority or causation, future-dated evidence, duplicate provenance roots, stale cross-case reuse, wrong subject or audience, and an unresolved contradiction;
- continuation without a human receipt, expired authority after derivation, revocation after approval, pause with queued work, stale approval after recomputation, capture without participant permission, and correction confused with erasure;
- a schema-complete trivial decision, a low-value AI-adoption near miss, slow or failed enrichment, a stale result, duplicate session opportunities, malformed input and a safe novel case;
- an incomplete forced choice, wrong premise, missing unit or comparator, hidden required note, refusal followed by re-ask or escalation, and an answer that attempts to promote human-owned state; and
- abstention that preserves the gap and contradiction, blocks the withheld diagnosis from question or session wording, names the smallest admissible next evidence, and reopens only on its declared trigger.

The semantic oracle must compare the emitted effect with the independently observed state diff and a competent same-evidence baseline. The rendered experience gate must still separately test unaided answer or defer, consequence comprehension, actor and state distinction, progressive disclosure, failed-save recovery, one-handed phone use and child-level language without flattening the decision.

## What not to add or claim

Do not use this repair to choose final table names, UI concepts, vendors, models, connectors, notification channels or external infrastructure. Do not open external research, model spend, customer data, capture, delivery or production state. Do not create separate evidence, permission, lifecycle or session brains; extend the R1 canonical records and event path.

Even after the architecture repair, keep these claims unproven until their later gates: intuitive or magical experience, reduced burden from voice or prepared choices, planner accuracy on unfamiliar real decisions, improved decision quality against a real competent baseline, useful operator load at portfolio scale, permission-safe real sessions, Qualified Judgement Transfer, retention, willingness to pay and commercial value.

## Clearance condition

Freeze revised blueprint, machine contract, delta and proof inputs under new hashes. Run the single adversarial matrix first. Then obtain seven fresh isolated specialist verdicts against those exact bytes and repeat non-voting prosecution. Clearance requires every root invariant above to be machine-inspectable, every listed attack to fail safely, every preserved strength to remain, and no specialist to retain a valid veto. The current sealed verdicts remain history and cannot certify the repair.

The call is to keep the direction, repair the contract before implementation, and refuse both a cosmetic prose patch and a new architecture layer for every symptom.
