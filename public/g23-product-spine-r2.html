CTRL
Product direction·Founder review

Built through Mindmake

CTRL is a private AI Brain built with one leader over 30 days.

It helps Krish and the leader prepare the big decisions AI is forcing across their market, organisation, people and own role. The leader makes the call.

The company pays for a leader who can redesign how the business works with AI without surrendering purpose, judgement or quality.

Brain preparesResearch, evidence, options and the question worth asking.
Krish challengesMissing context, weak logic and whether to interrupt.
Leader decidesPurpose, what stays human, the final standard and what ships.

The product is the relationship

What happens in the 30 days

It starts useful, learns through real work and leaves as something the leader owns.

Arrives preparedPublic research and supplied context create a provisional starting point before the first session.
Works on the first real decisionIt does not wait to finish a profile before becoming useful.
Learns and repairsMeetings, choices, corrections and outcomes sharpen what may guide later work.
Leaves ownedThe leader can inspect and take away a readable Brain plus a machine-usable version.

This is more than AI with good notes. A folder stores information. A general AI uses the context it is given now. CTRL carries authorised judgement with its evidence, scope, corrections and traceable later use.
